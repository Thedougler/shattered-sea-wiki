# Al Fondale

![[Al-Fondale.png|Al Fondale, a warm canal-side tavern in Le Paludi with amber light spilling into the narrow waterway]]

## Overview

Al Fondale sits on a side canal off Le Paludi's lower channel, in the part of [[Calveno]] where the buildings lean close enough that the upper floors almost touch and the light comes in at an angle. It has been a tavern for at least three owners. [[Bice-Riva|Bice]] and [[Aldo-Riva|Aldo Riva]] have run it for twenty-two years. Aldo cooks. Bice manages the room. No changes have been made except to improve the food.

It is the kind of place that has been there long enough to stop advertising. People who eat here know about it. People who need to know about it find out.

> [!read-aloud]
> The canal narrows here until the stone walls on both sides are close enough to touch from a punt. Al Fondale is the building with the oil lamp burning amber in the ground-floor window and the smell of onions and rendered fat drifting through the half-open door. Inside: low ceiling, worn tables, someone moving in the kitchen at the back. The canal outside is quiet. In here it is warm.

## Description

The ground floor is one main room: eight tables, a low bar with three stools, and a kitchen taking up the back third behind a half-wall. The ceiling is original stone and has been there long enough to acquire a colour. The floors slope slightly toward the canal side, which Bice says is the building settling and has been settling this way since before she arrived and has not settled any further since.

The tables are worn to the specific smooth that comes from thirty years of elbows and plates, not from sanding. The lamps are oil, on iron hooks, replaced at dusk. The kitchen smell — rendered fat, onions, bread in a clay oven — reaches the door and past it.

Four rooms upstairs, reached by a narrow stair at the back of the main hall. Rooms are small, beds are clean, linens are changed. The floors are thin. During [[Il-Gioco-delle-Beffe|festival week]], the noise of the main room — and Le Paludi generally — comes straight through: boots, singing, someone very loud outside, the particular sound of a group of people who have been drinking since the afternoon. Bice does not apologize for this. She does charge more.

> [!dm]
> The cellar is below the kitchen, accessed through a door behind the back shelf of dry goods. It holds wine racks, root vegetables, and preserved stock. Against the north wall, behind a row of barrels set on a timber skid, is a stone panel with a catch mechanism — a specific stone pressed at a specific point releases the panel inward. The passage beyond connects to the Warren's drainage network within forty feet.
>
> Nona established this entrance two years ago. Bice was told once, did not ask questions, and has not raised the subject since. She redirects anyone who lingers near the cellar door with a second portion and a topic change.
>
> **Unstable condition:** A Dravosi watch officer has taken a corner table three evenings running. He is eating, not asking questions, but he is watching the room — specifically who comes and goes from the back. He has not connected anything yet. If he builds a pattern of who uses the cellar stairs over two more days, it becomes an actionable report.
>
> **Consequence:** The Rattle gets called. Nona hears. The entrance gets closed. The tavern gets watched indefinitely.
>
> **Link:** Perrin — Passage connection through Nona; Bice will confirm Perrin is known to Nona if Perrin identifies themselves.

## People & Operations

**[[Bice-Riva|Bice Riva]]** — Halfling, white-haired, barely clears the tabletops. Moves between them with the efficiency of someone who has crossed this floor ten thousand times. Puts food in front of people before they finish deciding. Calls everyone *caro*. Does not ask where people are from, what they are doing, or how long they intend to stay.

**[[Aldo-Riva|Aldo Riva]]** — Dwarf, white-bearded, runs the kitchen and comes out to talk about it. Has been in Le Paludi long enough to have watched the canal layout change twice. Has a story about all of it and tells them at the same unhurried pace. Bice manages the room; Aldo fills it with noise.

One regular server works the evening rush — a teenage boy named Lucio who has the bearing of someone trying very hard not to spill anything.

## Notable Areas

**The cellar stairs** — Behind the dry-goods shelf at the back of the kitchen. Bice uses this access several times daily for stock. Other people using it get a second helping.

**The corner table by the window** — The one with a sightline to the door, the bar, and the cellar stairs. Currently favoured by a Dravosi watch officer.

## Access & Security

Street-side entrance and a narrow canal-side door that Bice keeps latched from inside and uses for deliveries. No posted hours. If the window lamp is lit, the kitchen is running. If it is out, Bice has decided the day is done.

## Current Hooks

- The four rooms are full during festival week. Anyone looking for accommodation in Le Paludi will be told Al Fondale is booked — unless Bice decides otherwise.
- Bice will tell a Passage-credentialed visitor, quietly and once, that "the stairs are for residents." This is the signal that the cellar access is available.
