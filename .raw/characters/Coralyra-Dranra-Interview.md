# Coralyra Dranra

> **Status: Joining Season 2.** This character is not present in the current campaign arc. Do not introduce her or reference her as active until Season 2 begins.

## Summary

Coralyra Dranra is 87 years old, which is young enough for a sea elf to still be considered unfinished, and she would be the first to confirm that assessment while disagreeing with the implication. She is beautiful in the way that stops people mid-sentence and makes them forget what they were going to say. That usually gives her what she wants, until her voice does, and then there is no going back. She grew up in [[Halythion]], known and liked, moving through the city's rhythms with the ease of someone who fits. She was happy enough, until the thing that made her exceptional made her inconvenient.

Her voice has always done more than carry a tune. The Halythion elders noticed. They framed it as an evaluation, not an expulsion, but she knew the shape of what was coming before they said a word. She left on her own terms, more or less, carrying a salvaged trident she pulled from the wreck of a ship she never knew the name of. She has been finding a home ever since. She has not found one yet.

She is careful and deliberate in a crisis — thinks first, moves second, survives because of it. When she is genuinely afraid, she runs, and she does not apologize for that. The thing that breaks her deliberateness is not danger to herself. It is watching something she loves get hurt while someone talks over her. She has a circle of trust so small it is mostly aquatic. Sea turtles. A seal named Flappers who is currently out chasing mackerel and has no idea she is out here. She holds an ongoing debt to a hermit crab she accidentally evicted and has not yet settled.

Her musical gifts are not limited to what her voice can produce. She has not told anyone that. She was raised in the tradition of raw/ingested/Deep-Sashelas, the sea elf deity of beauty and the ocean, and she finds Umberlee genuinely admirable — not as a patron, but as a fact of nature. Absolute authority over a domain. No apologies. Coralyra understands that impulse better than she would admit. What she wants, at the bottom of it, is not to be ignored. She is not entirely sure yet whether that means being loved or just being impossible to look away from.

## History

Coralyra grew up in [[Halythion]], the hidden underwater city of the sea elves. She was well-liked, comfortable, moving through the city's rhythms with ease. Her training as an Aoidos *(ah-OY-dos)* — one of Halythion's living-law singers, keepers of the Aoide — was underway and nearly complete. An incomplete title she carries by default.

The elders noticed her voice was disturbing people. They called it an evaluation. She recognized the shape of what came next before they said it. She swam her morning coral route one last time, then left on her own terms.

She salvaged a trident from a downed ship she never knew the name of. She has been looking for a home ever since.

Along the way: a [[Sawek]] inked her (worst day of her life). She accidentally evicted a hermit crab and owes it a good shell. She made a seal named Flappers who currently does not know she is out here.

## Personality & Drives

- **Stops people.** Her appearance first, then her voice. People want to keep listening and cannot explain why.
- **Careful and deliberate in a crisis.** Thinks first, moves second.
- **Runs when genuinely afraid.** Does not apologize for it.
- **Circle of trust is mostly aquatic.** Sea turtles. Flappers. Not many people.
- **Red button:** Animal friends in danger while someone is talking over her. The thinking stops entirely.
- **Firm moral line:** Will not harm dolphins or other marine mammals.
- **Faith:** Raised in the tradition of raw/ingested/Deep-Sashelas. Admires raw/ingested/Umberlee for her absolute authority over her domain — not as a patron, but as a fact of nature.
- **Secret:** Her musical gifts are not limited to sounds she can make with her mouth. She has not told anyone.
- **Deepest want:** To not be ignored. To matter. Whether that means being loved or just being impossible to look away from — the campaign will clarify it.
