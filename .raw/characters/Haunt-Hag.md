## Lair Actions (Initiative Count 20)

- **House of Horrors:** The lair takes the illusory form of a location embodying the target's deepest fear. The target is Frightened, its speed drops to 0, and attackers have advantage against it.
- **Hide and Seek:** Teleport up to 60 feet within the lair and immediately take the Hide action.
- **Lights Out:** Fill the lair with magical darkness. Dispels automatically on the next lair action.

---

## Lore

A fey hag who hunts through fear rather than force. She reads the unique terror of each intruder the moment they enter her lair, then plays the encounter like theatre — slow, building dread punctuated by sudden shock. She transforms herself into the thing each victim fears most, making every fight intensely personal. Encounters with a Haunt Hag are designed around her learning the party's fears early and weaponizing them.
