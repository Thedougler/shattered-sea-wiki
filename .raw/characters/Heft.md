# The *[[Heft]]*

## Appearance

A heavy brig, the largest ship in Fisk's Fleet after [[Red-Lady|the Red Lady]]. Broad in the beam and deep-keeled, high freeboard, the hull sitting low under its own weight. The hull was painted black from waterline to rail with white gun port lids. Ten gun mounts, five per side. She was a deliberate presence — nothing about her proportions suggested speed.

Below decks: a magazine below the waterline, a surgeon's berth, crew quarters for a full complement. The hold ran deep and wide. Heft was built to take punishment and hold position.
