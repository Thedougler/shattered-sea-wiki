# Pearl of Souls

## Description

One of the **Seven Pearls of Umberlee** — functional instruments of divine authority, not symbolic. Passively and continuously collects and holds the souls of those who drown within its proximity. Umberlee draws power from what it holds; its loss at depth reduced what she takes from the sea. The other six Pearls govern other aspects of her domain — their nature is not established.

---

## Current Location

*Red Lady* wreck site — eastern edge of the Shelfworks, where the shelf descends toward the trench floor. The wreck sits below the planar boundary: Umberlee's authority terminates above it, which is why she cannot retrieve her own Pearl. Still functioning, still collecting — holds all five crews from Fisk's fleet plus casualties from the Leviathan's incursion.

> [!secret] The Pearl's Contents
> What it does when recovered — and to whom the souls are delivered — is worth deciding before the party reaches the wreck site. It is not a neutral object.

---

## History

**Before the theft:** Held at a Waveservant shrine (location unspecified). Passive collection instrument for the recently drowned in nearby waters.

**The theft:** Stolen by [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Delmar-Fisk|Delmar Fisk]], who didn't know what it was. Shrine Waveservants know this. Waveservants elsewhere know a Pearl was stolen — none will confirm details without significant reason.

**Umberlee's response:** Sent the storm that sank the *Red Lady* in rage, before accounting for Maw waters. The storm took all five fleet ships simultaneously. The Pearl — still aboard — collected all five crews as they drowned. The *Red Lady* sank below the planar boundary before she could retrieve it. Souls and Pearl both lost to the Maw.

To prevent the captains' souls from being consumed, she pressed them into Fisk — the nearest survivor. The five captains are in his mind; the five crews remain in the Pearl. She made her own situation worse.

---

## The Maw Entanglement

The Pearl's soul-collection signal crosses the planar boundary and is detectable from the Elemental Plane of Water. The Leviathan's incursion was not random — it followed the signal. Every subsequent entity has followed the same beacon. [[Auralis]] woke specifically because the signal crossing the boundary triggered the incursion, not just because something large came through. The Leviathan is aware of the Pearl and has not moved to claim it — reasons not established.

- **Recovered:** signal stops; fissure reverts to passive geological anomaly. Most consequential single action available in Season 1.
- **Left in place:** continues broadcasting; next fissure event draws something larger.

---

## Aldric Risk

The [[Tessarine-Concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] believes [[Aldric-Drave|Aldric Drave]] could use the Pearl to expand his Hierarch abilities beyond his own bloodline. The artifact's ability to gather and hold drowned souls makes it a possible workaround for the central limit of Hierarch lichdom: the need for living descendants as phylacteries. In Tessarine threat models, Aldric with the Pearl might be able to stabilize himself with external souls, create non-bloodline anchors, or convert mass drowning into a phylactery resource.

That belief is why the Concordat moved through [[Chain-Council|the Chain Council]] to have [[The-Betrayal-of-Fisks-Fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] steal the Pearl. The stated Midchain rationale was protection from Umberlee's wrath. The hidden Tessarine rationale was containment: remove the Pearl from the shrine before Aldric could take it, then divert it into Concordat custody.

---

## The Admiral's Curse — Resolution Path

Umberlee wants: (1) Pearl returned from the Maw, (2) five captains released from Fisk's mind and delivered to her. She cannot retrieve the Pearl herself, and killing Fisk loses the souls again — he is holding something she needs and cannot take by force.

The Waveservants can broker a deal but won't raise it unprompted. Deal terms: Pearl returned, curse lifted, souls delivered — framed as Umberlee's generosity.

> [!secret] Waveservant Knowledge
> Senior Waveservant at Tidefall (Dockfront southern shrine) knows enough to broker this. If pushed on the *Red Lady* curse, she'll acknowledge an arrangement is possible and name a price — but won't admit Umberlee *needs* this rather than merely wants it. That distinction is leverage.

## Properties

One of the Seven Pearls of Umberlee. Passively collects and holds the souls of those who drown within its proximity — continuously, regardless of holder or location. Currently accumulating at the *Red Lady* wreck site. No attunement required; no activation needed. Its function cannot be suppressed. Its soul-collection signal crosses the planar boundary at the fissure and attracts entities from the Elemental Plane of Water.
