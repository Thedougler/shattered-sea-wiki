## Personality

### Personality Traits

Perrin is an experienced diplomat and, like most Fancy Rats, view social environments like children view a playground; a navigational joy.
He values harmony and likes to see everyone prosper when he can. He's also a sleazy rat by nature.
He loves fish, coin and the ocean.

### Ideals

Family comes first by any means. 
The best crumbs lie off the beaten path.
Socializing is a fun game, with big stakes

### Bonds

His only true loyalty is to Kin, but he understands the value of maintaining relationships and keeping as many active contacts as possible. 
...He probably likes Dry a lot more than he lets on.

### Flaws

He's good natured and kind, but a bit of a hoarder and a bit greedy for resources like food and coin. 
He can be manipulative to get the result he believes is best for his Kin/the greater good. Plays a little loose/fast.

Perrin has only a few things in this life that he is truly dedicated to; Family, Coin, the Sea and most importantly... Fish.

After years of learning the craft of merchants and swindlers from his mentors, then a few more amassing the capital and the connections to do so, he outfitted a fishing vessel with the best rigging, nets and crew he could muster. He spent the next year of his life in absolute bliss, eating fish every day, making heaps of coin and bringing both back to his beloved family.

Until one day, when Perrin caught something much larger than what he was looking for. To his horror, a giant ocean creature came to snack on his haul of fish and in doing so, ripped the vessel apart, sinking it in the process. He managed to make the long swim back to the closest shore and survive the ordeal.

## Treasure

Grief stricken and traumatized by the loss of not just his livelyhood, but his pride and joy, he spent weeks on the shore, begging for an answer... for a sign to go on.
From deep below, far deeper in the open oceans, beyond the creature that brought this upon him, a voice answered him. It gave him the power to smite the destructive creatures of the deep. To the voice, they are parasitic invaders.
The otherwordly voice is named Auralis, patron of the deep sea. The connection grows stronger when sea water is present.

From that day he ventured forth, seeking to build a crew, obtain a ship, and rid the Maw of such (a) havok-inducing monstrosity(ies).

....Then probably just go back to selling fish and making money for his family.
