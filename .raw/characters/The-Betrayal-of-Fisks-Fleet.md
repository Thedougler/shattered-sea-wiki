# Secrets of Fisk's Fleet

For public, spoiler-free player knowledge, see [[Fisks-Fleet|Fisk's Fleet]].

This page contains the hidden structure behind the fleet's destruction: the Pearl commission, the sinking mechanics, the soul consequences, and the wreck locations. Do not link this page from public pages.

---

## The Commission

The fleet's final commission came through [[Chain-Council|the Chain Council]]: the theft of the [[content/shattered-sea/items/Pearl-of-Souls|Pearl of Souls]] from Umberlee's shrine. The Council believed it was protecting Midchain waters from Umberlee's wrath. In truth, the Principal used the Council as the local authorizing mask because the [[Tessarine-Concordat]] wanted the Pearl kept away from [[Aldric-Drave|Aldric Drave]], who they believe could use it to expand his Hierarch abilities beyond his own bloodline. The captains executed a coordinated operation: two scout teams surveying the perimeter, one entry team inside the cave, one distraction team at the main entrance. The Pearl was secured. The fleet departed.

Umberlee's response came as fog first — so thick the water was invisible from the deck, surrounding all five ships simultaneously. Moments later a rogue trench opened in the fleet's path, plunging all five ships 80 feet down into a valley of water. The walls closed in and swallowed the fleet whole. [[Crissdalynn-Khinriss]] dove from the *Red Lady*'s crow's nest, grabbed [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Delmar-Fisk|the Admiral]], and flew straight back up — she had only risen 50 feet when the water closed in; momentum and buoyancy carried them both to the surface. They heard Umberlee's laughter on the wind, then her rage, as the ships went under.

## Soul Mechanics

All five ships sank simultaneously. The [[content/shattered-sea/items/Pearl-of-Souls|Pearl of Souls]] — still aboard the *Red Lady* — activated at the moment of sinking and collected the souls of all five ships' crews. The five captains' souls were pressed into [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Delmar-Fisk]] separately, by Umberlee's direct action, as punishment and leverage.

Fisk carries the five captains inside him. The crews are in the Pearl. Both the Pearl and the *Red Lady* are at the Shelfworks' eastern edge, at the point where the shelf descends toward the trench floor.

Rael was the only captain who entered the cave with Fisk; the other four commanded from outside. This proximity makes Rael's voice the loudest and most trusted of the five captains in Fisk's head.

**Delmar's return trigger:** A brass flagon — the sight or touch of one while any captain is surfaced brings Delmar back. Tied to the night after the mutiny when those five crewmates stayed: stranded, no ship, nothing left, one flagon passed around. The moment before the fleet existed. It anchors him to himself before any of this happened.

## Mortis — Captain Voices in Fisk

Each captain surfaces with distinct personality and a specific forced trigger. Full mechanics on [[Delmar-Sheet|Delmar's sheet]].

| [[Sienne-Orre\|Sienne Orre]] | Methodical, careful — slows down, wants more information | Arcana, Investigation | Targeted by spell 2nd level+, or fails Int save | A green mermaid sign or figurehead — quiet recognition; asks about the establishment |

Rael is the loudest voice — he was inside the cave with Fisk when the fleet sank. His forced trigger (ally at 0 HP) is also the one most likely to create compounding pressure in combat.

## Serafina — DM Notes

Serafina was not aboard the Red Lady when the fleet went down. She doesn't know Fisk is shipless and carrying five dead souls.

The marriage arrangement is open — physical encounters acceptable; the one line is denying each other's existence to get what you want. Fisk has never tested it. He knows she would know.
