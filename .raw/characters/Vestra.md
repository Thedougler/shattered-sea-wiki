![[The-Vestra.webp]]
> [!READ-ALOUD]
> A small two-masted brig rides low in the water, old-built and hard-worked, her topsides painted a deep green that has been reapplied so many times it no longer matches from plank to plank. At the prow stands a rat figurehead with one hand extended, palm up. She is compact, practical, and clearly modified for a small crew travelling light.

## Current State

The *Vestra* was [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw]]'s ship: a family heirloom he upgraded, crewed, and captained himself. She was destroyed pre-campaign when raw/ingested/Leviathan breached the hull from below on what began as an ordinary fishing day.

## Overview

A small two-masted brig, older than most Midchain traffic and sized well for a Rattkin captain and light crew. Perrin had re-rigged her and added two gun mounts before she was lost.

## Loss

The *Vestra* went down too fast for anyone aboard to understand what was happening. Perrin survived because of [[Perrins-Cloak-of-the-Manta-Ray]], and [[Auralis]] later found him in the water. The rest of the crew are assumed dead.

## Layout

The *Vestra* appears to have been fitted for a small independent crew rather than cargo hauling or line fighting. She offered only the spaces needed to sail, cook, sleep, and secure a little privacy for the captain.

### Weather Deck

Two-masted and compact, with Perrin's later gun-mount modifications and the distinctive rat figurehead at the prow. She reads as a vessel maintained for utility and personal ownership, not prestige.

### Belowdecks

Known spaces include crew hammocks, a compact galley, and a single locked cabin aft. The ship was fitted for a light crew moving without excess weight or comfort.

## Crew

[[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw]] captained and owned the vessel. The wider crew roster is not established. Those aboard when the Leviathan struck are assumed dead.
