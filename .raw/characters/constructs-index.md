# Constructs

Animated objects and magical sentinels. Typically stationary until a threshold is crossed.

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/constructs/animated-rug-of-smothering|Animated Rug of Smothering]] - A ward left among the wares at [[Cats-Curios|Cat's Curios]]; keep your hands where they can be seen
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/constructs/animated-armor|Animated Armor]] - Remnant sentinel of a sealed hall in [[Calveno|Calveno]]; motionless until a threshold is crossed
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/constructs/animated-broom|Animated Broom]] - Domestic construct in active service at [[Cats-Curios|Cat's Curios]]; considerably faster than it looks
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/constructs/animated-flying-sword|Animated Flying Sword]] - Freed guard-blade from a triggered ward in a sealed vault in [[Calveno|Calveno]]; does not distinguish between intruder and rescuer
