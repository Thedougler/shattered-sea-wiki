# Dragons

Dragons, dragon turtles, and great serpents. Most are apex threats — encounters are survival problems, not fights.

## Dragon Turtles

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/ancient-dragon-turtle|Ancient Dragon Turtle]] - Mythic deep-water predator known for boiling seas and scalding steam breath; found in the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]] and around [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]]
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/dragon-turtle|Dragon Turtle]] - Large enough to break a working boat; lurks at the edge of the [[content/shattered-sea/places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]] blue hole and eastern [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]]
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/young-dragon-turtle|Young Dragon Turtle]] - Dangerous enough to capsize small vessels; found where reef shallows give way to deep water
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/dragon-turtle-wyrmling|Dragon Turtle Wyrmling]] - Still large enough to wreck a skiff; sightings cluster around the outer beaches of the [[content/shattered-sea/places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]]

## Sea Serpents

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/ancient-sea-serpent|Ancient Sea Serpent]] - Cold-current predator that moves against the wind; haunts the deep lanes of the [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]]
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/young-sea-serpent|Young Sea Serpent]] - Follows warm currents through the [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] and along [[content/shattered-sea/places/tail/index|the Tail's]] shelf edge

## True Dragons

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/Ancient-Gold-Dragon|Ancient Gold Dragon]] - The oldest and most powerful of the metallic dragons; a legendary coastal presence
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/Adult-Gold-Dragon|Adult Gold Dragon]] - Commanding metallic dragon of coastal and island territories
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/ancient-bronze-dragon|Ancient Bronze Dragon]] - Elder coastal dragon; commanding the storm-wracked shorelines and deep harbour approaches
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/adult-bronze-dragon|Adult Bronze Dragon]] - Coastal dragon with an affinity for the sea; a serious threat in harbour and reef waters
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/ancient-black-dragon|Ancient Black Dragon]] - Apex swamp predator; old enough to have watched islands rise and sink
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/adult-black-dragon|Adult Black Dragon]] - Swamp and mangrove predator; territorial and corrosive
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/ancient-blue-dragon|Ancient Blue Dragon]] - Elder of the coastal cliffs and desert headlands; lightning breath that splits masts at range
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/adult-blue-dragon|Adult Blue Dragon]] - Coastal and desert dragon; ambushes from heights with lightning
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/ancient-green-dragon|Ancient Green Dragon]] - Old-growth forest predator; poison breath and a talent for manipulation
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/dragons/adult-green-dragon|Adult Green Dragon]] - Forest dragon; hunts the dense interior canopy of large islands
