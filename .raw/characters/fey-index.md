# Fey

Creatures with ties to the Feywild. Goblins are the primary fey threat encountered in the Scatter's interior islands.

## Goblins

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/fey/goblin-boss|Goblin Boss]] - Commands a goblin warband; smarter and more dangerous than the warriors it leads
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/fey/goblin-warrior|Goblin Warrior]] - Standard goblin raider; dangerous in numbers, quick to flee when the odds shift
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/fey/goblin-minion|Goblin Minion]] - The weakest goblin variant; used as fodder and distraction
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/fey/goblin-hexer|Goblin Hexer]] - Spellcasting goblin that curses and debilitates with fey magic; found alongside warbands

## Hags

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/fey/Gentle-Hag|Gentle Hag]] - CR 7 charm specialist; builds permanent thrall networks through persistent enchantment
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/fey/Haunt-Hag|Haunt Hag]] - CR 5 fear-and-illusion predator; reads victims' deepest terrors and embodies them

## Other Fey

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/fey/Moucheron|Moucheron]] - CR 8 fey mercenary from the Plane of Faerie; demands payment in blood, not gold
