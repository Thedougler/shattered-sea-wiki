# Humanoids

Pirates and raiders. Found wherever ships go.

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/humanoid/pirate-admiral|Pirate Admiral]] - Fleet commander controlling a stretch of sea; operates the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]] trade lanes and the [[Galewall|Galewall]] lee
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/humanoid/pirate-captain|Pirate Captain]] - Dangerous enough to take a working vessel; operates across the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]], [[Shelfworks|Shelfworks]], and the approaches to the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/humanoid/pirate|Pirate]] - The common crew of every raiding vessel in the Scatter; found everywhere ships go
