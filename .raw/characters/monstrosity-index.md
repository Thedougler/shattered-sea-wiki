# Monstrosities

Creatures that defy easy classification — part animal, part nightmare.

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/monstrosity/roc|Roc]] - Nests on the high basalt sea stacks near the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]]; large enough to carry off a longboat
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/monstrosity/harpy|Harpy]] - Nests in the sea cliffs of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]], [[Meth-Var|Meth-Var]], and rocky stretches of [[content/shattered-sea/places/tail/index|the Tail]]; the song carries further than it should
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/monstrosity/mimic|Mimic]] - Shape-shifting ambush predator found in urban ruins and sealed vaults; looks like furniture until it isn't
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/monstrosity/Unfamiliar|Unfamiliar]] - CR 5 familiar-hunter; instinctively targets anyone who has bonded a familiar; each melee strike triggers a random uncontrolled spell
