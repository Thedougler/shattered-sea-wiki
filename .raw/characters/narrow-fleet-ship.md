# The *Narrow*

## Appearance

A scout sloop, narrow-hulled and single-masted, the lightest ship in Fisk's Fleet. Long on the waterline relative to her beam, low freeboard, built for speed and shallow survey work. The hull was painted dark grey. Two swivel gun mounts only — she was not built to fight. She sat low and quiet in the water and made very little wake at speed.

Below decks: crew hammocks, a chart table bolted to the forward bulkhead, and a shallow hold fitted with diving equipment and survey gear. [[Narrow|The Narrow]] was built to go places other ships couldn't and come back with information.

Scout and underwater survey vessel. Second ship of [[The-Betrayal-of-Fisks-Fleet]]. Captained by [[Maren]] (Water Genasi), who led the aquatic perimeter survey that located the sub-surface entry point to raw/ingested/Umberlee's shrine during the Pearl theft.

Sank simultaneously with all five fleet ships when Umberlee opened the Maw beneath them. Wreck lies on the Shelfworks descent near [[content/shattered-sea/situations/reference/Red-Lady]].
