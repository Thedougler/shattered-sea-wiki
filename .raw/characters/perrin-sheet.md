## Personality

### Personality Traits

Perrin is an experienced diplomat and, like most Fancy Rats, view social environments like children view a playground; a navigational joy. 
He values harmony and likes to see everyone prosper when he can. He's also a sleazy rat by nature. 
He loves fish, coin and the ocean.

### Ideals

"You don't take the cheese... you're given it."  
Family comes first by any means.  
The best crumbs lie off the beaten path. 
Socializing is a fun game, with big stakes

### Bonds

His only true loyalty is to Kin, but he understands the value of maintaining relationships and keeping as many active contacts as possible.  
...He probably likes Dry a lot more than he lets on.

### Flaws

He's good natured and kind, but a bit of a hoarder and a bit greedy for resources like food and coin.

## Backstory

Perrin grew up in the Tangle — the family network at the heart of Warren, the only fixed Rattkin settlement in the Shattered Sea, built beneath the city of index. He was raised on the practical lessons of the Run: read people, build capital, stay useful, know which doors to use and which ledgers to stay out of. The Dravosi Crown keeps standing bounties on Rattkin that are rarely enforced and always present. He learned early what that background pressure means for how you move through the world. 

He was good at the Run’s lessons. Contacts became capital, capital became crew, crew became a ship. The ship had belonged to his grandfather — Nona Black-Jaw’s late husband built and captained her, then left her to the family when he died. When the Vestra passed to Perrin, it came with the weight of that inheritance and the responsibility of the route. He upgraded her, added gun mounts, crewed her himself, and ran a fishing operation. 

He genuinely loved the fishing. That tends to surprise people who meet the diplomat first, but it was always the point. The contacts and the capital were how you got to the part where you could drop a line into deep water and not need to be anywhere.
