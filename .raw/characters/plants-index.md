# Plants

Fungal colonies and animate plant-life. Primarily found in the deep underdark beneath the Scatter.

- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/plants/myconid-adult|Myconid Adult]] - The working body of a myconid colony; communicates through spore clouds and defers to its sovereign
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/plants/myconid-spore-servant|Myconid Spore Servant]] - A corpse reanimated by myconid spores; retains no personality, only function
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/plants/gas-spore-fungus|Gas Spore Fungus]] - Explosive underdark fungus that resembles a beholder at a glance; the resemblance ends when it detonates
- [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/plants/myconid-sovereign|Myconid Sovereign]] - CR 2 fungal colony leader; projects animating spores to reanimate dead as servants
