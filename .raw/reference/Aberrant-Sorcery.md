# Aberrant Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Psionic power derived from alien influence: a psychic wind from the Astral Plane, Far Realm exposure, or an unfinished mind flayer transformation.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Psionics-adjacent aberration magic with Warlock-style spellcasting and body horror.

**Strengths:**

- Psionic Spells list consistently excellent: Mind Sliver is a fantastic offensive cantrip, Hunger of Hadar is outstanding AOE + control
- Psionic Sorcery saves 1–2 Sorcery Points per spell and grants Subtle Spell effects for free
- Revelation in Flesh is cost-efficient: one Sorcery Point replaces 2nd-level spells (flight, swim, invisibility, escape)
- Psychic Defenses protects against charm and fear (common effects)
- Warping Implosion sets up devastating combo turns (teleport + quickened AOE damage/control)

**Weaknesses:**

- Telekinesis at Level 9 is the only telekinesis option, and telekinesis spells are situational
- Rary's Telepathic Bond seems redundant with Telepathic Speech (though Telepathic Speech is extremely limited)

---

## Features

**Psionic Spells (Level 3).** Always-prepared spells by level:

- 3: Arms of Hadar, Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Mind Sliver
- 5: Hunger of Hadar, Sending
- 7: Evard's Black Tentacles, Summon Aberration
- 9: Rary's Telepathic Bond, Telekinesis

**Telepathic Speech (Level 3).** Bonus Action: form a telepathic link with one visible creature within 30 feet. Link lasts Sorcerer level in minutes, range = Charisma modifier in miles (minimum 1). Ends early if you link to a different creature.

**Psionic Sorcery (Level 6).** Cast any level 1+ Psionic Spell by spending Sorcery Points equal to the spell's level instead of a spell slot. When cast this way, requires no Verbal, Somatic, or non-consumed Material components.

**Psychic Defenses (Level 6).** Resistance to Psychic damage. Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Charmed or Frightened condition.

**Revelation in Flesh (Level 14).** Bonus Action: spend 1+ Sorcery Points to alter your body for 10 minutes. Each point spent grants one benefit:

- *Aquatic Adaptation:* Swim Speed = twice Speed, breathe underwater.
- *Glistening Flight:* Fly Speed = Speed, can hover.
- *See the Invisible:* See Invisible creatures within 60 feet not behind Total Cover.
- *Wormlike Movement:* Squeeze through 1-inch spaces; spend 5 ft to escape nonmagical restraints or Grappled.

**Warping Implosion (Level 18).** Magic action: teleport to a visible unoccupied space within 120 feet. Each creature within 30 feet of your departure point makes a Strength save (your spell save DC) — fail: 3d10 Force damage and pulled toward the empty space; success: half damage only. Once per Long Rest; restore early by spending 5 Sorcery Points.
