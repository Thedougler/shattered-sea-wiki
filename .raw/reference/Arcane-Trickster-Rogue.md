# Arcane Trickster

Brings magic to the Rogue. Adding spellcasting makes the class more complex to build and play but also extremely effective. Can use spellcasting for buffs, illusions, utilities, or offensive scaling through True Strike and weapon attack spells.

## Core Features

**Spellcasting**
The Arcane Trickster's defining feature. Spell selection is critical:

- [[Sneak-Attack|Sneak Attack]] only works with weapon attacks using Finesse or ranged weapons, not spell attacks.
- Offensive options: raw/ingested/Booming-Blade, raw/ingested/Shadow-Blade, raw/ingested/True-Strike
- raw/ingested/True-Strike is uniquely interesting: allows weapon attacks using Intelligence (your spell ability), enabling focus on spellcasting over pure Dexterity. Provides scaling damage bonus that stacks with Sneak Attack.

**Mage Hand Legerdemain**
Control your Mage Hand as a Bonus Action (instead of an action). Enables:

- Picking locks and picking pockets while fighting, casting, or doing other things
- Invisible hand sends it unnoticed to perform actions while you and allies hide or fight
- Frees your action economy substantially for multi-tasking

**Magical Ambush**
When you hit with a spell attack while raw/ingested/Invisible, the target has Disadvantage on the saving throw. Since your spell DC won't match dedicated spellcasters, this significantly improves reliability. With 14+ Intelligence, your saves are roughly as reliable as a Wizard with 20 Intelligence.

**Key mechanic**: Invisibility from successful [[Hide]] action (via [[Cunning-Action|Cunning Action]]) triggers Magical Ambush on the same turn. Does not work on repeated saves (Hold Person) or saves triggered on target's turn (Web).

**Versatile Trickster**
Your Mage Hand can distract enemies (impose Disadvantage on attack rolls). You don't benefit directly, but allies benefit greatly in melee.

**Spell Thief**
Steal spells from enemy spellcasters. In games with few spellcaster encounters, alternative: "steal" spells from your party's non-Wizard spellcasters instead.

## Optimization Notes

- True Strike enables Intelligence-based builds, trading some AC and skill effectiveness for spellcasting focus.
- Spell selection matters more than raw spell power; prioritize utility, control, and weapon-synergizing spells.
- Magical Ambush requires Invisibility (real or Invisible condition). Cunning Action + Hide is your turn 1 setup.
- Mage Hand Legerdemain enables massive action economy gains; use it for locks, traps, and theft while fighting.
- Spell Thief is situational but powerful when available.
