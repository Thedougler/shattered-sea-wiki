# Assassin

Focused almost exclusively on dealing damage. Leans into the Rogue's role as Striker. Features enable substantial extra damage, but you're heavily dependent on acting early in initiative order. Against dexterous enemies, you may struggle to benefit from the biggest subclass features.

## Core Features

**Assassinate** (3rd level)
The Assassin's signature feature. All about going first and dealing massive turn-1 damage:

- **Initiative**: High Dexterity ensures you act early in initiative order (crucial for enabling Surprising Strikes).

- **Surprising Strikes**: Bonus damage on the first full round of combat. Not massive initially but scales throughout your career. Accessible and reliable—unless a poor dice roll on initiative prevents it entirely.

Applies during the first full round of combat, so if you manage a second [[Sneak-Attack|Sneak Attack]] in that window (difficult but possible with Battle Master allies), you can apply this twice.

**Assassin's Tools** (3rd level)
Proficiency in Disguise Kits and Poisoner's Kits:

- Disguise Kits: Situationally useful only
- Poisoner's Kits: Borderline useless without the Poisoner feat (which grants proficiency anyway)

**Infiltration Expertise** (9th level)
Two options:

- **Masterful Mimicry**: Impersonate NPCs convincingly. Situationally useful; depends on DM play-along willingness and your creativity.

- **Roving Aim**: Makes Advantage easier to obtain, ensuring you can use [[Sneak-Attack|Sneak Attack]] reliably on most attacks.

**Envenom Weapons** (13th level)
Cunning Strike (Poison) is great despite targeting Constitution saves. The bonus damage from this feature outright negates the damage cost to use it, making poison Cunning Strikes even better. Ignores Resistance to poison damage, but creatures with poison resistance almost always have Advantage on saves against poison.

**Death Strike** (17th level)
Poison a creature; if they fail a Constitution save, they take doubled poison damage. Damage is excellent when this works, but Constitution saves make it unreliable. Stack damage sources to maximize payoff: Basic Poison, buffs, raw/ingested/Booming-Blade, Piercer feat, etc.

No limit on uses per day, so deploy every encounter. However, suffers from the same limitation as Assassinate: if you lose initiative, it doesn't work at all.

## Optimization Notes

- **Initiative is everything**: Your entire subclass depends on acting first. High Dexterity, Initiative bonuses, and alertness are mandatory.
- **Poisoner's Kit is trap**: Skip it unless you really commit to the Poisoner feat.
- **Roving Aim is your only real option** at Infiltration Expertise unless your campaign emphasizes deception.
- **Envenom Weapons scales poison Cunning Strikes** to compensation territory. Poison is now viable damage.
- **Death Strike is high-variance**: Pile damage sources into your turn-1 strike. If you win initiative, it's devastating.
- **Con save scaling is your risk**: Building for Dexterity, you can't improve Death Strike's save DC. Accept the variance.
