# Battle Master

*Fighter subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Tactical fighters who augment weapon attacks with studied combat maneuvers. Uses a pool of Superiority Dice to fuel special techniques — tripping, menacing, riposting, and more. The most mechanically flexible of the Fighter subclasses.
