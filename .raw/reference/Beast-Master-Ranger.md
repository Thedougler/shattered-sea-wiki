# Beast Master

Beast Master, not beastmaster. You master beasts. You don't beastmast.

Commands a powerful beast companion in combat. The beast acts as both pet and weapon, with separate hit points and damage output matching the Ranger's own attacks. Effective as both Striker and Defender through unusual action economy.

## Core Features

**Primal Companion**
Your defining feature. The beast:

- Has a separate hit point pool, drawing damage from you and allies
- Attacks deal damage equal to your weapon attacks
- Stats scale with your Wisdom modifier
- Does not require summoning and remains permanent (can be revived with any spell slot)
- Functionally disposable if killed; easily replaced after a Long Rest

Commanding your beast uses your Bonus Action, directly conflicting with raw/ingested/Hunters-Mark. However, the beast's damage output compensates for this opportunity cost. At level 11, Bestial Fury makes the beast compatible with Hunter's Mark damage bonus, though by then the beast's raw attacks exceed Hunter's Mark value.

**Beast Options**

- **Beast of the Land**: 40 ft. land and climb speed. Iconic choice with Charge to knock foes prone.
- **Beast of the Sea**: 5-foot land speed limits effectiveness outside aquatic campaigns. Attacks grapple on hit.
- **Beast of the Sky**: 60-foot fly speed with Flyby. Lower hit points than other options; perpetually out of melee reach as both advantage and tactic limiter.

**Exceptional Training**
Bonus Action options (usable on Short or Long Rest):

- Disengage: Beast exits melee safely (but you want it tanking hits).
- Help: Grants Advantage on a high-value attack.
- Dodge: Significantly improves beast durability.
- Force Damage: Bypasses nearly all damage resistances.

**Bestial Fury** (11th level)
Doubles beast damage output. Allows Hunter's Mark to stack with beast attacks at this tier.

**Share Spells** (15th level)
Cast buff spells (Cure Wounds, etc.) on the beast with your spell slot, sharing effects.

## Optimization Notes

- Build around Wisdom: beast stats directly scale from your Wisdom modifier.
- Skip Hunter's Mark in multi-target encounters (you'll want the Bonus Action for beast commands). Use it only against single powerful foes.
- Force damage makes Exceptional Training's damage option more valuable as levels increase.
- The beast needs food, healing, and rest like any party member.
