# Calveno — District Layout

Calveno has no docks. Movement is by flat-bottomed boat (faster, 2–5 cp per trip) or on foot along canal walkways and bridges (free, slower, more exposure). The city runs east–west along the main canal with the Arsenal in the southwest and the Velo Quarter on a separate island north of the Bridge.

---

## Districts

### Mercatura

The commercial spine. The main canal runs through it from the harbour mouth inward; buildings press close on both sides; boats move in both directions. The Tessarine factor house anchors the harbour end — tallest building in the district, pale stone, no signage.

**Tone:** Loud, transactional, open. Numbers are said out loud. Nothing is confidential by default.

> [!read-aloud]
> The smell hits before the sound does — salt, rope, and something frying. Then the noise: boats on both sides of the main canal, voices calling across the water, the creak of rigging. At the harbour end, pale stone and no signage. The Tessarine factor house is the tallest building on the block.

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| Tessarine factor house | Harbour end. Tracks every deal worth tracking. |
| Moretti & Sons | Canal-front chandlery. Giacomo at the counter. |
| Waveservant Shrine | Canal-side. Umberlee tribute in full view of passing boats. |
| Beffa registration table | Festival week only. Bridge side of the Mercatura. |

**From the harbour:** The main canal leads directly in. Any boat entering Calveno comes through the Mercatura first. On foot, the widest walkway in the city — two abreast easy, three is crowd.

---

### The Bridge

Three-tier stone crossing at the main canal's midpoint. Wide enough for two boats abreast. Bottom tier: vendors, food, bodies. Middle tier: shuttered shops. Top tier: plain stone, narrow staircase that turns out of sight. You can hear voices up there; you cannot make out the words.

**Tone:** Transitional. Everyone passes through. No one stays unless they have business at a specific tier.

> [!read-aloud]
> The arch puts everything in shadow before you're under it. Bottom tier: vendors, food, bodies pressed close on both sides. A staircase leads to the upper levels and turns out of sight after three steps. Voices up there — words indistinct.

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| Ponte Bassa | Canal-level at the base of the Bridge. Oleandro's inn/tavern. Arrival memory, boat talk, hire. |
| Batta Zusto | Top tier. Accessible via the staircase only. Brokers unusual commissions. |
| Festival pinch point | Bottom of the arch — the crowd compression point the handlers have already timed. |

**From Mercatura:** Follow the canal east until the arch appears. Five minutes by foot, two by boat.

**From Le Paludi:** Twenty minutes on foot through the older canal network. Ten by boat through the covered channel.

---

### Le Paludi

The oldest part of the city. Buildings shorter, foundations settled unevenly. The channels narrow until two boats cannot pass without touching; the water moves differently — slower, darker. Numbers are spoken once, quietly. The canal walkways are tight enough that conversations are private by default.

**Tone:** Close, unhurried. The city's memory is here. Correct business done correctly; incorrect business done quietly.

> [!read-aloud]
> The walkway narrows past the arch — paving stones older, less even, the canal walls close enough to touch on both sides. The water here barely moves. Sound carries differently; a conversation two doors down is fully audible, which means yours is too.

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| Al Fondale | Side-canal tavern. Good food, cheap wine, keeper asks nothing. |
| Casa Lupo | General goods. Ettore prices from memory; has not been wrong. |
| Studio Orsini | Off the mapped path. Marta does not ask what the compounds are for. |
| Warren entry | Beneath Le Paludi. Access via Tangle routes; surface-visible only to those who know. |

**From The Bridge:** Ten minutes by foot through the narrowing canal network. The walkways get tighter and older; the smell shifts from salt and rope to green water and old stone. Five minutes by boat.

**From Arsenal:** Fifteen minutes by foot along the southwest canal. The Arsenal wall runs left; Le Paludi opens right where the fortified channel ends.

---

### The Arsenal

Walled shipyard in the southwest. A fortified canal separates it from the rest of the city — the gate is staffed. The pitch-and-timber smell reaches before the walls do. Non-House commissions: fourteen-month wait.

**Tone:** Closed. Entry requires a reason that holds up at the checkpoint. The Canal Commission papers check from [[content/shattered-sea/situations/islands/Calveno-Street-Encounters|Street Encounters 5]] is here.

> [!read-aloud]
> The pitch smell arrives before the walls do — sharp, fresh timber underneath it. The fortified canal on your left is still; nothing pleasure-craft moves there. The checkpoint arch appears before the wall proper. A guard with a ledger watches the water.

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| La Vasca | Private basin on the Arsenal's southern edge; not Canal Commission-flagged; Cobb operates here. |
| Carpenters' Slip | Just outside the Arsenal wall at the Le Paludi canal end. Savia Brentino; no Commission paperwork if she decides the job is urgent. |
| Acqua Nera | Outer harbour, south anchorage. Dario Vanni; hull conversion and repainting; double rate for quiet work; no Commission filing. |
| Arsenal gate | Dravosi Canal Commission checkpoint. Sergeant with a ledger. |

**Getting here:** From Le Paludi, follow the southwest canal until the fortified wall appears on the right. From the harbour, follow the outer canal south — Acqua Nera is at the tip, La Vasca is inside the south approach behind the Arsenal perimeter.

---

### Velo Quarter

A separate island attached by two low canal bridges. Lanterns at all hours. Music from more than one direction at once. Buildings painted in colours the rest of Calveno doesn't permit. Whatever authority governs the rest of the city stops at the bridges.

**Tone:** Permissive. The crowd is watching performances, not deals — and those two things are sometimes the same thing.

> [!read-aloud]
> The light changes before you reach the far side of the bridge — warmer, from lanterns hung at different heights. Two songs from different directions, not competing. The buildings here are painted in colours the rest of Calveno doesn't permit. Whatever governs this island, it is not the Canal Commission.

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| Cabinet of Morsani | Prospero's curio shop. Magical and exotic items; he knows the previous owner of everything. |
| Giacinto's theatrical booth | Wigs, prosthetics, ornamental beards, stage paint. Jean-Claude's moustache. |
| Cat's Curios | Referral only. No sign. Best artificer work in the Scatter. |
| Festival performance squares | Multiple stages active during the Beffa. Handler crowd timing was done here. |

**Getting here:** Two low canal bridges connect north from the Bridge district. Follow the main walkway north past the Bridge arch, take either span. Ten minutes from the Bridge on foot.

## Describing Movement

Use these when players say "we head to X":

**Mercatura → Bridge:**
> The canal narrows slightly as you leave the factor house's shadow. Vendors thin out before the arch comes into view. You hear the bottom tier before you see it.

**Bridge → Le Paludi:**
> The walkway narrows past the arch. The paving stones get older, less even. The canal walls on either side are close enough to touch both at once, and the water between them barely moves.

**Le Paludi → Warren (if authorized):**
> Tangle contact leads you through a door behind a waterwheel housing, down iron stairs to a landing that doesn't appear on any city plan.

**Anywhere → Velo:**
> The low spans have no toll. The light changes before you reach the far side — warmer, from lanterns hung at different heights. The music resolves from noise into two separate songs playing at once.

**Anywhere → Arsenal approach:**
> The fortified canal on your left is the signal. The water there doesn't move for pleasure craft. The checkpoint arch appears before the wall proper; the guard has a ledger.
