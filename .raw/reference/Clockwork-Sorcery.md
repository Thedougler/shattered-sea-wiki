# Clockwork Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Innate magic derived from precise, ordered nature — lawful magic that contrasts with Wild Magic. Borrows spells from Cleric and Wizard lists, providing tools for diverse problems through efficiency and order rather than chaos.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Order, rerolls, numerical minimums, and efficiency.

**Strengths:**

- Clockwork Spells list includes excellent options from Cleric (Aid) and Wizard (Summon Construct, Wall of Force)
- Spell selection covers a wide variety of problems
- Restore Balance solves Advantage/Disadvantage imbalances on saves
- Trance of Order guarantees minimum 10 on attacks, saves, and ability checks (1-minute duration)

**Weaknesses:**

- Heavily reliant on spell list; other features are situationally useful
- Trance of Order is underwhelming for Sorcerers: spell attacks mostly vanish past 2nd level, Sorcerers are Charisma-based so Persuasion in combat doesn't work well
- Clockwork Cavalcade (Level 18) only situationally useful — Heal outperforms, Repair rarely needed, Dispel is obsolete vs Dispel Magic

---

## Features

**Clockwork Spells (Level 3).** Always-prepared spells by level:

- 3: Alarm, Protection from Evil and Good
- 5: Aid, Knock
- 7: Dispel Magic, Protection from Energy
- 9: Freedom of Movement, Summon Construct
- 11: Greater Restoration, Wall of Force

**Restore Balance (Level 3).** Reaction when a creature you see within 60 feet makes a save against a condition you can see. You can flip the outcome:

- If the save granted advantage, remove the advantage.
- If the target has disadvantage, remove the disadvantage.

Limited uses (Charisma modifier); recharge on Long Rest.

**Bastion of Law (Level 6).** Bonus Action: grant a creature you touch a protective ward. Ward lasts until dispelled or triggered. When triggered, the warded creature reduces damage by 2d6 + Sorcerer level. Can have multiple wards active simultaneously.

**Trance of Order (Level 6).** Bonus Action: enter a 1-minute trance. Guarantee minimum 10 on attack rolls, saving throws, and ability checks. Once per Long Rest; restore early by spending 5 Sorcery Points.

**Clockwork Cavalcade (Level 18).** Magic action: instant effects appear:

- **Heal:** Restore up to 100 hit points to creatures you choose within 30 feet
- **Repair:** Repair one nonmagical object that is Large or smaller
- **Dispel:** Dispel all spell effects on creatures and objects within 30 feet (no ability checks required)

Once per Long Rest; restore early by spending 3 Sorcery Points.
