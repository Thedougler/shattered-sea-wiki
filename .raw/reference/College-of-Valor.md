# College of Valor

*Bard subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Bards who train in the College of Valor learn to fight alongside warriors, inspiring allies through martial deeds as well as music.

---

## Features

**Combat Inspiration (Level 3).** Bardic Inspiration die grants two uses:

- *Defense:* As a reaction when a creature with your die is hit, they add the roll to their AC — if the attack then misses, the die is consumed.
- *Offense:* After rolling a hit, add the Bardic die to the damage roll, then consume it.

**Martial Training (Level 3).** Proficiency with Martial weapons, Medium armor, and Shields. A weapon held may serve as a spellcasting focus.

**Extra Attack (Level 6).** Attack twice when you take the Attack action. One of those attacks can be replaced with a Bard cantrip that uses an action.

**Battle Magic (Level 14).** After casting a spell using an action, make one weapon attack as a bonus action.
