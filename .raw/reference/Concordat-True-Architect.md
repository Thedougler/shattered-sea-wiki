# What Is the Tessarine Concordat's True Architect

## Situation

The Tessarine Concordat is not a commercial empire. It is a hoard. Its true architect is a Magus Dragon who accumulates obligation — leverage over councils, trade routes, institutions, and inherited debt — rather than gold. The dragon has been building this for centuries and does not intend to reveal itself.

---

## Key Actors

- **The Magus Dragon** — true architect; the Concordat is its hoard; operates through Cosimo Verantio and the factor network
- [[Cosimo-Verantio]] — the public face the dragon wears when the hoard itself is threatened; white-bearded elderly factor; known only as "the Principal" to most of the Concordat; his signature appears on the oldest and largest debt instruments; never attends meetings unless the hoard is threatened
- [[Iacopo-Fieschi]] — Tessarine Envoy to Calveno; courtesy observer on the Seven Houses Council; holds leverage through six Houses' trade debt; actively extending a new credit line to one of the three holdout Houses with sweetened terms and no immediate conditions
- [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw]] — affected by the Calveno council deadlock above the Warren
- [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Jean-Claude-Tabarnack]] — strong fit for the Port Tidefall salvage contract

---

## The Hoard's Structure

The Concordat's factors are the dragon's Circle, but they do not know they are a Circle. They believe they are maintaining an institution. In practice, every contract, vote, route dependency, and inherited obligation feeds a hoard made of leverage instead of gold.

Where other Magus Dragons accumulate scholars, this one accumulates obligation. Destroying gold would not hurt this dragon nearly as much as invalidating debt, freeing councils from obligation, or making Tessarine paper unreliable.

The Vault of the First Factor — a hidden depository beneath Concordat territory — holds collateral from the region's earliest debtors. This is the nearest equivalent to a physical hoard.

---

## Immediate Secrets

The unusual money moving through Calveno's broker network toward the Maw is connected to a Tessarine-commissioned salvage operation targeting the Red Lady wreck. The public face is salvage financing. The hidden stakes are the Pearl of Souls, Fisk's captains, and whatever Concordat intelligence went down with the failed expedition. See [[Who-Commissioned-the-Theft]].

The Port Tidefall trade house is running a current Shearwall Reef salvage contract as deniable, profitable work. A factor there holds salvage manifests, processes cargo contracts, and can offer the party roughly 800 gp (40% share) for Shearwall Reef work.

---

## At-Table Reveal

Late-game Concordat play should reframe commerce as draconic hoarding without changing its texture. The contracts were always predatory. The reveal is that they were also treasure.

The Concordat's factors are sincere. They genuinely believe they maintain an institution. The most effective way to wound the dragon is not to burn the trade houses — it is to make the debt unenforceable, the councils ungrateful, and the paper worthless.
