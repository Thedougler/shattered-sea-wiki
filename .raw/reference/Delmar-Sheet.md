## Mortis: The Captains

> [!warning] Mortis Mark
> **Mark:** Disadvantage on all Wisdom saving throws. When a captain's trigger fires, that captain surfaces immediately — displacing whoever is currently active. Cannot dismiss a forced surfacing until the triggering condition fully resolves. Forced surfacing does not consume the short-rest use.
>
> **Gift:** Once per short rest (bonus action): surface one captain by name. Gain their two associated skills until you surface another or complete a long rest. If already proficient in a listed skill, gain Expertise instead.
>
> **Delmar's return trigger:** A brass flagon — seen or touched while any captain is currently surfaced. Delmar returns. The captain recedes. Does not interrupt a forced surfacing while its triggering condition is still active, but resolves immediately once that condition clears.

### Captain Voices

**Rael Corven** *(Red Lady)* — Steady, commanding, protective. Maternal and hard in equal measure. When Rael surfaces, Delmar stops performing and starts moving people. Less charm, more weight. Doesn't manage the room — fills it with authority.

**Maren** *(The Narrow)* — Sassy, warm, and impossible to talk over. When Maren surfaces, Delmar gets louder and less careful. The diplomatic posture drops and the opinions come out — about the Navy, about whoever is standing in the way, about the fact that there is always another door if you know where to look. She corrects her name. She corrects it every time.

**Detto Kalash** *(Heft)* — Blunt, physical, enduring. No angle, no charm — just what is true and what needs doing. When Detto surfaces, Delmar states facts plainly. "No" becomes a complete sentence. Will say the uncomfortable thing.

**Sienne Orre** *(Fernen)* — Methodical, careful, older than she had any business being on those routes. Slows down and wants more information before committing to anything. Asks questions that look like stalling. They are not.

**Bastian Crev** *(Loud Argument)* — Theatrical, confrontational, impossible to ignore. Made scenes as a leadership strategy. Goes all-in with total conviction. Cannot watch someone powerless get pushed around without making it everyone's problem.

## Backstory

Delmar grew up on the islands, raised by his mother — a woman the society around her considered second-class, who treated every person as having worth regardless of station. He never knew his father. What he knew of the man came second-hand from sailors; the pocket watch with his mother's picture inside, she had pickpocketed off him herself.

He came to the sea early, failing his way into loyalty across years of deckhand work. Five people from that first rough crew stayed with him through every ship and every rank. When he finally had five ships to command, those five captained them.

He stole the Pearl of Souls from one of Umberlee's shrines. She answered with fog, then a rogue trench that swallowed all five ships whole. Crissdalynn Khinriss dove through closing walls of water and pulled him clear. His five captains' souls were pressed into him as the hulls sank. He heard Umberlee's laughter on the wind, then her rage.

Two days on wreckage. Then the *Saltwright* found them.

The voices are always there. The mission is singular: resurrect the captains, or release them for peace.
