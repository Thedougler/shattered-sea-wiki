# Draconic Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Innate magic derived from draconic power — absorbed from a dragon's gift, a magic-infused site, a hoard artifact, or draconic ancestry. The iconic Sorcerer, perfectly complementing the core class with tools to close defensive gaps and staple spells for diverse challenges.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Draconic resilience, elemental affinity, and self-sufficiency.

**Verdict:** Generalist powerhouse. Won't excel in any single role compared to specialists, but hard to beat as a well-rounded subclass. Elemental Affinity predisposes blaster builds, but full breadth available.

**Strengths:**

- Draconic Resilience closes AC gap (permanent +CHA to AC, +3 HP per level)
- Elemental Affinity boosts single element damage significantly (Resistance + damage bonus)
- Strong spell list covers variety of challenges
- Dragon Wings and Dragon Companion provide high-level utility and combat options

**Weaknesses:**

- No major weakness; the strength is being generalist, not specialized

---

## Features

**Draconic Resilience (Level 3).** HP maximum increases by 3, plus 1 per additional Sorcerer level. While unarmored, base AC = 10 + Dexterity modifier + Charisma modifier.

**Draconic Spells (Level 3).** Always-prepared spells by level:

- 3: Alter Self, Chromatic Orb, Command, Dragon's Breath
- 5: Fear, Fly
- 7: Arcane Eye, Charm Monster
- 9: Legend Lore, Summon Dragon

**Elemental Affinity (Level 6).** Choose one damage type: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. Gain Resistance to that type. When casting a spell dealing that damage type, add Charisma modifier to one damage roll.

**Dragon Wings (Level 14).** Bonus Action: sprout draconic wings for 1 hour (or dismiss). Fly Speed 60 feet. Once per Long Rest; can restore early by spending 3 Sorcery Points.

**Dragon Companion (Level 18).** Cast Summon Dragon without a Material component. Can cast it once without a spell slot per Long Rest. Can modify it to not require Concentration (duration drops to 1 minute).
