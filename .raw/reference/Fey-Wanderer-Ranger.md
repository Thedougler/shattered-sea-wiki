# Fey Wanderer

Tricky and challenging yet very effective. Emphasizes spellcasting and Face skills over bow-wielding damage. Synergizes strongly with Wisdom-based Druidic Warrior builds and is often overlooked as a pure damage subclass because it rewards knowledge of mechanics.

## Core Features

**Dreadful Strikes**
Passive bonus damage on hits (Wisdom modifier per turn):

- Applies once per target per turn
- Damage type is psychic (rarely resisted)
- Free, no action cost, stacks with raw/ingested/Hunters-Mark
- Works on Opportunity Attacks
- Incentivizes hitting multiple targets rather than focusing fire

Roughly matches Hunter's Mark in single-target scenarios but provides more flexibility in multi-target encounters. Psychic damage avoids many resistances.

**Fey Wanderer Spells**
Spell list emphasizes mobility and control:

- **Misty Step** (2nd level): Best combat teleportation option. Bonus Action, short range, no line of sight needed.
- **Charm Person** (1st level): Save DC won't match dedicated casters unless you build Wisdom-heavy. No visual indicator of success.
- **Summon Fey** (3rd level): Fey AC and hit points are frail compared to full caster summons; fewer attacks. Rewards Druidic Warrior builds.
- **Dimension Door** (4th level): Better range than Misty Step, no line of sight. Useful for escaping or reaching locked areas. Higher spell slot cost.
- **Invisibility** (5th level): Out-of-combat scouting. Situational in combat.

**Otherworldly Glamour** (3rd level)
You learn one Face skill. Add your Wisdom modifier to all Charisma checks in addition to your Charisma modifier. Enables effective Face play without investing in Charisma, though 8 Charisma still penalties.

**Beguiling Twist** (3rd level)
Redirect charm and fear effects. Powerful in two ways:

- Defensively: Take charm/fear effects from enemies and redirect to other enemies (e.g., dragon frightened by its own Frightful Presence).
- Offensively: Capitalize on creatures that pass saves against allies' charm/fear abilities; force them to save against Beguiling Twist instead.

Best used with allies who reliably pass charm/fear saves (high Wisdom/Charisma, proficiencies, traits like Brave, Aura of Protection).

**Fey Reinforcements** (5th level)
Cast raw/ingested/Summon-Fey once per day for free (no material component). Fey stats scale from your Spellcasting Modifier. Alternative: cast shorter duration version without Concentration, though this strains Ranger's limited spell slots.

The free daily casting doesn't use a slot, enabling tactical synergy with same-turn leveled spellcasting.

**Misty Wanderer** (11th level)
raw/ingested/Misty-Step multiple times per day for free. Bring one ally along. Useful for rescuing grappled/restrained allies; enables mobility defensive play.

## Optimization Notes

- Build Wisdom-heavy (at minimum 14 Charisma to avoid dragging down Face checks).
- Use Dreadful Strikes to motivate multi-target tactics. Ranged weapons are mechanically optimal but may conflict with high-Wisdom build; consider two-weapon fighting or melee spells like Zephyr Strike.
- Summon Fey is a better use of Concentration than Hunter's Mark if built around Druidic Warrior and spellcasting.
- Beguiling Twist requires party coordination; most effective with charm/fear-capable allies.
- Misty Step is good on 100% of characters; this subclass makes it free.
