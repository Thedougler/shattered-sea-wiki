# Gloom Stalker

Fantastic Scout subclass, especially in darkness where Umbral Sight grants near-permanent invisibility. The subclass's highlight feature is its invisibility; spell list is disappointing and Dread Ambusher damage boost receives too few uses per day to feel impactful.

Gloom Stalker, not Gloomstalker. You stalk the gloom. You don't "gloomstalk".

## Core Features

**Dread Ambusher** (3rd level)
First-round bonus to damage and tactics:

- **Ambusher's Leap**: Modest mobility boost on turn one. Useful for rushing into melee.
- **Dreadful Strike**: 2d6 damage (scales to 2d8 at 11th level). Assumes ~14 Wisdom for two uses per Long Rest. Very underwhelming; better saved for critical hits than burned casually. Stalker's Flurry at level 11 significantly improves this.
- **Initiative Bonus**: Always useful.

**Gloom Stalker Spells**
Four of these spells are good; one is normally exclusive to Wizards/Artificers:

- **Minor Illusion** (1st level): Situational.
- **Rope Trick** (2nd level): Fantastic way to rest safely (normally Artificer/Wizard exclusive). High spell slot cost for Ranger's limited slots; avoid needing this.
- **Disguise Self** (2nd level): Requires good spell save DC; situational without caster investment.
- **Invisibility** (4th level): Amazing on stealthy characters. Backs up Umbral Sight when enemies bring light sources.
- **Greater Invisibility** (4th level): Maintain invisibility while attacking. Ideal fallback if Umbral Sight fails.
- **Fear** (3rd level): Requires good spell save DC; best used against encounters with numerous weak foes.
- **Dimension Door** (4th level): Escape utility.

**Umbral Sight** (3rd level)
The subclass's defining feature:

- Free Darkvision (if you don't have it)
- Invisibility to creatures relying on Darkvision alone (no action cost, Concentration, or restrictions)
- Functionally grants Improved Invisibility in total darkness

Most creatures ambushing via Darkvision lack light sources, making you effectively invisible. Even if enemies find a light source, extinguishing it restores invisibility. This is one of the best class features in the game—but only in total darkness.

**Limitations**: Does not work in dim light (candles, etc.). Blocked by truesight, tremorsense, blindsight—only Darkvision is fooled.

**Strategy**: Coordinate with party to ensure allies can fight without light, maximizing enemy reliance on Darkvision.

**Iron Mind** (5th level)
Gain proficiency in Wisdom and Intelligence saves. Commonly-targeted saves.

**Stalker's Flurry** (9th level)
Damage scaling is minimal; the rider effects are useful but don't occur often enough to feel impactful:

- **Sudden Strike**: Extra damage if enemies are adjacent. Good for multi-target tactics even if targets aren't under Hunter's Mark.
- **Mass Fear**: Decent control, but poor spell save DC without caster investment.

**Shadowy Dodge** (11th level)
Reaction ability (once per round). Powerful insurance if enemies attack while you're not in total darkness or running Improved Invisibility. Synergizes well with Umbral Sight's high-risk/high-reward invisibility.

## Optimization Notes

- Prioritize darkness and Darkvision-reliant enemies. Use light manipulation (spells, allies with darkness abilities) to maintain invisibility advantage.
- Coordinate with party to operate effectively without light.
- Dread Ambusher's Dreadful Strike is weak until level 11. Save for critical hits or skip entirely.
- Rope Trick is a luxury; avoid needing it.
- Invisibility and Greater Invisibility back up Umbral Sight when enemies bring light sources.
- Shadowy Dodge is your reaction safety net for turns outside total darkness.
