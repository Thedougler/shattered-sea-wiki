# Great Old One

*Warlock subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Warlocks bound to an inscrutable entity from beyond the stars or beneath the deep — something vast, ancient, and fundamentally alien. Gains telepathy, expanded knowledge of minds and aberrations, and eventually the ability to enter or influence other creatures' dreams.
