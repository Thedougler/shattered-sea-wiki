# Hexblade

*Warlock subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014 edition)*

Pact with a shadowy Shadowfell entity that manifests in sentient magic weapons (possibly the Raven Queen). Turns Warlocks into effective melee combatants using CHA for weapon attacks.

## Features

**Hexblade's Curse (Level 1).** Bonus action: curse a creature within 30 ft for 1 minute (or until target dies / you die / incapacitated). While cursed:

- +Prof bonus to damage rolls vs. target
- Critical on 19–20 vs. target
- If target dies: regain HP equal to Warlock level + CHA modifier (min 1)

Recharge: 1/SR or LR.

**Hex Warrior (Level 1).** Proficiency with Medium armor, Shields, and Martial weapons. Once per Long Rest, touch one non-Two-Handed weapon you're proficient with — use CHA for attack and damage rolls with it until next Long Rest. Extends to all Pact of the Blade weapons if you later take that invocation.

**Accursed Specter (Level 6).** When you slay a humanoid, raise it as a Specter (bonus THP = half Warlock level, attacks get +CHA mod). Lasts until next Long Rest. 1/LR.

**Armor of Hexes (Level 10).** When the cursed target hits you with an attack, reaction: roll d6 — on 4+, the attack misses.

**Master of Hexes (Level 14).** When the cursed creature dies, immediately transfer the curse to another creature within 30 ft (no HP regain for the death).
