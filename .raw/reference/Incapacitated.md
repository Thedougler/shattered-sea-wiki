# Incapacitated

## Rule Text

While you have the Incapacitated condition, you experience the following effects.
***Inactive.*** You can't take any action, XPHB.
***No Concentration.*** Your Concentration is broken.
***Speechless.*** You can't speak.
***Surprised.*** If you're Incapacitated when you roll XPHB on the roll.

## Context

Standard D&D 5e condition. Applies when effects impose this status.
