# Kalowe — The Bad Receipt's Cache

## Situation

Two seasons ago, [[The-Bad-Receipt|the Bad Receipt]] — a Kalowe-linked schooner with a flexible relationship to cargo manifests — took a merchant prize in the Central Strait and ran for the Midchain. Her pilot, knowing the Crown patrol routes better than anyone alive at the time, led them to a nameless low rock 3 miles east-southeast of the Kalowe gap and they offloaded nine crates there while the heat died down. The pilot died four months later of a fever in Veth. The Bad Receipt's captain, Orina Fale, has been operating without being able to collect the cache ever since — nobody else aboard knew the exact rock.

A fisherman named Damu saw them anchor there.

He has been sitting on this information for two seasons, trying to decide if selling it would get him killed. Damu is twenty-two years old, has a boat and a family, and is reasonably sure the answer is "probably not if I sell to someone who can actually reach the rock first." He's been drinking at [[The-Flat-Water|The Flat Water]] for three days, working up to it.

The Bad Receipt sailed into Kalowe this morning. Orina Fale is on the causeways, quietly asking the same question she's been asking for eight months — does anyone know where her old pilot's last job took him.

---

## Current State

**Damu** is at The Flat Water, second table from the terrace, nursing an ale. He is visibly nervous. Reweti Kahurangi has noticed but says nothing. If the party looks like capable operators who are not affiliated with any Kalowe crew Reweti has reason to distrust, she will catch their eye and nod toward him. She won't say why.

Damu will sell the rock's location for 50gp. He wants coin, not a cut — a cut means involvement, and he wants no involvement. He'll describe the rock precisely: a flat limestone shelf, barely above waterline at low tide, with a single wind-bent palm and a notch on the eastern face where the Bad Receipt tied off. He marked it on his own chart and will sell a traced copy.

**Orina Fale** — the Bad Receipt's captain — is on the second causeway today with two crew. She is asking harbour workers and rope-pullers if anyone remembers a small job Vell Kamu (the dead pilot; coincidentally, no relation to Davu) did before he went to Veth. She is not offering money yet. She will.

**The window:**
If the party moves immediately (that day), they reach the rock with a 12-hour head start. The Bad Receipt is a faster ship than the *Surety* in open water but she's currently at anchor, and Fale doesn't have the location yet.

If the party waits until the next morning, Fale will have found someone who remembers something — not the exact rock, but enough to narrow it to the eastern approaches. She'll reach the rock 4 hours after the party, which may or may not matter depending on how fast they work.

If the party waits two days, Fale finds the rock herself. The cache is gone.

## Key Facts

- The rock is 3 miles east-southeast, 45 minutes at typical small-boat pace
- The nine crates are above the waterline, covered with salvaged canvas and weighted with stones — rough concealment, still intact
- No guardians on the rock; no traps; the Bad Receipt never expected to be away this long
- The crates are heavy — moving all nine requires multiple trips or a large boat

## DM Notes

**The Rock:**
A flat limestone shelf, 60ft wide, barely a foot above waterline at mean tide. The single palm is real and recognisable. The crates are on the eastern face behind a natural rock shelf. They are not hidden cleverly — they are hidden quickly. Anyone who lands on the rock and looks around will find them in 5 minutes.

**The cargo:**
- 3 crates: spice (pepper, nutmeg, clove) — 300gp wholesale to Taufa Fifita & Sons
- 3 crates: undyed trade cloth — 200gp wholesale
- 3 sealed crates: silver plate tableware, Dravosi merchant mark — 300gp wholesale (or 350gp to a non-Kalowe buyer who doesn't care about the provenance)
- Total: 800gp wholesale through Taufa (he'll take 10% for moving it quietly = 720gp net)

**Selling options:**
- Taufa Fifita & Sons: 720gp (10% cut, no questions, 2-day wait for buyers)
- Sell directly at the harbour: 680gp (slightly less; fewer buyers, faster)
- Hold the silver plate and sell elsewhere: 350gp for those 3 crates at any port without Dravosi factors

**If the Bad Receipt arrives while the party is loading:**
Orина Fale's schooner carries 8 crew + Fale. She has a light cannon (not a broadside ship — one swivel gun, 1d10 bludgeoning, 1/round). She will not open fire at the rock — she wants the cargo undamaged. She will hail the party and demand to know what they're doing. She is not stupid and she is not a murderer; she is a businesswoman who is angry. If the party is clearly armed and capable and already has most of the cargo, she will negotiate — she wants the silver plate specifically (it's the identifiable piece) and will let the rest go for the right price.

**Orина Fale (NPC):**
Captain of the Bad Receipt. Midchain mixed heritage, forties, sharp-featured, always dressed well for a pirate. Runs her operation on business logic. Violence is expensive and messy. She respects people who make sensible decisions under pressure.

*If the party negotiates:* She'll trade the spice and cloth for the silver plate + 100gp. Net for the party: 500gp + 100gp = still decent.
*If the party refuses:* She does not attack. She makes a note and leaves. She will remember the party next time they need a reef pilot or a fast ship in the Midchain.
*If the party tries to fight her at sea:* A swivel gun and 8 crew versus the party on an open rock with no cover. This is not recommended.

**Damu's concern:**
Damu will not be at The Flat Water when the party returns. He will have quietly gone home. He does not want to know how it went. If the party asks Reweti, she will say he had fishing to do.

## Triggers

- Party asks Reweti about work, news, or local opportunities

- Party notices the Bad Receipt in the harbour and asks who she belongs to

## Consequences

- **Full success (party moves fast):** 670gp net; no confrontation; Damu is fine; no one in Kalowe is sure what happened
- **Partial success (Fale arrives during loading):** Negotiated split; party gets ~500gp; Fale respects them
- **Delayed (Fale gets there first):** Cache is gone; Damu got paid for nothing and feels bad about it; Fale is satisfied and in a better mood around the party in future ports
- **Party sells Damu's information to Fale instead:** Fale pays 80gp for confirmed location; Damu learns this has happened within a week; he does not forget

## Open Questions

- Does the party want a relationship with Orina Fale going forward? She is a useful contact in the Midchain.
- Does anyone investigate what's in the sealed silver plate crates more carefully? (One of the three contains an engraved service bearing a noble house seal — potentially worth 200gp to the right buyer, or trouble if the wrong people recognise it.)
