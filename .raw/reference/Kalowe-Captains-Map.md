# Kalowe — The Captain's Map

## Situation

Sera Maddock, seventy-one years old and three days from dead by Ilaria Grimaldi's estimate, is occupying the dispensary's back room on a charitable arrangement neither of them discusses out loud. She was a privateer on the Dravosi-licensed runs forty years ago, back when the Crown was paying letters of marque to anyone who could sink an Imperial merchant and had a ship to do it with. Her crew buried a cache from one of those runs on [[Orak]] — a small eastern reef island — and she is the last person alive who knows where.

Half the chart is tattooed on her left forearm. She burned the other half ten years ago when the man she was going to share it with tried to sell her to the Crown instead. The tattoo half shows the eastern face of Orak, a sea-cave entrance marked with a compass bearing, and the depth of the cache in fathoms. It is enough to find it.

She will sell the information for 200gp and passage out of Kalowe on whatever ship the party has access to. She does not want to go to Tidefall. She is not particular beyond that.

---

## Current State

Grimaldi has not told anyone about Sera directly. She mentioned, to the party when they bought supplies, that she has a patient with an unusual forearm tattoo. That is all she will say unprompted. If the party follows up and she decides they are capable enough, she will arrange the introduction.

Sera has been in the dispensary for four days. Her condition is lung sickness — treatable in Tidefall with the right herbalist, not treatable in Kalowe. She is not in pain but she is slow and short of breath and aware that she is on a clock.

**What Sera will tell the party:**
- The cache is in a sea cave on Orak's eastern face, compass bearing 087° from the rock stack at the island's southeastern tip
- The cave entrance is at the waterline at mid-tide; at low tide it is walkable; at high tide it is 8 feet underwater
- The cache is buried 6 feet deep in the cave's interior, under a flat stone she placed there herself
- The cave has "some kind of animal" in it — she hasn't been there in 40 years and the last time she was, the interior had something moving in the dark. She does not know what kind of animal.
- She estimates "a chest of old coin and some jewelry." She is underselling this. She knows exactly what is there.

**What she won't say:**
- That the full cache includes 800gp in old Imperial coin (collectors' value 1,000gp) plus a set of matched silver earrings and bracelet worth 180gp
- That the cave floods to the ceiling at full high tide — the window is the 3 hours either side of low tide
- That the "some kind of animal" was, 40 years ago, a giant constrictor snake she and her crew drove back with torches; she assumes it's dead but didn't bet her life on it

---

## Key Facts

- Orak is a 2-hour sail east-southeast of Kalowe; small, low, overgrown, paired with [[Dreth]] across a narrow channel
- The sea cave is accessible on foot at low tide: 60ft deep, 20ft wide at the entrance, narrowing to 10ft inside
- High tide floods the cave to the ceiling in approximately 3 hours; party needs to be out (or have Water Breathing) before then
- The cache is 6ft under a flat stone, 40ft into the cave interior; digging takes 20 minutes with tools, 40 without

## DM Notes

**Sera Maddock:**
Old pirate, compact and wheezing, very still. She makes eye contact the way someone does when they've spent a career reading strangers fast. She is not sentimental about the money — she spent forty years not being able to use it, and she would rather someone get it than it rot under that rock. Her one requirement is leaving Kalowe. Tidefall has healers who can treat lung sickness. She'd like to live, if someone will take her.

*Sample lines:*
- *"The map's on my arm. I'll show you the bearing if you show me a ship."*
- *"I burned the other half. The man I was going to share it with deserved less."*
- *"There's something in that cave. I'm not telling you it's nothing, I'm telling you I don't know what it is now."*

**Ilaria Grimaldi's role:**
She will not introduce Sera to anyone she thinks is going to take the information without honouring the deal. She watched one privateer die badly in her dispensary and she is not adding to that count. If the party intends to betray Sera — she will notice, and she will not forget.

**The cave:**

*Encounter 1: Giant Crabs (×4, CR 1/8)*
The entrance area is home to four giant crabs that have colonised the tidal zone. They scatter from torchlight but will attack if the party blocks the exit. They are a nuisance, not a threat. Each has 13 HP, AC 15, and Claw +3 / 1d6+1 bludgeoning.

*Encounter 2: Giant Constrictor Snake (×1, CR 2)*
The interior — past the 30ft mark — is where the snake lives. It has been here for decades, long past the original animal Sera's crew encountered (that one is bones somewhere). HP 60, AC 12, Bite +5 / 2d6+4 piercing + grapple, Constrict +5 / 2d8+4 bludgeoning + restrained (escape DC 16). It is not aggressive unless cornered or if the party makes noise near its nest.

*Torch behaviour:* Snakes don't like fire. A torch held toward it while backing away = DC 11 Animal Handling to keep it at bay without a fight. On a failure (or without trying), it strikes.

**The tidal clock:**
Establish low tide when the party arrives. The cave has 3 hours before it becomes impassable (water rises 1 foot every 10 minutes; at 2 hours the entrance is waist-deep; at 3 hours the cave floor at the entrance is neck-deep). The dig takes 20–40 minutes. The snake fight takes time. This is a pressure mechanic — the party should feel the clock, not die to it unless they are catastrophically slow.

**The flat stone:**
2ft × 3ft, flush with the cave floor, unmarked. Under it: a wrapped oilskin bundle (still dry — she packed it well), containing a wooden chest latched with a corroded bronze clasp (no lock). Inside: the coin in cloth bags by denomination, and the jewelry in a felt roll.

## Consequences

- **If the party honours the deal:** 780–980gp net; Sera gets passage and treatment; Grimaldi is satisfied; Algernon Clyde is delighted if he's in port
- **If the party betrays Sera (takes the info, abandons her):** Grimaldi will not deal with them again; the harbour bosses hear about it within a day — non-interference has limits when it comes to bedsick old women
- **If the party goes to Orak without waiting:** They don't have the compass bearing from the tattoo, and the eastern face of Orak has seven sea cave entrances

## Open Questions

- Does Algernon Clyde buy the coin? He'll be excited about the provenance.
