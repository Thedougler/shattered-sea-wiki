# Kalowe — The Drifting Hulk

## Situation

The *Pale Covenant*, a two-masted trading schooner, has been drifting 4 miles north-northeast of the Kalowe gap for two nights. No lights. No answer to hails from passing vessels. A salt merchant who spotted her reports no visible crew and one hatch standing open amidships. Under Midchain free-port custom, the first boarding party to establish presence and bring cargo to port holds salvage rights. The harbour bosses will record the claim and take 10% of cargo value as finder's fee.

The *Pale Covenant*'s captain, Orlen Vass, died still owing eighteen months of unpaid Umberlee tithe — a deliberate snub he'd made public in Tidefall before his last run. The entire crew of nine went down with him. Their shadows and his specter now inhabit the ship, and they will not leave it.

---

## Current State

Word of the drifting hulk has reached the causeway by the morning market. Two other crews in Kalowe are aware of it and are discussing whether to try for it. The party has a narrow window — probably until the next morning — before one of those crews moves.

**What Suryo Wibowo knows:** The salt merchant who spotted her came through for provisions yesterday. He described the ship's colours (green stripe, ochre hull), position, and the open hatch. He mentioned it smelled strange even from a distance — like old copper and wet stone. He did not board.

**What the Waveservant shrine knows:** Brother Tamoa at the Kalowe shrine recognizes the *Pale Covenant*'s colours from the ledger — Orlen Vass, tithe balance outstanding. He will tell the party the ship's tithe status if they describe the colours. He will also perform a consecration ritual (components: 10gp of incense and saltwater poured into the basin) that, if completed before boarding, suppresses the specters for 1 hour — enough to load the hold.

**What the party doesn't know:**
- The crew were killed by the specters and their own undead shadows within hours of Vass dying
- The captain's specter is anchored to the wheel — he cannot move from the helm deck
- Destroying the specters permanently requires either the tithe be paid posthumously (any Waveservant can receive it) or the wheel be destroyed (it is AC 15, 20hp, and the specter fights to prevent this)

---

## Key Facts

- The *Pale Covenant* carries mixed cargo: spice, undyed cloth, and 3 sealed wax crates of silver plate tableware
- Cargo value: ~1,000gp total; harbour fee 10% = 100gp; party nets 900gp if they sell everything in Kalowe (Taufa Fifita & Sons will handle it for a further 10% cut, netting 810gp)
- The specter is tied to the wheel; shadows roam the ship freely
- The ship herself is intact and seaworthy — she could be taken as a prize (see **Prize Option** below)

## DM Notes

**Aboard the *Pale Covenant*:**
- Below decks: 4 Shadows — they haunt the cargo hold and crew quarters; they emerge on any light source entering the hold
- Helm deck: 1 Specter (Captain Orlen Vass) — he won't move from the wheel and cannot be driven off, only destroyed or appeased

**Shadows (4, CR 1/2):** HP 16, AC 12, Strength Drain (hit + DC 13 Con or -1d4 Str; 0 Str = new shadow). Resistant to most damage; immune to exhaustion, frightened, grappled. Vulnerable to radiant. In dim light they attack with advantage. The hold is dim.

*If the party brought torches or a light source:* the hold becomes bright; shadows lose their advantage and become agitated — they attack immediately rather than lurking.

**The Specter (CR 1):** HP 22, AC 12, Life Drain +4 / 3d6 necrotic (DC 13 Con or max HP reduced until long rest). Incorporeal Movement — moves through objects. He looks like a weathered sea captain and says nothing except *"Pay what you owe"* on repeat, directed at nobody visible.

**The tithe shortcut:** If the party learns about the tithe (from Tamoa at the shrine or from papers in the captain's cabin), they can pay it — 54gp covers the outstanding 18 months at standard maritime rate. Tamoa can receive the posthumous payment. Once paid, all three specters dissolve without a fight. The shadows take 1d6 rounds to follow, dissipating one at a time. This is the cleanest resolution.

**The consecration shortcut:** 10gp and a short ritual from Tamoa suppresses the undead for 1 hour starting from the moment the party boards. Tight timing for loading cargo, but doable.

**The Prize Option:** If the party wants to claim the ship itself rather than just the cargo, they need to stay aboard, stabilise her course, and bring her back through the gap. The harbour bosses will record the prize claim. The *Pale Covenant* is a 60-ton trading schooner — bigger than the *Surety*, worth ~4,000gp in sale value but requiring a crew of 6 to sail. She could be sold here (offer: 2,400gp, fast sale) or crewed and sailed out.

**Captain's cabin (unlocked):** logbook showing the tithe dispute, a silver compass worth 120gp, and a letter from a factor in Tidefall expecting delivery of the silver plate.

## Consequences

- **If the party boards and clears it:** 810gp cargo + possible prize ship; they are now known at Kalowe as effective operators
- **If the party uses the tithe shortcut:** costs 54gp, saves a fight, and Brother Tamoa at the shrine will remember the favour — future shrine services at half price
- **If another crew gets there first:** the cargo is gone; if that crew doesn't know about the tithe, they come back short two sailors and badly shaken
