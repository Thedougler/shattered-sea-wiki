# Mira's Blade

## Situation

A sealed confiscation crate in the Surety's hold contained a +1 shortsword (no name, no maker's mark, notched pommel worn smooth by use) and a locked jewelry box with a pendant engraved *For Mira, from the sea*. Rook's ledger lists it as "bladed goods, unlicensed" — no name, no inspection location, no date.
