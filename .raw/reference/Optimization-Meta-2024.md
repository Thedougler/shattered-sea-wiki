# Optimization Meta (2024 D&D)

DM-facing reference for 2024 5e balance and build optimization. Based on RPGBOT's state-of-meta analysis (December 2025). Subject to change as new supplements release.

## Fall 2025 Changes

Four supplements released, including *Heroes of Faerun*, which added: expanded Backgrounds/[[content/shattered-sea/private/rules/Feats]], updated Artificer class, new spells, and Circle Casting rules. Circle Casting made all-caster parties so disproportionately powerful that non-casters are actively detrimental by comparison. New spells also worsened the martial/caster gap.

## Key Meta Observations

**Martial vs. Caster Disparity.** Unchanged and worsening. Conjure Minor Elementals alone lets a single Wizard out-damage optimized martial builds. The 2024 rules improved martial play but did not close the gap.

**Healing in Combat.** Still mostly inefficient. Healing Word typically undoes ~60% of one enemy's turn. Optimal play: let an ally fall to 0, then revive with Healing Word. Never spend slots preemptively on healing.

**Forced Movement.** Extremely prevalent in 2024. Weapon Mastery (Push), Grappler feat, and class features all offer it from level 1. Dragging creatures into hazards or off ledges is broadly viable.

**Save-or-Suck Spells.** Reliable if you target the right save. INT saves = most reliable. CON saves = least reliable. Legendary Resistances are the intended countermeasure for bosses.

**Spirit Guardians equivalents.** Every full spellcaster now has access to a Spirit Guardians-style emanation/portable AoE spell. An all-caster party can potentially solve most encounters by activating all of them simultaneously. Damage types and conditions vary enough to cover most resistances.

**Circle Casting.** See [[Circle-Magic]]. Broken. Limit by keeping NPC spellcasters rare.

**Grappling.** Changed in 2024 — Grapple now uses STR/DEX Athletics vs. STR/DEX Save. No longer imposes Restrained; now just reduces speed to 0. Combined with Weapon Mastery (Push) or Topple to trigger Prone, then attack with advantage. Still viable but different from 2014.

## Best of the Meta

| Category | Best Pick | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Background | **Scribe** | Skilled feat gives huge versatility; Investigation + Perception covers most non-combat needs |
| Class | **Wizard** (or Cleric) | Full spellcasters dominate; all top 5 are full casters |
| [[Species]] | **Bugbear** | Surprise Attack (+2d6 per attack) remains outlier damage; flight species also very strong |
| Origin Feat | **Musician** | Heroic Inspiration for 2–6 party members; whole-party force multiplier |
| General Feat | **Undecided** | No single standout as of 2025 |
| Epic Boon | **Boon of Fate** | +/- 2d4 on any D20 Test (own or ally/enemy); offensive or defensive; every character benefits |
| Multiclass Dip | **Cleric / Paladin / Fighter** | Cleric: heavy armor + level 1 spells; Fighter: Con saves + heavy armor + Weapon Mastery; Paladin: balance |

## Worst of the Meta

| Category | Worst Pick | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Background | **Artisan** | Narrow ability score spread; Persuasion useless to most builds; saddled with Crafter feat |
| Class | **Ranger** (maybe) | Locked into [[Hunters-Mark|Hunter's Mark]] for class and subclass features; limits build diversity |
| Species | **Halfling** | Lucky is good but not enough; Naturally Stealthy only useful for Rogues |
| Origin Feat | **Crafter** | 20% discount on nonmagical items; gold rarely matters after early levels |
| General Feat | **Martial Weapon Training** | A full class feature reduced to a feat with no other benefits |
