# Prone

## Rule Text

While you have the Prone condition, you experience the following effects.
***Restricted Movement.*** Your only movement options are to crawl or to spend an amount of movement equal to half your XPHB is 0, you can't right yourself.
***Attacks Affected.*** You have XPHB if the attacker is within 5 feet of you. Otherwise, that attack roll has Disadvantage.

## Context

Standard D&D 5e condition. Applies when effects impose this status.
