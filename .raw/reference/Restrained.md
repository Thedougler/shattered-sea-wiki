# Restrained

## Rule Text

While you have the Restrained condition, you experience the following effects.
***Speed 0.*** Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.
***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have XPHB.
***Saving Throws Affected.*** You have XPHB|saving throws.

## Context

Standard D&D 5e condition. Applies when effects impose this status.
