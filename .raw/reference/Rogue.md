# Rogue

*Class — Player's Handbook (2024)*

**Primary Ability:** Dexterity · **Hit Die:** d8 · **Saves:** Dexterity, Intelligence · **Armor:** Light · **Weapons:** Simple + Finesse/Light martial · **Tools:** Thieves' Tools

---

## Sneak Attack Scaling

1d6 at L1 → +1d6 every 2 levels → 10d6 at L20.

Condition: Advantage on attack roll, OR ally within 5 ft of target (no Disadvantage). Weapon must be Finesse or Ranged.

---

## Core Features

| Level | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1 | Expertise (2 skills), Sneak Attack (1d6), Thieves' Cant, Weapon Mastery (2) |
| 2 | Cunning Action (bonus Dash/Disengage/Hide) |
| 3 | Subclass, Steady Aim (bonus action → Advantage next attack; can't move this turn) |
| 5 | Cunning Strike, Uncanny Dodge (reaction: halve damage from seen attacker) |
| 6 | Expertise (2 more skills) |
| 7 | Evasion, Reliable Talent (roll 9 or lower = 10 on proficient skill checks) |
| 11 | Improved Cunning Strike (use 2 effects per Sneak Attack) |
| 14 | Devious Strikes (Daze/Knock Out/Obscure added to Cunning Strike) |
| 15 | Slippery Mind (proficiency in WIS + CHA saves) |
| 18 | Elusive (no attack has Advantage against you unless Incapacitated) |
| 20 | Stroke of Luck (turn a failed d20 Test into a 20, 1/SR) |

---

## Cunning Strike Options (Level 5+)

| Effect | Cost | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Poison | 1d6 | CON save or Poisoned 1 min (repeatable save) |
| Trip | 1d6 | DEX save or Prone (Large or smaller) |
| Withdraw | 1d6 | Move half speed without OA |
| Daze | 2d6 | CON save or limited to move OR action/bonus on next turn |
| Obscure | 3d6 | DEX save or Blinded until end of next turn |
| Knock Out | 6d6 | CON save or Unconscious 1 min (or until damaged) |

Save DC = 8 + DEX mod + Prof bonus.
