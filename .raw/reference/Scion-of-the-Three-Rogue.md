# Scion of the Three

Even more than the Assassin, this subclass is all about stabbing and being terrifying in combat. Playstyle is straightforward with fun options. Very little here builds on top of the Rogue's core features—it's narrowly focused on direct damage and fear tactics.

## Core Features

**Bloodthirst** (3rd level)
Gain an extra [[Sneak-Attack|Sneak Attack]] by attacking outside your turn (Reaction trigger). Only available a few times per day and must be used strategically.

**Critical**: Do not use when you were the one to trigger the creature's Bloodied status—you get no benefit. Spend your limited uses carefully.

**Dread Allegiance** (3rd level)
Pick a deity at the end of each Long Rest; gain two benefits:

- **Bane**: Damage resistance is situationally useful; the cantrip is fantastic for its damage type and utility.

- **Bhaal**: Resistance to an extremely common damage type; cantrip boosts your defenses.

- **Myrkul**: Damage resistance is situationally useful; no reason to use the cantrip.

Most campaigns will use Bhaal for the common resistance and defensive cantrip. Switch to Bane if you expect less direct violence that day.

**Strike Fear** (9th level)
Two mechanics:

- **Terrify**: A fantastic debuff for minimal cost. Grants Advantage on your future attacks against the target, significantly improving damage output. Can be effective enough to replace Vex weapons in some builds.

**Aura of Malevolence** (13th level)
Creatures within 5 feet take psychic damage when they start their turn near you. Feels cool but deals pitiful damage. Not a reliable damage source.

**Dread Incarnate** (17th level)
Two options:

- **Cutthroat**: Finally some sustainability. Should have arrived earlier, but better late than never. Provides healing/temp HP on successful critical strikes or when Bloodthirst triggers.

- **Murderous Intent**: Insurance against poor rolls. Raises average on d6 from 3.5 to 4—not a huge damage boost but welcome.

## Optimization Notes

- **Bloodthirst is your extra Sneak Attack valve**: Use it strategically. Only triggers on Reaction attacks outside your turn.
- **Bhaal is the default deity choice**: Resistance to an extremely common damage type. Switch to Bane if tactical need arises.
- **Terrify is your primary control tool**: Advantage on attacks significantly improves damage. Use this instead of seeking weapon enchantments.
- **Aura of Malevolence is flavor**: The damage is negligible. Don't plan around it.
- **Cutthroat provides sustainability late**: Healing trigger makes you harder to kill in prolonged combats.
- **Murderous Intent is insurance**: Not a damage game-changer but reliable.
