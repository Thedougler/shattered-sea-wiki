# Soulknife

Psionics-based Rogue option. Subclass features fueled by a pool of Psionic Energy Dice (similar to Battle Master Fighter's Superiority Dice). Unlike most dice-spending features, Soulknife often gets benefits even on failed rolls, making the subclass reliable and satisfying to play.

## Core Features

**Psionic Power** (3rd level)
Your defining resource: Psionic Energy Dice. Pool:

- Size scales from d6 (start) to d12 (level 17)
- Increase die count every few levels
- Recharge mostly on Long Rest; only one die recharges per Short Rest
- Under typical Adventuring Day rules (3-4 encounters per day): ~2 dice per day available

Pool is smaller than it appears. Be cautious about spending; don't burn all dice in the first encounter.

Advantage over Battle Master: Psionic dice often don't expend if you fail the roll, making this subclass more forgiving.

**Sub-features**:

- **Psi-Bolstered Knack**: Fail a skill check? Roll a die after seeing the result. No waste if you still fail. Nearly every other feature requires you to gamble before knowing the outcome. Requires table conversation about what counts as "success" (e.g., Investigation finding nothing).

- **Psychic Whispers**: Replicate raw/ingested/Rarys-Telepathic-Bond (mostly) at level 3. Grant allies walkie-talkies. Free once per day (1d6 hours, scaling to 1d12 at 17th level). Add 2 allies per casting (scales with Proficiency Bonus). Tactical multiplier for scouting and positioning.

**Psychic Blades** (3rd level)
Your primary combat option. Psychic Energy Die-fueled attacks:

- Melee or ranged (60-foot range, far beyond dagger range)
- Deal psychic damage (rarely resisted, bypasses non-magical weapon resistances)
- d6 damage die (vs. dagger's d4)
- Bonus Action attack option (but consumes your Bonus Action; compare to two-weapon fighting with daggers, which doesn't consume Bonus Action)
- Bonus Action attack applies ability modifier to damage

**Drawbacks**: Blades only manifest on Attack action or Opportunity Attack (incompatible with Battle Master Commander's Strike or Order Domain Voice of Authority). Locked into psychic damage type (some creatures resist/immune, especially unintelligent ones). No attack bonuses like magic weapons.

**Mitigation**: Carry backup daggers. Consider Fighting Style (Thrown Weapon) if leaning into Reaction attacks, though this is costly.

**Soul Blades** (5th level)
Two Psionic Energy Dice options:

- **Homing Strikes**: Spend die on your last attack of turn (or any attack, really). If you miss, try again (no cost if you still miss). If you hit, trade die for Sneak Attack. Excellent trade—but don't use constantly (depletes dice quickly). Use when you need crucial blows.

- **Psychic Teleportation**: Bonus Action short-range teleport (10-30 feet, unpredictable). Gets you out of grapples, area control, tight openings. You choose after rolling whether to teleport. Annoying if you need to cross large gaps.

**Psychic Veil** (9th level)
Invisibility for 1 hour, once per day for free. Long duration allows non-aggressive acts (disarming traps, Help action in combat) without breaking Invisibility. With [[Psychic-Whispers|Psychic Whispers]] and Expertise, you're an amazing Scout.

Don't spend Psionic Dice to recharge Psychic Veil; mundane Stealth usually suffices.

**Rend Mind** (13th level)
Psychic attack forcing Dexterity save (Stunned on failure). Close to raw/ingested/Hold-Monster but not quite as good. Stun takes target out of fight and makes killing easy. DC matches spellcasters (Dexterity-based). 1-minute duration is nice.

Free once per day. Spending a quarter of your Psionic Energy pool per use is very costly, so save for powerful single foes. Hit with Cunning Strike effects first to cut through Legendary Resistances.

## Optimization Notes

- **Resource management is critical**: Pool is smaller than it appears. Be cautious about spending.
- **Psychic damage is mostly unresisted**: Use it reliably across campaigns.
- **Carry backup daggers**: Psychic Blades have limitations; daggers fill gaps.
- **Psychic Whispers is free and amazing**: Use it every day for scouting/positioning.
- **Psi-Bolstered Knack enables skill monkey play**: Talk to your table about success definition (especially Investigation).
- **Homing Strikes on crucial attacks**: Don't spam it; you'll run out of dice.
- **Psychic Veil for scouting**: 1-hour invisibility is long. Use mundane Stealth for shorter-term needs.
- **Rend Mind for single powerful foes**: Not for every encounter. Invest Cunning Strikes first to clear Legendary Resistances.
