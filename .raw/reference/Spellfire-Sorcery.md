# Spellfire Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Fiends of Fey & Flame (2024)*

Divine magic derived from radiant power — healing and condition removal similar to Cleric, with emphasis on Counterspell defense and Spellfire burst effects.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Defense through countermagic, support through healing, weak offensive options.

**Verdict:** Inferior to Divine Soul — both provide healing and radiant damage, but Divine Soul's other features are more powerful. Spellfire can replace a Cleric mechanically but at a disadvantage.

**Strengths:**

- Spellfire Burst grants Temporary Hit Points when casting spells with Sorcery Points (Bolstering Flames)
- Healing and condition removal from Cleric list makes it a viable party healer
- Guiding Bolt as a go-to early offense

**Weaknesses:**

- Radiant Fire damage is negligible (1d4, scales to 1d8 at Level 14)
- Absorb Spells is useless against non-spellcaster enemies and generates few Sorcery Points even against casters
- Honed Spellfire adds THP but Radiant Fire damage increase is poor
- Crown of Spellfire is reliant on Hit Dice (finite resource) and becomes less useful if you take sustained damage

---

## Features

**Spellfire Burst (Level 3).** When you cast a spell using Sorcery Points, you gain rider effects:

- **Bolstering Flames:** Gain Temporary Hit Points equal to 1 + spell level
- **Radiant Fire:** Creatures within 5 feet of you take 1d4 Radiant damage

**Spellfire Sorcery Spells (Level 3).** Always-prepared spells by level:

- 3: Command, Guiding Bolt
- 5: Lesser Restoration, Scorching Ray
- 7: Aura of Vitality, Dispel Magic
- 9: Wall of Fire, Flame Strike
- 11: Greater Restoration, Flame Strike

**Absorb Spells (Level 6).** Reaction when a creature within 30 feet casts a spell. Make a Charisma save (spell save DC). Success: spell is countered; gain Sorcery Points equal to spell's level (max Charisma modifier). Fail: spell goes off normally.

**Honed Spellfire (Level 14).** Temporary Hit Points from Spellfire Burst increase to 2 + spell level. Radiant Fire damage increases to 1d6.

**Crown of Spellfire (Level 18).** Bonus Action: spend up to 5 Hit Dice. For each die spent, gain benefit until end of turn:

- **Flight:** Gain flying speed equal to walking speed
- **Spell Avoidance:** Add 1d4 to AC rolls and Dexterity saves

Roll spent dice and gain Temporary Hit Points equal to the total.
