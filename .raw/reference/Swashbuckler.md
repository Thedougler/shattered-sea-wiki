# Swashbuckler

*Rogue subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014 edition)*

Blade-focused rogues who combine speed, elegance, and Charisma. Duelists and pirates. Fights with style — melee strikes without opportunity attack reprisal, and Sneak Attack without an ally nearby.

---

## Features

**Fancy Footwork (Level 3).** After making a melee attack against a creature on your turn, that creature cannot make opportunity attacks against you for the rest of your turn.

**Rakish Audacity (Level 3).**

- Add CHA modifier to Initiative rolls.
- Sneak Attack with no Advantage if: within 5 ft of target, no other creature within 5 ft of you, no Disadvantage on the attack.

**Panache (Level 9).** Action: CHA (Persuasion) vs. WIS (Insight).

- *Hostile target:* Disadvantage on attacks against targets other than you; can't make OA against targets other than you. Lasts 1 min or until ally attacks/spells target, or distance >60 ft.
- *Non-hostile target:* Charmed for 1 min, regards you as friendly acquaintance.

**Elegant Maneuver (Level 13).** Bonus action → Advantage on your next Acrobatics or Athletics check this turn.

**Master Duelist (Level 17).** If you miss with an attack roll, reroll it with Advantage. 1/SR or LR.
