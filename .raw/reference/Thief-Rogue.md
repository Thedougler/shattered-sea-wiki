# Thief

The iconic Rogue, specializing in moving and acting quickly. Fantastic Scout but gets nothing significantly improving Striker capability. Many features depend on magic item availability and expensive single-use mundane items (Alchemist's Fire, Caltrops). If magic items are unavailable, the Thief loses substantial effectiveness, especially at high levels.

## Core Features

**Fast Hands** (3rd level)
Use Bonus Action for Sleight of Hand (disarm traps, pick locks) or Utilize action:

- **Sleight of Hand**: Rarely needed in combat; better for outside-combat scenarios.

- **Utilize (formerly "Use an Object")**: Covers caltrops, healer's kits, dousing spaces in oil, binding creatures in manacles, and many other uses. Fantastically versatile.

Also enables **Magic action as Bonus Action** to use magic items (scrolls, wands, etc. with activated abilities). Most magic items with activated abilities require the Magic action, making this a massive action economy boost.

**Second-Story Work** (3rd level)
Two options:

- **Climber**: Climb speed matches your walk speed. Next best thing to a fly speed for vertical movement.

- **Jumper**: Long jump and high jump distances scale massively. In campaigns with magical flight, less essential; in others, bypasses pits and difficult terrain reliably.

**Supreme Sneak** (9th level)
**Stealth Attack**: Move out of cover, attack, return to cover without hiding again. Enables sniping without constantly spending Bonus Action to Hide. Use Dash to move greater distances, spend Bonus Action with Fast Hands, or do other things.

**Critical**: Cunning Strike only triggers if your [[Sneak-Attack|Sneak Attack]] hits. If you miss, you're exposed and need to Hide again.

**Use Magic Device** (13th level)
Three effects:

- **Attunement**: Add 4th attunement slot. By this level, Attunement is a significant bottleneck. 4 slots is still valuable.

- **Charges**: 1-in-6 chance when using a charged item to not expend the charge. Extends item lifespan. Lucky Ring of Wishes would feel great.

- **Scrolls**: Use spell scrolls without knowing the spell. Requires Arcana check (hope you have proficiency). Massive utility potential. Do not use offensively; scrolls are for utility and out-of-combat options.

**Thief's Reflexes** (17th level)
Gain an additional turn on initiative order 20 (before turn 1). Take your full turn twice at start of combat:

- Two [[Sneak-Attack|Sneak Attack]]s if positioned well
- Two Fast Hands activations (scrolls, items, etc.)
- Massive damage and utility front-load if initiative is good

## Optimization Notes

- **Fast Hands is your core feature**: Bonus Action activation of scrolls/wands and Utilize actions massively expand action economy.
- **Stealth Attack enables sniping**: Keep cover nearby. Miss = you're exposed; have a Plan B.
- **Climber > Jumper in most campaigns**: Vertical mobility often more useful than horizontal jumps.
- **Scroll usage is non-negotiable**: Grab every utility scroll you can find. Arcana proficiency is mandatory for reliably using them.
- **Magic item availability affects power level significantly**: Heavy magic item campaigns favor Thief; low-magic campaigns reduce effectiveness.
- **Thief's Reflexes is devastating**: Two turns at initiative 20 can win entire encounters. Initiative rolls matter hugely.
- **You're a Scout first, Striker second**: Build around scouting, mobility, and utility rather than damage.
