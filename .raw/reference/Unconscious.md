# Unconscious

## Rule Text

While you have the Unconscious condition, you experience the following effects.
***Inert.*** You have the [[content/shattered-sea/lore/conditions/Incapacitated]] and [[content/shattered-sea/lore/conditions/Prone]] conditions, and you drop whatever you're holding. When this condition ends, you remain Prone.
***Speed 0.*** Your Speed is 0 and can't increase.
***Attacks Affected.*** Attack rolls against you have Advantage.
***Saving Throws Affected.*** You automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
***Automatic Critical Hits.*** Any attack roll that hits you is a Critical Hit if the attacker is within 5 feet of you.
***Unaware.*** You're unaware of your surroundings.

## Context

Standard D&D 5e condition. Applies when effects impose this status.
