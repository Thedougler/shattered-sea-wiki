# Way of Mercy

*Monk subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Healing and damage output combined through Focus Point expenditure, converting martial arts into both offense and medicine.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Combat medic who heals allies and damages foes through martial arts discipline.

**Strengths:**

- Hand of Harm converts Focus Points to consistent bonus necrotic damage, multiplied on critical hits
- Hand of Healing provides action-economic healing during Flurry of Blows without competing for action economy
- Physician's Touch adds condition removal (Lesser Restoration, Stunned) to healing, plus Poisoned debuff to damage
- Flurry of Healing and Harm triples healing output when used with Flurry of Blows at higher levels
- Hand of Ultimate Mercy provides low-cost resurrection (5 FP) without expensive material components

**Weaknesses:**

- Healing output cannot compete with dedicated healers like Clerics
- Hand of Harm damage scales with single d8 + Wisdom, less effective than pure striker subclasses
- Healing during combat only efficient in attrition scenarios; generally should focus on damage/control

---

## Features

**Hand of Harm (Level 3).** Melee weapon attack that hits: spend 1 Focus Point to add extra necrotic damage equal to one Martial Arts die. Damage multiplied on critical hits (like Divine Smite).

**Hand of Healing (Level 3).** Bonus action or replacement during Flurry of Blows: spend 1 Focus Point to heal a creature within 5 feet by rolling your Martial Arts die plus your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1 HP).

**Implements of Mercy (Level 3).** Gain proficiency in Insight and Medicine (if already proficient, gain expertise). Can use Herbalism Kit to craft Antitoxins and Potions of Healing.

**Physician's Touch (Level 6).** Hand of Harm: add Poisoned condition (no save). Hand of Healing: add Lesser Restoration effect (remove one disease or condition) and can also remove Stunned condition.

**Flurry of Healing and Harm (Level 11).** When you use Flurry of Blows, you can replace one unarmed strike with Hand of Harm or Hand of Healing. This usage doesn't consume an additional Focus Point. (You still spend 1 FP to activate Flurry of Blows itself.)

**Hand of Ultimate Mercy (Level 17).** Action: spend 5 Focus Points to touch a creature that has died within the last 24 hours and return it to life with 1 HP. Once per Long Rest; can be restored early by spending 5 more Focus Points.
