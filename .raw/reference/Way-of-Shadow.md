# Way of Shadow

*Monk subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Shadow magic and magical darkness grant consistent combat advantage, making the Warrior of Shadow a powerful striker with excellent offense and defense.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Darkness-powered ninja with teleportation and invisibility options.

**Strengths:**

- Shadow Arts (Darkness) is extremely efficient (1 FP) for consistent Advantage on all attacks and Disadvantage on enemy attacks
- Darkvision and Minor Illusion from Shadow Arts provide utility beyond combat
- Shadow Step teleportation useful for repositioning and escape
- Cloak of Shadows provides invisibility with free Flurry of Blows, competing with Darkness but offering independent advantage source
- Combined with Darkness, enemy control and positioning is exceptional

**Weaknesses:**

- Advantage on single attack from Shadow Step (without Improved Shadow Step) is low value; Bonus Action cost is too high
- Darkness and Cloak of Shadows compete for the same combat slot (both require action to activate)
- Teleportation requires darkness-to-darkness path, limiting flexibility

---

## Features

**Shadow Arts (Level 3).** Spend 1 Focus Point to cast one of:

- *Darkness:* Standard spell (20-foot radius, you see through it). Can cast on object and carry it.
- *Darkvision:* Gain 120 feet of Darkvision for 8 hours.
- *Minor Illusion:* Standard spell (sensory illusion within 30 feet).

**Shadow Step (Level 6).** Reaction when you move 5+ feet: teleport from dim light or darkness to dim light or darkness within 30 feet. Gain Advantage on the next melee attack this turn. Bonus Action alternative: spend 1 Focus Point to teleport without the attack Advantage.

**Improved Shadow Step (Level 6).** Bonus action version of Shadow Step (1 FP) removes the darkness-to-darkness requirement. Easier repositioning mid-combat.

**Cloak of Shadows (Level 11).** Bonus action: spend 1 Focus Point to become Invisible until the start of your next turn. Your next Flurry of Blows doesn't consume a Focus Point. (Invisibility breaks on attack, but you've already committed to attacking.)
