# Way of the Elements

*Monk subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Elemental attunement grants reach, damage versatility, forced movement, and eventually flight — making Warrior of the Elements exceptional for striker and control roles.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Avatar-style elemental monk with enhanced reach, flight, and area-of-effect damage.

**Strengths:**

- Elemental Attunement (1 FP, no action) provides 10-foot reach (doubles Monk's normal reach) or damage type flexibility
- Push/pull effects with Elemental Strikes enable battlefield control: knock enemies into pits, AOE damage, and falling damage
- Stride of the Elements provides 15-foot flight (and forced movement for landing damage combos)
- Elemental Burst scales to Fireball radius with customizable damage type
- Elemental Epitome adds damage resistance and destructive movement (Step of the Wind AoE)

**Weaknesses:**

- Each Elemental Attunement effect costs 1 FP per turn (reach OR damage type OR push/pull, not stacking)
- Elemental Burst (3d6) significantly less powerful than Fireball (8d6), though customizable damage type is valuable
- Destructive Stride damage is low unless facing large groups in tight spaces

---

## Features

**Elemental Attunement (Level 3).** Bonus action (no Focus Point cost) or can be activated passively:

- *Extended Reach:* Your unarmed strikes have 10 feet of reach for one turn.
- *Elemental Strikes:* Your unarmed strikes deal cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage (your choice) instead of their normal type. Can move enemies toward or away (no "directly" restriction, allowing vertical knockback for falling damage).

**Manipulate Elements (Level 3).** Cantrip-like minor elemental effects (sensory, cosmetic). No mechanical benefit.

**Elemental Burst (Level 6).** Action: spend 2 Focus Points to cast fireball (3d6 damage, 20-foot radius). You can change the damage type. Position enemies into the area with Elemental Strikes (push/pull) before activation.

**Stride of the Elements (Level 11).** Bonus action: spend 1 Focus Point to gain a Fly Speed equal to your movement for the rest of the turn (15 feet at base). No landing damage vulnerability. Use Flurry of Blows to attack 3 times while airborne before descending.

**Elemental Epitome (Level 17).** Add to Elemental Attunement:

- *Resistance:* Gain resistance to cold, fire, lightning, or thunder (your choice).
- *Destructive Stride:* Step of the Wind costs 1 FP instead of 2 and forces each creature in your path to make a Dexterity save (spell save DC) or take 2d6 damage matching your chosen element.
- *Empowered Strikes:* Once per turn, add 1d12 to a damage roll.
