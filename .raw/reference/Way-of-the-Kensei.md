# Way of the Kensei

*Monk subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014 edition)*

Monks who master the Kensei tradition treat weapons as extensions of the body — tools of precise, martial artistry rather than mere instruments of violence.

---

## Features

**Path of the Kensei (Level 3).**

- *Kensei Weapons:* Choose 1 melee + 1 ranged weapon (any simple/martial without Heavy or Special properties; Longbow valid). They become Monk weapons. Gain proficiency if lacking. Add 1 additional weapon at levels 6, 11, and 17.
- *Agile Parry:* If you make an unarmed strike as part of the Attack action while holding a melee kensei weapon, gain +2 AC until start of your next turn.
- *Kensei's Shot:* Bonus action — ranged kensei weapon attacks deal +1d4 damage of weapon's type until end of turn.
- *Way of the Brush:* Proficiency with calligrapher's or painter's supplies.

**One with the Blade (Level 6).**

- *Magic Kensei Weapons:* Kensei weapon attacks count as magical.
- *Deft Strike:* On hit with kensei weapon, spend 1 ki point to add Martial Arts die to damage. Once per turn.

**Sharpen the Blade (Level 11).** Bonus action: spend 1–3 ki points to grant a kensei weapon +1/+2/+3 to attack and damage rolls for 1 minute. No effect on weapons already with a bonus.

**Unerring Accuracy (Level 17).** Once per turn, if you miss with a Monk weapon attack, reroll it.
