# Way of the Open Hand

*Monk subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

The classic Monk subclass. Open Hand Technique enhances Flurry of Blows with powerful forced movement and control, while late-game features provide massive damage and mobility.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Iconic martial artist with enhanced unarmed combat control and legendary finishing moves.

**Strengths:**

- Open Hand Technique dramatically improves Flurry of Blows, adding pushed enemies (with falling damage) and status effects
- Push effect (upward push for falling damage + prone) is exceptionally strong: prevents opportunity attacks and sets up advantage
- Fleet Step provides free movement every turn, enabling aggressive repositioning and grapple/control combos
- Quivering Palm is a high-damage finisher (10d12) with constitution save debuff potential
- Wholeness of Body provides long-rest healing, though rarely used in combat

**Weaknesses:**

- Wholeness of Body is ineffective for combat healing (too much overkill on short healing amounts)
- Quivering Palm is inconsistent (constitution saves are high) and competes with other Focus Point uses
- Open Hand Technique's benefits are excellent, but features after Level 6 are less transformative compared to other subclasses

---

## Features

**Open Hand Technique (Level 3).** When you use Flurry of Blows, choose one of your three attacks (or one attack if you only made two) to trigger an effect:

- *Addle:* Target makes a Wisdom save (your spell save DC); fail: can't take Reactions until end of its next turn.
- *Push:* Target makes a Strength save; fail: pushed away up to 5 feet (push direction is "away" not "directly away," enabling vertical knockback for falling damage).
- *Topple:* Target makes a Strength save; fail: knocked Prone.

**Wholeness of Body (Level 6).** Bonus action: spend Focus Points (1 per point) to regain HP equal to spent FP × your Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier. Rarely useful in combat (inefficient healing rate).

**Fleet Step (Level 6).** Bonus action: dash as a bonus action without spending a Focus Point. Use this repeatedly to reposition for grapple combos or to chase enemies.

**Quivering Palm (Level 11).** Melee attack that hits: spend 4 Focus Points to create internal vibrations. The target must make a Constitution save (your spell save DC) at the start of your next turn or take 10d12 force damage. Average 65 damage, but Constitution saves are reliably high. Compare this to Flurry of Blows + Stunning Fist (4 attacks + 2 Stunning Strikes for control).
