# Wild Magic Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Chaotic magic from unpredictable sources — whimsical, fun, but underpowered compared to other subclasses due to lack of prepared spell list and reliance on random effects.

---

## RPGBOT Assessment

**Theme:** Chaos, randomness, rerolls, and unpredictable buffs.

**Verdict:** Fun but unreliable. The single biggest weakness: Wild Magic Sorcery lacks a spell list (5.5 PHB subclasses get 10 prepared spells). This is a major disadvantage compared to every other subclass.

**Strengths:**

- Tides of Chaos grants easy Advantage, recharges trivially (via 1st-level spell slots → Font of Magic)
- Bend Luck is exceptional at saving allies from near-death (2 Sorcery Points for +1/−1 to saves)
- Controlled Chaos mitigates Wild Magic table threat
- Can trigger surges when you need them (unlike relying on 1-in-20 spell casting)

**Weaknesses:**

- No spell list = severe versatility loss. Every other subclass has 10 additional prepared spells
- Wild Magic effects unpredictable: can Polymorph you into a goat or Fireball your allies
- Tamed Surge (once per Long Rest, no recharge) is the only way to control when effects trigger
- High-level combats need reliable tools; randomness is a liability against dangerous enemies

**Wild Magic Table Notes:** 2024 rules changed from 2014 — no longer forced to center Fireball on yourself.

---

## Features

**Wild Magic (Level 3).** When you cast a leveled spell, roll a d20. On a 1, you trigger a random effect from the Wild Magic Surge table (see [[Wild-Magic-Surge-Table]]). Surges are usually benign, often helpful, but occasionally problematic.

**Tides of Chaos (Level 3).** Bonus Action: gain Advantage on next ability check, attack roll, or save before end of your next turn. Recharge by casting a leveled spell (no action required).

**Bend Luck (Level 6).** Reaction when a creature you see within 60 feet makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. Spend 1–2 Sorcery Points: add or subtract that many points from the roll.

**Controlled Chaos (Level 6).** When you roll on Wild Magic table, roll twice and choose which effect to apply.

**Tamed Surge (Level 14).** Once per Long Rest: when you cast a leveled spell, you can trigger a Wild Magic effect without rolling. Choose which effect occurs. Can recharge early by spending 3 Sorcery Points.
