# Winter Walker

Focuses on cold damage and sharing cold effects with enemies. Features emphasize direct damage; high-level abilities offer less-directly offensive options. Works well with conventional Dexterity-based Ranger builds, but available of the Cold Caster feat and subclass features incentivize Wisdom-based raw/ingested/Shillelagh builds.

## Core Features

**Frigid Explorer** (3rd level)
Three complementary features:

- **Biting Cold**: Ignore resistance to cold damage. Polar Strikes, Frozen Soul, and several subclass spells deal cold damage, making resistance-ignoring invaluable as levels increase.

- **Frost Resistance**: Permanent resistance to cold damage. A reasonably common damage type makes this consistently useful.

- **Polar Strikes**: Add Wisdom modifier to cold damage rolls (1d6+WIS on average). Small but consistent damage boost. Biting Cold ensures this triggers reliably even against cold-resistant creatures.

**Synergy with Cold Caster**: Cold Caster feat applies save debuffs on cold damage triggers (Polar Strikes). This allows you to apply -1d4 save penalties every turn at no recurring cost.

**Winter Walker Ranger Spells** (3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th level)
Offensive cold damage spells. Most arrive several levels later than full casters, reducing excitement:

- **Ice Knife** (1st level): Already mediocre as a base spell. Delayed availability doesn't improve it.
- **Hold Person** (2nd level): Decent save-or-suck on humanoids only. Situationally useful.
- **Sleet Storm** (3rd level): Only situationally useful. Delayed 3 levels behind full casters.
- **Cone of Cold** (5th level): Dismal damage (8d8); area control lasts one round.
- **Cone of Cold** (5th level): Decent damage but 8 levels behind full casters. Targets Constitution save.

**Hunter's Rime** (5th level)
Gain temporary hit points equal to your Ranger level when you cast raw/ingested/Hunters-Mark. Decent survivability boost, but locks you further into Hunter's Mark casting (already essential for Rangers). Adds value to mandatory spell usage.

**Fortifying Soul** (7th level)
Healing on demand (1d8 + Ranger level) once per Long Rest. Heal one creature you can see at range (line of sight required). Small buff against Frightened condition if prepared. Limited to one use per Long Rest—not as valuable as the use frequency suggests. The line-of-sight telescope scenario is fun to imagine but impractical.

**Chilling Retribution** (9th level)
When a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with an attack, you can use Reaction to force a Dexterity save. Failed save: target is incapacitated and speed is 0 (can't move). Very powerful; target can't take turns if they fail. Only a few uses per Long Rest, but functionally paralyzes attackers. Consider binding incapacitated enemies in chains/manacles/rope before they recover.

**Frozen Haunt** (11th level)
When you cast raw/ingested/Hunters-Mark, you can gain benefits without maintaining Concentration:

- **Frozen Soul**: You gain immunity to cold damage. Creatures starting their turn within 30 feet of you take 1d6 cold damage (your choice if they're in the radius). Guaranteed damage as long as creatures are positioned; not a large amount but automatic.

- **Partially Incorporeal**: You can move through objects and creatures (treating them as difficult terrain). Insanely powerful mobility and escape option. Lasts for 1 hour or until you use an action to end it.

This is only usable once per Long Rest unless you upcast Hunter's Mark to extend duration beyond the 1-hour default. Consider upcasting to maximize Frozen Haunt uptime.

## Optimization Notes

- **Cold Caster synergy is powerful**: Polar Strikes triggers -1d4 save debuffs every turn at no cost. Invest in Cold Caster if available.
- **Build Wisdom-heavy** to maximize cold damage scaling and Shillelagh option.
- **Dexterity-based builds work** but sacrifice Cold Caster synergy and Shillelagh option.
- **Hunter's Rime locks you into Hunter's Mark** (already essential). Reframe it as bonus survivability on mandatory casting, not a new tactic.
- **Chilling Retribution is powerful crowd control**: Incapacitate attackers and bind them before they recover.
- **Frozen Haunt is a significant power spike** at 11th level. Upcast Hunter's Mark to extend duration and maintain Frozen Soul longer.
- **Partially Incorporeal mobility** is your strongest defensive/escape tool. Use it to move through terrain and creatures.
