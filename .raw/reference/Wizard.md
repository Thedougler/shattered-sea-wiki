# Wizard

*Class — Player's Handbook (2024)*

**Primary Ability:** Intelligence · **Hit Die:** d6 · **Saves:** Intelligence, Wisdom · **Armor:** None · **Weapons:** Simple · **Spellcasting:** INT (prepared, spellbook)

---

## Overview

The Wizard has the largest spell list in the game. They can copy spells into their spellbook from scrolls and other wizards' books, and can cast any ritual they know without preparing it. This combination of breadth and ritual casting makes them exceptional utility spellcasters in addition to their offensive capabilities.
