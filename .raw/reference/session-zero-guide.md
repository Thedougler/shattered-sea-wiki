# Session Zero

> Agent use: Read before table setup, safety boundaries, expectations, and campaign buy-in.

## Fast Rules

- Establish tone, boundaries, table expectations, and character wants.
- Record lines and veils in the wiki after the conversation.
- Ask what each player wants to feel and do in play.
- Use answers to create gravity wells and prep priorities.


A dedicated session before the campaign begins to establish table expectations, safety tools, and shared context. One hour before the first session prevents hours of mid-campaign corrections. Not optional.

## What to Cover

**Tone and content** — what is on the table and what isn't; lines (content that won't appear at all) and veils (content that happens off-screen). Write them down; revisit if something new comes up mid-campaign.

**Table expectations** — phones and side conversations; attendance and cancellation norms; how to flag when something isn't working.

**Character creation** — guidelines and restrictions; how characters connect to each other or the world; whether the campaign is player-driven sandbox or structured narrative.

**Death rules** — whether PC death is on the table, how it works, resurrection options available.

**Player goals (not character goals)** — ask every player what they want to get out of the campaign as a player. That answer tells you more about how to run the game than the entire character sheet.

## The Key Question

*"What do you want to get out of this campaign as a player?"*

Not their character's motivation — their motivation as a person showing up to the table. The answer reveals whether they want tactical challenge, collaborative storytelling, character drama, or exploration, and tells you how to structure the game to serve them.
