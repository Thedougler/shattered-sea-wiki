## Marker Legend

- **Blue markers**: Major settlements and ports
- **Green markers**: Regional groupings and island chains
- **Yellow markers**: Major water features and passages
- **Red markers**: Landmarks and danger zones
- **Purple markers**: Specialized territories (Grung, etc.)
- **Gray/Black markers**: Boundaries and poorly charted areas

## Coordinates Reference

All marker coordinates can be adjusted. The map uses pixel coordinates (0-800 horizontally, 0-600 vertically).
