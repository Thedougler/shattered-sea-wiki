# Session 00 — Run Guide

**Today.** ~2–3 hours. Goal: table norms locked, all five characters established, party aboard the Saltwright with a reason to stay together.

---

## State of Prep

**Ready to go:**
- Campaign primer, tone, world — fully prepped and published [[content/shattered-sea/private/system/guides/Shattered-Sea-Tone-Guide]]
- Gravity wells: Perrin, JC, Delmar, Crissdalynn — complete
- Mortises: Perrin, JC, Delmar — locked. Crissdalynn — intentionally none (no defined Mortis; kid still coming into herself)
- Session 1 fully scripted (9 scenes), ready to run the moment Session 0 closes

**Gaps to close today:**
1. **Stripes** — no interview captured, no gravity wells, no Mortis, player name still TBD. The whole profile is blank. Handle in a separate pre-session conversation before the player joins Session 1.
2. **Lines and veils** — not written down for this table. Establish and record them today.
3. **Table expectations** — no formal doc. Verbal is fine; just cover it.

---

## Agenda

### 1. Table Talk (15–20 min)

Cover once, write it down.

**Tone and content**
- Lines (won't appear at all): ask each player. Common candidates for nautical/colonial campaigns: sexual violence, child harm.
- Veils (happens off-screen): torture details, extreme body horror. Confirm or adjust to this table.
- X-card: always on the table. No explanation needed, no questions asked.

**Table expectations**
- Phones: call it. This table's norm is ____.
- Attendance: how many to run? What's the cancellation window?
- "It's not working" signal: how does a player flag something without killing the session?

**Death rules** (already in the primer — just confirm verbally)
- Death is on the table. DM won't aim for it but won't pad it.
- Options: resurrection or new same-level character.

---

### 2. Campaign Pitch (10 min)

Don't recite the primer — they've read it. Hit the tone in one sentence:

> *Swashbuckling sophistication complicated by pettiness. Real stakes, stupid triggers, stylish people behaving badly.*

Then: **Archer but Age of Sail high magic.** That's the register.

Confirm everyone has answered the four pre-session questions:
- Why are you aboard the Saltwright?
- What do you want badly enough to cross dangerous water for?
- What kind of trouble follows you onto a ship?
- What would make you stay with a crew when leaving would be easier?

If anyone hasn't — handle it now, briefly.

---

### 3. Character Check-ins (20–30 min, established PCs)

Quick pass on Perrin, JC, Delmar, Crissdalynn. You have the gravity wells — this is a confirm, not an interview.

**For each:** "Tell me one thing your character wants right now and one thing they're afraid of losing." Listen for anything that contradicts or updates the gravity well notes.

Watch for:
- **Perrin + Crissdalynn friction** — prey response to large birds. Confirm Kaden knows this is a live dynamic.
- **JC's slave-catcher secret** — confirm Chad is comfortable with it surfacing mid-campaign as a grenade.
- **Delmar's five captains** — confirm Frederick knows this is an obsession, not a long game.
- **Crissdalynn's pilgrimage** — Drowned Maw convergence; confirm player knows it points the same direction as Delmar's mission.

---

### 4. Stripes Interview — Full Session Zero (30–40 min)

> [!warning] Stripes not present at Session 0
> Run this section separately before the player joins Session 1. Nothing is on paper — the whole profile is blank.

**Opening:**
> "Tell me about Stripes — not the stats, the person. What do they look like and what's the first thing someone notices?"

**Prioritise these questions from [[Character-Interview-Guide]]:**

*Who they are:*
- What do they want right now?
- What are they afraid of losing?
- What do they believe that most people would disagree with?
- What's a line they won't cross?

*Where they've been:*
- What did their life look like before all this?
- What's the worst thing that's ever happened to them?
- What's the worst thing they've ever done?

*For the DM (most important):*
- Give me one person from their past I can bring back — name, relationship, good or bad reunion?
- Give me one thing they want badly enough to do something questionable for.
- Give me one secret.
- Is there anything you want to explore through this character — themes, type of story?

**Umberlee hook — probe this specifically:**
Three other PCs have direct Umberlee entanglement. Stripes is described as a primal shrine guardian whose goal is to "set the seas right."
- What does "setting the seas right" mean to them specifically?
- What do they think is wrong?
- Does Umberlee speak to them, or is it one-way devotion?

**After the interview, extract:**
- Two Dials (internal tensions — not surface traits)
- Terminal Node (deepest long-term desire — asymptotic)
- Active Friction (what blocks them right now)

File to [[Player-Gravity-Wells]] after the session.

---

### 5. Mortis for Stripes (10 min)

> [!warning] Deferred — run with section 4 above

After the interview, offer the Mortis. Walk through the rule:

> "A Mark and a Gift — both describe the same person. The Mark must hurt. The Gift must feel like you."

**Current party Mortises:**

| PC | Mark | Gift |
|---|---|---|
| Perrin | Disadv. all Str checks/saves | +2 Charisma |
| JC | Disadv. Persuasion + Deception | Adv. Perception + Survival |
| Delmar | Disadv. all Wis saves; captain surfacing triggers involuntary | Surface 1 captain 1/short rest (bonus action): 2 skills, Expertise if proficient |
| Crissdalynn | None | None |
| Stripes | **TBD** | **TBD** |

Process: player names the flaw → you translate to mechanical condition → you propose a Gift that exceeds the Mark → lock it in. DM has final say on both sides.

See [[Mortis]] for design rules and constraints.

---

### 6. Player Goals — Round Table (10–15 min)

Ask the player, not the character:

1. What is your character trying to *become* by the end of this campaign?
2. What are they *afraid* of losing?
3. Who do they love, or who did they love?
4. What does failure look like for them personally?
5. What "red button" makes them throw caution to the wind?

Note the answers. These wire the campaign's tentpoles.

---

### 7. Party Connection (10 min)

Everyone's aboard the Saltwright. Establish:
- Which PCs have history before this voyage?
- Which are strangers?
- Is there anyone who doesn't want to be here?

Enough so that [[Scene-07-The-Hold]] — full party together for the first time — plays naturally.

That's where they wake up in Session 1. Ten seconds, then end.
