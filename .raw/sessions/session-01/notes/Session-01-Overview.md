## Summary

Session 01 ran through all opening scenes and reached Phase 3 of the Scene-08 boarding encounter, where it paused mid-combat. The party established themselves quickly — strong ambush discipline, smart use of terrain, and creative problem-solving throughout. Stripes was absent this session and sat out the encounter. The party moved through Phases 1 and 2 without significant resource expenditure and opened Phase 3 with an unconventional Flask of Endless Water play that separated Barnaby Rook from his remaining crew. The session ended with Rook alone and unharmed, all minions dead, Beaumont having deflected a flintlock round off his shell and entered the fight.

## Key Events

- **Cap'n Gorgeous killed in Phase 1:** The Dravosi Enforcer leading the hold inspection (nicknamed for his athletic build, golden locks, and square jaw) walked into a perfect ambush and was killed before he could act or call a shout.
- **Phase 2 breached clean:** Crissdalynn launched through the hatch boosted by Delmar, knocking two Dravosi prone. Perrin and Jean-Claude came through a gap in the decking for a flanking breach. All Dravosi Enforcers and one deckhand killed.
- **Geoffrey Draves recruited:** Young Dravosi deckhand who surrendered when his allies fell. Delmar returned his sword and recruited him. First crew member — no ship yet.
- **Decanter gambit:** The party used Jean-Claude's Flask of Endless Water to blast Rook off the gangplank at the start of Phase 3, isolating him from the Enforcer and Alchemist.
- **The Canister Reveal:** The Alchemist threw a gas canister; Jean-Claude and Perrin were caught in it. Both resisted. Jean-Claude identified the batch as Simone's work — cheap Grung hunting stock, his sister's hand, sold to the Dravosi Crown. He said nothing.
- **Beaumont enters:** Rook fired his flintlock at Beaumont from the gangplank. Round deflected off his shell. Beaumont joined combat: *"Boy, I shit out harder things than you every mornin'."*
- **Session break point:** All minions dead. Barnaby Rook alone, unharmed, facing the full party plus Beaumont.
