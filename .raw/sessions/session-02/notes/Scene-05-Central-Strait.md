# Central Strait Events

*These fire during any transit through the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]], regardless of destination. Use them to fill travel time, establish the world, and pull on PC threads. Fire 2–3 per leg.*

---

## Generic Strait — Roll or Choose

### The Dolphins

A pod of spinner dolphins finds the bow wake somewhere in the mid-Strait and rides it, leaping for no reason other than that they can. They stay for an hour. Then, in unison, they sound. Every one of them, gone.

Thirty minutes later: the squall. It comes up fast, no warning, over in twenty minutes. Anyone who watched the dolphins had the only warning available.

> This one is free. No roll needed. Just atmosphere, and the lesson that the strait talks if you know how to listen.

---

### The Becalmed Merchant

A wide-hulled brig sitting dead still in flat calm, crew lounging on the rail. As the Surety passes, her captain hails across. Friendly enough.

*"Did you hear about the Crown cutter gone missing off the eastern waypoints? Ours was due for inspection last week and I can't find anyone to process the papers."*

He is genuinely worried — not about the missing ship, about the paperwork delay. He has a cargo of pressed salt fish and a delivery window and the bureaucracy has failed him.

> [!DM]
> He is looking at the Surety. He does not know it is the missing Crown cutter. If anyone waves a Crown manifest at him, he will be absurdly grateful and ask if they can stamp his papers right now, from the deck, across the water.

---

### Night Watch

Second night out. The watch hears something moving alongside in the dark. Not breaking surface. Parallel course, maybe half a mile to port, for thirty minutes — then nothing. No splash. Just displacement in the water and the sound of something heavy and unhurried.

Geoffrey, when told in the morning: *"Probably nothing."* He does not sound like he believes this.

> [!DM]
> It is not the [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/sea-life/Sawek|Sawek]] — not this far west, probably. It is a very large shark working a current edge. But "probably" is doing a lot of work, and the party doesn't know the difference. Leave it unresolved.

---

### The Bad Receipt

A schooner on a converging course, no flag flying. When it sees the Surety, it changes course. Hard, clean, fast. If the Crown pennant is still flying, it peels south immediately. If the pennant is down, it closes to hailing distance first.

From fifty yards, the crew looks at the Surety. Then at each other. Then the schooner peels away anyway, running south hard.

Whatever they decided, they decided in about four seconds.

> [!DM]
> That's the [[The-Bad-Receipt|Bad Receipt]] — a Kalowe-refit schooner that works the Tidefall Gate approaches. They saw the hull name, ran the numbers on a stolen Crown cutter, and decided today was not the day. If the party gives chase: she's faster in a straight line, but the Surety has the wind angle. It becomes a choice, not a given. The Bad Receipt is not a threat — it's the first glimpse of the world the party is about to enter.

---

### Convoy Ahead

Three merchant hulls running east in loose formation, Crown escort frigate at the rear. The escort makes the Surety immediately — hails across, professional, clipped.

*"Identify and state your destination."*

A formality. Mostly. The escort captain is young and has not yet developed the habit of looking at things less carefully than they deserve.

> Drama suite: DC 10 / 15 / 20 Deception. Each tier determines how long the escort keeps pace alongside before accepting the answer and returning to the convoy.

## Party-Specific Hooks

*Each fires once, on the appropriate watch or quiet moment. Don't stack them. One per day of travel.*

---

### Delmar — Fleet Signal

Halfway through the Strait, a ship on the northern horizon. Running east, no acknowledgment of the Surety. Distance makes the hull name unreadable.

The pennant is [[Fisks-Fleet|Fisk's Fleet]].

Geoffrey watches [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Delmar-Fisk|Delmar]] watching it for a while, then goes back to work without saying anything.

> Play this straight. No confrontation, no revelation — just the pennant at distance. Whatever it costs Delmar to watch it pass, that's the scene.

---

### Jean-Claude — The Collar

Something bobs past the hull on the port side, late afternoon. Small. White. Noor fishes it out with a boathook, not sure what it is.

It's a Grung slave collar. Iron ring, locking pin, a Crown registry mark stamped into the metal. Cracked down the middle, snapped off someone's neck by force or the sea.

No context. No body. Just the collar and the registration number, which Jean-Claude can read if he gets close enough, and which he will recognize as a clan identification code.

> Don't editorialize. Hand [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Jean-Claude-Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] the collar and let the player decide what it costs him. He can say nothing, as he did in the hold. That's the thread.

---

### Perrin — The Hull Name

A passing merchant running west — Midchain registry, Kalowe home port. Routine. But one of the deck crew leans over the rail as she passes and stares.

Not at the ship. At [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin]]. He says something to the man beside him. The merchant doesn't stop.

[[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin]] might catch the hull name on the stern — *The Clement Sorrow*. He might recognize it. A Kalowe salvage operator, one of the sister ships to the [[Vestra|Vestra]], bought at auction after the disaster and repurposed. That deckhand's face rings a bell that shouldn't ring anymore.

> The thread is: someone from before the Vestra is alive, and they've seen Perrin, and they're heading to Kalowe. What happens next depends entirely on whether Perrin tells anyone.

---

### Crissdalynn — The Compass

The ship's compass nudges northeast for two hours in the mid-Strait. No cause. The current is steady, the heading is held, but the needle disagrees. Then it corrects, clean, like it never happened.

[[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Crissdalynn-Khinriss|Crissdalynn]] remembers what [[Master-Kyzil|Kyzil]] told her about the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]] — that it distorts instruments in the eastern passages, bends compass needles toward itself as a ship approaches. The Narrows. The Tail. Far east.

This is not the eastern passage.

> Don't explain it. One look at the compass, one beat where Crissdalynn knows what it means, and move on. The Maw is reaching west. That's all.
