## Session Start

The session opened exactly where Session 01 ended: aboard the [[shattered-sea/ships/Saltwright|Saltwright]] and [[HCS-Surety]], mid-boarding action. [[shattered-sea/characters/npcs/Barnaby-Rook|Barnaby Rook]] was alone, angry, and still dangerous after being blasted off the gangplank by Jean-Claude's [[Flask-of-Endless-Water]] and hauling himself back aboard. [[Beaumont-Sel]] had entered the fight after Rook's shot sparked off his tortle shell.

The DM recapped the party:

- [[Delmar-Fisk]], red admiral's coat, former fleet commander, carrying the loss of the [[shattered-sea/ships/Red-Lady|Red Lady]] and his fleet.
- [[Crissdalynn-Khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]], crow aarakocra monk, Sentinel of the [[Drowned-Maw]], who helped pull Delmar from the Maw.
- [[Jean-Claude-Tabarnack]], blue [[Grung]] with red beret and false mustache, still concealing what he recognized about [[Simone-Tabarnack|Simone]]'s poison.
- [[Perrin-Black-Jaw]], small Rattkin with an oversized sword and urgent business with his family.

Initiative was rolled. Rook went early, with a lair action track at initiative 20. Beaumont and Geoffrey were added after the first round began.

## Rook Final

Rook tried to recover control of the Surety but the ship environment and party control effects dominated the fight. Crissdalynn used gust effects repeatedly to force Rook toward or off the deck. Rook pulled himself back up once, shot Crissdalynn with a [[Flintlock-Pistol|flintlock]], and Crissdalynn deflected most of the damage with monk training. She maintained concentration on the wind effect.

Rook used the ship's bell to order the guns armed. The Surety's gun ports opened and gunnery crew below began moving cannon into position. Beaumont responded with the Tidefall Maneuver: he tossed [[Bisou]] through an open gun port, and Bisou soaked one cannon's powder with urine, disabling it. Delmar then shot into the other gun port activity from the weather deck, hitting at least one crewman and delaying or disrupting the firing sequence. The transcript confirms the gun crew were ordinary sailors, not armored soldiers.

[[Geoffrey-Draves]] was frightened and uncertain, but the party treated him as their first crew member rather than expendable help. Delmar directed him to assist Beaumont and stay alive. Geoffrey followed Beaumont rather than charging Rook.

Crissdalynn forced Rook off the side again. Rook kept trying to climb back via the rigging and hull. Perrin and Delmar discussed using shipboard debris with Crissdalynn's wind. Delmar improvised a chair-and-rigging throw, calling for Crissdalynn's help, and hurled the chair into the gust path. The chair struck Rook as he was climbing, cracked against him, and knocked him back into the water.

Rook disappeared into the chummed, dark water. The table canonized that a long eel-like shark took him. Rook is presumed dead unless the DM later decides otherwise, but in-session the party treated combat as over and the DM accepted the shark death as canon.

Crissdalynn recovered Rook's large admiral-style hat from the water and returned it to Jean-Claude. Jean-Claude presented the trophy to Beaumont, saying it belonged to the captain.

## Beaumont's Reckoning

After the fight, Beaumont thanked the party for fighting for him and his ship. He addressed Perrin first. Beaumont understood why a Rattkin might stow away and revealed he is a Friend of the [[Passage]]. He told Perrin he did not need to be a ghost on Beaumont's ship; any Kin in need could ask.

Beaumont then turned to Jean-Claude. He recognized Jean-Claude as a blue Grung and explained that blue caste usually means scout, handler, and advance sign of a raid. He was worried about whether more Grung were nearby and whether the violence of the night was truly over. Beaumont tossed Jean-Claude a [[Truth-Stone]] and asked him to repeat that he was not traveling ahead of a Grung raiding party.

Jean-Claude answered truthfully: former kinsmen had been pursuing him, he stowed away for survival, and he was not there as part of a raid. Beaumont accepted the answer. Jean-Claude retained the thread that Simone Tabarnack's work is tied to Crown poison supply, but he did not fully explain Simone to the party at this point.

Perrin had a second private-adjacent exchange with Beaumont around Passage matters. Beaumont mentioned that [[Nona-Black-Jaw]] was looking for her lost grandson and believed [[Anzolo]] had already been sent out. Perrin visibly tightened but tried to hide his reaction. Beaumont failed to read the full implication at first, but later Perrin quietly told him, "The Black-Jaw will remember this," signaling his identity without wanting it public yet.

## Opening The Surety

Delmar tried to call all hands to stand down and prepare to weigh anchor, but the lower door was locked. The transcript is noisy about who was physically doing what at the door; the canonical result is that the party used lock work and/or small-space access to gain entry below deck without a fight.

Geoffrey suggested they meet the rest of the crew and mentioned that [[shattered-sea/characters/npcs/Shepherd-Grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]'s cooking was excellent. He led the party below.

## Shepherd Grigori

The galley smelled far better than the ship had any right to smell. Shepherd Grigori was waiting at a table with food, wine, and places set. He had dark wild hair, a thick beard, a red-and-gold [[Dravosi-Crown|Dravosi]] naval coat rolled to the sleeves, and an extravagant [[Tessarine-Concordat|Tessarine]]-style shirt. Perrin identified the Tessarine styling with a history check.

Grigori introduced himself as the ship's cook and a simple holy man. He was warm, theatrical, and unbothered. He offered food and wine. His cooking provided restorative comfort during the scene.

Grigori asked what kind of pirates the party were. The party's answer emerged through debate rather than a single speech: they do not traffic people, they do not eat people, and they intend to be the "good" or Robin Hood type rather than businessmen or incomprehensible weird pirates. Perrin was particularly firm that trafficking and cannibalism were hard lines.

Grigori asked about Cap'n Gorgeous. He learned that Gorgeous died during the boarding. Grigori explained that [[Capn-Gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]] was connected to House Knighton and that [[Rupert-Knighton]], his father, was likely to send ships rather than forgive or investigate gently. The party now knows that killing Cap'n Gorgeous creates a pursuit/retribution problem.

Grigori's condition for full crew cooperation was simple: any crew member who wants off at the next port must be allowed off. The party accepted.

## Ket And The Moral Debate

Grigori revealed that the crew had forgotten to feed Ket. The crew reacted by touching their noses to avoid being chosen for the task. [[Ket]] was not a cat; he was a captured [[Moucheron]] from [[shattered-sea/places/midchain/Murrat|Murrat]], held in a brass birdcage in the brig.

Ket appeared as a pigeon-sized mosquito-like fey humanoid with a proboscis, wings, and speech. He was starving and hostile, but sentient. Rook had taken him from Murrat three weeks earlier as a specimen after hearing of Moucheron mercenaries in [[shattered-sea/places/midchain/Kalowe|Kalowe]]. The crew had been feeding him by rotation and hated the arrangement.

Jean-Claude fed Ket a small amount of his own blood in a wine glass, using care to avoid the proboscis. Ket drank it, his abdomen filling bright red. The blood calmed him somewhat but did not make him safe.

The party then debated what to do. Key facts established in play:

- Moucherons are native to Murrat.
- Island-born Moucherons like Ket are feral and predatory, unlike contract-disciplined mercenaries.
- They feed on blood and can die after days without it.
- Some Moucherons work as mercenaries out of Kalowe, including the [[Five-Blades]].
- Ket can find his way home from the party's current position.
- Releasing him in open air is risky, but not the same as unleashing him onto a helpless port.

Perrin, under pressure from his Black-Jaw obligations and fear of delay, drew his longsword and moved to execute Ket directly. Crissdalynn got there first — she deflected the blade and put herself between Perrin and the cage. In the rush of the intervention she stepped past the safe distance from the bars. Ket took the opening immediately, driving his proboscis into her upper arm and feeding. She pulled back. Perrin cast Tasha's Hideous Laughter. Ket went incapacitated.

Jean-Claude also refused to participate directly in an execution, though he acknowledged that someone might decide it was necessary. Ultimately the group chose release instead. They left a corpse on deck as a blood lure, cleared a path upward, armed themselves, and used Mage Hand to open Ket's cage from a distance. Ket flew out, fed noisily on the corpse for about a minute, then left the ship and flew away toward home.

Grigori later said he was glad they set Ket free; the crew had always hated what Rook did to him.

## Crew Introductions

After Ket was released, Grigori introduced the surviving Surety crew. Canonical crew present at end of session:

- Geoffrey Draves — human Dravosi carpenter, already defected to the party.
- [[Sem-Holst]] — carpenter/shipwright, Geoffrey's mentor or senior counterpart; transcript says "Sam Holtz" but wiki canon is Sem Holst.
- [[Alys-Kuiper]] — dwarven surgeon/medical officer; transcript initially hears "Ellis," corrected to A-L-Y-S.
- [[Old-Faas]] — old rigger/bosun type, rope and sailing specialist.
- [[Thunk]] — enormous orc gunner; handles guns and explosions, "the good kind of explosion, not the other kind."
- [[Ensign-Wouters]] — not cleanly introduced in the transcript excerpt, but referenced elsewhere as the navigator/records-minded crew member who was shot in the cock by Delmar during the earlier fight with Rook ending his life, then who's blood was consumed by Ket
- [[Noor]] — a young dwarven boy.
- Shepherd Grigori — cook/holy man, but explicitly plans to get off at the next stop, hopefully when they reach Calveno.

Grigori stated he would absolutely get off at [[shattered-sea/places/calveno/Calveno|Calveno]] if the party went there, because he wanted to attend the city's festival. The transcript's "contest of jokes" matches Calveno's [[Il-Gioco-delle-Beffe]], the public prank/hoax festival. The party leaned into Calveno as the next destination.

## Ship Ownership, Direction, And Black-Jaw Reveal

The party accepted that they had effectively taken over the HCS Surety. Beaumont did not object; it was not his ship. He preferred fewer complications aboard the Saltwright and was happy for the party to take responsibility for the prize.

The party discussed the need to repaint or disguise the Crown vessel before taking it into [[Port-Tidefall]]. Calveno was favored because it could repaint/modify the ship and because Grigori had business there. Kalowe was also mentioned as a possible no-Crown port, but the final chosen heading was Calveno.

Delmar took the role of captain/admiral in practice, though the formal command structure was still emerging. The party discussed a treasurer/quartermaster role. Perrin, both in and out of character, was positioned as the best person to keep the ship's books and finance log. Delmar agreed to handle the ship's finances until a proper treasurer structure existed, but Perrin's accounting role is likely.

Perrin publicly revealed to the crew that he is Black-Jaw and that Black-Jaw law would apply alongside the captain's law. The intent was to reassure the crew that they would be looked after in Calveno by his people, but also to set the ship's ethical code: no trafficking, no cannibalism, voluntary service, and fair treatment. Grigori interpreted "Black-Jaw" as a crime family/business-associate structure; the DM clarified that the Black-Jaws are honor-code thieves, Passage/Tangle-adjacent, anti-Dravosi, and serious political movers in Calveno.

Jean-Claude did not know the Black-Jaw context, but pledged to sail with Perrin regardless.

## Ship Search And Loot

The party cleaned up bodies, moved away from the immediate battle site, and searched the Surety.

Confirmed acquisitions and discoveries:

- Rook's captain's cabin contained a locked chest with 45 gp and letters of marque naming Barnaby Rook as a Dravosi privateer.
- The cabin also contained a cutlass and a loaded blunderbuss. Delmar, proficient with firearms, took interest in the blunderbuss.
- A hidden lockbox under the cabin floor held 110 gp and two garnets, total value 150 gp.
- The alchemist's body had five remaining canisters: one green Grung poison canister, two yellow canisters, and two red canisters. Jean-Claude insisted on taking or controlling the green Grung poison evidence. The yellow and red canisters remain unidentified in this summary.
- Seized crates contained approximately 97 gp in goods: silver candelabras, a fine brandy cask, Calveno silk, and a locked jewelry box/pendant connected to Mira. Transcript wording is noisy, but wiki canon says the pendant is engraved "For Mira, from the sea."
- A second confiscation crate contained multiple flintlocks, [[Miras-Blade|Mira's Blade]], and twenty vials of concentrated Grung poison tincture. Jean-Claude recognized the poison preparation as the kind Simone presumably sells. The party decided not to sell the poison. It remains aboard as a dangerous resource/evidence, not common loot.
- Mira's Blade was identified as an unusually fine +1 shortsword. Delmar and Perrin both had interest; after a light contest/decision, Delmar handed it to Perrin. Perrin is the current holder of Mira's Blade.
- The powder magazine still contained twelve cannon charges.

Bisou stole 15 gp worth of shinies while leaving with Beaumont. Crissdalynn tried to chase her but failed; Beaumont treated it as fair business. Deduct 15 gp from loose treasure.

Known but not fully confirmed from transcript excerpts:

- The Galewall map and potion cache from prep may or may not have been found in play. The excerpted search did not clearly confirm them. Do not mark those found without a second pass.
- Blank Crown writs and the sealed Harbormaster letter from prep were not clearly confirmed in the transcript excerpts. Do not mark them found without a second pass.

## Beaumont's Departure

The next morning, Beaumont prepared to return to his route aboard the Saltwright. He shook hands where welcome, respected no-touch boundaries with Jean-Claude, and warned that weather looked dicey toward Calveno. Delmar thanked him for his kindness and for saving them, and said Beaumont had a friend in Admiral Fisk.

Beaumont said he could be found in Kalowe when off route; the tavern name in the transcript is noisy and should be verified before wiki update. He offered to work as navigator or helmsman if their routes aligned in the future. He called Bisou back from the Surety; she returned with stolen 15 gp.

## Setting Sail

The party set heading for Calveno. The first group sailing check succeeded overall:

- Crissdalynn contributed perception/weather or lookout work.
- Jean-Claude used mobility/rigging work successfully during departure; the transcript also discusses cartographer's/navigator's tools and druidcraft for route/weather awareness, but exact role assignment is noisy.
- Delmar took the wheel and leaned on his sailing experience; the navigation/ship-handling result was strong.
- The group succeeded well enough to begin the Calveno journey cleanly.

The crew set sail under party command. The Surety remained a Crown-marked prize vessel and still needs repainting, registry stripping, and a new name.

## Storm And Cliffhanger

After Beaumont departed, the wind picked up into a storm from the east. The party saw a wall of rain and chose not to risk the crew. They dropped anchor, brought in the sails, and hunkered down rather than pressing onward.

During the storm, Jean-Claude became seasick. The table joked through poison/venom distinctions for Grung physiology; no major mechanical consequence was finalized in the summary-worthy transcript.

About three hours into the storm, the clouds briefly opened and the party glimpsed a Crown Isle northwest on the route toward Calveno, likely near [[Aldenmere]]. Geoffrey had been working continuously to keep the ship stable.

Then something struck the hull. The first impacts could have been waves, but one sounded wrong: a solid thunk and scrape against the wood, followed by the ship lurching hard to the left and another high-speed hull impact. The session ended on that cliffhanger.

A player mentioned using a spyglass to look out.

The table strongly implied this cliffhanger is tied to a Delmar trigger involving a brass or silver spyglass and one of the souls/omens connected to his backstory. The transcript does not reveal the in-world explanation yet. Treat as unresolved.

## End State

- Cap'n Gorgeous: dead; Knighton family retribution now a known threat.
- Beaumont Sel: alive, friendly, returned to the Saltwright and his route.
- Bisou: alive, stole 15 gp from the party as she left.
- Geoffrey Draves: alive, first party crew member, assisting with ship operations.
- Shepherd Grigori: friendly and helpful for now; wants off at Calveno.
- Ket: freed, fed on a corpse, flew away toward home/Murrat.
- HCS Surety: taken as party prize, still Crown-marked, heading to Calveno for repainting/disguise.
- Destination: Calveno.
- Party level: the table began level-up discussion at the end; the DM awarded level up after the cliffhanger.
