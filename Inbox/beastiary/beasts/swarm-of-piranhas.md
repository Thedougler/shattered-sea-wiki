# Swarm of Piranhas

Piranha swarms favour still or slow-moving freshwater with something bleeding in it. Several [[content/shattered-sea/places/midchain/index|Midchain]] islands have interior lakes and spring-fed pools that look safe for watering — and a few are not. The permanent freshwater lake at Vatumei has piranha swarms in the shallower margins, which local pilots know and most visiting crews do not until they are knee-deep filling casks. The standard advice from anyone who has made the run before is to lower the casks on a line from the bank, not wade in. ^[inferred]
