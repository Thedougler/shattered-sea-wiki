# Image Generation Art Style

> Agent use: Read before creating Shattered Sea image prompts or visual art direction.

## Fast Rules

- Default to Archer-style adult animated illustration.
- Keep characters readable, specific, and materially grounded.
- Use Age of Sail high-magic props, fabric, brass, tar, rope, and salt-worn detail.
- Never include text in generated images unless explicitly requested.


All generated art for [[content/shattered-sea/index|The Shattered Sea]] defaults to an Archer-style adult animated look: crisp, graphic, cinematic, and grounded in physical detail. Use this direction unless a prompt explicitly says otherwise.

## Default Style Prompt

Use this language as the base style clause for image prompts:

> Archer-style adult animated illustration, clean vector-like linework, sharp silhouettes, realistic proportions, expressive but restrained faces, dramatic cel-shaded lighting, rich flat colors with subtle gradients, high-contrast shadows, detailed Age of Sail fantasy costuming and props, cinematic composition, polished TV animation still, no text.

## Core Traits

- **Linework:** precise black or dark contour lines, clean interior detail, minimal sketchiness, no painterly brush texture.
- **Anatomy:** mostly realistic proportions with slight caricature in faces, posture, and attitude; characters should feel designed, not cartoonish.
- **Faces:** angular planes, strong brows, sharp noses, expressive eyes, controlled mouth shapes, dry comedic tension when appropriate.
- **Lighting:** hard directional light, strong cel-shaded shadow blocks, readable rim light, deliberate contrast.
- **Color:** saturated but not neon; flat color fields with restrained gradients and selective texture.
- **Rendering:** polished 2D animation still, not oil painting, not watercolor, not photorealistic, not anime.
- **Detail:** costumes, weapons, ships, tattoos, scars, shells, feathers, scales, rope, brass, wood, and salt-worn surfaces should be specific and legible.

## Shattered Sea Adaptation

The campaign version is Age of Sail high-magic D&D with Renaissance-punk edges. Characters should look competent, stylish, dangerous, and slightly absurd without becoming parody. The world should feel tactile: canvas, tar, polished brass, wet decks, reef water, old stone, tailored uniforms, practical armor, and magic treated as part of the material culture.

Use the style for:

- NPC portraits
- ship and location banners
- session recap scenes
- magic items and faction imagery
- player-facing visual aids

## Composition Defaults

- Prefer landscape framing for banners and recap scenes.
- Prefer waist-up or three-quarter framing for NPC portraits unless the full body matters.
- Put the subject in a clear silhouette with readable props.
- Use cinematic staging: foreground, midground, background, and a strong light source.
- Keep backgrounds specific enough to anchor place and faction, but never so busy that they obscure the subject.
- Include no captions, labels, speech bubbles, UI, signatures, or visible text unless explicitly requested.

## Avoid

- Photorealism or live-action concept art.
- Anime, chibi, Disney, Pixar, western comic-book superhero rendering, painterly fantasy illustration, or grimdark oil painting.
- Over-soft airbrushed faces.
- Generic pirate costumes without Shattered Sea specificity.
- Random modern objects unless the prompt calls for an intentional anachronism.
- Copying characters from Archer; use the visual grammar only.

## Prompt Rule

When generating images, start with the subject and lore-specific visual requirements, then append the default style clause. If a prompt has to be short, preserve these essentials: Archer-style adult animated illustration, clean vector-like linework, realistic proportions, cel-shaded lighting, rich flat colors, cinematic Age of Sail high-magic fantasy, no text.
