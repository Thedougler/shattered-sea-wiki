# Stripes Primer

> Agent use: Read before using Stripes in prep; current mechanics are incomplete and require verification before encounter tuning.

## Fast Read

- **Table role:** unverified martial frontliner; do not tune encounters around unknown features.
- **Spotlight with:** physical courage, direct threats, tribe/community stakes, and simple tactical space.
- **Pressure with:** visible danger and hard choices rather than feature-specific counters.
- **Avoid:** assuming subclass, fighting style, feats, spells, or exact damage output.

## Encounter Levers

- **Give them:** clear front-line space and one obvious physical problem to solve.
- **If struggling:** reduce tactical complexity until sheet details are confirmed.
- **If dominating:** pressure with positioning and objectives, not unverified counters.
- **Avoid:** building around Battle Master, Champion, or Eldritch Knight assumptions.

## Roleplay Levers

- **Escalates:** threats to tribe, duty, or the reason he was sent from home.
- **Calms:** concrete evidence that action protects the community.
- **Dramatic lever:** put personal glory against communal obligation.
