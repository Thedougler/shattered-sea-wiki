# The Saltwright Boarding

*This encounter begins the moment the party commits at the end of [[Scene-07-The-Hold]]. The three crew descend as soon as the hatch opens. Initiative starts on the first hostile action — the party attacking before the crew reaches the floor, or the crew drawing weapons when they find five armed strangers in the dark.*

## Setup

[[Barnaby-Rook]] has left the [[HCS-Surety]] and boarded the [[Saltwright]], shaking down [[Beaumont-Sel]] under the pretense of an inspection. The party is below deck. When they're found, the best Rook offers is forty gold Beaumont doesn't have.

### Phase 1 — The Hold

*Party has every advantage. Act on it — but the Enforcer makes it a real fight.*

Three of Rook's crew descend for the inspection: two deckhands up front, an Enforcer behind them with a heavy crossbow. Standard procedure for holds with undeclared cargo. They expect shrimp and provisions, not five armed strangers in the dark. The crossbow is nearly useless in here: tight lanes, five-foot minimum range, and the Enforcer can't see what he'd be shooting at anyway.

Beaumont keeps a small emergency stash behind a false panel in the hold's aft wall: three potions of healing (2d4+2 each), visible to any PC who looks aft.

| Field             | Content                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Combatants**    | 2 × Dravosi Deckhands, 1 × Dravosi Enforcer (heavy crossbow — effectively a melee combatant in here)                                                         |
| **Terrain**       | Cramped interior of the cargo hold, filled with barrels of shrimp, fish and crates of provisions. Maximum engagement range ~20 ft; most lanes are 5 ft wide. |
| **Lighting**      | Dark, dim lighting throughout the hold except the hatch opening. The Enforcer has no darkvision — he is fighting blind unless he reaches a lantern cone.     |
| **Loot**          | Beaumont's potion stash: 3 × Potion of Healing (2d4+2) behind a false panel in the aft wall. Visible from the aft end of the hold.                           |
| **Surprise**      | Party has it — the crew have no reason to expect resistance.                                                                                                 |
| **Pressure**      | If any crewman gets off an audible shout, Phase 2 loses surprise.                                                                                            |
| **Win condition** | All three crewmen silenced before they can signal above.                                                                                                     |

**PC Spotlights — Phase 1**

- **JC:** Dark hold = Umbral Sight active. Invisible to darkvision creatures, advantage on attacks. The Enforcer has no darkvision either; JC is the only one in here who can see and fight without penalty. Priority target: the Enforcer before he closes to melee range.
- **Crissdalynn:** No darkvision, so disadvantage on attacks and effectively blind beyond the hatch spill. Best position is near the hatch; if a crewman breaks for the ladder she can intercept even at disadvantage.
- **Delmar:** Cramped lanes isolate targets. Rakish Audacity fires freely. The Enforcer is the real threat: 32 HP and multiattack. Burn SA on him first, not the low-HP deckhands.
- **Perrin:** Vicious Mockery costs no attack roll and doesn't need line of sight beyond 60 ft. He can hear the Enforcer even if he can't see him. Good opener to shave the Enforcer's first attack before JC engages.
- **Stripes:** The hold aisle is what a Battle Master wants, a single chokepoint he can own. Hold the lane between crates while JC and Delmar work the flanks. Trip Attack keeps the Enforcer prone in tight quarters; he can't stand without eating an opportunity attack. Menacing Attack burns his first turn to fear while the party closes. If the Enforcer is about to drop someone, Action Surge ends him in one round.

**Dial — Phase 1**

| Direction | Lever | Effect |
| --------- | ----- | ------ |
| **Easier** | Remove the Enforcer — only two deckhands descend | Drops adjusted XP back to ~75 (Trivial); use if the party already looks depleted |
| **Easier** | Alert Call requires the crewman's action, not a free reaction | Gives the party a full round to silence them before any shout is possible |
| **Harder** | The Enforcer has a hooded lantern on his belt, already lit | Bright light in a 10-ft radius follows him; collapses JC's Umbral Sight if they're in the same zone; forces repositioning |
| **Harder** | One deckhand was already below when the party hid — he's been watching quietly from a dark corner | Party doesn't have full surprise; he acts on round 1 and shouts immediately unless grappled or silenced |

### Phase 2 — Above Deck

*Pirates have position; party has momentum.*

The party emerges through the hatch. If Phase 1 was clean, the topside crew is watching, not expecting trouble to come up through it. If Phase 1 was loud, they're braced.

| Field             | Content                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Combatants**    | 2 × Dravosi Enforcers at the hatch, 2 x Dravosi Deckhands at the rail                                                                       |
| **Terrain**       | Open deck, night. Beaumont is at the wheel. Boarding hooks on the rail.                                                                    |
| **Lighting**      | Dim — full night, open deck, only the Saltwright's lanterns. No total darkness zones.                                                      |
| **Pressure**      | The deckhands at the rail are the retreat route to the cutter — if they reach the gangplank and signal, it pulls before Phase 3 is possible. |
| **Win condition** | Deck crew defeated or driven back before the gangplank is pulled.                                                                          |

**PC Spotlights — Phase 2**

- **Crissdalynn:** This is her phase. Open deck, dim twilight, she can see and fly. Send her airborne toward the rail deckhands immediately; she can intercept before they reach the gangplank. Don't waste her at the hatch.
- **JC:** Dim light and scattered shadow near the hull and rigging mean Umbral Sight is still operational in patches. If he steps into a lantern's cone, he loses it. Position matters.
- **Delmar:** Open deck breaks the cramped geometry. Enforcers at the hatch are clustered; he needs one ally adjacent or an isolatable target to fire Sneak Attack. Position an ally to set the angle.
- **Perrin:** Clear sight lines to the rail deckhands = Eldritch Blast lanes. Priority target: whichever deckhand is closer to the gangplank. Vicious Mockery can stall a retreating deckhand's action.
- **Stripes:** Feline Agility means he can explode up the ladder and close instantly. First out, first into the Enforcer cluster at the hatch. Disarming Strike breaks one Enforcer's Pack Tactics setup and hands Delmar a clean Sneak Attack angle. If both Enforcers are adjacent, Trip Attack drops one prone and turns the next ally's attack into an auto-hit. Action Surge can pull an Enforcer out before he gets a second action, which changes the math on the whole phase.

**Dial — Phase 2**

| Direction | Lever | Effect |
| --------- | ----- | ------ |
| **Easier** | Split the Enforcers — one at the hatch, one at the rail with the deckhands | Breaks Pack Tactics (no ally within 5 ft.); eliminates the focused-fire danger; reduces them to single +4 attacks |
| **Easier** | Deckhands break and run for the gangplank on their first turn instead of fighting | Converts them to a chase objective rather than combatants; the party can ignore or pursue |
| **Easier** | If Phase 1 was clean, the topside crew is flat-footed — give the party a surprise round | Removes one full enemy action economy round; particularly useful if PCs took significant damage in Phase 1 |
| **Harder** | Both Enforcers immediately focus the first PC out of the hatch using Pack Tactics | ~75% hit chance with advantage at +4 vs AC 15; avg 18 damage in a single round on a focused PC — enough to drop Perrin or Crissdalynn |
| **Harder** | The Alchemist has moved forward from Phase 3 and throws an Incendiary Flask through the open hatch as the party emerges | DC 13 Dex save, 2d6 fire + burning on fail; affects anyone clustered at the hatch opening; raises Phase 2 adjusted XP to ~575 (Medium) and burns one Alchemist use before Phase 3 |

## Summary

The party cleared all three phases of the boarding encounter and ended the session with Barnaby Rook isolated, unharmed, and out of allies. Phase 1 went fast — the party had the drop on the Dravosi crew in the hold. Cap'n Gorgeous (the Enforcer, named for his golden locks and square jaw) walked into the ambush and was killed instantly before he could call a shout. The two deckhands followed quickly. Phase 1 complete without alerting the topside crew to the party's position. Phase 2: Delmar boosted Crissdalynn up through the hatchway, who burst out and knocked two deck Dravosi prone. Perrin and Jean-Claude came up through a gap in the decking and got the drop on the others. Both Dravosi Enforcers were dispatched; one deckhand named Geoffrey Draves surrendered when he saw his allies fall. Delmar picked up Geoffrey's sword after he dropped it and gave it back to him — Geoffrey joined the party as the first member of their crew. Phase 3: The party used Jean-Claude's Flask of Endless Water to blast Barnaby Rook off the gangplank, separating him from the Enforcer and Alchemist. They quickly finished the Enforcer and wounded the Alchemist severely. The Alchemist threw a gas canister; only Jean-Claude and Perrin were caught in the cloud and both resisted. Jean-Claude identified the batch — standard Grung hunting stock, and specifically Simone's hand (see below). The Alchemist was then dispatched. Rook hauled himself back aboard and fired his flintlock at Beaumont; the round bounced off Beaumont's shell. Beaumont entered the fight with the words: *"Boy, I shit out harder things than you every mornin'."* Session wrapped here: all minions down, Rook unharmed and alone.

## Key Events

- **Phase 1 — The Hold:** Party ambushed the boarding crew in the dark. Cap'n Gorgeous (Dravosi Enforcer, nicknamed for his appearance) was killed in a single round before he could alert topside. Both deckhands dispatched. Phase 1 complete with surprise intact for Phase 2.
- **Phase 2 — Above Deck:** Crissdalynn launched through the hatch with Delmar's assist, knocking two Dravosi prone. Perrin and Jean-Claude breached from a gap in the decking and flanked the others. Dravosi Enforcers and one deckhand killed. Geoffrey Draves surrendered. Delmar returned his sword and recruited him; Geoffrey became the party's first crew member.
- **Geoffrey Draves recruited:** A young Dravosi deckhand who dropped his sword when he saw the fight turn. Delmar convinced him, returned the blade, and he fought on the party's side. First crew acquisition despite the party having no ship yet.
- **Phase 3 — The Gangplank:** Barnaby Rook separated from his men by a Decanter blast off the gangplank. Party killed the Enforcer and wounded the Alchemist. Canister thrown; Jean-Claude and Perrin both resisted.
- **The Canister Reveal:** Jean-Claude passed the Constitution save and placed the gas completely. Cheap Grung hunting stock — the kind he and Simone made together. Specifically Simone's batch. His sister is supplying the Dravosi Crown. He said nothing to the party. Phase 3 continued.
- **Beaumont enters the fight:** Rook fired his flintlock at Beaumont from the gangplank; the round deflected off his shell. Beaumont joined combat with: *"Boy, I shit out harder things than you every mornin'."* Session ended here.
- **Current state:** Barnaby Rook — unharmed, alone, all minions dead. Party — full resources largely intact. Encounter still in progress.
