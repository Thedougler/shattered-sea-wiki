# Session 02 — Run Guide: Conflict is a Surety

**4–6 hours. Opens with The Bell, then combat. Ends wherever the party sets course.**

The session has a scripted first act (finish the fight, meet Grigori, explore the ship) and then opens into full sandbox. The party has a ship, a small crew, and three ports within reach. By the end of Scene 5 they should understand what the ship needs to run, which crew members might stay, and why sailing straight for Tidefall is a risk. What matters is that they leave going somewhere, with a reason.

---

## Session Open — Read Aloud

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> The hold of the [[Saltwright]] smelled of bilge water and old rope, and four strangers were in it — not friends yet, just people who'd made the same calculation about the same ship on the same bad day.
>
> [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Delmar-Fisk|Delmar Fisk]] was the one in the red admiral's coat — forty-one, no fleet left to lead, and a smile that arrived before he opened his mouth. Beside him, [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Crissdalynn-Khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]], a crow aarakocra, large and dark-feathered, fourteen years old and a Sentinel of the Drowned Maw; she'd pulled Delmar out of a closing trench when the Red Lady went down, and she'd been able to see daylight the whole way. [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Jean-Claude-Tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] stood with a poison-tipped arrow nocked and the stillness of someone who hasn't missed in a long time — three feet tall, electric-blue, red beret, false moustache, the first Grung most people here have met who wasn't hunting them. And in the shadows, [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] — a Rattkin barely three feet tall with a longsword that shouldn't fit his frame and a strength behind it he doesn't fully understand.
>
> [[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont]] held [[Barnaby-Rook|Rook]] at the wheel above while they cleared the hold. When the hatch opened, Crissdalynn hit Delmar's hands at a run and he launched her up — wings snapping wide into grey sea air, two Dravosi sprawling. The deck was theirs in moments. One deckhand dropped his sword. Delmar picked it up, said something quiet and reasonable, and handed it back. [[Geoffrey-Draves|Geoffrey Draves]] made his choice before he'd quite finished deciding. He is, at current count, your entire crew.
>
> At the gangplank Jean-Claude sent Rook off the rail with the [[Decanter-of-Endless-Water|Decanter]]. The Alchemist cracked a canister and the gas rolled out. Jean-Claude recognised it the moment it touched his skin — his sister makes it. [[Simone-Tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]] is supplying the Dravosi Crown with Grung toxin. He said nothing. He kept fighting.
>
> Rook hauled himself back over the rail and fired at Beaumont. The round sparked off his shell. Beaumont cracked his knuckles.
>
> *"Boy, I shit out harder things than you every mornin'."*
>
> That is where we left it. Rook alone. Beaumont in the fight. And somewhere below, someone is not coming up.

## Scene Order

> [!DM]
> **Sequence:** Rook → Deck Confrontation → Grigori → Ship → Course → Travel.
> Session opens mid-combat — initiative was already rolled at the end of Session 01. No pre-combat beat. The Bell (Call to Arms) happens inside Round 3 of the fight, not before it.

---

### 1 — The Rook Resolution (30–60 min)

**File:** [[Scene-01-Rook-Final]]

Keep [[content/shattered-sea/beastiary/humanoid/Barnaby-Rook|Rook's statblock]] and [[Scene-01-Rook-Final]] open together — statblock for the numbers, scene file for terrain, lair actions, Geoffrey crisis, mid-fight dial, and negotiation off-ramp.

**Lair action arc:** Round 1 — Arm the Guns (ports open). Round 1 (Beaumont's turn) — Tidefall Maneuver: [[Bisou]] soaks one gun's powder, disabling it. Round 2 — Fire the Guns (one gun only). Round 3 — Call to Arms: Rook rings the bell, crew refuses, [[Shepherd-Grigori|Grigori]]'s voice comes up from below.

**Beaumont** — full statblock in [[Beaumont-Sel]]. Keep it open.

**Three outcomes:**
- *Party wins:* They own the [[HCS-Surety]]. → Scene 2, then Scene 3.
- *Rook negotiates:* Manifest goes dark, he keeps his ship, everyone parts. → Scene 2 on the [[Saltwright]] deck, then Scene 3 on the Saltwright.
- *Rook escapes:* Over the side. Future problem. → Scene 2, then Scene 3 on the Saltwright.

> [!DM]
> Scenes 3–6 below assume the party took the Surety. If they didn't, stay aboard the Saltwright; Scene 3 (Grigori) plays on the Saltwright instead.

### 2 — The Deck Confrontation (5–15 min)

**File:** [[Scene-02b-Deck-Confrontation]]

[[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont]] turns to the two stowaways. Short character scene — earns everything it costs.

**Key beats:**
1. Gratitude, stated plainly. He does not linger on it.
2. **Perrin** — Friend of [[situations/The-Passage|the Passage]]. *"You didn't need to be a ghost on my ship. You only had to ask."*
3. **Jean-Claude** — Blue caste. Beaumont names what he knows. [[Truth-Stone|Truth Stone]] produced from his coat, tossed across the deck. *"Hold that."*
4. Jean-Claude answers honestly. Stone stays cool.
5. *"Either of you needs passage, you ask."* He turns back to the ship.

**If you need to compress:** one Beaumont line, fold the rest into a quiet beat during Scene 4. Don't cut it entirely — it sets the relationship for the campaign.

---

### 3 — Below Deck: Shepherd Grigori (20–40 min)

**File:** [[Scene-03-Grigori]]

Fires on the first character who goes below after the fight. [[Shepherd-Grigori|Grigori]] alone at the galley table, eating, tying off a bandage on his forearm he won't explain. Crew behind a locked door — muffled voices, not coming out until he signals.

**Key beats:**
1. Places set at the table like he was expecting company.
2. *"What kind of pirates are you?"* — weird, practical, or robin hood. Let the party argue. That argument is the scene.
3. His condition: anyone who wants off at the next port gets off. Non-negotiable; he doesn't raise his voice about it.
4. The Pretty One — Cap'n Gorgeous was House Knighton. [[Rupert-Knighton|Commodore Knighton]] will send ships. *"[[Kalowe]]. No Crown presence. Or [[Calveno]] — I have business there."*
5. Grigori signals. The locked door opens. The crew files out.

### 4 — The Ship (45–75 min)

**File:** [[Scene-04-Ship-Exploration]]

**Beat 1 — Ship Roles (~10 min)**
[[Geoffrey-Draves|Geoffrey]] stops them at the wheel. What the [[HCS-Surety|Surety]] needs to run: Captain (empty), Navigator, Bosun, Carpenter, Gunner, Surgeon, Cook, deckhands. Minimum four; ten to run properly. [[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont]] won't take the wheel — he has the [[Saltwright]] and his own route.

**Beat 2 — The Tour (~20 min)**
Don't read the whole layout ([[HCS-Surety-Layout]]). Slow down for:
- **Boarding locker** — arm up; Weapons Locker bastion context emerges here
- **Captain's log** — Beaumont appears three times, increasingly terse
- **Medic's cabinet** — 2 healer's kits, 1 potion of healing, 1 potion of greater healing
- **Captain's cabin** — blunderbuss (Geoffrey didn't know); locked chest (DC 15): 45 gp, papers, 1 potion of greater healing, 1 [[Potion-of-Water-Breathing|potion of water breathing]]; **hidden stash** under bunk (DC 13 Investigation): 110 gp + 2 garnets
- **Seized crates (×2)** — Crate 1: ~97 gp in goods, pendant engraved *"For Mira, from the sea"*; Crate 2: 2 flintlocks, [[Miras-Blade|Mira's Blade]] (+1 shortsword, attunement), 20 Grung tincture vials (~100 gp black market) — Jean-Claude recognizes the prep method
- **Blank Crown writs** — Ensign Wouters looks at them and very deliberately looks at something else
- **Locked cargo crate** (DC 14) — 150 gp, folded map to "Galewall anchorage"
- **Powder magazine** — Geoffrey's one warning: *"No open flames. Not ever."*

**Loot total: ~587 gp coin/goods + [[Miras-Blade|Mira's Blade]], 2× potion of greater healing, 1× [[Potion-of-Water-Breathing|potion of water breathing]], 1× potion of healing. Full breakdown in [[Scene-04-Ship-Exploration]].**

> [!DM] Party Treasury
> Before individual shares, the party should pull ~120 gp off the top into a ship's fund. Crew wages run ~36 gp/month (6 crew at 2 sp/day). Repainting the Crown registry costs 75 gp at Kalowe or 150 gp at [[Port-Tidefall|Tidefall]]. If nobody raises this, [[Geoffrey-Draves|Geoffrey]] will — quietly, once.

**Beat 3 — The Crew (~15–20 min)**
Each defaults to leaving at next port. What changes the math:
- **Holst** — ask a real question about the hull, not the speed
- **Wouters** — give him a formal title; he will write it down
- **Alys** — acknowledge the Surgeon post and the Surgeon's Berth
- **Old Faas** — ask about the rigging, listen to all twelve minutes
- **Noor** — name an interesting destination
- **[[Thunk]]** *(hidden hire)* — ask anything about the guns or powder; that's the whole condition

Track who commits. Each is a real crew gap if lost.

**Beat 4 — The Bastion (~5 min, woven through Beat 2)**
Boarding Locker = Weapons Locker. Chart Table = Chart Archive. Two slots installed, two open. Don't announce this as a rules beat — let them discover what they already have.

Close when Geoffrey asks: *"What do you want to do with her?"*

### 5 — Set Course: The Sandbox Opens

Three options within reach of the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]]:

| Destination | Travel | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| **[[Port-Tidefall\|Port Tidefall]]** | ~1 day | Beaumont's home port; Crown checkpoint; [[Master-Kyzil|Kyzil]] waiting for Crissdalynn |
| **[[Kalowe]]** | ~2 days SE | Free port; no Crown; The Takowan in harbour; good repainting yard |
| **[[Calveno]]** | ~3 days ENE | Grigori's destination; canal city; information economy |

Don't push. Let the conversation happen. Grigori's Calveno interest may tip the vote, or they may ignore it.

---

### 6 — Travel: Central Strait

**File:** [[Scene-05-Central-Strait]]

Fire 2–3 events depending on pace. Don't stack party-specific hooks — one per day of travel.

**Generic events** (any order, any route):
- **The Dolphins** — free atmosphere; squall follows thirty minutes later
- **The Becalmed Merchant** — asks about the missing Crown cutter; is looking at the Surety
- **Night Watch** — something large and unhurried alongside in the dark; Geoffrey says "probably nothing"
- **The Bad Receipt** — pirate schooner peels away hard on sight of the Crown hull
- **Convoy Ahead** — Crown escort hails, wants papers; DC 10/15/20 Deception determines how long they linger

**Party-specific hooks** (one per day):
- **Delmar** — [[Fisks-Fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] pennant on the northern horizon, running east, no acknowledgment
- **Jean-Claude** — a Grung slave collar fished out of the water; Crown registry number; clan identification code
- **Perrin** — a deckhand on a passing merchant stares, says something to the man beside him; ship doesn't stop; hull name: *The Clement Sorrow*, [[Vestra|Vestra]] sister ship
- **Crissdalynn** — compass nudges northeast for two hours in the mid-Strait, then corrects; [[Drowned-Maw|the Maw]] reaching west
