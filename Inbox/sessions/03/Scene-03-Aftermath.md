# Aftermath

*Keep this short. The party needs to breathe, not linger. Three beats: damage, Grigori, depart.*

---

## Beat 1 — The Damage

Sem's report, delivered at the breach:

- The tail stub is wedged in the hull; he can work it free with Geoffrey's help, two hours.
- The breach itself: manageable. Packed timber and pitch will hold for calm water. Not for another storm.
- They need a dry dock. The ship needs proper work before they take her into anything rough.
- ETA to Calveno at current pace: five days. If the weather holds.

> [!dm] Don't turn this into a logistics scene. One exchange with Sem, one clear message: *dry dock or she sinks in the next bad weather.* Then move on.

---

## Beat 2 — Grigori

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-03-03-grigori-healing.png|Grigori heals a wounded crewman too quickly on the wet dawn deck while the breach work continues behind him]]

First light. Grigori comes up the companionway with a tin mug in each hand. He looks at the rail, the breach work, the state of the crew, and does not appear alarmed.

He offers the mugs to whoever is nearest. He checks on the crew by name. If anyone is hurt, he crouches next to them and asks if he can look — not a command, a question. If they say yes, he puts a hand on the injury and murmurs something. It heals faster than it should.

> [!check] Medicine — The healing
> DC 13 casual observation, DC 17 close examination.
> Crit fail: reads as normal magical healing — faster maybe, but nothing that breaks from expected output.
> Fail: faster than expected, but maybe he's just skilled.
> Success: tissue knitting at a rate unrelated to what Grigori put into it. Injury site is too warm after.
> Crit success (DC 17+): the warmth in his hands doesn't come from body heat and doesn't withdraw when he lifts his hand.

> [!dm] Don't explain the healing or name it. Move on before anyone asks a follow-up: Sem needs the stub, Noor needs something, Geoffrey drops a tool. Grigori stands, picks up his mug, watches the horizon.

Nobody has seen him sleep. If a player asks when he slept: he smiles and says the sea keeps him company.

> [!check] Insight — Reading Grigori
> DC 14 surface read, DC 18 sustained attention.
> Crit fail: reads him as tense beneath the calm — misread entirely.
> Fail: unreadable; warm and professional, nothing leaks through.
> Success: genuinely unbothered. No alarm behind the calm.
> Crit success (DC 18+): whatever he is, fatigue isn't part of it. His eyes haven't changed since last night.

---

## Beat 3 — Depart

The stub comes free. Sem patches the breach. The crew runs the sails up in the early morning light. The storm has left the water flat and grey and cold.

> The Surety moves. For the first time since the anchor dropped, the hull answers the wind the way it's supposed to — straight, clean, no drag. Old Faas ties off the last line and looks back at the water behind them. The bioluminescent wake is long gone. The sea gives nothing back.

Set heading: Calveno. Five days.
