# Calveno Approach

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-03-05-calveno-approach.png|The damaged HCS Surety approaches Calveno's crowded canal harbour with its patched Crown-cutter silhouette exposed]]

*The problem is real before the city is visible. The party has five days to think about it. When Calveno appears on the horizon, the problem is still there.*

> [!read-aloud]
> Calveno arrives in layers: sound first, then the smell of coal smoke and salt water, then the city itself — pale stone rising four and five stories straight from the canal, flat-bottomed boats threading every channel in both directions, festival cloth already strung between the warehouses. Then the harbourmaster's tower comes into view, and the problem arrives with it: you are steering a damaged Crown cutter into a city that knows exactly what Crown cutters are supposed to look like.

> [!dm]
> Let the city land. Then bring in the problem: that beautiful harbour is full of people who know what a Crown cutter looks like, and this one is crewed by four adventurers and six non-Dravosi sailors, and hull-patched at the waterline.

---

## Run This Scene

**Target time:** 25-35 minutes.

| Beat | What Happens | If Players Stall |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Show Calveno on the horizon, then state the ship problem. | Ask Sem what must happen next: dry dock, not anchorage. |
| Choice | Let the party decide: main harbour or Le Paludi route. | Perrin knows the second option. If it doesn't come up, ask him directly. |
| La Vasca | Once Perrin calls it or the main harbour fails, the Black-Jaw route works. | Do not make them roll for established network access. |
| Handoff | Sem takes ship, Cobb warns Perrin, Grigori leaves. | Move to Day One or open sandbox. |

## The Problem

The *HCS Surety* is a Crown patrol cutter. It is flying no colours. It has a patched hull breach at the waterline. It is crewed by four adventurers and six sailors who are obviously not Dravosi.

Crown cutters do pass through Calveno. They are crewed by Dravosi and arrive in good order with a manifest. This is not that.

Calveno is a busy commercial port. The harbourmaster will ask questions. The Crown chandlery has eyes on the water. They cannot tie up at a public berth without someone noticing what they're sitting in.

> [!dm] The question lands before they're in the harbour mouth. Two approaches, two different kinds of cost. Perrin knows about the second one. If the party is problem-solving without him speaking up, ask his player: *"Perrin — you know this port. Is there another way in?"* Then let the player decide.

---

## Two Approaches

### Option A — Main Harbour

Straight in on the standard harbour approach. The city's canal entrance is open water, watched from the harbourmaster's tower and the Crown chandlery's upper dock.

The *Surety* is identifiable as a Crown cutter at half a mile. Two naval uniforms from the officer's berth footlockers (about 15 gp and a spare uniform each) buy a bluff attempt.

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Basic bluff | DC 13 Deception against harbour watch |
| Borrowed colours also flown | Advantage |
| Hull damage visible or Crown officer on duty | Disadvantage |
| **Success** | Buys approach and anchorage — not dry dock. Still need a private yard. |
| **Failure** | Questions, delay, escort; Perrin's route becomes the only exit. |

Even a clean bluff lands them in the public harbour with a Crown cutter they cannot put in a public yard. The problem deferred, not solved.

> [!dm] Let them try the bluff if they want it. Success is a real outcome — they make it in. But Sem still needs the ship out of the water, and there's no public drydock that won't file a manifest. Failing the main harbour is fast and clean: harbour watch flags them, questions start, and the party has to move or talk their way out. Either outcome routes back to Perrin.

### Option B — Le Paludi Route

Perrin knows this. Before the *Surety* reaches the main harbour approaches, they can bear wide — north along the outer Calven shoreline — and come at the city from its older edge. Le Paludi is the lowest district: short buildings, narrow channels, canal water that moves slower and darker than the main harbour. The Black-Jaw Run has kept a private drydock here for three generations. Perrin knows it as an address, not a description.

The approach is unglamorous. The Calven Roads are open water; bearing north means a longer run, visible to any ship on the main approach. But the chandlery and the harbourmaster are watching the main channel, not a patrol cutter drifting wide toward the industrial edge.

The cost is not the route. The cost is announcing himself. The Calveno Black-Jaw network will pass his name up the moment the Basin is used. Nona will know he is here within the hour.

> [!dm] Perrin has been on this ship for five days knowing this moment was coming. He knows the route. He knows what using it means. Don't tell him — he already knows. When the city appears on the horizon, let his player sit in that for a beat before anyone asks. If Perrin doesn't speak first, ask him directly, in front of the table. Let the choice happen out loud.

## Taking the Le Paludi Route

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-03-05-black-jaw-channel.png|A Rattkin skiff guides the damaged Surety out of the public harbour and into the Black-Jaw private canal route]]

However Perrin makes contact — a chandlery message, a coded phrase to a canal skiff, a Rattkin face at the outer approaches — it works. A skiff meets them before they reach the public harbour mouth and guides them north and wide, into the narrower channels.

Once inside Le Paludi the buildings come close on both sides. The canal is quiet. Then an arch, low enough that anyone standing on deck has to duck, and on the far side: [[content/shattered-sea/places/calveno/La-Vasca|La Vasca]].

Cobb has the cradle ready before he asks questions. Within the first few minutes he tells Perrin that Nona has been asking after him and she'll know he's here within the hour. *"You should go see her before she comes to you. It'll go better."* He says this the way someone says it when they know from experience.

## Getting In

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-03-05-la-vasca.png|The HCS Surety sits inside La Vasca's covered Black-Jaw dry dock while basin water stirs near the gangplank]]

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-03-05-the-watcher.png|Perrin looks across La Vasca as the damaged Surety is hauled into the hidden Black-Jaw dry dock]]

Once Perrin makes contact, the process is smooth — that's what money and reputation buy. A canal skiff with a Rattkin pilot meets them at the outer approaches and guides them off the main harbour channel, into a narrower waterway between two chandlery warehouses. The basin at the end is small, private, walled on three sides. A yard crew is already waiting. Sem is off the ship before the lines are fully tied.

> [!dm] Entry should feel like relief — they made it, ship is safe, nobody is arresting them. Grigori thanks the party warmly, picks up his bag, and walks into the city at an unhurried pace. He does not say when they'll see him again. Give the table a moment, then move to the handoff.

### La Vasca Handoff

| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| What La Vasca buys | Hidden berth, no public manifest, 3-5 days of uninterrupted repair work |
| What it does not solve | Crown cutter silhouette, false registry plate, new ship name, future inspection |
| Sem's first priority | Hull breach, then registry plate and paint |
| Cobb's line | "You should go see her before she comes to you. It'll go better." |

While she walks the waterline, she mentions that three ships this month asked her about discreet work — she refused all three and remembers them. She also mentions, without particular weight, that a broker on the Bridge's top tier has been paying above-market rates for *Red Lady* survivor accounts. She says it the way she mentions the weather. *Delmar's hook — deliver to him specifically, not the group.* |
| **Jean-Claude entry** | As soon as any PC crosses into open Calveno — the Mercatura, the Bridge, anywhere with a crowd — offer Jean-Claude his observation: a Grung in festival half-mask is standing at the Bridge pillar in the single position that gives sightlines to three exits without appearing to watch any of them. Jean-Claude has stood in that position. *Private sensory note to his player. No announcement to the group.* |

## The Watcher

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-03-05-watcher-close.png|A hooded Rattkin watcher holds Perrin's gaze from the far end of La Vasca's wet stone pier]]

At the far end of the pier, where the basin meets the canal, a figure is standing. Small. A Rattkin, hood up, hands in pockets. Not working. Not passing through. Just standing there, watching the *Surety* come in.

Any PC with passive Perception 13 or higher notices the figure without being told. Any PC who looks directly at them: the Watcher holds their gaze for a full beat — unhurried, deliberate — then turns and walks away. Not quickly. They want to be seen.

They do not follow. They do not approach. They are simply gone.

> [!dm] Perrin knows what that means. Let the player react or not react. Nona is not a threat today. She just wanted him to know she knows.

> [!dm] Don't linger. The weight has landed. Move into Day One and watch Delmar cross the gangplank. See [[Scene-06-Calveno-Day-One]].
