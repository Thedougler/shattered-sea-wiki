# Calveno Day Two

*Crissdalynn spotlight. Sudden reunion first, formal master second, sparring lesson if the table wants the fight.*

---

## Setup

Kyzil ruled out the Midchain late on the day the party arrived. No sightings past the wayport, no trail worth following east. He corrected course and flew through the night. He had a confirmed description: an aarakocra monk aboard a captured Crown patrol cutter heading west. Calveno was the obvious destination.

He has been high over the city since dawn, using the sun as cover and the harbour wind as lift. He is looking for a crow aarakocra on the ground: blue-black iridescent feathers, chart satchel, probably near the water.

He knows she is alive. Seeing her in front of him still breaks the line between master and frightened guardian for one breath.

---

## Perception Before Impact

Only call for this if the party is in open daylight with sky visible.

- **Passive Perception 18+ or DC 18 Perception:** a winged silhouette impossibly high above Calveno, almost swallowed by the sun.
- **DC 22 Perception:** the silhouette folds into a dive with almost no wingbeat. Barn owl wings. Fast, silent, aimed at Crissdalynn.
- **Failure:** no warning. The city noise covers everything until feathers hit the street.

Do not make this feel like an attack roll. The point is suddenness, not threat. If a player reacts fast with a plausible spell, shout, interception, or shield move, let them roll. Kyzil changes course instantly rather than collide with anyone else.

---

## Read Aloud

> [!read-aloud]
> Calveno keeps its noise close to the water: bells, canal voices, gulls, the clatter of cart wheels over stone. Above it, almost lost in the white glare of the sun, a small winged shape folds itself out of the sky. No cry. No wingbeat. Just a shadow dropping faster than it should, and then Crissdalynn is hit by a rush of pale feathers and wrapped in both arms, hard enough that her talons scrape stone before balance catches up.

Give Crissdalynn one beat to respond before Kyzil speaks.

> [!dm] Put this in Crissdalynn's body: sudden weight, feathers, talons scraping stone, a grip that does not let go right away. Kyzil is not performing calm yet. He spent days searching east of Midchain and expecting wreckage.

---

## The Formal Mask Returns

Kyzil straightens first. He smooths his robes, folds his wings, and becomes Master Kyzil again with visible effort.

**Roleplay prompt:** Owl Obi-Wan who just panic-hugged his missing apprentice and is now trying to recover his dignity in front of witnesses.

**Delivery:** warm, spare, precise. Wind and tide metaphors. Questions before answers. Let silence do work.

First line, once he has himself back:

> *"The wind carries poor rumors, little crow. I am pleased this one lied."*

Then:

- *"Tell me the course of your pilgrimage. Not the story. The course."*
- *"Where did the storm take you? Where did you take yourself?"*
- *"What did the Maw show you that the ledgers could not?"*
- *"A tide may throw a vessel from its heading. It does not excuse the navigator from knowing where she is."*
- *"You have grown stronger. Good. Strength is wind in canvas. Direction is the hand on the line."*

Kyzil wants progress, not confession. If Crissdalynn gives names, he records them mentally. If she gives technical details, he sharpens immediately: water temperature, current angle, cloud rotation, compass behavior, whether the charts survived, whether the Maw felt like storm, trench, creature, or will.

---

## The Rest of the Party

He notices the party after Crissdalynn. That is deliberate. He looks each person over once, head tilting slightly, then bows.

He is polite and evaluating. He does not ask them to justify themselves. Crissdalynn chose to travel with them, so he treats them as part of her weather.

Useful lines:

- *"You are the current she chose. I will not insult her by pretending that means nothing."*
- *"If you kept her alive, you have my thanks. If she kept you alive, you have my sympathy."*
- *"Names, when you are ready. A ledger with no names is merely paper."*

## Sparring Offer

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-03-07-kyzil-spar.png|Master Kyzil spreads his wings and uses separate hands to redirect the party during a four-on-one canal-side sparring lesson]]

Kyzil asks only after the reunion has breathed.

> *"Have you practiced, Crissdalynn? Or has the sea been kind enough to let you remain theoretical?"*

If she pushes back, he almost smiles.

> *"Then let us test the wind. Four against one, if your companions are willing. No killing blows. No pride worth keeping."*

Run this as a short demonstration, not a full boss fight.

- **Arena:** a canal-side plaza, La Vasca yard, quiet rooftop, or open training court. Give Crissdalynn vertical space.
- **Length:** 3 rounds, or until the party earns four clean respect moments or the table has had the point.
- **Tone:** Kyzil is confident, playful only at the edges, and absolutely nonlethal by choice.
- **Feel:** four people punching air. He redirects force, steps through gaps, lets attacks miss by inches, and corrects footwork while moving.
- **Rules:** Use the full statblock on [[content/shattered-sea/characters/npcs/Master-Kyzil|Master Kyzil]]. The statblock has no training-mode limits. His restraint is narrative: turned dagger edges, wind-caught falls, flat strikes, and no follow-up on a downed creature.

If the party earns his respect, he is delighted and hides about half of it.

> *"Good. The tide has teeth."*

If he outclasses them without being meaningfully pressured, he is gentle.

> *"Again, someday. Not because you failed. Because wind that stops moving becomes weather."*

Commend the party individually after the fight. Pick one real thing each player did well:

- protected an ally
- changed altitude
- forced Kyzil to spend movement
- held an action intelligently
- read the terrain
- made him dodge instead of parry
- used restraint in a spar

## Crissdalynn Reward: Read the Current

Kyzil teaches this whether he wins or loses. The difference is framing:

- **If Crissdalynn impresses him:** reward.
- **If she struggles:** instruction.
- **If the party refuses the spar:** quiet lesson, practiced with two fingers against her wrist and a single pressure-point tap.

> *"A strike is not only force. It is a question. Bone, breath, balance, fear. Ask correctly, and the body answers before the mouth has time to lie."*

**Optional Kensei Lesson: Read the Current**

Homebrew intent: this is not a new resource, stance, token, or tracker. It is an optional way for Crissdalynn to spend her existing monk ki/focus on a hit, using the same timing space as Stunning Strike and Kensei's Deft Strike.

**Rule:** Once on her turn when Crissdalynn hits a creature with an unarmed strike or Kensei weapon, she can spend 1 ki/focus point to read the target through the contact. That hit cannot also carry another paid monk rider such as Stunning Strike or Deft Strike. She chooses one question, and the DM answers truthfully using combat-readable information.

- **Guard:** What is this creature relying on most for defense right now: armor, agility, toughness, will, magic, distance, or terrain?
- **Opening:** Which saving throw appears easiest to pressure: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma?
- **Pattern:** What is one combat habit the creature is relying on right now: counterattacks, reach, mobility, guarding an ally, protecting an object, concentration, brute force, or escape?

Limits:

- No action required; spend the ki/focus point on the hit.
- No exact numbers, full statblocks, secret identity, hidden lore, or motives.
- Repeating it on the same creature should reveal a different category or a deeper version of an earlier answer, not the same fact again.

## High Eyrie Warning

Kyzil gives this after the spar or before leaving.

The High Eyrie has recorded this over the Maw this season:

- storms forming without a pressure line
- sinkings with no normal wreck pattern
- wind shear that breaks approach from the Maw side
- turbulent weather pushing into the Central Strait earlier than it should
- Sentinel routes becoming dangerous from both the Maw and Strait approaches

Use his language:

> *"The Eyrie is not surrounded by weather. It is being taught weather."*

> *"The Maw throws storms now like a wounded thing throws blood into water. From the Maw, the climb is dangerous. From the Central Strait, it is merely arrogant."*

> *"If you come home by that road, come with a pilot who respects fear, or with wings you are prepared to lose."*

He does not forbid her from returning. He makes the route honest.

## Exit

Kyzil does not become a follower. He has High Eyrie business in Calveno: messages to send, ledgers to compare, and a route home to reassess.

Before leaving, he tells Crissdalynn:

> *"You are not the bird I sent away. Good. I had begun to suspect the one I sent away was not the one I meant to teach."*
