**Characters present:** [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]], [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Delmar-Fisk|Delmar Fisk]] (as Admiral Fisk), [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Jean-Claude-Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Crissdalynn-Khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]]

## Part 1 — Perrin's Vision

*Perrin is on watch. The storm has thinned to hard sideways rain. The ship groans at anchor.*

**DM:** Perrin. The rail is cold under your hands, the storm thinning to hard sideways rain while the Surety groans at anchor beneath you. We're scooting back just a couple minutes. You're on watch. The water below gives nothing back. For one breath, the black surface seems to hold still, and then the world drops away. Your eyes go white, your knees buckle. There's no sensation of falling. You're simply somewhere else. You feel the cold and then the enormous pressure pushing the air out of your chest — everywhere at once, the weight of countless fathoms pressing down on you. There's something solid beneath you in this black, sightless abyss. The absolute bottom of the world. Your lungs scream for air. You open your mouth.

Your hands find your throat. There is no up from here. There is no surface. There is only the weight and the dark and the stone beneath your feet. You breathe. You gasp for air, and somehow you breathe. A shape moves at the edges of the dark. Pale things lit from within, pulsing in long, slow rhythms like breath — dozens of them, hundreds, moving in loose constellations through the water around you, completely unbothered by your presence.

The floor beneath you is worked stone, worn smooth. In the bioluminescent light, you see channels running through it in long, deliberate lines toward the darkness. There are no walls, no ceiling. The floor simply continues forever into the black abyss. Something in this space turns its attention towards you — not from any direction you can sense, though you feel watched nonetheless. What do you do?

**Perrin:** Perrin would check over himself immediately — is he injured? Is he bleeding? Can he breathe okay?

**DM:** Your arms move very slowly through the water. The pressure holds you down, difficult to fight the immense weight.

**Perrin:** I can breathe, at least? Somehow?

**DM:** The water seems to flow through you as if you're meant to breathe it.

**Perrin:** I want to call out to it, and say — "Man, have, have we met, si? Do I, do I know you?"

*Perrin rolls 14 insight.*

**DM:** You know this is not hunting. Something is paying attention to you — not in a threatening manner, more curious. What do you do?

**Perrin:** I'm going to call out to it. "Have, have we met? Do I know you?"

**DM:** You find it comes out very difficult to speak with the water. Nonetheless, you feel it — the pale life forms, all the jellyfish creatures in the distance, their lights scatter and move, reacting to your presence. You can see a sickly red-pinkish glow in the distance. You can't see the source of it, just this ominous pink, almost like a sunrise happening far off in the black.

*The darkness closes in — not the patient ocean dark, but something total moving, pulled against every surface at once. The floor disappears beneath you. There's only water everywhere, and your lungs empty on reflex. You struggle for breath, and then finally the breath floods back. Your mouth opens, pulling water in, and you feel something behind your ears — the water flows out. Something behind your jaw you're not familiar with. Your chest isn't involved.*

**DM:** You breathe, and yet your chest does not rise. You feel enormous and powerful — more so than you've ever felt in your life. Your body, you can feel every inch of sixty feet of muscle. The tail is last — your signature weapon. The lower lobe trailing far behind with a sharp barbed hook grown and sharpened over centuries of combat. Your skin reads the water in frequencies you cannot name. Your whiskers — if they are whiskers anymore — sense everything around you. You can feel the presence of every creature in the water. Temperature, pressure differentials, current — all of it is as clear to you as sight on land. You smell or taste something above: blood diluted, days old, mixing with storm-churned rain. Fresh. From a hull above. The hunger awakens in you. That slow, gnawing, constant hunger that drives you constantly forward toward the next meal.

*If it's not obvious, you have been polymorphed into a whip shark.*

There are two things: you can smell blood above from a hull, and you can sense the presence of something below you — a large life form. Larger than you. Something you've hunted before, and are very good at hunting. In fact, it's what you evolved to hunt.

**Perrin:** If I had any capacity for thought at all, Perrin would be like, "In the one-off chance this is real, maybe I shouldn't go up and potentially kill people I don't want to kill. What's going on down here? I'm a whip shark? I'm hungry? Okay." I go below. Go down to whatever that is.

**DM:** As you dive deeper, the shape comes into view. Longer than two ships laid end to end — probably two hundred feet, nose to tail. Moving through the dark water in a slow, unhurried arc. Its breath comes in long, slow cycles, each exhale a warm column of bubbles rising. A deep pulse — the body you inhabit reads its heart rate. It has no idea you're there. At the exact moment in the cycle when you feel your instinct take over, years of practice from previous generations have taught you how to hunt these things, and your body tenses in preparation. What do you do?

**Perrin:** I'll fucking go for it.

**DM:** You compress your long eel-like body like a bowstring and rocket toward it, slipping through the water at frankly incredible speeds. Diving beneath the whale, you whip your tail up toward the exposed underbelly and drive the barb into it, then begin the pull.

*Rolling a constitution check — modifier is plus eight. That's a dirty 20. You push harder and seat the hook deeper in the whale and pull. You begin the second phase of the hunt.*

The whale dives, pitches, sounds into the dark. You go with it. You feel the barb in your tail holding. The whale is going nowhere it does not want to go.

*Your vision goes blank once again. The ocean leaves. You're standing back at the bottom of the sea. The bioluminescent things drift back to the edge of the zone. The floor is beneath your feet. The smell of blood from above is still there, and you are back to the size you've always been.*

You're looking up through black water at the underside of a ship. Not this ship — older timber, different hull. From below, from the dark where the light does not reach, something rises. Fast. Faster than anything that size should move. The lower lobe of its tail streams behind it as it climbs. The long, pale whip ending in a hook that catches the light for just a moment before the hull above you explodes inward. Timber and copper sheathing. A muffled roar of water rushing into the hole that wasn't there a second ago. The hook is through. The creature drives its weight sideways, and the ship above lurches. You can see the whole hull shift, and it pulls. The barb seats deeper with every foot of drag.

Your vision shifts once again. Something below, far below the current floor, below the worked stone — whatever the foundation of this world is — pulls the attention of the space away. A fraction of it remains, the way a door left open is still open even with no one standing in it. The pale things begin to drift back toward the edge of the zone.

One word arrives, burning into your mind. You can feel the cost and effort that went into communicating just this one word:

**"Grow."**

And then you wake up.

**Perrin:** *(quietly)* Grow?

**DM:** Your knees have not even finished buckling yet. As far as anyone's concerned, only a second took place. Anyone who was nearby, Perrin might notice something happened.

## Part 2 — The Fall

*Perrin is in the rigging, approximately thirty feet up.*

**DM:** Roll a dex check.

**Perrin:** I rolled a one. Down we go.

**Jean-Claude:** If I see him go "ugh" and start to fall, I'll lash him with my tongue, brace myself, and try to arrest his fall.

**DM:** You're within ten feet — roll 11 on luck, that makes it possible. Roll the actual attack check.

**Jean-Claude:** Plus six to attack — eleven.

**DM:** You caught him, but he brought you down with him. You're sharing the damage — half each, one D6 each. Both of you falling together.

**Crissdalynn:** I'll try and catch them. I'm on the crow's nest. I'll fly down.

**DM:** Roll acrobatics.

**Crissdalynn:** Sixteen.

**DM:** Yeah, you nullified it. You guys were going to take four bludgeoning damage each — Crissdalynn just saved you.

*Crissdalynn swoops from the crow's nest, catches Perrin mid-fall — Jean-Claude dangling from his tongue, dragged along. She sets all three of them down on the weather deck.*

**Perrin:** *(on all fours on the deck)* Ugh. Thank you, my, my... Man, thanks, my friend. Pardon my momentary lapse of judgment. I appreciate the save.

**Crissdalynn:** *(carefully releasing him)* It's okay. I only eat rats.

**Perrin:** *(looking up at Jean-Claude)* How's he doing? Is he actually just smoking a cigarette?

**Jean-Claude:** I light a cigarette and ask what's good with you.

*The first impact strikes the side of the ship from below.*

**DM:** Everyone roll dex saves. The ship rocks hard.

*Rolls: Jean-Claude 20, Perrin 16, Crissdalynn pass, Delmar inside.*

**DM:** You're all ready. This is not a wave, not a storm. The ship lurches. A second impact hits — roll again, everyone. Keep both rolls, choose one.

*Delmar sacrifices his high roll for the crew's roll. He fails, slides across the gun deck but catches Geoffrey before he's thrown clear. The rigger [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Old-Faas|Old Faas]] is launched off the rigging, falls ten feet, takes 5 damage, incapacitated.*

**DM:** [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Sem-Holst|Sem Holst]], below decks, "Oh, fuck. Okay, okay." He manages to keep himself up.

*Perrin spots the fin.*

**Perrin:** Okay. *(scampers up a few feet of rigging, raises voice)* Listen to me, everybody! This here's a whip shark, see? A big long fucker. It's gonna give us a hard time. I know everything about it. Listen to me and we'll make her through. Get back, Admiral. Apologies, Crystal.

*Perrin gives bardic inspiration to Delmar, Jean-Claude, and Crissdalynn.*

## Part 3 — The Whip Shark Combat

**The shark is over sixty feet long — longer than the ship itself. Body the size of the ship, tail double that.**

**Initiative order:**
- Jean-Claude — 20
- Shark — 19
- Delmar — 9
- Perrin — 9
- Crissdalynn — 6

---

### Round 1

**Jean-Claude:** I move to Old Faas and cast Cure Wounds on him — 10 hit points. Then as my bonus action, I cast Hunter's Mark on the shark.

**Shark:** *Nat 1.* You see the dorsal fin charging toward the ship, the wake rising — it dives under, and the impact you brace for never comes.

**Delmar:** All guns! Prepare to fire! Starboard guns — swivel guns and broadsides. When you see the fish, put a cannonball in it!

*Fires all guns simultaneously — one roll, dex plus proficiency. 21 to hit. Deals 16 piercing damage — two D8 and two D6.*

**DM:** A massive boom. The two swivel guns and the broadside lock on and impact the water. Blood-red mist splashes up as the shark takes the hit. It immediately swims off into the distance, diving below. You don't think it's gone, but you certainly hurt it.

**Delmar:** *(to [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Geoffrey-Draves|Geoffrey]], nodding)* Good shooting. Reload. *(takes Steady Aim for next turn)*

**Perrin:** I cast Mirror Image — three copies of me scattered through the rigging. Bonus action: keep drumming, telling everyone everything I know about whip sharks. And I'll use Cutting Words as a reaction the next time the shark makes an attack roll.

**Crissdalynn:** I fly out over the water. I'm going to stay about fifteen feet back. Bonus action — activating my tattoo.

**DM:** You all see her fly out toward the shark. The spaces between her feathers start to glow white-blue, outlining geometric patterns across her skin in a language none of you can read.

**Crissdalynn:** I punch it with wind. Fifteen feet out, using the tattoo tendril as an airbender strike. Rolls 10 — doesn't hit.

---

### Round 2

**Jean-Claude:** Shark's surfaced enough — I'll shoot it with my bow. 25 to hit. Five piercing, five force from Hunter's Mark, then bonus — seven. Solid hit.

**DM:** The arrow finds its mark. You can almost hear a deep bellow from underwater as the arrow lands. *(to table)* That hurt and pissed it off.

**Shark:** It surfaces. Its eyes study Jean-Claude for a moment. Back down. Then — whip crack — the tail sweeps across the deck.

*26 to hit vs Crissdalynn. Crissdalynn tries Cutting Words reaction — Perrin burns bardic inspiration. With Cutting Words applied the roll still hits.* 

*Actually: Shark attacks Jean-Claude — 26 to hit, AC 16. Jean-Claude takes 13 slashing damage. Strength save DC 17 — rolls 16, fails by one. Perrin uses Cutting Words — rolls a D6 but 26 minus D6 still exceeds the save DC. Jean-Claude holds on.*

**DM:** The barb slices across your chest. You hear Perrin's song from the rigging: *"Careful about the tail — it'll pull you off the edge and then..."* The thought flashes through your mind: *oh no.* You hold on. You manage to stay on the ship.

**Delmar:** *(stepping forward, leveling the blunderbuss)* You're not even food. You're the food that my food eats.

*Steady Aim (bonus action already used), fires — 18 + 7 = 25 to hit. Two D8 plus 5 piercing — 14 damage.*

**DM:** Big hit. Bloodied. It's looking rough.

**Perrin:** Healing Word, ritual — heals Jean-Claude for 13 hit points. Then bardic inspiration to Crissdalynn. In my song I weave something about the frog-man needing healing. 

**Jean-Claude:** *(from the deck)* Jeremiah was a bullfrog. He needs some healing.

**Crissdalynn:** *(attack of opportunity as shark passes within 15 feet)* 18 to hit — 12 plus 5 is 17 damage. Six of that is force, rest bludgeoning. These are magical weapons.

**DM:** You can hear cartilage creak. The shark is limping.

---

### Round 3

**Jean-Claude:** *(top of round 3)* Does it have advantage from being grappled? It will be grappled. I have Sharpshooter. Roll — 16 to hit. DC?

**DM:** Sixteen is a hit. That was exactly what you needed.

**Jean-Claude:** Piercing, force, psychic — Dread Strike. Total: 22 damage.

**DM:** How do you want to do this?

*But first — Crissdalynn acts.*

**Crissdalynn:** This is my Kamehameha moment. I'm going to grapple it. I hit for 14 damage on the first attack — then, using Punch and Grab, I attempt the grapple. Contested strength check, using bardic inspiration.

*Crissdalynn rolls 15. Shark rolls nat 1.*

**DM:** You are going to grab a sixty-foot shark?

**Crissdalynn:** Yes I am.

**DM:** Roll it.

**Crissdalynn:** I grabbed it. I am lifting this fish out of the water. I have it by the tail. I have advantage on attacks against creatures I'm grappling. Fury of Blows — two more attacks. First: 21 to hit, 14 damage. Second: 18 to hit, 10 damage. Is it still alive?

**DM:** It's out of the water, thrashing. Finish it.

**Crissdalynn:** *(shouting)* Finish it!

**DM:** And for anyone here familiar with sharks — it cannot breathe while it's no longer moving. Forced induction.

**Jean-Claude:** *(from the deck, bowstring drawn)* She's holding it by the tail. Its entire underside is exposed. The anus is basically a bullseye target. If I fire directly into it, the arrow could conceivably come out its mouth.

**DM:** It is fully visible. It's a long fish.

*Jean-Claude releases the arrow. The world slows. Crissdalynn holds the shark's tail up, the creature thrashing — and the arrow goes in the back door, sailing forward through sixty feet of whip shark. The thrashing stops.*

**DM:** With a tell, it stills. The fight's over.

**Perrin:** *(one last boom on the drum, then silence)*

*Delmar calls up from the gun deck:* We have learned much today.

**Jean-Claude:** Turns out the only way to kill a shark is to shoot it in the butt. That's all it takes.

## Part 4 — Aftermath

*The crew makes their way above deck. [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Sem-Holst|Sem Holst]], bandaged, heads straight for Delmar.*

**DM:** *(as Sem)* "Sir, we cannot take any weight right now. Those hits were bad. We cannot take any extra weight. If anything, we need to throw stuff overboard."

**Jean-Claude:** I head below decks to find the impacts and start casting Mending — over and over, as long as it takes.

**DM:** *(as Sem)* "Three impacts. Some quite big. The hull is holding, just barely — two inches of water in the bilge. The standard is ten inches for this ship, so we're not sinking yet. Mending helps stem the tide. It's a patch at best — you'll need dry dock, a shipwright, tar. But for now, it'll hold."

*(pause)* "Samuel, I've never seen or heard tell of a whip shark this far west. As far as I know, they never left the Maw. I can't believe we got hit by one this far out."

*Delmar uses Alter Self — his appearance shifts: Manta Ray form internally, and his disguise resolves into Admiral Fisk. His hair pulled high and tight, dark brown mustache thick over his upper lip where before there was none. He removes his Dravosi red coat and locks it in the captain's chest.*

**Delmar:** *(to the crew)* Right now you're mostly men. I will soon train you to be gladiators. And then — Fisks.

*Delmar dives into the water in Manta Ray form. He retrieves the shark's tail barb — a four-foot spike of bone and cartilage, serrated on every edge except the joint at the base where it can be safely held. He gets the sense this would make an excellent weapon if shaped into one.*

*The party also cuts loose several plate-sized shark scales for hull patching, collects shiny teeth as trophies, and takes some meat in case anyone feels adventurous enough to cook whip shark.*

*Jean-Claude and Perrin (with bardic inspiration) attempt to find fertilized egg sacs. Survival roll — 13 base, with bardic 15. Partial success: three eggs recovered, all fertilized.*

**Jean-Claude:** *(holding up an egg)* It's a fertilized whip shark egg. If it's worth what I think it's worth—

**Delmar:** We're going to a port. We have fertilized whip shark eggs. Do you know what that means?

*[[content/shattered-sea/characters/npcs/Shepherd-Grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] comes up to the weather deck, carrying tins of a pale healing liquid, distributing them to the crew. He walks to Jean-Claude — who took 8 lightning damage for something entirely unrelated — and extends his hand. A red, viscous, gaseous light flows from his wrist and settles on Jean-Claude's wound. Flesh knits together, perfectly, silently. No components. No words. No incantation. He says nothing else.*

**Perrin:** *(arcana roll, 18)* He used no components, no somatic components. He spoke no words. The magic just seemed to flow.

**DM:** *(as Grigori)* "It looks like we had a rather fortunate encounter. I'm very glad of this. We are still on course to Calveno, yes? I do hope we will get there in time. I have to be there with Impuni, so I would—"

**Perrin:** Don't worry, friend. I've got a deadline as well, see.

**Delmar:** It looks like it's going to take us about four days. *(to the group, showing Rook's charts)* I've gone through the captain's logs and manifest — I have patrol routes and boarding records. I've plotted us a course that keeps us on shipping lanes but avoids anywhere Rook's old victims might recognize this hull.

**DM:** With the shark-scale patching Jean-Claude and Sem did together, four days.

**Grigori:** *(before returning below)* "I sense great potential in you. And in the Geoffrey boy."

## Part 5 — Five Days at Sea

*The storm settles. Long, gray swells. Old Faas lashes the sail to what the damaged ship can manage. Thunk bails constantly. The ship moves slowly but surely.*

**DM:** Here's how the next four days work. Eight hours sleep, eight hours ship work — watches, rigging, bailing, whatever [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Sem-Holst|Sem]] needs. That leaves eight hours a day free. Sem can teach anyone carpentry or hull patching. [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Old-Faas|Old Faas]] can teach anyone charts or sail work. The captain's chart archive is yours — harbor approaches, city layout, anything you want to know about Calveno. [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Alys-Kuiper|Alys Kuiper]] has the surgeon's bath if anyone needs to remove exhaustion levels. And if you want to get to know the crew, you'll have four days to do it.

---

**Jean-Claude** spends all four days helping [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Sem-Holst|Sem Holst]] with hull repairs. He casts Mending repeatedly, filling gaps and patching with shark scales while Sem quietly fixes what Jean-Claude misses without saying a word. Sem confides: the figurehead is gone — good mahogany — and he's worried about the hull. *"If we hit another storm, that's going to pop right out."*

**Perrin** works the rigging with Old Faas. Day one, Faas strips his shirt off — turns out the old man is built like a brick shithouse, faster up the rigging than any rat. Perrin rolls 16 acrobatics. Faas is impressed, tells stories about the old days, running cargo with Rook when he was just a pirate. Some of the stories are almost certainly bullshit.

**Crissdalynn** spends her time training and working through Rook's chart archive. Her investigation of the Drowned Maw records returns a nat 19 — Rook gave the Maw a wide berth, broke Imperial rules about entering those waters twice. His records are deliberately light. *"He went in, but he didn't want anyone knowing what he found."*

**Delmar** runs the ship's administration — rations, payroll, bilge rotation, course corrections, training [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Geoffrey-Draves|Geoffrey]] in swordsmanship. Finds locked crew chests in the hold: six sets of naval uniforms, fifteen gold.

*During the downtime, Delmar's Admiral Fisk persona gets strange enough for Crissdalynn to notice. His hair and mustache sit wrong, his cadence shifts, and he calls her "Crystalline" instead of Miss Khinriss. Crissdalynn tells him to take a drink from her flask. He does, the brown fades back toward red, the looseness returns to his hair, and he smiles.*

**Delmar:** "Thank you, Miss Khinriss. You're right. I was acting a little strange, wasn't I?"

*He then resumes training Geoffrey, calling him "Mr. James" in the formal Fisk mode. The lesson is brutal and flashy; Geoffrey learns less than Delmar hopes, mostly because Delmar outclasses him.*

Simply going in the front door in a stolen government ship will draw eyes. Fortunately, the [[content/shattered-sea/factions/Black-Jaw-Run|Black-Jaw Run]] has a dry dock at [[content/shattered-sea/places/calveno/La-Vasca|La Vasca]] in Le Paludi — the old part of the city.

**Jean-Claude:** I suggest we take the back door.

**Jean-Claude:** What about the name? The ship is still the [[content/shattered-sea/ships/HCS-Surety|HCS Surety]].

**Crissdalynn:** I vote *Uncertainty.*

**Jean-Claude:** HCS Uncertainty — from HCS Surety. I like that. *(pause)* Actually, drop the prefix. Just *Uncertainty*, private vessel. The Dravosi are the only ones who use HCS. That'd be a target.

*Delmar removes his Dravosi red coat and locks it in the captain's chest.*

**Perrin:** The Dravosi come here, but this is the heart of the resistance against them. They overreach and overstep constantly. People are watching for their colors.

**Cobb:** "Full paint coat, new name plates, fake papers. Anything custom?"

*As the group steps off the gangplank onto shore — Delmar first:*

**DM:** The wind seems to pick up momentarily. You see the water beneath you as you step across the gangplank. The moment you step off that ship, it seems to reach up toward you, and you swear you can hear your name on the wind — and it's gone the second you step onto shore.

*Shepherd Grigori comes up behind:*

**DM:** *(as Grigori)* "It's been a pleasure doing this voyage with you. Unfortunately, this is where we part ways. I have much business to take care of here in Calveno. It's been a pleasure meeting you."

**Grigori:** "I am doubtful I'll need employment. I expect my business here concluded in a week or two. Perhaps our paths will cross again."

*He leaves. Delmar notes: Grigori's handshake is icy cold. Not normal warmth. Like touching a dead body. But perfectly composed.*

*[[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Sem-Holst|Sem]], [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Thunk|Thunk]], and Old Faas head off to find a tavern. ("Oh, we're gonna get hammered.")*

## Part 7 — Calveno: Day One (Shore Leave)

### Jean-Claude and Thunk (and Sam)

Jean-Claude goes with [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Thunk|Thunk]] and Sem — wants to see the city, but with people he trusts. They head to a tavern in the Mercatura district. He later finds his way to a shop called **[[content/shattered-sea/places/calveno/Casa-Lupo|Casa Lupo]]** in Le Paludi.

*Before heading out, Jean-Claude casts Disguise Self and changes only one thing: he is green instead of blue.*

**Jean-Claude:** St. Grande may be looking for a blue drunk with a mustache and a beret, but a green drunk with a mustache and a beret? They are not looking for him.

*With festival preparations underway and people already in costume, he draws far less attention than usual. Sem and Thunk pay for his drinking; Thunk proves dumb except about one thing, and that one thing is guns.*

**DM:** *(as old shopkeeper)* "Now what can I get for you?"

**Jean-Claude:** "I would like to inspect your finest firearms."

*The shopkeeper sets out a flintlock musket, blunderbuss, ammo.*

**Jean-Claude:** Actually — something more silent. Do you have bows? Crossbows?

But if you go south down [[content/shattered-sea/places/calveno/Studio-Orsini|Studio Orsini]], there's an alchemist workshop — [[content/shattered-sea/characters/minor/Marta-Orsini|Marta Orsini]]. She's into all that weird stuff. She'll buy it off you."

[[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Delmar-Fisk|Delmar]] and [[content/shattered-sea/characters/crew/Geoffrey-Draves|Geoffrey]] head toward the Mercatura district looking for a weaponsmith to assess the barb spike. En route — Delmar gets the sense someone in the crowd is looking for him.

**DM:** An older woman, mid-forties. White hair pulled back in a ponytail, blue-gray robes with a slight limp. She's scanning the crowd very intensely. Her eyes fall on you and lock on. She walks up quickly, pulling a small leather book from her robes.

### Perrin and Nona

*Perrin runs to Le Paludi, to [[content/shattered-sea/characters/npcs/Nona-Black-Jaw|Nona]]'s kitchen. This is Perrin's home family.*

**DM:** [[content/shattered-sea/characters/npcs/Nona-Black-Jaw|Nona]] is at the stove on a large stool, back to you, holding an enormous spatula. The kitchen is warm. [[content/shattered-sea/characters/minor/Enzo|Enzo]] — the black jaguar tabaxi bodyguard in a tailored suit — leans against the kitchen door. [[content/shattered-sea/characters/minor/Ruk|Ruk]], the lizardfolk, stands against the wall. Another rat-king family is just finishing at the table — a mother, crying. "I don't know where he's gone. He didn't show up last night. I think he got kidnapped."

*Ruk: "Don't worry. Nona will take care of it."*

*Nona: "Yes. Yes." She looks up — sees Perrin standing there early. Enzo pulls Perrin back outside. The other family leaves, tearful.*


*Enzo brings Perrin back in. Nona, without turning:*

**DM:** *(as Nona)* "Enzo, make sure you have someone looking for whoever picked that boy up. Not for me. Not for me." *(still stirring)* Enzo and Rook are staring daggers at you from either side. Waiting.

**Perrin:** I stand in the center of the room. Very small. Waiting for her to address me.

**DM:** *(as Nona, eventually)* "Peron."

**DM:** *(as Nona)* "What does it mean, Peron, when a rat king doesn't meet their check-in?"

*Perrin knows the answer: caught, killed, or captured. Rat kings are actively hunted. If one fails to check in, Nona assumes the worst.*

**Perrin:** "Nona. It's, it's a miracle I'm here, see? I've got some terrible news for you, see?"

*Nona's hand tightens on the wooden spoon. A crack — she discards a piece of it and keeps stirring.*

**Perrin:** "It's gone, Nona. The Vestra. It's all gone, Nona."

**DM:** *(as Nona)* "I don't care about the Vestra, Peron."

**DM:** *(as Nona)* "I do not care about Vincenzo's boat. Vincenzo has been gone many years. I miss him dearly, but we lost so much money on that stupid talking boat. I am glad it is at the bottom of the ocean. Now you, though — I need to know what attacked my boy. How many men must we kill to put its head on my wall?"

*Perrin gives the thousand-yard stare for five seconds — staring completely past Nona and through her at the wall behind. Then looks down.*

**Perrin:** "It was, it was big, see? And the water, it get all bubbly, hot and boiling, see? And then like nothing, like lightning. Oh, it was all gone." *(voice breaks)*

**DM:** *(as Nona)* "I could have sworn you got kidnapped by the Tarahs. After Cobb told me one of their ships came in, I thought—"

**Perrin:** "No, no, Nona. But, but you ain't gotta be too sad or too broken up, Nona, see, 'cause I got it all figured out. We got a new ship. We got a new crew. We're gonna make her all back. Don't, don't you worry, Nona. We got it all figured out, see."

**DM:** *(as Nona, eventually)* "I am..."

**DM:** *(as Nona)* "The crew on the Vestra. What happened to them? Were you the only survivor?"

*Perrin looks away. He does not say it plainly, but Nona understands there were no other survivors.*

**DM:** *(as Nona)* "I will ensure their families are taken care of. Enzo, make sure the crew of the Vestra have consolation."

*Nona also realizes she had assumed Perrin was captured by the Tarahs and ordered retaliation.*

**DM:** *(as Nona)* "Enzo, before you go — call off the attacks. I thought you were captured."

*The table notes how lucky Delmar was to have removed his Dravosi red coat before this visit; if he had entered with Perrin in those colors, Enzo would likely have put him into a wall by the throat.*

*The scene continues — Perrin tells Nona about the crew, that they were all stowaways, that he trusts them. [[content/shattered-sea/characters/minor/Vincenzo-Black-Jaw|Vincenzo]]'s seat at the table is left open, as is custom. Nona has a favor to ask.*

**Perrin:** "For you, Nona? Absolutely. Anything I can do for you."

*Ruk licks his lips. Enzo watches in silence.*

*Before leaving, Perrin gets a sending stone from Nona.*

## Part 8 — Master Kyzil

*Later that day. Delmar, Crissdalynn, and Jean-Claude are back on the streets of Le Paludi, having converged near Perrin's old neighborhood after their various errands. (Perrin is still with Nona.)*

**DM:** I'd like all of you to roll perception.

*Crissdalynn — 19. Delmar and Jean-Claude — lower. Nobody looks up.*

**DM:** You know when it goes silent out in the wilderness? That happened. Just silence and a slight whistle in the wind. None of you are looking up. Even if you were, it's flying down toward you with the sun behind it — very hard to pick up. On this silent breeze, it's noticed the three of you and is diving at high speed.

*Crissdalynn is knocked off her feet. Two arms wrap around her from the front. She's driven twenty feet back, wind knocked out.*

**DM:** Without warning. You recognize the sight and smell, Crissdalynn.

*It's [[content/shattered-sea/characters/npcs/Master-Kyzil|Master Kyzil]].*

**DM:** *(as Kyzil)* "Oh my God, Priscilla, you're alive. Thank God."

*He gets back up. Straightens himself. Looks at the others.*

**DM:** "Hi, I'm — I'm Master Kyzil. Nice to meet you. What happened?"

*Crissdalynn breaks down.*

**Crissdalynn:** *(in tears)* "Oh, my God. Oh, my God, I'm so—"

**DM:** *(as Kyzil)* "I'm so glad you survived. I heard the ship was down. A foul wind came from the south — I knew something had happened. I looked through Kylo for you. I looked in Firefall. I can't believe you ended up here. I'm so glad you're alive."

**Crissdalynn:** *(composing herself)* "There were only two of us that survived. From the flagship." *(turning to Delmar)* "This is the admiral. Sorry."

**Delmar:** *(formally)* "Master Kyzil. I have heard nothing but good things. Admiral Delmar Thaddeus Icabog Michaelis, at your service."

**DM:** *(as Kyzil)* "It's a pleasure. I'm sure she has spoken very highly of you."

*Perrin, who arrived at some point and saw a large owl-like figure dive-bomb Crissdalynn, is currently hiding in a barrel.*

*After a moment:*

**Perrin:** *(emerging very slowly from the barrel)* "Perrin's the name, see. That's all you need to know. Nice to meet ya."

*No extended hand. Standing very still, visibly tense, ready to retreat.*

*Kyzil slowly kneels down and extends one finger.*

**DM:** *(as Kyzil)* "It is a pleasure to make your acquaintance, Master Perrin."

**Perrin:** *(gingerly touching the finger)* "Yeah."

**DM:** With that, I think that's a great stopping point.
