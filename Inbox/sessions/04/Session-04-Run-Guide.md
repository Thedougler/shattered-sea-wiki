## Opening Scene — Kyzil (Fixed, 30–45 min)

*Run this before opening the sandbox. Crissdalynn's scene. Route other PC intent to Fast Hooks if players wander during it.*

> [!dm]
> Kyzil has recovered his formality — the hug was on impact, now it's locked away. Everything from here is Owl Obi-Wan: spare, precise, questions before answers, wind and tide metaphors. Silence does work.

**First line once introductions settle:**

> *"The wind carries poor rumors, little crow. I am pleased this one lied."*

**Questions for Crissdalynn:**
- *"Tell me the course. Not the story. The course."*
- *"Where did the storm take you? Where did you take yourself?"*
- *"What did the Maw show you that the ledgers could not?"*

**For the party (Kyzil notices them after Crissdalynn — deliberate):**
- *"You are the current she chose. I will not insult her by pretending that means nothing."*
- *"If you kept her alive, you have my thanks. If she kept you alive, you have my sympathy."*
- *"Names, when you are ready. A ledger with no names is merely paper."*

### Sparring — Offer If The Table Wants A Fight

> *"Have you practiced, Crissdalynn? Or has the sea been kind enough to let you remain theoretical?"*
> *"Then let us test the wind. Four against one. No killing blows. No pride worth keeping."*

- 3 rounds max. Use full Kyzil statblock — restraint is narrative, not mechanical.
- Tone: confident, nonlethal. He redirects force, steps through gaps, corrects footwork while moving.
- After: commend each PC on one real thing they did.

---

### Read The Current — Teach Whether Or Not They Spar

> *"A strike is not only force. It is a question. Bone, breath, balance, fear. Ask correctly, and the body answers before the mouth has time to lie."*

When Crissdalynn hits with an unarmed strike or Kensei weapon, she can spend 1 ki/focus (cannot also carry Stunning Strike or Deft Strike) to ask one:
- **Guard:** What is this creature relying on most for defense right now?
- **Opening:** Which saving throw appears easiest to pressure?
- **Pattern:** What combat habit is the creature relying on right now?

No exact numbers, full statblocks, or hidden lore. Answer in combat-readable terms.

### Umberlee's Appointment
*PC pull: Delmar*

The servant found Delmar this afternoon, identified herself as Umberlee's agent in Calveno, and told him the goddess expects him at the shrine. She has been waiting since midday.

**Situation:** Umberlee marked Delmar's arrival before he docked. The La Vasca basin water recognized him. This is a notice, not an invitation.

### Bela Silt-Paw — The Rescue
*PC pull: Perrin via Nona's lead. Anyone can go. **Clock expires in 1–2 days.***

A young Rattkin Passage scout (22, first solo assignment) is trapped alive below the Warren. Broken forearm. Water Weird controls the flooded chamber between her and the exit. Her message shard signals every few hours — it will stop when rations run out.

**Situation:** Day Two ends today. Bela has been down there fourteen days. This is the last window.

**Site:**
```
[Approach tunnel] — 5ft wide, 5ft ceiling (Medium creatures squeeze)
     ↓
[Entry shelf] — 3ft water · Bela's kit bag floating here
     ↓ (main channel)
[Central chamber] — 8ft deep · WATER WEIRD HERE
     ↓
[Far shelf → Dry alcove] — BELA HERE: broken arm, shard, 1 torch stub
```

**The Weird:** AC 13, HP 58, Constrict +5 (3d8+3, grapple DC 13). Regenerates 10 HP/round in water. Fire or cold suppresses regeneration. Shelf edges at 4ft depth offer a traversal route — the Weird can reach them but loses its transparent advantage. Fighting it in the deep channel is death pressure.

> [!mechanic]
> Shelf traverse: DC 14 Athletics or Acrobatics per round to stay on the edge at 4ft depth. Half-cover from Weird's attacks while pressed to wall. Failure = fall into 8ft channel with the Weird.

**Cache in the dry alcove** (Bela knows it's there): 340 gp mixed coin, 2 potions of water breathing, sealed Dravosi dispatch marked with a naval seal. Contents: Commendant Ulrik Faste's fraudulent supply contract. Worth 500 gp to the right buyer. Leave it closed until the party opens it.

**Bela when found:** calm the way frightened people get when they have been frightened too long. Her arm is splinted with her own kit. She stayed put as trained.

**Rewards:** 200 gp from the Tangle, harbour-foundation route access (bypasses Canal Commission checkpoints entirely), major Tangle goodwill. Nona's "one other thing — not tonight" becomes possible.

**If ignored:** Shard goes quiet. Tangle cuts the loss. Nona does not mention Bela again.

### The Handler Cell
*PC pull: Jean-Claude exclusively at first. Clock: Day 4 raid window.*

Blue-caste Grung handlers are running extraction-route reconnaissance under festival cover. Jean-Claude is the only party member who can read what they are doing. The raid window is Beffa Day 4 — two days from now.

**Situation:** Handlers are already mapped into the city. Each day Jean-Claude doesn't act is a day more of the route locks in.

> [!dm]
> Offer Jean-Claude one private sensory note per session day. Do not announce this to the table. Let the player decide what he does with it.

**Handler observations (use one per day, unprompted):**
- A Grung at the Bridge or Velo standing in the one position with sightlines to three exits — Jean-Claude has stood there. It is not instinctive unless someone taught you.
- A small figure near the harbour briefly assesses a working dockhand — build, tools, guild mark — and looks away. Jean-Claude has made that appraisal hundreds of times.
- A Grung in trade cover walks a Le Paludi route twice. Not shopping. Marking the time.

**If engaged:** Two blue-caste handlers (Scout MM p.349, 16 HP, AC 13). Shortbow +4, 1d8+3 + Grung Poison DC 11 Con. Break contact below half HP. One carries a sealed wax cylinder — wax pressed with a Botukuri shipping mark Jean-Claude recognizes.

| Outcome | Effect |
|---|---|
| Capture alive | Handler knows arrival timing and extraction canal route. Telling the Warren closes one raid lane. |
| Kill both | Simone doesn't know the cell was made. One lane goes dark without explanation. |
| Let them go | Simone learns the party is in Calveno. Jean-Claude's face is now a report. |

**If ignored all session:** reconnaissance completes. Day 4 raid window opens fully mapped.

### Red Lady Salvage Money
*PC pull: Delmar, Crissdalynn*

Someone is assembling a deep-access Maw salvage commission. Diving gear, sealed chart package, crew contacts in two port cities. They are 8–10 days from departure. The party arrived today. Batta Zusto on the Bridge knows the commission's shape; he is not the funder.

**Entry points:**
- Delmar asks about the Maw or Red Lady anywhere in Calveno → chandler drops his voice: *"There's money moving. Serious money."*
- Crissdalynn asks about Red Lady survivors or Maw charts → broker shows her a copy of a search query from last week, prepaid, client never returned for the report.
- Party has Warren intro and visits Savia → she names Zusto once, flatly.

**What Zusto knows:** commission is for a Maw wreck, deep access, cargo recovery. Three supply chains. Money through a Port Tidefall factor he knows. One other party is also asking Calveno brokers about Maw access this month — not the funder. He noticed.

**Price:** 40 gp, or current Maw navigation intelligence (he'll treat that as 80 gp equivalent).

**Zusto voice:**
> *"You're not the first person to ask about Maw salvage this month. Three separate buyers, or one buyer with three hands — nobody's sure. Whoever it is, they're not advertising a target."*

**If Delmar becomes visible as Maw expert:** the funder makes contact. The terms are not innocent.

**If ignored:** commission departs. The Red Lady is not empty when they arrive.

### The Ship Problem — Papers And Plate
*PC pull: whole party*

The *Uncertainty* is hidden, not solved. A Calveno repaint does not erase a Crown cutter's silhouette. The actual fixes are a new bow registry plate and false papers. Savia Brentino at Carpenters' Slip does discreet hull work without Canal Commission filings — but she requires a Warren introduction. The party now has one.

**If they approach Savia via Warren intro:** she inspects the waterline, agrees to the job, quotes 220 gp + five city days + one Tangle favour recorded and unassigned. Then, once, flatly, at the end of the assessment:
> *"She'll still read as a cutter to anyone who knows cutters. That's not a paint problem."*

The party decides what to do about the bow registry plate. Acqua-Nera on the harbour edge does re-identification work for money and no questions asked.

**If they approach Savia cold (no Warren intro):** she adds them to her list of suspicious vessels. She does not take the work. She will not be asked twice.

## Spontaneous City Play

This is the first full sandbox session. Players will do things you didn't prepare for. This section gives you a repeatable framework and enough district knowledge to stay in the driver's seat.

---

### The Four-Step Rule

Use this for any player intent that's not in the scene menu above.

> **1. Place it.** Which district? → see table below.
> **2. Give one person.** Someone who knows their work and wants one small thing.
> **3. Attach one pressure.** Choose any from: ship exposure, Warren memory, festival crowd, Tessarine ledgers, Red Lady salvage money, Grung foreshadowing.
> **4. Let the choice stand.** If they decline, note what changed, move the clock. No repeat offers through a different NPC.

You do not need to have a prepared scene. You need a person, a want, and one thing the city is already doing. That is enough.

### Jean-Claude at Studio Orsini

*Jean-Claude is selling a whip shark egg to the Le Paludi alchemist to fund a shortbow. This is player-directed — run it when he goes.*

**Getting There**

Studio Orsini is not on any Canal Commission map. Jean-Claude needs to ask a Le Paludi local — Ettore at Casa Lupo or any ferryman will give him the side canal. Or he can wander Le Paludi until the smell finds him first. No skill check required; the studio is findable to anyone who asks.

> [!read-aloud]
> The smell arrives before the building — not one smell, several, layered in a way that implies years of processing. Inside: glass jars floor to ceiling, a worktable with four ongoing processes, a low lamp over each station. A woman in her late forties — ash-pale skin, two short horns filed flat — looks up from her instruments. She looks at what's in Jean-Claude's hands before she looks at his face.

**What Marta Does**

She takes the egg without asking permission, holds it up to the lamp, tilts it twice, sets it down on the worktable.

> *"Fertilized. Viable. You got this off a live specimen."*

She picks up a caliper and measures the casing without comment.

> *"I'll take it. I'll take all three if you have them. Eighty gold each. I won't negotiate down. You can try negotiating up if you think you know something about venom-precursor extraction that I don't — I'll hear the argument."*

> [!mechanic]
> DC 15 Arcana or Nature (Jean-Claude qualifies on Nature): success → Marta pays 95 gp per egg and hands over one antitoxin at no charge.

**What She Volunteers Without Being Asked**

After she pays, while she's putting the egg into a sealed container:

> *"Three orders for Bile of the Drowned in the last thirty days. Same address. Upper Mercatura — a notary's office. I don't usually discuss my clients. I'm telling you because you came from the water, and that compound is used to make wounds look older than they are. Someone is staging an injury or covering one up. Not my business. Possibly yours."*

She then closes the transaction and returns to her instruments. She does not offer to explain further. She expects people to act on information themselves.

**After the Sale: The Shortbow**

Jean-Claude has 80 gp (or 95 gp) to spend. A shortbow costs 25 gp. He needs to know where to buy one.

Marta, if asked: *"Moretti & Sons on the Mercatura. Giacomo keeps a few for crew. Or Velo — there's a gear dealer near the south bridge who supplies Beffa teams. He'll have arrows the chandlery won't."*

**Moretti & Sons (Mercatura):** Giacomo has three shortbows in the back — standard issue, 25 gp, condition good. He sells without ceremony and mentions that two went to a festival team registration earlier that week. He finds this odd; Beffa teams don't usually want distance weapons.

**Velo dealer (unnamed — improvise as needed):** 25–30 gp for a standard bow. Also stocks: 1 gp each for blunt arrows, 2 gp each for barbed arrows. If Jean-Claude asks about anything unusual: *"I've got signal arrows. They burn red for thirty seconds. Popular with festival teams this year. Two gp."* He has 8 in stock.

> [!dm]
> Jean-Claude ends up with a shortbow and roughly 50–70 gp in personal funds (depending on rolls and which dealer he chose). The Bile of the Drowned hook is a loaded gun — the notary address connects to the Serin Haut / Ferrin Locke thread if the party pulls on it later. Don't force it. Marta mentioned it once.

### City Texture — Le Paludi

*Quick sense impressions for when the party is walking through Le Paludi without a specific destination. Use one or two, not all.*

- The canal here is darker than the main waterway — not dirty, just old. The buildings on both sides are close enough that upper floors almost touch. Laundry hangs at second-story level.
- A flat-bottomed boat slides past with two figures watching the water. They're moving fast for the time of day, no cargo visible. One has a coil of rope.
- Someone is cooking something with fennel on an upper floor. The smell drops into the canal corridor and stays.
- A cat on a window ledge watches the party cross the bridge plank without moving. When Jean-Claude passes, it tracks him — head turning, eyes level.
- The festival noise from the main channels arrives here as a low wash, two turns of canal away — present but muffled, like sound through water.
- A Le Paludi resident stops to let the party pass on a narrow section of footpath, back flat to the wall. He holds a parcel against his chest and watches the water.

## Secrets And Clues
