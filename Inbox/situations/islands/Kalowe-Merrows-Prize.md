# Kalowe — The Merrow's Prize

## Situation

The *Cormorant*, a small merchant sloop, went down three months ago on the reef shelf southwest of the Kalowe gap after her pilot misjudged the channel at night. The captain's strongbox — 600gp in mixed coin and a pair of matched rubies — sank with her. Her first mate, Davu Kamu, survived and has been drinking at [[The-Flat-Water|The Flat Water]] ever since, trying to find anyone willing to dive for it and not having much luck. The wreck is 30 feet down. A [[Sawek|merrow]] has since claimed the hull as a den and dragged the strongbox into the grotto it made under the collapsed stern.

---

## Current State

Davu is two months past his savings and is now offering 30% of whatever is recovered, which he cannot actually guarantee since the money isn't his to split — the captain's widow in Tidefall has a claim. He has not mentioned this. He knows the dive location within 200 yards, knows the strongbox is sealed in iron with a captain's key he still carries, and knows there was something large living in the wreck the last time a diver he hired got close and came back empty-handed.

**What the party knows (if they talk to Davu):**
- Sunken sloop, 2 miles southwest, reef shelf, ~30ft
- 600gp in a locked iron strongbox (he has the key)
- 30% cut (180gp + the rubies, which he'll value at 60gp — they're worth 90gp each to a jeweler)
- "Something in the wreck" — he'll admit this if pressed, describe it as "big, pale, wrong-shaped"

**What the party doesn't know:**
- The merrow has been there six weeks and has reinforced the grotto with salvaged chain and hull planking
- The two hunter sharks that patrol the outer reef are not in the grotto but will investigate noise and blood
- The rubies are worth 180gp total, not 60gp; Davu is underselling them in case he can buy them back later

---

## Key Facts

- The *Cormorant* sank 3 months ago; she's 30ft down on a sandy reef shelf, listing badly to starboard
- The merrow lair is under the collapsed stern — tight quarters, 10ft ceiling, poor visibility
- Hunter sharks patrol the reef approach but can be avoided with quiet movement (Stealth DC 12 underwater, disadvantage without Swim speed)
- Davu's key opens the strongbox; the lock can also be picked (DC 14 Thieves' Tools, underwater disadvantage applies)

## DM Notes

**Davu Kamu** — Midchain mixed heritage, late thirties, former first mate. He drinks steadily rather than heavily and talks in the careful way of someone who knows he is leaving things out. If the party agrees to the job, he insists on coming along in a boat to navigate, but will not enter the water. If they discover the rubies' real value and confront him, he admits it without much shame: *"I needed someone to say yes."*

**The wreck approach:**
- Small boat or swimming — the party needs a way to the site; Davu can borrow a dinghy from the Fifita family quay for 2sp
- Visibility underwater: 30 feet in open water; 10 feet near the wreck (silt disturbed by the merrow's movement)
- Athletics DC 12 to swim to depth without a Swim speed; failure costs a turn, not drowning (yet)

**The merrow:**
It has built a proper den. Bones of previous prey are stacked at the grotto entrance as a territorial marker — hard to miss. It will attack the moment anything enters the grotto. It is not subtle about this. HP 45, AC 13, Spear +6 to hit / 2d6+4 piercing, Claws +6 / 2d6+4 slashing, 40ft swim speed.

*If the party kills the merrow outside the grotto* (baiting it with blood or noise), they can then enter freely.

**The hunter sharks:**
They become interested if blood enters the water. Roll a d6 at the start of each round after blood appears: on a 4+, one shark investigates. Two sharks total; they circle and attack anyone wounded. This is a pressure mechanic, not a second encounter — they can be driven off by a successful DC 13 Animal Handling check or by leaving the water quickly.

| *Cormorant*'s spyglass | 50 gp | Still functional; waterlogged leather case |
| Bolt of indigo silk | 80 gp | Salvage from the hold; heavy, awkward to carry |

**If the party negotiates a better cut** (DC 14 Persuasion with leverage about the sharks/merrow): Davu will go to 40%, which is 240gp + rubies.

**If the party cuts out Davu entirely** (takes the strongbox and doesn't tell him): the 600gp is all theirs, plus rubies. The captain's widow in Tidefall has a legal claim, but she's in Tidefall and this is Kalowe.

## Triggers

- Party talks to Reweti Kahurangi about new work, or sits near Davu long enough for him to make his pitch
- Party asks anyone in port about local salvage opportunities

## Consequences

- **If the party succeeds:** 180–600gp (depending on cut negotiated), the spyglass, possibly the silk. Davu buys them a round and disappears within two days.
- **If the party ignores it:** The merrow expands its territory; it kills a diver from another crew within the month. The wreck becomes a local hazard warning.
- **If the party kills the merrow but leaves the box:** The strongbox is openly salvageable — another crew will get it within a week.

## Open Questions

- Does the party tell Davu about the rubies' real value?
- Does anyone contact the captain's widow in Tidefall about the legal claim?
