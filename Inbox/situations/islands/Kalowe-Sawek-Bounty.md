# Kalowe — The Sawek Bounty

## Situation

A [[Sawek|Sawek]] has settled into the blue hole at the base of the channel between Kalowe's second and third limestone islands — the same narrow passage the inter-island skiffs use at night. Three fishing boats have been hit in the last two weeks. One man is dead. The harbour bosses are offering 300gp for proof of kill: the distinctive bony ridge-plate from the creature's forehead, which is hard to fake and easy to identify. Every serious salvager in the Midchain knows what a Sawek looks like.

---

## Current State

The Sawek is well-fed and territorial rather than desperate. It is not attacking out of starvation — it attacked the first boat that passed over its hole, and now treats the channel as its hunting ground. The fishermen have stopped using the channel. The dry-dock workers who need to move materials between the second and third islands by water are adding an hour to every run.

**Kettil Ormsson at [[Ormssons-Fittings|Ormsson's Fittings]]** has the cracked keel plank from one of the damaged fishing boats propped against the wall outside — he's repairing it as a favour. He'll mention the Sawek without drama if the party asks about local trouble.

**Ilaria Grimaldi at [[Grimaldis-Dispensary|Grimaldi's Dispensary]]** treated the surviving fisherman from the fatal attack. She can describe the attack pattern: the Sawek came up from directly below, seized the hull with its tentacles, and pulled the boat partially under before losing interest when it broke apart. The man who died went into the water.

**The harbour bosses** will pay the bounty on sight of the ridge-plate. They don't care how it was killed. They don't require witnesses.

---

## Key Facts

- The blue hole is 20 feet deep at the base; the channel above it is 40 feet wide and 15 feet deep at high water
- The Sawek emerges at night, 2–3 hours after dark, and patrols the channel for 1–2 hours before returning
- Its lair has accumulated debris: previous victims' belongings, coins that fell from damaged boats, and one specific piece of cargo it has been sitting on
- The ridge-plate is a wedge of dense bone, cream-coloured, about 18 inches across — harvested by removing the top of the skull post-mortem

## DM Notes

**The Sawek (CR 5):** HP 95, AC 14 (natural), swim 40ft, land 5ft. Tentacle attacks grapple on hit (escape DC 14). Ink cloud reaction (20ft, heavily obscured, recharge on surfacing). Sunlight sensitivity — disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception in direct sunlight.

Full stats in [[Sawek]].

**Tactics — fighting it underwater:**
Don't. The Sawek in its hole has a 40ft swim speed, total cover from the channel walls, and its ink cloud makes retreat lethal. The party should not initiate the fight underwater unless they have unusual resources (Water Breathing, high Swim speeds, etc.).

**The bait approach (recommended):**
Wibowo's Provisions sells offal and waste fish — 1sp worth will create a blood trail in the channel. The Sawek will investigate a blood trail in 1d6+2 rounds. If the party is in a boat above the hole when it surfaces, they fight it at the waterline: half its body above water, half below. It can still grab the boat (grapple on hit, DC 14 Str to break free per round, failing means the boat is pulled 10ft down toward the hole).

**The trap approach:**
The channel narrows to 20 feet at one point — about 60 yards north of the blue hole. Chain stretched across the channel at depth (chain is available from [[Ormssons-Fittings|Ormsson's Fittings]], 5sp/ft) and anchored to both walls could foul the Sawek's tentacles if it passes through chasing bait. DC 13 Dex save or tentacles fouled (restrained, no tentacle attacks until freed — it uses an action to attempt DC 16 Str, 2 attempts per round). This doesn't stop it fighting, but removes its main grapple attack.

**The lair:**
After the Sawek is dead, the blue hole can be entered. Visibility is nil without a light source. The lair floor has:
- 6 weeks of accumulated debris from passing boats
- The Sawek has been lying on a sealed iron box that was in a skiff it sank two weeks before setting up at Kalowe — the box contains dry-stored pilot charts for the eastern reef channels (worth 60gp standard; 90gp to [[Algernon-Reginald-Clyde|Algernon Reginald Clyde]] if he's in port)
- Mixed coin from broken purses and gear that sank: 185gp in copper, silver, and gold, gritty with silt
- A gold chain, thick, with a Midchain trading house seal pendant — identifiable as belonging to a merchant missing since last season; his family in Veth would pay 50gp reward for its return (nobody in Kalowe knows whose it is without investigation)

**The ridge-plate:**
It takes 10 minutes and a cutting tool to harvest. It looks like a flattened arch of bone with a serrated outer edge. Anyone who has seen a Sawek or read [[Algernon-Reginald-Clyde#Clyde's Bestiary|Clyde's Bestiary of Oceanic Creatures]] recognises it immediately.

## Triggers

- Party asks Kettil about what happened to the boat outside his shop
- Party asks Grimaldi about recent injuries
- Party asks at the harbour offices about local hazards or open contracts

## Consequences

- **If the party kills it:** 735gp total, harbour bosses are grateful, the inter-island channel reopens — the dry-dock crew will remember this
- **If the party avoids it:** The Sawek kills again within two weeks; harbour bosses quietly double the bounty to 500gp
- **If the party is dragged under:** Drowning rules apply; the Sawek attempts to drag grappled creatures to the bottom of the blue hole (20ft descent per round of failing to escape)

## Open Questions

- Does the party return the merchant's gold chain to Veth for the reward, or just sell it?
- Does anyone want the Sawek's remaining body parts? (Teeth, ink sacs, and tentacle segments have value to an alchemist — 40gp if sold carefully)
