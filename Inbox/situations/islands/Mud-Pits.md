# The Mud Pits — DM Notes

## Situation

The Mud Pits are a sandbox attraction designed to drain the party's resources and win them local reputation. Bala Tane calibrates the fight to the crowd's entertainment, not to party safety — she will send them enough to make it look interesting, which means enough to genuinely threaten them. The party can enter as a full team or in any subset, and Bala scales accordingly.

---

## Running the Fight

**Goal:** exhaust the party (hit dice, spell slots, resources) while giving them something satisfying to win. Don't kill anyone — the crowd would actually hate it.

**Format:** Brawl to unconsciousness (0 HP). No death saving throws — a fighter at 0 HP is out. Lethal damage does not apply unless a fighter deliberately chooses it, which Bala stops immediately and ejects the fighter.

**Mud effects:** The pit is difficult terrain throughout. Any creature knocked prone must succeed on a DC 12 STR save to stand as a bonus action or spend their full movement to rise. Ranged attacks from outside the pit have half cover on targets inside.

---

## Calibration Table

| Party Size | Base Matchup | Crowd Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 PC | 2–3 dock bruisers (CR 1/8 each) | Curious; picks sides early |
| 2 PCs | 3–4 bruisers or 1 veteran + 2 bruisers | Invested; bets split 60/40 |
| 3 PCs | 4–5 bruisers or 2 veterans | Loud; lots of side money |
| Full party (4–5) | 6–8 opponents or a special matchup | Frenzy; Bala watching closely |

**Bruiser** — Use Guard (MM) or Thug (MM) statblock. Remove any lethal special attacks.

**Veteran** — Use Veteran (MM). Bala uses these for upset matchups she wants to be close.

Increase difficulty by adding one opponent at a time if the party is winning too cleanly. Bala will do this mid-fight; the crowd loves it and she calls it "Kalowe rules."

---

## Opponent Roster

**Standard card (dock bruisers)** — Mixed orc, human, tortle dockworkers who fight at the Pits on weekends. Reliable. Underpaid. Good sports about losing.

**Vort Kessen (champion)** — Retired orc sailor, late fifties, built like he was assembled from ship timbers. Use Gladiator (MM) statblock. He fights defensively and patiently and wins by outlasting people. If the party beats Vort, Bala gives them the champion's cut (50gp bonus) and the crowd buys them drinks for the rest of the evening. Vort buys his own drink and nods at them.

**The Mud Moucheron (special event)** — Bala occasionally books a Moucheron who is between contracts and needs pocket money. Use the Moucheron statblock without the proboscis drain (honour rules). The crowd loses their minds. The party will have heard the warnings about Moucherons. This is technically safe. Bala finds the look on their faces very satisfying.

**Escaped Giant Crab (chaos event)** — Once per campaign, a giant crab from the lagoon gets into the pit. Bala lets the fight happen for two rounds before calling it. Whoever is still standing gets the prize. No entry fee refunds.

---

## Betting Mechanics

Bala's runners set opening odds before the fight based on her read. If the party looks capable, they open as slight underdogs (2:1). Upsets pay more.

The party can bet on themselves (max 20gp per fighter through the runners; higher bets go directly to Bala and she will remember them). Side bets between spectators are unregulated.

**The crowd as resource:** after a win, use the crowd as a social asset. Bettors who won on the party become friendly. The harbour boss has a runner here most nights — a win against a stiff matchup gets the party a meeting. Vort, if beaten, will tell them one true thing about Kalowe he has never told anyone, because he respects the outcome.

## Key Facts

- The fight hook is visible from the causeway on approach to Kalowe — the party sees the torchlight and hears the crowd as they tie up.
- Reweti at The Flat Water will mention "the pits tonight" unprompted if she likes the party.
- The Five Blades are watching from the rim. If the party wins impressively, the Blades are interested. If they lose impressively, the Blades are *also* interested.

## DM Notes

Bala's tell for a hard match: she looks at the party, looks at the crowd, then says "Kalowe rules" before announcing the opponent count. This means she is adding a fighter. The crowd knows what it means. The party will learn.

Don't play this fight to kill. Play it to the last fighter still standing in the mud, covered in it, crowd screaming, money changing hands. That is the correct outcome regardless of who wins.
