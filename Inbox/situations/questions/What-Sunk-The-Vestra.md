# What Sunk The Vestra?

## Best Guess

The Leviathan sank the [[content/shattered-sea/situations/reference/Vestra|Vestra]]. Umberlee sank the [[content/shattered-sea/situations/reference/Red-Lady|Red Lady]] and Fisk's fleet. These were separate events with separate immediate causes, but the [[content/shattered-sea/situations/active/Pearl-of-Souls|Maw Pearl Crisis]] links them. The unresolved question is whether the Pearl theft contractor arranged the fleet's position, the Vestra's exposure, or both.

---

## Evidence

- Vestra evidence fits Leviathan attack pattern: hull breach from below, heat before impact, ordinary fishing day turned catastrophic.
- [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin]] survived because [[Auralis]] found him afterward; that makes his survival selected, not merely lucky.
- Umberlee's storm sank all five fleet ships at once: fog, rogue trench, then laughter followed by rage.
- Laughter-then-rage suggests Umberlee expected to reclaim the Pearl after sinking the fleet, then discovered it had fallen beyond her domain.
- The *Red Lady* Fleet Intelligence Room contains encoded commission records with the contractor's cipher name.
- The Leviathan is aware of the Pearl at the wreck site and has not claimed it.

## Against

- Umberlee had sufficient independent motive to sink Fisk's fleet after the theft.
- The Leviathan may have been acting as an apex predator following the Pearl's beacon, not targeting the Vestra for a plot reason.
- Two major powers operating independently in the same waters may explain both disasters without a single architect.

## What Would Resolve This

- Recover the *Red Lady* Fleet Intelligence Room records.
- Establish why the Vestra was near the Maw on that day.
- Ask Auralis why he saved Perrin.
- Identify the Pearl theft contractor and their motive for the Maw location.

## Sub-questions

- Did the contractor intend the Pearl to sink beyond Umberlee's reach?
- Was the Vestra drawn into the Leviathan's path by route, beacon, or bad luck?
- What did Umberlee discover after the fleet sank?
- Why did Auralis choose Perrin?
