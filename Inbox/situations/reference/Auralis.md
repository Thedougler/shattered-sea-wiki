# Auralis

> *The last Antheri. Transformed by a soul-pledge ritual into something that watches a crack in the world slowly get wider. He has been at this longer than Umberlee's claim to the sea has existed.*

---

## Lore Sheet

Not a natural deity or a chosen champion. Auralis is the pinnacle of Antheri technology — the final output of a technocratic civilization that calculated they needed a god to guard a broken planar door, and possessed the brutal brilliance to simply build one.

Through a combination of biomancy, soul-binding, and planar mechanics, his consciousness was extracted from his physical body and fused into an artificial, immortal framework engineered to survive the absolute bottom of the trench. He is built into the foundational core of the Antheri machine — not placed at the bottom of the Maw to guard the basement, but constructed as part of the engine itself, bound irrevocably to a single purpose: hold the fissure.

He predates Umberlee's claim to the sea. He operates entirely outside normal divine jurisdiction. Neither the [[Waveservants]] nor the [[Sentinels-Of-The-Eyrie]] know he exists. The current entity incursion is the first significant breach he has had to respond to; the fissure is widening faster than his containment can compensate for, and his passive wards have broken — he is now in active containment.

Neither the [[Waveservants]] nor the [[Sentinels-Of-The-Eyrie]] know he exists. Two hundred years of aerial observation from the High Eyrie has produced no evidence of him. He operates entirely below the mortal sea, in the boundary zone between the Shattered Sea and the Elemental Plane of Water.

He chose [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw]] as his warlock patron after the *Vestra* was destroyed by [[Leviathan]] and Perrin went into the water. The reasons for that choice are not yet established.
