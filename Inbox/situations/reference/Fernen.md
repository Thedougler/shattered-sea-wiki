# The *[[Fernen]]*

![[Fernen.webp]]

## Appearance

A two-masted brigantine, mid-sized, the fourth ship of Fisk's Fleet. Plain-hulled, painted dark brown below the waterline and weathered timber above it. Six gun mounts, three per side. She rode at a moderate draft — neither as deep as [[Heft]] nor as light as [[Narrow|the Narrow]]. A working ship without distinguishing characteristics.

Below decks: crew quarters, a compact galley, and a divided hold fitted for long-range independent operation — extended provisions stowage, an oversized water cistern, a navigator's station with charts covering the full eastern Scatter.

Fourth ship of [[The-Betrayal-of-Fisks-Fleet]]. Captained by [[Sienne-Orre]] (Druid), who could wildshape into aquatic forms and ran the second scout team alongside [[Maren]] during the perimeter survey of raw/ingested/Umberlee's shrine island.

Sank simultaneously with all five fleet ships when Umberlee opened the Maw beneath them. Wreck lies on the Shelfworks descent near [[content/shattered-sea/situations/reference/Red-Lady]].
