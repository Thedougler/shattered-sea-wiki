# [[Saltwright|The Saltwright]]

![[Saltwright.webp]]

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> A two-masted merchant brig rides broad and low in the water, her hull worked pale grey-brown by years of salt and weather. Nothing about her is ornamental: no figurehead, no bright rail, no paint left where the sea could take it. Below, the smell is brine, old cedar, and the cold deep water pressing through the seams. Every timber sounds used.

## Current State

The *Saltwright* is underway on her regular [[content/shattered-sea/places/midchain/index|The Midchain]] cargo run under [[Beaumont-Sel]]. In Session 01 she carries salt, dried fish, and cloth out of [[Kalowe]], riding low but steady as an ordinary working cargo brig.

## Overview

A two-masted merchant brig out of [[Kalowe]], built for trade rather than display. She is old, plain, and thoroughly practical: a deep hold, a small captain's cabin, and no space wasted on comfort or prestige.

## History

[[Beaumont-Sel]] has captained the ship on this route for eleven years. Shortly before the campaign opened, [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] stowed away aboard the Saltwright at [[Keth-Naar]] after the Leviathan destroyed [[content/shattered-sea/situations/reference/Vestra]]. [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Stripes-Bitemore]] bartered for passage west from the same harbour after his tribe sent him to learn why Umberlee was furious. Two days before campaign start, the Saltwright picked up [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Delmar-Fisk|Delmar Fisk]] and [[Crissdalynn-Khinriss]] from driftwood after Umberlee's storm sank Fisk's fleet. [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Jean-Claude-Tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] boarded at campaign start by leaping onto the ship while pursued on a Midchain island.

## Current Notes

Documented cargo includes fish and shrimp in barrels on earlier runs, and salt, dried fish, and cloth in Session 01. A small gap in the aft hull panels above the waterline lets in fresh air and a sliver of outside light. [[Beaumont-Sel]]'s capuchin, Bisou, roams freely and keeps a hoard somewhere in the hold.

## Layout

The *Saltwright* runs three decks. The weather deck handles sail work and loading; a covered stern deckhouse gives the captain limited privacy at that level; the middle gun deck has been partly domesticated but still holds the ship's only real weapons; and the cargo hold below does the real commercial work.

### Weather Deck

Plain and unadorned, with no figurehead and no decorative railwork. A working surface for sail handling, loading, and routine [[content/shattered-sea/places/midchain/index|Midchain]] passage. A low deckhouse covers the stern aft of the mainmast, sheltering the captain's quarters below it.

### Captain's Quarters

Set into the covered stern at weather deck level. Small and practical — [[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont Sel]] keeps a lockbox under the bed. The only space aboard with any real privacy. Accessible from the main deck via a short companionway.

### Gun Deck

The middle deck. Originally fitted for cannon; the gun ports survive, now plugged with tarred wood against the sea. The forward portion has been converted to crew's mess and additional storage — barrels of salt pork, rope coils, spare rigging, a small table bolted to the deck. Aft, two 12-pound guns sit dismounted and lashed down on their sides. They are in serviceable condition but not crewed, not rigged, and not immediately ready to fire. Remounting them would take time and hands.

### Cargo Hold

The lowest deck. Roughly six feet of headroom beneath the deck beams. Cargo stacked tight among barrels, crates, and sacks, with two lanterns hanging amidships and the hull always creaking under sail. In the aft corner, a small gap above the waterline admits fresh air and a [[Narrow]] view out. [[Bisou|Bisou]]'s hoard is hidden somewhere in this clutter.

## Crew

[[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont Sel]] commands the ship and has done so on this route for eleven years. The full crew roster is not yet established. At least one crew member carries stank leaf in a glass jar and rolled cigarettes. [[Bisou|Bisou]], Beaumont's capuchin, has free run of the ship even if he is not crew in any formal sense.

## Operational Notes

**Roles:** Only the captain is firmly established: [[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont Sel]]. Navigator, bosun, carpenter, cook, surgeon, gunner, and full sailor complement are not yet documented.

**Living space:** Compact and cargo-first. The captain has a private cabin with a lockbox; the rest of the ship prioritizes cargo over comfort.

**Registry and reputation:** An ordinary [[content/shattered-sea/places/midchain/index|Midchain]] cargo brig out of [[Kalowe|Kalowe]], old and salt-worked enough to disappear into normal trade traffic. No unusual registry mark, faction pennant, or special reputation is documented.

## Session Events

### Session 01

The party's first meeting point. [[Crissdalynn-Khinriss]] scouts above, [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Delmar-Fisk|Delmar Fisk]] rides as a passenger, [[content/shattered-sea/private/players/Stripes-Bitemore]] has bargained for passage west, and [[content/shattered-sea/characters/player/Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] is hidden below. Jean-Claude boards by leaping onto the ship while pursued, and a Dravosi patrol cutter comes alongside at the end of the session.

### Session 02

After the HCS Surety was taken and the party settled aboard, Beaumont settled accounts on deck — Friend of the Passage offer to Perrin, Truth Stone to Jean-Claude, warnings about the weather toward Calveno. He said he could be found in Kalowe when off route.

[[Bisou]] spent the morning removing 15 gp of shinies from the Surety. Beaumont treated it as fair business.

The Saltwright pulled away once Beaumont had said his goodbyes. She is now back on the Midchain route under Beaumont's command. The party is no longer aboard.
