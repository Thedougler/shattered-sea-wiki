# [[Umberlee]] — The Bitch Queen

> *The Queen of the Depths does not forgive theft.*

*Chaotic Evil. Goddess of the sea in all its destructive capacity.*

The dominant deity of the Scatter by default. No competing sea god has comparable reach or a comparable argument for why sailors should fear them instead. Her clergy, the [[Waveservants]], maintain shrines at every port of any size.

---
---

## Domain and Authority

Umberlee claims the sea. All of it — every mortal ocean, every strait, every harbour. Her authority terminates at the planar boundary of the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]. The Maw's deep waters are not mortal sea. She has no reach below the boundary zone.

This is why she cannot retrieve the [[content/shattered-sea/situations/active/Pearl-of-Souls]] herself. The Pearl sank past her jurisdictional limit. She knows exactly where it is and cannot touch it.

The [[Waveservants]] are aware she cannot access the deep Maw and do not discuss it. Their working position: the Maw is an anomaly that will eventually return within her dominion.

## Legendary Treasures

- **[[The-Tithe-of-the-Bitch-Queen]]** — the accumulated divine collection of beautiful things Umberlee claimed from mortal ships over centuries; scattered across the Sunken Crown seafloor; any item taken brings localized squalls until the sea reclaims it

## Theological Tensions

She claims the sea. The Maw sits outside that claim. The Waveservants have never satisfactorily resolved this and prefer not to examine it. The sea elves of [[Halythion]] predate her claim and do not acknowledge it. The grung do not recognize her authority over waters they cross.
