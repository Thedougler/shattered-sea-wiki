# Vaka

> *A scatter of Grung defectors and freed slaves across Midchain and Crown Island ports — not a movement, just the thing that kept them alive long enough to find each other.*

---

## Lore

Eight years old. Founded by [[Felix-Aho|Félix Aho]] after his defection from Karath — green caste, the lowest freeborn, which means the Vaka was built by the Grung the Gold considered least capable of building anything. Membership is roughly a dozen: mostly green and blue caste defectors, a handful of freed non-Grung slaves who chose to stay connected rather than disappear entirely. No manifesto, no ideology — it is closer to a recovery group than anything else. Eight years of Félix making himself available to whoever finds him, making introductions, and occasionally talking someone through the specific disorientation of living in a world that was not built with your body, your caste, or your history in mind.

What members tend to accumulate over time, without trying, is knowledge: the Gold caste of each Verdant Tooth island operates independently, argues with the other islands' Gold regularly, and enforces the appearance of unity by controlling what their own lower castes hear. The Grung caste order looks monolithic from outside and from below. The Vaka know it isn't — because they came from inside it.

---

## Structure

No ranks, no meetings, no formal hierarchy. [[Felix-Aho|Félix Aho]] is the most connected member, not a leader — he knows more people than anyone else, and he makes himself findable. Members locate each other through port traders and known gathering points; the Vaka does not seek people out. What it offers any Grung who finds it is practical: somewhere to sleep, someone who has been through the same adjustment, and the knowledge that others made it. Known gathering points in [[Port-Tidefall]] and [[Kalowe]].
