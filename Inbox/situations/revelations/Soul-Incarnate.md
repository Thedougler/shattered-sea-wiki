# Who Is the True Head of the Sentinels

## Situation

The unbroken 200-year observation record is no coincidence of institutional continuity. The same consciousness has been watching the Maw for centuries. The true head of the [[Sentinels-Of-The-Eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] is a Soul Incarnate — a truly neutral lich of the monk variety, their mummified body-phylactery sealed somewhere within the basalt sea stack beneath [[High-Eyrie]].

It holds no colonial allegiances, seeks no power, and issues no commands that a living master like [[Master-Kyzil|Master Kyzil]] would recognize as originating from above. It simply observes: the ultimate expression of the order's doctrine pushed past the threshold of death.

---

## Key Actors

- **The Soul Incarnate** — the order's undying head; has watched the Maw for two centuries; the current Maw escalation is the first time the record provides no comparison
- [[Master-Kyzil]] — senior master; carries current party pressure; does not know the Soul Incarnate exists as a conscious entity, only that the order's founding documents are "very old"
- [[Crissdalynn-Khinriss]] — the party member with the deepest connection to the order; her pilgrimage places her closer to the Soul Incarnate's attention than anyone alive
- [[High-Eyrie]] — where the phylactery is sealed, within the basalt beneath the monastery

---

## The Record's Secret Implications

The fissure record shows a clear pattern: after each disturbance, anomaly activity increases for weeks or months, then stabilizes at a slightly higher baseline. The current period is the most significant in the record's history. The Sentinels logged all of it faithfully and drew no conclusions.

The Sentinels have two centuries of surface observation and have never been below the Maw's surface. They do not know it is a planar breach. They do not know [[Auralis]] exists. Their discipline has kept them from the contact that would have given them the correct framework.

The Soul Incarnate has been waiting, in the only way it understands waiting, for an event with no precedent in the record. The current Maw escalation is the first time in two centuries that the order's long memory cannot provide comparison. This does not make the entity panic. It makes it more attentive.

---

## At-Table Reveal

Late-game Sentinel play should reframe "unbroken institutional memory" as literal undead continuity. The order's doctrine was always extreme; the reveal is that one consciousness carried it beyond death and made the record an extension of its own neutral observation.

The Soul Incarnate is not an enemy. It is the most complete witness to the Maw's history. It can tell the party what two hundred years of observation actually shows — something no living scholar could reconstruct from the ledgers alone.
