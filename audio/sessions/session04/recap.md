## Scene 1: Le Paludi Market — Whip Shark Egg Sale
*0:00:19–0:03:24 | Jean-Claude, DM (unnamed merchant), Crissdalynn*

Jean-Claude approached a market vendor in Le Paludi to sell his fertilized whip shark eggs. The merchant identified the eggs immediately, noting they are difficult to acquire. She explained that fertilization reduces value because it worsens the taste — the eggs are prized as a culinary delicacy, not for breeding. The merchant initially offered 75gp per egg. Jean-Claude expressed reluctance, wanting to sell to someone who would hatch them rather than eat them. The merchant offered 100gp per egg and promised not to eat them (a promise Jean-Claude saw through but accepted). Jean-Claude sold all three eggs for 300gp total, paid as 30 platinum. The merchant invited him to return if he acquired more.

## Scene 2: Casa Lupo — Jean-Claude's Shopping
*0:03:24–0:12:31 | Jean-Claude, DM (old man shopkeeper), Crissdalynn, Delmar*

Jean-Claude returned to Casa Lupo, a pawn shop in Le Paludi, to buy a bow he'd previously spotted. The shopkeeper demonstrated a sleek, compact shortbow with a Renaissance-era recurve design and folding mechanism. Its string release was completely silent. The shopkeeper said it was made of yew, acquired from someone passing through quickly, and designed for stealth. Jean-Claude bought the bow for 50gp. DM confirmed the item: +1 to attack and damage rolls, completely silent when drawn and fired, does not reveal the shooter's position when firing from hidden. No attunement required.

The shopkeeper also had a potion of gaseous form (50gp), a healing potion (25gp), and boots of flying (125gp, uncommon, requires attunement). The boots grant up to 4 hours of flight, regaining 2 hours per 12 hours not in use. Jean-Claude bought the boots of flying for 125gp, intending to offer them to a party member rather than use them himself — flying is unnatural for a frog. Jean-Claude left with 250gp remaining and went to find his companions.

## Scene 3: Le Paludi — Party Reunion with Kyzil
*0:12:31–0:16:24 | All PCs, DM (Kyzil)*

Jean-Claude caught up with the rest of the party, who were still with Master Kyzil in Le Paludi. Jean-Claude approached silently and waited for introductions. Kyzil addressed Jean-Claude directly: "Your kind isn't from here. Why are you here?" Jean-Claude answered simply that he traveled with his newfound compatriots. Crissdalynn vouched for him cautiously. Kyzil accepted him on Crissdalynn's word. When Kyzil stated "I know you are no threat to me," Delmar burst into laughter, and then bear-hugged Kyzil. Kyzil asked to find somewhere to talk — somewhere less public and more safe.

## Scene 4: Ponte Bassa Tavern — Kyzil Conversation Begins
*0:16:24–0:25:00 | All PCs, DM (Kyzil, tavern proprietor)*

Perrin led the group to the Ponte Bassa, a cheap dive bar tavern near where the Uncertainty is moored at La Vasca. The Ponte Bassa is known to Perrin as a Passage front — the constant activity of the party venue hides people using it as a safe entrance. He chose it over Rattkin-specific venues to avoid bringing two avian predators into a Rattkin establishment. The tavern has a wide-shouldered female proprietor.

At the tavern, Kyzil asked Crissdalynn about her pilgrimage. She told him it was close — she nearly died in the wreck. Kyzil responded that he'd feared he'd sent his apprentice to her death, but he gave her the hardest pilgrimage in Eyrie history because he knew she could do it. Crissdalynn mentioned the Maw opening and trailed off. Kyzil said the weather has been odd since the wreck, the Maw has been unruly and dangerous, and some sentinels saw the wreck. He senses something foul coming from the south. Delmar pulled out his flask and drank before the waitress arrived — unusual behavior for him. Delmar ordered drinks for the table and is abstaining from alcohol.

The Ponte Bassa menu: fish broth, eel, meat plate, wine, rum, spirits. Beds available at 2 silver per night. Total tab for food for all of them: 9 silver; beds bring it to about 2 gold. Delmar paid the tab (gave the proprietor a gold). The tavern has only two rooms available. Jean-Claude suggested sleeping in shifts with two per room; he doesn't need a bed. Perrin offered to sleep in a corner or cabinet.

## Scene 5: Ponte Bassa Rooftop — Boots of Flying & Sparring Setup
*0:31:00–0:41:00 | All PCs, DM (Kyzil)*

Kyzil, now two or three drinks in, expressed concern that Crissdalynn hadn't been practicing and suggested they spar. Crissdalynn spun her staff in response. Perrin suggested the rooftop of the Ponte Bassa as a sparring venue. Jean-Claude offered the boots of flying to Perrin and Delmar as a gift; they rolled to decide. Perrin won the boots.

Kyzil moved to the far side of the rooftop, threw off his robe, rolled up his sleeves, and drew two sharp daggers. He invited anyone else to test their mettle. The sparring was conducted with non-lethal intent — Kyzil was built to take on all four PCs at once. Initiative: Kyzil 26, Delmar 22, Crissdalynn 14.

## Scene 6: Ponte Bassa Rooftop — Kyzil Sparring Match (Rounds 1-2)
*0:41:00–1:09:00 | Crissdalynn, Delmar, Perrin, DM (Kyzil)*

**Round 1:** Kyzil opened with four attacks against Crissdalynn — three open palm strikes and one additional. Crissdalynn dodged all of them, demonstrating she knows Kyzil's fighting patterns. Kyzil settled into patient defense as a bonus action. Crissdalynn attacked once (dirty 20 — miss against Kyzil's high AC), then activated her tattoo for extended reach. She attempted a bonus action hit and grapple. Her 25 to hit (18+7) connected for 13 damage, and she successfully grappled Kyzil (he rolled a nat 1 on the contested check). Kyzil acknowledged her growth. Delmar joined from the sidelines, throwing a chair at Kyzil with steady aim (hit for 4 bludgeoning via Tavern Brawler). Perrin watched but prepared to support.

**Round 2:** With Kyzil grappled (zero movement), he attacked Crissdalynn — first strike missed (13), second hit (27) for 13 bludgeoning. Crissdalynn deflected all 13 damage and spent a focus point to redirect it back; Kyzil made the DC 13 Dex save (rolled 17). Kyzil then threw a returning dagger at Delmar — it hit (29) for 19 total damage, leaving Delmar at 12 HP. The dagger disappeared into smoke and reappeared in Kyzil's hand. He threw again; Delmar burned a luck point, forcing disadvantage — Kyzil rolled a nat 1 and the dagger went wide, pinning Delmar's coat to the wall. Perrin started drumming and used healing word on Delmar (1d4+4 healing). Delmar drew the Red Lady (his rapier), attacked Kyzil with sneak attack from flanking — missed even after burning his last luck point. He disengaged for free (Swashbuckler) and bonus-action dashed away. Crissdalynn attacked with advantage from grapple, hitting for 14 damage, then used flurry of blows for a second hit.
