# Combat Analytics — Shattered Sea

## Design Adjustments (Active Flags)

- Bardic inspiration is the party's force multiplier. Perrin's d6 repeatedly turned misses into hits and failed saves into passes. Design encounters where the inspiration economy matters — multiple saves per round, contested checks, AC breakpoints at +3–5 above average rolls.
- Crissdalynn's grapple is a dominant opener. Enemies with high contested Athletics/Acrobatics or Freedom of Movement effects counter this; enemies without it get locked down.
- Gloom Stalker in darkness is near-invisible ambush. Any encounter in darkness where enemies rely on darkvision gives Jean-Claude effective surprise. Counter: tremorsense, blindsight, or lit environments.

## Patterns Observed

- **Party coordination is their biggest asset.** Bardic inspiration, flanking for sneak attack, grapple-to-advantage — they chain abilities across turns. Solo monsters without legendary actions get locked down fast.
- **Crissdalynn dominates physical contests.** Grapple, aerial pursuit (45ft fly), deflect attacks. She controls positioning. Enemies need ways to break grapple or deny flight.
- **Perrin is a combat multiplier, not a damage dealer.** Healing word, bardic inspiration, cutting words — all from behind cover while drumming. Targeting him is the smart tactical play. Kyzil identified this: "that drum is troubling."
- **Delmar is feast-or-famine.** High damage when sneak attack lands (17 damage through Empty Wing Parry), but misses often against high AC even with advantage. Luck points are a limited resource he burns fast.
- **Jean-Claude in darkness is lethal.** Gloom Stalker invisibility + ceiling movement + Dread Strike = ambush kills. In lit environments he loses his biggest advantage.
- **The party burns resources unevenly.** Perrin's spell slots and inspiration go fast. Delmar's luck points are gone by round 2. Crissdalynn's focus points and deflects are steady. Jean-Claude is resource-light.

## Calibration Notes

- CR 14 four-on-one held for exactly 3 rounds before the party would have trivialized it. For boss fights: CR 12–14 with legendary actions is the sweet spot for a tense 3–4 round encounter.
- Mook-tier enemies (CR 1/2 to CR 1) are ambush fodder. Don't run them straight — use terrain, numbers (6+), or environmental hazards to create challenge.
- The party lacks AoE damage. Clustered enemies are a valid pressure — they can't clear groups efficiently.
