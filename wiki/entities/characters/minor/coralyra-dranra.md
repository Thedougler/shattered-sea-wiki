# Coralyra Dranra

*Season 2 PC concept — not yet in play*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Species / Class** | Elf Sorcerer/Bard 5 |
| **Background** | Incomplete Aoidos *(ah-OY-dos)* of Halythion; self-exiled |
| **Age** | 87 (young for an elf; teenage in elf terms) |
| **Home** | [[halythion|Halythion]] |

Beautiful siren-like elf. Her voice stops people mid-conversation. It was already in her — she learned to weaponize it. Joins after the first arc.

## Personality

Wants an audience. Wants to be listened to, to matter, to not have the world ignore her. What she actually wants: to go home safely, acceptance, apologies, her music recognized. Fairly immature (young for an elf). Doesn't know the history of her salvaged trident. Treats beggars, servants, and children with respect.

**Red line:** Will not mess with dolphins or other marine mammals.
**Red button:** Her animal friends in danger, AND being talked over simultaneously. Take both at once and she stops thinking.

## Faith

Raised to follow Deep Sashelas. Admires Umberlee for her absolute control and authority over her domain — wants that kind of certainty.

## Mechanics

Sorcerer/Bard multiclass. Innate magical gift weaponized through training. Produces music and magical effects beyond what her voice alone could explain (the full scope of this is a secret). Trident salvaged from a downed ship (origin unknown to her).

## Active Situations

- **Self-exile from Halythion** — left before the evaluation could force a conclusion; a Council hearing presumably still waits. She has not gone back.
- **Incomplete Aoidos title** — the title and its obligations are unresolved; carries it by default
- **The hermit crab** — she owes it a good shell; still looking
- **Flappers** — out chasing mackerel; does not know where Coralyra is

## What She's Carrying

- A trident salvaged from a downed ship she never knew the name of (magical, origin unknown to her)
- Her voice (the weapon and the wound)
- The hermit crab debt

## Backstory

Raised in [[halythion|Halythion]]. Liked, comfortable, normal. Told her music was disturbing people and she needed evaluation — she left before that happened. Worst thing: got inked by a [[sawek|Sawek]]. Worst thing she's done: accidentally made a hermit crab homeless.

## Key Relationships

- [[flappers-the-seal|Flappers the Seal]] — closest relationship; whose opinion matters most; currently chasing mackerel
- Sea turtles — the creatures she'd trust with her life

## Connections

- [[halythion|Halythion]] — home she left
