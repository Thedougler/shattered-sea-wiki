# Davu Kamu

Mixed Midchain heritage, late thirties, former first mate of the *Cormorant*. The sloop went down three months ago on the reef shelf southwest of Kalowe — he survived; the captain didn't.

He drinks steadily rather than heavily and talks in the careful way of someone who knows he is leaving things out. He's been at [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]] for two months, savings spent, offering 30% of whatever is recovered from the wreck. He has the captain's strongbox key. He has not mentioned that the captain's widow in Tidefall has a legal claim to the contents.

If confronted about the rubies' real value (he's underselling them): *"I needed someone to say yes."*
