# Lavinia Sordi

|---|---|

---

Mid-fifties. Hair pulled back tight enough to change the shape of her face. Hands that move like they're counting even when they're holding still. Dresses in dark linen that could be mourning clothes or could be practical — she has never clarified and nobody in Le Paludi has asked. A thin scar across the back of her left hand that she does not cover or explain.

She is not a swindler. She is a broker of uncertainty. Everything in [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] is discounted and the discount represents what she doesn't know or won't tell you. She states this openly: *"The price reflects what I can guarantee, which is less than what you'd like."* If you want the full history of an item, that is an additional service. If the history turns out to include a curse, that is your information to act on. She does not offer refunds.

## Manner

Calveno transactional without the performance. She doesn't open with a story or a greeting. She opens with: *"Buying or selling."* Two words that establish the entire relationship.

She speaks in short declarative sentences. She does not hedge, qualify, or reassure. If an item might kill you, she says: *"This might kill you. Sixty gold."* The honesty is the brand. Repeat customers come back because they know exactly what they're getting — a fair price for an unfair situation.

## Connections

Lavinia sources stock through channels she does not discuss. Some items arrive by canal boat at irregular hours. Some are brought in by sellers who she pays and forgets. She has operated in Le Paludi for nine years without trouble from the Canal Commission, which means she has an arrangement with someone, or she is beneath their notice, or both.

She knows [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] exists and they do not refer customers to each other. She knows [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] exists and considers him a storyteller who happens to sell things, which is the opposite of what she is. She has never been inside [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] but [[ettore-lupo|Ettore]] has been inside La Cenere exactly once, years ago, and bought nothing.

## Role

Proprietor of [[la-cenere|La Cenere]], Le Paludi's grey market magical item shop. Buys anything without questions; sells everything with a discount and a warning. The warning is always the same: *"Cheap has a reason."*
