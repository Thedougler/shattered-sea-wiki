# Pell

> *A gnome who died trying to be free.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Species** | Gnome |
| **Role** | Labour slave; [[sorn|Sorn]] |
| **Status** | Deceased |

## Overview

Pell was a labour slave on [[sorn|Sorn]] and the person who made [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] understand that the Grung caste order was not the only possible shape of the world.

## Appearance & Manner

Slight even for a gnome. Deep-brown skin, a tight braid kept behind one ear, calloused hands. Small in the way of someone who had spent years making herself smaller than she was — but the eyes gave her away. Too steady, too still.

## Who She Was

Pell talked to Jean-Claude as if he were a person — not an authority, not a threat, not a caste above her. In fragments of conversation stolen between her work and his guard duty, she told him things he had never heard said aloud: that she had come from somewhere else, that the world outside the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] was full of peoples who had built differently.

She never told him what to think. She just described what she had seen, and waited to see what he did with it.

The night before everything went wrong, she told him that every race has something the others lack, and that the only way to grow is to reach across that gap. He has been trying to prove her right ever since.

## The Plan and Its End

Jean-Claude proposed faking her death and smuggling her west. She said yes.

He walked to his parents' hut on what felt like the best day of his life. He walked in and found his parents, his sister [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]], and Pell already in chains. Simone had told them everything.

**Thirty-one days.** Pell died in a cell beneath [[sorn|Sorn]] while Jean-Claude was already in open water, already free. She died not knowing whether he had made it.
