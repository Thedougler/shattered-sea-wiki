# Stripes Bitemore

![[Belmore-Archer.webp|Belmore, a tiger tabaxi fighter, crouched on a ship's deck with spear ready]]

A tiger tabaxi fighter, shrine guardian, and former passenger aboard the [[saltwright|Saltwright]]. His tribe watched the sea go wrong and sent their strongest hunter west to find the cause.

He is not subtle. He's not a diplomat. Where most sailors deal with problems by talking their way around them, Stripes looks built to stand in the middle and not move.

## Backstory

Stripes comes from [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]], one of five small islands around the Blue Hole at the heart of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]. His tribe has guarded a primal shrine of [[umberlee|Umberlee]] for generations. When the sea around the Blue Hole started going wrong, the elders sent their strongest hunter west.

He bartered passage aboard the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] with [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] and has not been home since.

The Keth-Naar accent — clipped consonants, a slight Germanic lilt — marks him immediately as someone from somewhere else.

## Character

Stripes is a showoff. Spends voyages performing for the crew — climbing rigging, demonstrating shots, showing off the [[bracers-of-archery|bracers]], narrating his own maneuvers with obvious satisfaction.
