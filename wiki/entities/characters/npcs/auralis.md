## Overview

Not a natural deity or chosen champion. Auralis is the pinnacle of [[antheri|Antheri]] technology — the final output of a technocratic civilization that calculated they needed a god to guard a broken planar door, and possessed the brutal brilliance to build one.

Through biomancy, soul-binding, and planar mechanics, his consciousness was extracted from his physical body and fused into an artificial, immortal framework engineered to survive the absolute bottom of the trench. He is built into the foundational core of the Antheri machine — not placed at the bottom of the Maw to guard it, but constructed as part of the engine itself, bound irrevocably to a single purpose: hold the fissure.

He predates [[umberlee|Umberlee's]] claim to the sea. He operates entirely outside normal divine jurisdiction. Two hundred years of aerial observation from the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] has produced no evidence of him.

## Current State

The current entity incursion is the first significant breach he has had to respond to. The fissure is widening faster than his containment can compensate for; his passive wards have broken — he is now in active containment.

> [!dm]
> Perrin believes his cloak saved him from the Vestra wreck. Auralis actually did. Perrin forged a warlock pact in the wreck without knowing it. The reasons Auralis chose Perrin are not yet established.

## Voice & Delivery

- Refers to elapsed time in geological units: *"Not long. Perhaps four hundred years."*
- Addresses people at a slight angle — as if watching the conversation from just outside it.

## Primary Goal

Guard the Maw: prevent what comes through the fissure from reaching the mortal world; keep the boundary zone intact.
