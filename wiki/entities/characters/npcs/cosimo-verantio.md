> [!read-aloud]
> The man at the head of the table has not moved since you entered. White beard to the collarbone, immaculately kept. Plain dark cloth, good weight, no insignia. His hands rest flat on the table. His eyes found you when you walked in and have not left you since. He does not look like he is waiting for anything. He looks like he has already been here long enough that waiting is not really the word for it anymore.

## Overview

Cosimo Verantio is the visible human face of the Tessarine Concordat's true architect. He belongs at the top of debt, leverage, and Concordat pressure, surfacing only when proxies are no longer enough. The "Verantio family" appears in Tessarine records as far back as the Concordat's founding — always a white-bearded patriarch of late middle age. No one has found this remarkable because the documents are in order and the interest payments have never been late.

He does not need the Concordat's wealth. He needs its leverage — the accumulated obligation of every city, council, and merchant family that owes him a vote or a debt. This is his hoard. He tends it the way other dragons tend gold.

## Appearance & Manner

Elderly, spare, dressed in dark merchant cloth that costs considerably more than it looks. The beard is the first thing anyone notices — white, long, forked, kept with precision that suggests tending for a very long time. He sits perfectly still: not patience or discipline, but the stillness of something that has not been uncomfortable in centuries. Amber eyes, slightly too wide, tracking with exactness that is easy to mistake for attentiveness and harder to explain on reflection.

**Roleplay concept:** The oldest Medici banker, if he had never needed to sleep.

## Voice & Delivery

- Quiet, measured, each word chosen before it is spoken. Never rushes.
- Hands remain flat on the table or folded in his lap.
- **Warmth:** Genuinely interested, asks the one question that proves he has been listening more carefully than you realised.
- **Crack:** Someone displays innate or gifted magic carelessly in his presence — sorcery, warlock invocations, divine channeling — and something cold crosses his face before warmth returns. He does not explain it.

> *"I have found that people who need to know the terms urgently tend to accept worse ones. We are in no hurry."*
>
> *"You were about to mention the Greyteeth salvage. Please, go ahead."*

> [!dm]
> **The Dragon Truth:** Cosimo Verantio is the Magus Dragon that architected the Tessarine Concordat. The human form is a polymorph maintained, with minor adjustments, for over two hundred years. The beard is not incidental — all Magus Dragons have them; this one chose a form where it reads as distinguished rather than strange. His amber eyes, perfect stillness, and tendency to already know what you were about to say are the hardest tells to suppress.
>
> He despises sorcerers, warlocks, and clerics — magic that is given rather than earned. Will not extend credit to one. This contempt is visible for a fraction of a second when such magic is displayed carelessly in his presence.
>
> The Concordat's factors are his Circle. None of them know what they serve.
>
> **Reveal conditions:** The party has uncovered enough Concordat threads to reach the top, or has done something that threatens the hoard directly. He will not surface for minor problems — that is what proxies are for.

## Active Problem

The Leviathan's emergence and Umberlee's anger are destabilising trade routes that underpin Concordat debt instruments across the [[midchain|Midchain]]. Paid for information on the Red Lady wreck site and any survivors — the inquiry failed and leaked. Port-watchers and harbour-broker channels now know the Concordat is buying on this question. He does not yet know Fisk is alive.
