# Ket

![[Ket.webp|Ket, a hungry mosquito-like Moucheron, reaching through the bars of a brass birdcage in the HCS Surety brig]]

A [[moucheron|Moucheron]] from [[murrat|Murrat]]. Not integrated. Not a mercenary. [[barnaby-rook|Rook]] went ashore on Murrat and took him — trapped, caged, brought back as a specimen before the village could respond. That was three weeks before the party freed him. He is a feral island Moucheron who treats every warm-blooded creature as potential prey, and three weeks in a cage made him hungrier.

---

## The Cage

A brass birdcage, roughly two feet across, hanging from a bent ship nail in the keel timber of the [[hcs-surety|HCS Surety]]'s brig. The bars kept him in. His proboscis reached through them. The crew learned this the hard way.

> [!dm]
> He speaks Common fluently — Moucherons aren't unintelligent, just predatory. He'd answer questions, hold a conversation, even be briefly interesting. None of it was goodwill. It was bait — he wanted whoever was talking to forget for one moment how close they were standing.
>
> Even [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] looked at him once and left without getting near the cage. Ket found this funny. He told the crew afterward that the "big one smells wrong." Nobody wanted to ask what that meant.

**Sample lines:**
- *"Come closer. I won't bite."*
- *"You're bleeding a little. From the fight. I can smell it."*
- *"Just your hand. Just for a moment."*

## Session 02 — Freed

[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] offered a wine glass of his own blood; Ket drank, his abdomen went red, calmer but not safe. The party debated killing him versus release.

[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] drew his longsword and moved to execute Ket. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] deflected the blade and put herself between him and the cage — in the rush she stepped past the safe distance from the bars. Ket drove his proboscis into her upper arm and fed. She pulled back. Perrin cast Tasha's Hideous Laughter; Ket went down incapacitated.

The party chose release. Ensign Wouters' body was placed on deck as a lure; Mage Hand opened the cage from a safe distance. Ket fed for about a minute on the body, then lifted into the air and flew toward home.

[[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] said he was glad. The crew had always hated what Rook did to him.

**Current status:** Free. Flying toward [[murrat|Murrat]].
