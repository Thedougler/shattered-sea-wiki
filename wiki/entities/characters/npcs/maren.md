## Overview

Maren captained the *Narrow*, the fleet's scout vessel. Fast in the water, able to breathe beneath the surface, and skilled enough to read a reef by feel alone. She and the scouts who worked the air above while she worked the water below made the fleet's scouting unparalleled. No approach they surveyed surprised them twice.

She was outside the cave when the fleet sank. She drowned in the Maw when [[umberlee|Umberlee]] closed the water over all five ships. Her soul was pressed into [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] at the moment of sinking.

## Appearance & Manner

Deep brown skin with a subtle blue-green iridescence that shifts in open water, brightening at the surface in full sun the way a reef does when the light hits it right. Her locs are heavy and perpetually salt-dampened, threaded through with small shells and copper beads that click softly when she moves. Strong through the shoulders and chest. Deep teal eyes.

She will correct you on the pronunciation. MARE-EN. She has always corrected people. She always will.

## Backstory

She comes from a family of twelve. The house was always full — noise, food, argument, someone borrowing something, someone else returning something broken. She loved every moment of it. Siblings scattered south by trade, east by the current, wherever the sea took them.

She was already an accomplished swimmer and fisherman by the time she joined her first crew — breathing underwater was ordinary to her. She met [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] during one of his early privateering runs. He had an instinct for people who could do what no one else on board could. She had an instinct for going where she wasn't supposed to. They got along immediately.

She has strong opinions about the Navy. The sea belongs to everyone. Two decades of watching Navy ships enforce arbitrary passage rights has made her angrier, not less.
