**Quote (Session 03):** *"Oh my God, Priscilla, you're alive. Thank God."*

He dove from the sun in silence onto [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] in the Le Paludi streets — knocked her back twenty feet, arms around her from the front. She recognized the smell before she could see him. He calls her Priscilla.

## Overview

Master Kyzil is a senior master of the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]], the aarakocra observation order at [[high-eyrie|The High Eyrie]]. He assigned [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] her pilgrimage — map the Drowned Maw from inside a storm — and staked his own reputation on her readiness when he did it.

He tracked her by current and weather patterns after the *Red Lady* went down, deduced where she would wash ashore, and came to [[tidefall|Port Tidefall]] to find her. When she never arrived, he learned the ship had sunk and searched east into the Midchain first — reasonable model, wrong answer. A maritime contact who had seen an aarakocra monk aboard a captured Crown patrol cutter sent him back west.

## Appearance & Manner

Tall and lean, built and coloured like a barn owl. His facial disc is pale and broad, the feathers around it tawny-brown fading to ash at the crown and mantle. He wears plain sentinel robes. He stands with wings folded tight, feet set with the still grip of something perched. When he turns, his head moves before his body follows.

He asks questions instead of giving answers. He does not raise his voice. He does not repeat himself.

His formality cracks once, and only where Crissdalynn is concerned. The reunion hug is immediate, physical, overwhelming, and over fast — robes smoothed, wings folded right over left, hood adjusted. The party sees the crack and the cover both.

## Voice & Delivery

**Speech patterns:**

- Questions over statements. When he wants something, he asks. When he's teaching, he still asks.
- Stops before the emotional word. "I corrected course—" [beat] "—three times." The pause is the feeling.
- Agrees when he wants to apply pressure: student says something self-critical, he says "Yes." No elaboration.
- Wind and tide are his vocabulary for relationships. He doesn't say *I was worried*. He says *I corrected course three times looking for you*.

**Physical tic:** After composure breaks, he smooths robes, refolds wings right over left, then adjusts hood. The party sees it once. He doesn't know it shows.

## Crissdalynn

He assigned her the hardest pilgrimage on record because he believed she was capable of it. He has told her the pilgrimage is not over. He has not elaborated on what finishing it looks like.

In Calveno, he asks for the course of her pilgrimage rather than a confession. He wants what she saw, what she recorded, and what the Maw did to wind, tide, current, cloud, compass, and courage.

> [!dm]
> The "foul wind from the south" he mentioned when the ship went down — that thread is available if you want it. He hasn't explained his own business in the region or why he was close enough to hear about the ship. His pilgrimage assignment for Crissdalynn is on record; his motives for tracking her specifically and personally are not.

## Calveno Spar

Kyzil may invite Crissdalynn and her companions into a nonlethal four-on-one spar. His restraint is narrative: he turns edges, catches falls on the wind, drops a student cleanly instead of maiming them, and stops before harm becomes permanent.

The level 4 party should not be able to beat him by trading actions. The victory condition is respect, not HP. Count clean moments: forcing Legendary Resistance, making him spend Empty Wing Parry, landing a hit after baiting movement, protecting an ally from a gale line, disarming one dagger, or using terrain to deny a line. Four clean moments is extraordinary; Kyzil still ends standing.

> [!dm]
> **Spar Quick Card**
> **Opening**: "I'd like to show you something. Please try to hit me."
> **Routine**: Multiattack (4 attacks, pulling edges) → Patient Defense → Empty Wing Parry on incoming hits. Round 3: Updraft Step to elevated position, full pressure.
> **Cutting Gale**: Use when two or more PCs stand in a line. Say "He exhales." That is the warning.
> **Win condition**: Four clean moments. Kyzil still ends standing.
> **Ending**: Kyzil stops, not the party. He catches a weapon or fist mid-swing, holds it one beat, says "There." Done.
> **The Moment**: Cutting Gale fires down the line with Crissdalynn in it. If she moved: "You moved. Good." If she didn't and takes the damage, Kyzil catches her before she hits the ground.

## Post-Session 04 State

Kyzil investigated his "foul wind" instinct between the spar and the next morning — heard fragments about small cloaked figures near storm drains and acidic smells underground. Festival noise blocks specificity, but his instinct is confirmed: something is moving beneath Calveno. He is primed to snap into tactical assessment the moment the party shares their intel.
