## Overview

Nona Black-Jaw has been at war with the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] for nearly 40 years. Not loudly. Not with speeches. She fed the hungry, built a home for those in hiding, and has never stopped resisting.

To the Crown she is a smuggler, terrorist, and fugitive — they called her **the Calveno Candle** in her youth, when she was caught multiple times for ambushes on Dravosi ships, usually involving improvised incendiary devices. To the Kin of the Warren, she is the reason there's a Warren to come home to.

She feeds people first. Not as a tactic. Because people deserve to eat.

## Appearance & Manner

Very small, even by Rattkin measure. Fur almost entirely silver-white, except for the dark brown-black mask along her jaw that gives the family its name. Sharp pink-rimmed eyes; calloused paws. She sits perfectly straight, wears a flour-dusted floral apron over dark, well-made cloth. Smells of garlic and fresh bread.

She discusses Dravosi inspection schedules and burning things down in the same tone she uses for the weather. Urgency stopped impressing her decades ago. Runs the kitchen like a courtroom — she hears cases and issues rulings. Grief handled sideways: she moves the problem.

## The Cheese Rule

A Black-Jaw saying: **"You don't take the cheese. You're given it."**

It started as tradecraft — don't be the rat in someone else's trap. But Nona taught it to Perrin as something larger: the difference between a person the Crown has defined and a person it hasn't. A signed release from a frightened official who accepted a pastry and apologized for the delay is cleaner than robbery, and it costs them more.

She taught it early. He'd reach for a sweet roll before dinner. She'd smack his paw with a spoon: *"You don't take the cheese."* He would sigh: *"You're given it."* Then she'd give him two.

## Black-Jaw Rules

Nona's Run commits crimes, but not all crimes:
- Do not traffic people.
- Do not use poison.
- Do not betray sanctuary.
- Do not steal from the Warren.

*"If you make a coin selling someone's chain, I break your hands, take the coin, and spend it getting them free."*

## Vincenzo

Her late husband [[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo Black-Jaw]] built the *[[vestra|Vestra]]* and captained her for decades. His chair at family meals stays empty. Their love was passionate, complicated, and singular — with frequent arguments and four good frying pans broken over his stubborn gambler skull. She's still a little angry at Vincenzo. He left her to run the whole thing alone, and she's been doing it, and she misses him every single day.
