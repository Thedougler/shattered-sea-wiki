# Ruma Delacroix

Blue-caste handler, quartermaster for [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s sewer magazine operation beneath [[calveno|Calveno]]. Sits at a plank desk in Room 6 of the [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|magazine network]], cross-referencing route charts and tide markings.

## At the Table

**Voice:** Flat, unhurried, no hesitation when negotiating. Short sentences. Does not waste words on people who might kill her.

**Wants:** To survive. Operational loyalty extends exactly as far as her life expectancy.

**Stat block:** [[grung-elite-warrior|Grung Elite Warrior]] (CR 2). AC 13, HP 49. Hand crossbow + signal whistle. If she blows the whistle, all sentry teams go to active defense.

## What She Knows

- Four secondary magazine sites and their status
- Accelerated timeline ("detonation was two days — now less")
- A fifth site exists under red-caste authority ("the circle site")
- Sentry compositions at each site
- Egress vent positions

## What She Doesn't Know

- Primary site location
- Circle purpose (the Slaad summoning)
- [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]]'s identity
- [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]]'s identity

## Social Triggers

- Naming [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]] — same rotation; she reacts
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] speaking Grung in handler-register — freezes; profoundly wrong to her
