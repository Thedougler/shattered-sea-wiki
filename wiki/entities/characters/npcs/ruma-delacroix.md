# Ruma Delacroix

Blue-caste handler for [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s sewer magazine operation beneath [[calveno|Calveno]]. Sat at a plank desk in Room 6 of the [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|magazine network]], cross-referencing route charts and tide markings — until the party snuck up and grabbed her in Session 05. No fight followed; there was nothing to fight with.

**Status: ally, not hostile.** The party found her doodles — chemical formulas rendered like star charts, complete with invented mythology and fan-fiction about the compounds — while restraining her, and that was the whole negotiation. She was never enthused about this posting: her first ocean crossing brought her to a sewer, and the job since has been 18-hour shifts of nothing. She's hiding the party in what she calls her "nap room" (Room 6 — dry, swept, hers) and keeping her check-ins with the network sounding normal so neither she nor they get caught.

## At the Table

**Voice:** Flat and unhurried used to be a defense — now it's just how she talks. Dry, a little wry, visibly relieved to have people to talk to who aren't reporting on barrel counts. Doesn't perform gratitude; shows it by doing more than she has to.

**Wants:** To keep the cover intact so nobody — her or the party — gets caught. Genuinely rooting for them now, but not brave: she'll help from Room 6, not walk into a fight.

**Stat block:** none worth rolling. No weapon, no meaningful combat stats — basic NPC, non-combatant. If somehow forced into a fight she flees or freezes; do not treat her as a threat or a resource in a fight.

**Check-ins:** she still answers the network's pipe-click check-ins herself, in character, to hold the cover. This is a source of quiet tension for her (not a puzzle the party has to solve) — a botched check-in exposes everyone in the room. See [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Room 6 key]] for the check-in clock timing.

## What She Knows

- Four secondary magazine sites and their status
- Accelerated timeline ("detonation was two days — now less")
- A fifth site exists under red-caste authority ("the circle site")
- Sentry compositions at each site
- Egress vent positions

## What She Doesn't Know

- Primary site location
- Circle purpose (the Slaad summoning)
- [[solange-barret|Solange Barret]]'s identity
- [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]]'s identity

## Social Triggers

- Naming [[felix-aho|Felix Aho]] — same rotation; she reacts
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] speaking Grung in handler-register — freezes; profoundly wrong to her, though she's since decided she doesn't mind him personally
- Her doodles — anyone who engages with them sincerely instead of dismissing them gets a warmer read; this is exactly how the party won her over

- [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]] — employer (does not know her by name); Ruma is still nominally working for her, which is the whole danger of the arrangement
