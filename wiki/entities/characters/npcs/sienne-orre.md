# Sienne Orre

![[Sienne-Orre.webp]]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Captain, [[fernen|The Fernen]]; [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] |
| **Status** | Deceased — soul inside [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] |

Sienne captained the *[[fernen|Fernen]]*, the fleet's fourth vessel. Druid, capable of wildshaping into aquatic forms. She ran the second underwater scout team alongside [[maren|Maren]] during the perimeter survey of Umberlee's shrine island. She was outside the cave when the fleet sank.

Her soul was pressed into [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] at the moment of sinking, along with the other four captains.

---

## Appearance & Manner

Silver-streaked hair cut close, older than her role implied she needed to be. She carries that without embarrassment. Unhurried — she moves like someone who has learned that rushing produces worse information. Wherever she goes, she is writing something down.

## The Voice

Meticulous report delivered after three drafts. Worn by how rarely other people come as prepared as she does.

**At table:**
> *"We have the perimeter, we have the chart, and we have one piece that isn't matching. I need five minutes."*
> *"I know it's not what you wanted to hear. I wrote down what I saw."*

## Backstory

She kept a room at the Green Mermaid — a harborside tavern in Kettermoor — where she stored fourteen years of research notes. Tide charts, druidic correspondences, migration patterns, contracts annotated in the margins. She had been meaning to organize them properly. She did not get to.

She went to sea because the sea had a set of systems she didn't yet understand, and she was constitutionally incapable of leaving a system unexplained.
