## Overview

Simone Tabarnack is [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude's]] younger sister. She is purple-caste — skilled, disciplined, and fully committed to the Botukuri clan and its caste order. She was a garrison soldier on [[sorn|Sorn]] when Jean-Claude left.

She is the one who told their parents about [[pell|Pell]].

Jean-Claude walked into that hut expecting the best day of his life. He found Simone already there, Pell already in chains, and every part of the plan already known. What followed was a month of torture. By the time he ran, he understood exactly what she had done and why. She had done it because she believed in the clan. She still does.

## Appearance & Manner

Purple Grung, compact and precise. Fitted armor, spear in hand. She carries herself like someone used to giving orders from a position of caste authority — still when things around her are moving, watching before committing. Reads as a soldier first and a person second.

## What Jean-Claude Knows

She was younger than him and always more certain. Where he questioned the system quietly, she moved through it with the confidence of someone who has never seen a reason to doubt it. Reporting him wasn't a betrayal in her frame. It was the correct action. That is the part that stays with him: not that she did it, but that she thought she was right to.

## Session 01 — The Canister

During the Session 01 boarding of the [[saltwright|Saltwright]], a Dravosi Alchemist threw a gas canister into the fight. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] was caught in the cloud. He placed the compound immediately — standard Grung hunting toxin, cheap bulk stock. What stopped him was the preparation method. He knew that hand. He and Simone had brewed versions of it together before he left.

His sister is supplying the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]].

She sold them something disposable — cheap enough to pass Crown intake as "amphibian alkaloid derivative," generic enough to disappear from any supply ledger. Jean-Claude said nothing. The party doesn't know yet.
