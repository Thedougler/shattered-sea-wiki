# Verity Hollowell

| | |
|---|---|
| **Species** | Human (Dravosi) |
| **Role** | Secondary account manager, Hollowell Import Agency |
| **Location** | [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]] |

## Overview

Verity Hollowell is the older daughter of [[cedric-hollowell|Cedric Hollowell]], whose import agency operates near the Tidefall inspection pier. She came into the ledger work sideways — she caught errors faster than the men paid to catch them, and eventually her father stopped pretending the arrangement was temporary.

She met a sailor once on a shore leave dock, years ago. They spent several hours moving together in the dark, no conversation that needed words, just movement and the sound of the water under the dock. Then a ship's bell called him back and he went. She returned to that dock on subsequent evenings for a long time. He was not there.

Then her father went instead, one evening, and waited.

She does not speak about what was said. She has continued her ledger work. She is careful now about what she brings home.

## Role

If the party arrives in Port Tidefall and [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey's]] name surfaces, she already knows it is him. She does not volunteer this. She answers what is asked.

She is more useful than her father in most practical conversations — she has the same Tidefall access and considerably better judgment about when to use it.

## Manner

Twenty-four. Dark hair kept pinned under a practical hat when working. A particular stillness people mistake for disinterest until they notice she has already catalogued everything in the room. Level tone. Complete sentences. Does not reach for warmth or frost on strangers.

> *"The secondary accounts are mine. The primary ones are my father's. If you need access to pier scheduling, that runs through my desk."*

> [!dm]
> **The ledger problem:** Sitting on a gap in her father's primary accounts — bonded cargo moved through Hollowell licensing without a formal manifest entry. Whether her father placed it or someone exploited his credentials is unresolved. She has reported nothing. The gap implicates her family's license and her father's standing.
