# Catarina Da'Virelli

Youthful face, white hair that says otherwise — the kind of double-take that lands before she's said a word. Lean, quick-handed, a mind that outpaces a body built for benchwork, not the rough stuff. In [[calveno|Calveno]] she *is* [[kats-curios|Cat's Curios]]: a narrow side-street door with brass fittings and no name, referral only, no walk-ins. She is the foremost living expert on [[antheri|Antheri]] materials in the Scatter — the woman who touched off the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] Goldrush in 1488 DR, then came home and put her own workshop into production instead of joining the rush.

Carries her father's flask of scotch, sealed, saved for a celebration she can't yet name — and **Strix**, the mechanical owl she built early in her career, one of her first real creations, still ticking at her side. It doesn't talk. It doesn't need to. (That owl is the working prototype behind the [[clockwork-owl-familiar|Clockwork Owl Familiar]] she now sells across the Shattered Sea.)

> [!dm]
> The worst thing she's ever done: the binding contract with the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] fleet — ship upgrades and soldier kit, made with open eyes, for money and protection. She doesn't talk about it. Her blind spot: she's certain the flying machine is a pure pursuit — no magic, for ordinary people — and can't yet see how far that conviction might take her.

## Backstory

Kidnapped as a child during a raid; her father created a distraction so she could escape, and she jumped from cliffs into the water below to get clear. Whether he survived is still open — she carries that uncertainty, and his sealed flask, everywhere. The freefall is where the obsession with flight was born: next time, she wants that choice to be hers.

In 1488 DR she salvaged functional Antheri components from the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] above the [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]] and reverse-engineered several into working devices — the first person to do it. The rush of prospectors, scholars, and criminal contractors that followed became the Shelfworks Goldrush. She didn't join it; she went home to Calveno and put her own workshop into production.

Operates [[kats-curios|Cat's Curios]] by referral only — no advertising, no walk-ins, the circle of people who can reach her deliberately small. Trades information and Antheri metalwork rather than selling outright. Holds a binding contract with the Dravosi Crown fleet (ship upgrades, soldier kit) that she won't take commissions against — reliable work, for as long as the utility holds, and the thing she'd name as the worst compromise she's made.

**The wound:** Her father's fate is unresolved. Everything else — the work ethic, the philosophy, the flask, the flying machine — orbits his absence.

## What She Wants

- **Says:** Build the first flying machine using no magic at all — proof that ingenuity alone can do what people reach for spells to do.
- **Actually:** Fame that means something — a legacy that leaves the world better, not just louder. She hasn't fully reckoned with what she'd compromise to get there.
- **Core belief:** Magic used carelessly is cheap — it steals the discovery. It's also exclusionary by nature, hoarded by the born-lucky and the wealthy; enchanted items should be distributed to ordinary people instead. She's said this out loud, more than once, to people who didn't want to hear it.
- **Red line:** Won't let her inventions be used to harm people or fall into the wrong hands. She watched power get misused growing up and decided early she'd never be that person.
- **Manner:** Treats everyone the same — beggars, servants, children, nobles. No hierarchy. Precise, honest to a fault about what she can and can't make; tells you upfront if something is impossible and explains exactly why. Will not pretend a prototype is finished when it isn't.
