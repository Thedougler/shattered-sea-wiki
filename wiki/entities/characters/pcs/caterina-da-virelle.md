# Caterina Da Virelle

Youthful face, white hair that says otherwise. The kind of double-take that happens before she's said a word. Sharp, insatiably curious, built for thinking rather than fighting — a mind that outpaces a body that was clearly an afterthought.

Works [[calveno|Calveno]] as an artificer for hire, best known for commissioned work with the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] — ship upgrades, soldier kit. Calls herself neutral and mostly believes it. Carries a mechanical owl (one of her first builds, ticking at her side, speaks only in clicks and whirs) and her father's flask of scotch, sealed, kept for a celebration she can't yet name.

*(Also recorded as "Catarina Da Vrelle" in early interview notes — same character; sheet spelling used here as canonical.)*

> [!dm]
> The worst thing she's ever done: working for the Dravosi Crown, eyes open, for money and protection. She doesn't talk about it. Her blind spot: she's certain the flying machine is a pure pursuit — no magic, for ordinary people — and can't yet see how far that conviction might take her.

## Backstory

Kidnapped as a child during a raid; her father created a distraction so she could escape, and she jumped from cliffs into the water below to get clear. Whether he survived is still open — she carries that uncertainty, and his sealed flask, everywhere. The freefall is where the obsession with flight was born: next time, she wants that choice to be hers.

Before adventuring: commissioned artificer work for the Crown out of Calveno, useful enough that they keep calling, careful enough that they've never quite owned her. Trusts the Crown "like a well-maintained tool" — reliably, for as long as the utility holds.

**The wound:** Her father's fate is unresolved. Everything else — the work ethic, the philosophy, the flask, the flying machine — orbits his absence.

## What She Wants

- **Says:** To build the first flying machine using no magic at all — proof that ingenuity alone can do what people reach for spells to do.
- **Actually:** Fame that means something — a legacy that leaves the world better, not just louder. She hasn't fully reckoned with what she'd compromise to get there.
- **Core belief:** Magic used carelessly is cheap — it steals the discovery. It's also exclusionary by nature, hoarded by the born-lucky and the wealthy; enchanted items should be distributed to ordinary people instead. She's said this out loud, more than once, to people who didn't want to hear it.
- **Red line:** Won't let her inventions be used to harm people or fall into the wrong hands. She watched power get misused growing up and decided early she'd never be that person.
- **Treats everyone the same** — beggars, servants, children, nobles. No hierarchy. Likely her father's example.
