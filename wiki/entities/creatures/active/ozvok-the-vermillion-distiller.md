# Ozvok, the Vermillion Distiller

*Small Humanoid (grung), Typically Lawful Evil*

**Armor Class** 14 (natural armor) &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Phase 2: AC 17*
**Hit Points** 130 (20d6 + 60)
**Speed** 25 ft., climb 25 ft. &nbsp;·&nbsp; *Phase 2: 40 ft., climb 40 ft.*

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) |

**Saving Throws** DEX +6, CON +6, INT +7
**Skills** Nature +7, Perception +4, Intimidation +6
**Damage Immunities** poison
**Condition Immunities** poisoned
**Senses** passive Perception 14
**Languages** Grung, Common
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Proficiency Bonus** +3

---

## Traits

***Poisonous Skin.*** Any creature that grapples Ozvok or otherwise touches him, or hits him with a melee attack while within 5 feet, takes 5 (2d4) poison damage.

***Standing Leap.*** Ozvok's long jump is up to 25 feet and his high jump up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.

***Amphibious.*** Ozvok can breathe air and water.

***Drunken Nonchalance.*** Ozvok is too far gone to rattle. He has advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

---

## Actions

***Hurl Concoction.*** Ozvok throws a bottle at a point he can see within 60 feet, where it bursts in a 15-foot radius. He chooses one concoction as he throws:

- ***Caustic Flask.*** Each creature in the area makes a DC 15 Dexterity save, taking 21 (6d6) acid damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. The ground in the area becomes difficult terrain until the end of Ozvok's next turn; a creature that starts its turn there takes 5 (2d4) acid damage.
- ***Reeking Draught.*** Each creature in the area makes a DC 15 Constitution save, taking 18 (4d8) poison damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. A creature that fails is also poisoned until the end of its next turn.
- ***Sticky Bomb.*** Each creature in the area makes a DC 15 Dexterity save, taking 10 (3d6) acid damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. A creature that fails is also restrained as the tar hardens, and can use its action to make a DC 15 Strength check, freeing itself on a success.

***Sludge Bottle.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 30/90 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) poison damage.

***The Vintage (Recharge 5-6).*** Ozvok uncorks his prize vial into a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on a point he can see within 60 feet. Each creature in the area makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 35 (10d6) poison damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. A creature that fails is also poisoned until the end of its next turn. The area is lightly obscured and is difficult terrain until the end of Ozvok's next turn; a creature that starts its turn there takes 7 (2d6) poison damage.

---

## Bonus Actions

***Bark Orders.*** Ozvok commands one ally he can see within 60 feet. That ally can immediately use its reaction to move up to its speed and make one weapon attack.

***Swig.*** Ozvok takes a long pull from his cask. He gains 7 temporary hit points and ends the frightened or poisoned condition on himself.

---

## Reactions

***Grease the Approach.*** When a creature enters a space within 10 feet of Ozvok, he flicks a slick vial at its feet. The creature must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone, and its speed becomes 0 until the end of its turn.

***Vanishing Draught (1/Day).*** When Ozvok is reduced to 45 hit points or fewer, or when he chooses to flee, he smashes a vial at his feet in a burst of red smoke, teleports up to 90 feet to an unoccupied space he can see, and is invisible until the end of his next turn.

---

## Chemical Awakening (Phase 2)

The first time Ozvok takes damage from a melee attack, or the first time he is reduced to 90 hit points or fewer, whichever comes first, he uses his reaction to slam back a fistful of elixirs and lurch out of his chair, veins glowing. (If he has no reaction available, this instead happens at the start of his next turn.) His statistics change as follows, and remain changed for the rest of the encounter. Initiative order is not interrupted.

- His Armor Class becomes 17.
- His speed becomes 40 ft., climb 40 ft.
- He gains 22 temporary hit points.
- He can Dash or Disengage as a bonus action.
- He gains the Multiattack and Toxin-Slick Blade actions and the Chemical Leap bonus action, below.

***Multiattack.*** Ozvok makes two Toxin-Slick Blade attacks. He can replace both attacks with one use of Hurl Concoction.

***Toxin-Slick Blade.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d4 + 4) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.

***Chemical Leap (Bonus Action).*** Ozvok jumps up to 30 feet without provoking opportunity attacks, then can make one Toxin-Slick Blade attack against a creature within 5 feet of where he lands.

---

## Legendary Actions (Optional)

Use this suite only if running Ozvok solo or with few minions. Drop it entirely if he has three or more minions. Ozvok can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Ozvok regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

***Toss a Bottle.*** Ozvok makes one Sludge Bottle attack.

***Rally.*** Ozvok uses Bark Orders.

***Caustic Leap (Costs 2 Actions).*** Ozvok moves up to his speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Each creature within 5 feet of where he lands makes a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) acid damage on a failure, or half as much on a success.

## Notes

**Shelved from the Session 06 lineup (2026-07-03)** — [[ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth, the Twiceborn]] took the Delta/T2 slot so the three magazine lieutenants read as three distinct concepts (drunk alchemist / blind monk / mage-abomination), and Ozvok is a second drunk alchemist alongside [[bazzoth-the-steeped|Bazzoth]]. This block is retained for future use — the tidal harbour setting suited his Amphibious trait and his optional Legendary Action suite fits solo placement whenever he's next deployed.

Chemical Awakening is a free reaction trigger (no player choice) — fires on first melee hit taken or at 90 HP, whichever comes first. Bark Orders synergizes with minion escorts before Phase 2; after Awakening he's mobile enough to disengage from minions and dive the backline. Legendary suite is opt-in for solo/duo runs only.
