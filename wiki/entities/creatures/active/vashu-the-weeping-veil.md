## Notes

**Appearance:** an older female purple-caste grung — lean and sinewy with age, smooth deep-purple hide, blind eyes gone fully blank milk-white; a tattered grey rag veil draped over her head and simple monastic robes and wraps.

Guards **Room T1 (Magazine Gamma)** in the [[wiki/sessions/calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|Calveno sewer magazine dungeon]], with 1 [[purple-caste-enforcer|Purple-Caste Enforcer]] escort (assigned 2026-07-01 — party had reached Room 6/Ruma and neutralized Room 4/Magazine Alpha).

Blind Discipline defeats invisibility, darkness, and obscurement-based tactics within 30 ft — including her own Weeping Veil. Fits the low 5-ft ceiling and cramped footprint of T1 especially well: blindsight covers the whole room regardless of light or fog. Evasion plus Still-Water Deflection make her resilient against ranged and AoE parties.
