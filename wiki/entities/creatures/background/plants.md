# Plants

Fungal colonies and animate plant-life. Primarily found in the deep underdark beneath the Scatter.

- [[myconid-adult|Myconid Adult]] — The working body of a myconid colony; communicates through spore clouds and defers to its sovereign
- [[myconid-spore-servant|Myconid Spore Servant]] — A corpse reanimated by myconid spores; retains no personality, only function
- [[gas-spore-fungus|Gas Spore Fungus]] — Explosive underdark fungus that resembles a beholder at a glance; the resemblance ends when it detonates
- [[myconid-sovereign|Myconid Sovereign]] — CR 2 fungal colony leader; projects animating spores to reanimate dead as servants
