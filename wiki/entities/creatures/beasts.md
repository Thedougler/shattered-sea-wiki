# Beasts

Natural predators, swarms, and wildlife of the Scatter's islands and waters.

## Large Predators

- [[giant-crocodile|Giant Crocodile]] — Apex ambush predator of the tidal rivers and mangrove channels of the [[midchain|Midchain]]
- [[giant-constrictor-snake|Giant Constrictor Snake]] — Found in rainforest interiors of the [[midchain|Midchain]] and [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]; large enough to pin and suffocate a horse
- [[giant-ape|Giant Ape]] — Territorial apex predator of dense rainforest interiors; sightings at [[sorn|Sorn]] suggest at least one family controls the highland canopy
- [[giant-boar|Giant Boar]] — Charges without warning from undergrowth of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]; big enough to stave in a ship's boat
- [[giant-shark|Giant Shark]] — Follows cold upwellings and blood trails; a serious threat near [[galewall|Galewall]]'s wreckage and along [[tail|the Tail]]'s longline grounds
- [[giant-squid|Giant Squid]] — Deep-water ambush predator that rises from the drop-off at night; a hazard for divers at the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]]
- [[panther|Panther]] — Silent apex predator of the dense canopy of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]; a party may be stalked for hours before it commits
- [[tiger|Tiger]] — Forest and grassland predator; fast, powerful, and rarely seen before the first strike
- [[owlbear|Owlbear]] — Ambush predator of the dense interior forests of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]; the screech is usually the last thing heard before contact
- [[primeval-owlbear|Primeval Owlbear]] — A massive prehistoric variant, CR 7; significantly stronger and more durable than its common cousin
- [[giant-axe-beak|Giant Axe Beak]] — Aggressive flightless predator on the clifftop scrublands of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] and exposed ridgelines; fast enough to run down a horse

## Birds

- [[giant-eagle|Giant Eagle]] — Nests in the clifftop aeries of the High Eyrie; large enough to carry a grown sailor
- [[giant-vulture|Giant Vulture]] — Carrion bird found wherever the sea deposits its dead; circles the clifftops of the High Eyrie and the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]]
- [[giant-owl|Giant Owl]] — Nocturnal forest hunter of the old-growth interiors of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]; near-silent on the wing
- [[pegasus|Pegasus]] — Rumoured to range the updrafts above the High Eyrie; the Sentinels claim sightings but treat the subject as a private matter
- [[unicorn|Unicorn]] — Unconfirmed; a single account from a [[midchain|Midchain]] interior expedition describes a white-maned creature that vanished before anyone else could see it

## Reptiles and Amphibians

- [[giant-lizard|Giant Lizard]] — Rocky-shore and ruin predator of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] and [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]; territorial and fast
- [[giant-toad|Giant Toad]] — Ambush predator of boggy lowlands and freshwater margins of [[karath|Karath]]
- [[giant-frog|Giant Frog]] — Marshland and tidal-flat ambush predator of [[sorn|Sorn]], the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], and [[orak|Orak]]
- [[constrictor-snake|Constrictor Snake]] — Common tree-and-water snake of the [[midchain|Midchain]] lowlands; large enough to take a dog
- [[giant-venomous-snake|Giant Venomous Snake]] — Large ambush serpent of the mid-canopy and river margins in the [[verdant-scatter|Verdant Scatter]] and [[karath|Karath]]
- [[venomous-snake|Venomous Snake]] — Common bite hazard of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] undergrowth; small enough to go unnoticed until stepped on
- [[giant-seahorse|Giant Seahorse]] — Large enough to serve as a mount; found in sheltered reef waters of the [[central-strait|Central Strait]]

## Arthropods and Arachnids

- [[giant-scorpion|Giant Scorpion]] — Found in dry rocky terrain of the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]]; hides under flat stone and inside hollow logs
- [[giant-spider|Giant Spider]] — Web-builder of the deep jungle interior; found across the [[verdant-scatter|Verdant Scatter]], [[karath|Karath]], and [[orak|Orak]]
- [[giant-wolf-spider|Giant Wolf Spider]] — Hunting spider of the undergrowth of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] and [[ral-arn|Ral-Arn]]; doesn't web, runs fast
- [[giant-wasp|Giant Wasp]] — Nests in cliff overhangs and hollow shoreline trees around [[doldrums|the Doldrums]] and [[kalowe|Kalowe]]; disturbing one nest sends the whole colony
- [[giant-centipede|Giant Centipede]] — Found under loose stone and damp timber throughout [[sorn|Sorn]]; bites rather than charges
- [[giant-crab|Giant Crab]] — Intertidal heavyweight of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] beaches; large enough to shear rope
- [[giant-octopus|Giant Octopus]] — Reef-dwelling ambush predator in the rocky shallows of the [[central-strait|Central Strait]]

## Small Creatures and Pests

- [[giant-bat|Giant Bat]] — Cave-roosting hunter of the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]] and [[tail|the Tail]]; emerges at dusk in columns thick enough to blot the stars
- [[giant-rat|Giant Rat]] — Dock-bred pest of [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s warehouses; larger than a cat and aggressive when cornered
- [[giant-weasel|Giant Weasel]] — Burrow hunter of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] forest floor; occasionally found in cargo holds after a Crown Islands layover
- [[boar|Boar]] — Common feral hog of the scrubland and coastal heath of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]

## Swarms

- [[swarm-of-bats|Swarm of Bats]] — Vast cave colony of the sea-cliff overhangs of the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]]; the evening departure sounds like distant thunder
- [[swarm-of-rats|Swarm of Rats]] — Hull-rot hazard of [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s waterfront; infests badly-kept stowage and spreads between vessels in harbour
- [[swarm-of-ravens|Swarm of Ravens]] — Carrion flock from the cliffs of [[vel-orn|Vel-Orn]]; circles over a vessel in distress is a universally understood bad sign
- [[swarm-of-insects|Swarm of Insects]] — Working hazard of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] jungle floor; biting clouds that can force a camp to move in under an hour
- [[swarm-of-piranhas|Swarm of Piranhas]] — Found in still interior lakes and spring-fed pools of [[midchain|Midchain]] islands
- [[swarm-of-venomous-snakes|Swarm of Venomous Snakes]] — Disturbed nest or flooded burrow; the interior undergrowth harbours more than it shows
- [[swarm-of-larvae|Swarm of Larvae]] — Linked to planar scarring near [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]]; larval forms of the damned that swarm toward the living
