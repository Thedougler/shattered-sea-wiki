# Celestials

Divine and sacred creatures. Encountering one is rarely accidental.

- [[guardian-naga|Guardian Naga]] — Ancient guardian of a jungle shrine somewhere in the [[midchain|Midchain]] interior; the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] have ruins older than any colonial map, and locals know which temples are not visited twice
- [[pegasus|Pegasus]] — Rumoured to range the updrafts above the High Eyrie; the Sentinels claim sightings but treat the subject as a private matter
- [[unicorn|Unicorn]] — Unconfirmed; a single account from a [[midchain|Midchain]] interior expedition describes a white-maned creature that vanished before anyone else could see it
