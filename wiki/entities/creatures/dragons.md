# Dragons

Dragons, dragon turtles, and great serpents. Most are apex threats — encounters are survival problems, not fights.

## Dragon Turtles

- [[ancient-dragon-turtle|Ancient Dragon Turtle]] — Mythic deep-water predator known for boiling seas and scalding steam breath; found in the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] and around [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]
- [[dragon-turtle|Dragon Turtle]] — Large enough to break a working boat; lurks at the edge of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] blue hole and eastern [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]]
- [[young-dragon-turtle|Young Dragon Turtle]] — Dangerous enough to capsize small vessels; found where reef shallows give way to deep water
- [[dragon-turtle-wyrmling|Dragon Turtle Wyrmling]] — Still large enough to wreck a skiff; sightings cluster around the outer beaches of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]

## Sea Serpents

- [[ancient-sea-serpent|Ancient Sea Serpent]] — Cold-current predator that moves against the wind; haunts the deep lanes of the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]]
- [[young-sea-serpent|Young Sea Serpent]] — Follows warm currents through the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] and along the Tail's shelf edge

## True Dragons

- [[ancient-gold-dragon|Ancient Gold Dragon]] — The oldest and most powerful of the metallic dragons; a legendary coastal presence
- [[adult-gold-dragon|Adult Gold Dragon]] — Commanding metallic dragon of coastal and island territories
- [[ancient-bronze-dragon|Ancient Bronze Dragon]] — Elder coastal metallic dragon; watches the Shelfworks and Drowned Maw from the western shelf
- [[adult-bronze-dragon|Adult Bronze Dragon]] — The Stormwarden of the Galewall; appears during the worst crossings in humanoid form
- [[ancient-black-dragon|Ancient Black Dragon]] — The reason Aruhe is avoided; old enough to have dissolved the island's terraces from the base up
- [[adult-black-dragon|Adult Black Dragon]] — Swamp and mangrove predator of the Doldrums; a becalmed ship is close to ideal hunting
- [[ancient-blue-dragon|Ancient Blue Dragon]] — Laired in the Redwind Isles of the Outer Reach; has made the islands uninhabitable without needing to explain itself
- [[adult-blue-dragon|Adult Blue Dragon]] — Taxes Crown Islands shipping through the Greyteeth; the Crown's arrangement with it is unacknowledged but functional
- [[ancient-green-dragon|Ancient Green Dragon]] — Old-growth forest predator; master manipulator with poison breath and a taste for long schemes
- [[adult-green-dragon|Adult Green Dragon]] — Forest dragon; hunts the dense interior canopy of large islands
