# Fey

Creatures with ties to the Feywild. Goblins are the primary fey threat encountered in the Scatter's interior islands.

## Goblins

- [[goblin-boss|Goblin Boss]] — Commands a goblin warband; smarter and more dangerous than the warriors it leads
- [[goblin-warrior|Goblin Warrior]] — Standard goblin raider; dangerous in numbers, quick to flee when the odds shift
- [[goblin-minion|Goblin Minion]] — The weakest goblin variant; used as fodder and distraction
- [[goblin-hexer|Goblin Hexer]] — Spellcasting goblin that curses and debilitates with fey magic; found alongside warbands

## Hags

- [[gentle-hag|Gentle Hag]] — CR 7 charm specialist; builds permanent thrall networks through persistent enchantment
- [[haunt-hag|Haunt Hag]] — CR 5 fear-and-illusion predator; reads victims' deepest terrors and embodies them

## Other Fey

- [[moucheron|Moucheron]] — CR 8 fey mercenary from the Plane of Faerie; demands payment in blood, not gold
