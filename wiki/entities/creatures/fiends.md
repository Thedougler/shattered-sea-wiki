# Fiends

Sahuagin warbands are the primary fiendish threat in the Scatter — they raid from the deep shelf and outer reef walls.

- [[sahuagin-baron|Sahuagin Baron]] — Warlord commanding shark-riders from the deep shelf of the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] and outer reef walls of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]
- [[sahuagin-priest|Sahuagin Priest]] — Conducts blood rites at submerged shrines in the blue holes of the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]; encountered where warbands gather before a raid
- [[sahuagin-warrior|Sahuagin Warrior]] — The raiding soldiers of the deep; encountered in packs along shelf walls and reef drop-offs
- [[swarm-of-larvae|Swarm of Larvae]] — Linked to planar scarring near [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]]; larval forms of the damned that swarm toward the living
