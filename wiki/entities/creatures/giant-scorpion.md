# Giant Scorpion

Giant scorpions in the Shattered Sea don't need desert. They need heat, dry crevice space, and something to hunt in the dark — and the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]] give them all three. The volcanic vent system keeps the high stone warm even in cold sea air, and the same fissures that shelter roosting [[giant-bat|giant bats]] make ideal scorpion dens: narrow, black, warm at the back, and invisible from outside until something already inside moves.

They appear across the [[midchain|Midchain]] wherever limestone terrace walls or ridge stone hold daytime heat — the abandoned island of [[aruhe|Aruhe]] has them living behind dry-stacked terrace blocks, and [[veth|Veth]]'s limestone ridges push them down to the trade beach at night when the rock is still warm. In the Midchain the threat is intermittent and local; in the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]], where warm vent systems run through the high stone year-round, it is consistent.

Most crews learn about them from repair work. A hull carpenter named Duvane, sent up the cliff face of the largest Ashwall spire to assess a spar that had fouled against the rock, came down with a claw wound across his forearm and a sting taken through his boot. He described not seeing it at all until both claws were engaged. The poison put him down for two days. He kept the boot as evidence and spent the recovery time drawing what he remembered of the fissure opening. Neither detail made the pilot's charts, but Ashwall crews who know Duvane's account now send two people up the rock face and have one watch the stone. ^[inferred]
