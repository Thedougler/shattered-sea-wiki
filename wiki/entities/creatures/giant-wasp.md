## Habitat

Cliff overhangs, hollow trees, and forest margins. In the Shattered Sea, nests around the [[doldrums|Doldrums]], [[kalowe|Kalowe]], [[sorn|Sorn]], and [[dreth|Dreth]]. Disturbing one nest sends the whole colony.
