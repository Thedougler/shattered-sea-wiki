## Habitat

Nests in the sea cliffs of the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]], [[meth-var|Meth-Var]], and rocky stretches of [[tail|the Tail]]. The song carries further than expected; crews on watch who can't see the source are the primary risk.
