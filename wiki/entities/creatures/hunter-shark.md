## Habitat

Hunter sharks follow the things ships leave behind: bilge discharge, fish-cleaning waste, blood from cargo loading. Where there are boats working water regularly, they learn the schedule.

The [[central-strait|Central Strait]] has them year-round in the deeper lanes, trailing merchant convoys and patrol routes alike. The [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] harbour approaches see more incidents than any port authority records cleanly — most involve swimmers. At [[kalowe|Kalowe]], the reef gap that opens the lagoon holds baitfish, which holds hunter sharks, which the dry-dock workers have quietly learned to account for. The gap now has a rope with a bell on it. ^[inferred]
