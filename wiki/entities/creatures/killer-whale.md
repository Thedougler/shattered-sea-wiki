## Habitat

Works the cold lanes — [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] deep water, the storm-cold approaches to the [[galewall|Galewall]], the upwelling edges where the water turns dark. They move in pods and hunt in pods, which is what distinguishes them from sharks. A pod will follow debris and survivors for hours — not always attacking, sometimes only circling. ^[inferred]
