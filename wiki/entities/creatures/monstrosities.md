# Monstrosities

Creatures that defy easy classification — part animal, part nightmare.

- [[roc|Roc]] — Nests on the high basalt sea stacks near the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]]; large enough to carry off a longboat
- [[harpy|Harpy]] — Nests in the sea cliffs of the [[ashwall-islands|Ashwall Islands]], [[meth-var|Meth-Var]], and rocky stretches of [[tail|the Tail]]; the song carries further than it should
- [[mimic|Mimic]] — Shape-shifting ambush predator found in urban ruins and sealed vaults; looks like furniture until it isn't
- [[unfamiliar|Unfamiliar]] — CR 5 familiar-hunter; instinctively targets anyone who has bonded a familiar; each melee strike triggers a random uncontrolled spell
- [[owlbear|Owlbear]] — Ambush predator of the Crown Islands forest interior
- [[primeval-owlbear|Primeval Owlbear]] — A massive prehistoric variant, CR 7; significantly more dangerous than its common cousin
