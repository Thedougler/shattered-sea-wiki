Deep old-growth forest, likely the densest interior of the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]]. Larger, older, and considerably more dangerous than the common [[owlbear|Owlbear]]; Magic Resistance and the charging Ravage make it a credible apex threat for a seasoned party.
