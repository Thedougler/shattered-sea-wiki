# Sea Life

Creatures of the open water, reef, and deep.

- [[leviathan|The Leviathan]] — Rumoured titanic black eel that boils the sea around it; sightings limited to the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]
- [[ridgeback|Ridgeback (The Roller)]] — Elemental serpent from the Plane of Water; second entity through the Maw fissure; identified by rolling hump movement and tribute-demanding behaviour
- [[sawek|Sawek (Blue Devil)]] — Ambush predator of blue holes in the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] and [[midchain|Midchain]]
- [[whip-shark|Whip Shark (Welak)]] — Open-ocean hunter big enough to sink small ships, found in the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] and deep open water
- [[krakling|Krakling (Young Kraken)]] — Eyeless, eight-armed black predator; recent reports of arm-attacks on ships in the outer [[midchain|Midchain]]; possible juvenile form of the Kraken
- [[kraken|Kraken]] — Titanic deep-sea predator capable of destroying ships; rumoured in the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] and beyond the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]
- [[killer-whale|Killer Whale]] — Hunts in pods through the cold open water of the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] and approaches to the [[galewall|Galewall]]
- [[hunter-shark|Hunter Shark]] — Common working shark of the Scatter, following ship routes and harbour chum
- [[reef-shark|Reef Shark]] — Common in shallow reef channels of the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] and the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]]
- [[sea-hag|Sea Hag]] — Fey predator of isolated coastal caves and wreck-strewn shallows; approaches ships in the guise of a survivor
