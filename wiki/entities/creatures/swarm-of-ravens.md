## Habitat

Carrion flock from the cliffs of [[vel-orn|Vel-Orn]] and [[ral-arn|Ral-Arn]]. A swarm circling over a vessel in distress is a universally read sign; sailors who see them already circling know someone is likely dead or close to it.
