# Umberlee

Sea goddess. Sailors pay because the sea kills, not because she is kind. Her public title is the Bitch Queen.

Her [[waveservants|Waveservants]] operate as collectors, witnesses, and ritual functionaries. Public-facing doctrine and shrine custom live in [[umberlee-and-waveservants|Umberlee and the Waveservants]].

## Campaign Secret

When [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s fleet sank after stealing the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], she pressed five captains' souls into him rather than releasing them. They ride in his head as personalities. The debt is open — she has not collected it, and [[branca|Branca]], her Waveservant agent in [[calveno|Calveno]], is not sleeping until he comes.

## Domain & Limits

She claims the sea. Her authority terminates at the planar boundary of the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] — the Maw's deep waters are not mortal sea. This is why she cannot retrieve the Pearl herself. The [[waveservants|Waveservants]] do not discuss this. Their working position: the Maw is an anomaly that will eventually return within her dominion.

Umberlee never appears directly — she acts through storms, through her clergy, and through the [[dead-lady|Dead Lady]]. Her presence is felt in weather that turns wrong, in Waveservants who look at Fisk with recognition before they speak.

## Legendary Assets

- **[[dead-lady|The Dead Lady]]** — Umberlee's instrument for the Fisk retrieval; the shape her approach takes when the Waveservants aren't enough
- **[[the-tithe-of-the-bitch-queen|The Tithe of the Bitch Queen]]** — the accumulated collection of beautiful things Umberlee claimed from mortal ships over centuries; scattered across the Sunken Crown seafloor; any item taken brings localised squalls until the sea reclaims it

## Theological Tensions

She claims the sea. The Maw sits outside that claim — the Waveservants have never satisfactorily resolved this and prefer not to examine it. The sea elves of Halythion predate her claim and do not acknowledge it.
