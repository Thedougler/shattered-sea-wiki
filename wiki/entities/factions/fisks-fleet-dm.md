# Secrets of Fisk's Fleet

**Public profile:** [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]]

---

## The Commission

The fleet's final commission came through [[chain-council|the Chain Council]]: theft of the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from Umberlee's shrine. The Council believed it was protecting Midchain waters from Umberlee's wrath. In truth, the Principal used the Council as a local authorizing mask — the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] wanted the Pearl kept away from [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]], who they believe could use it to expand his Hierarch abilities beyond his own bloodline.

**The operation:** Two scout teams surveying the perimeter, one entry team inside the cave, one distraction team at the main entrance. The Pearl was secured. The fleet departed.

**Umberlee's response:** Fog so thick the water was invisible, surrounding all five ships simultaneously. A rogue trench opened — all five ships plunged 80 feet down. The walls closed. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] dove from the *Red Lady*'s crow's nest, grabbed [[delmar-fisk|Fisk]], and flew straight back up — she had only risen 50 feet when the water closed; momentum and buoyancy carried them both to the surface.

## Soul Mechanics

All five ships sank simultaneously. The [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] — still aboard the *Red Lady* — activated at the moment of sinking and collected the souls of all five ships' crews. The five captains' souls were pressed into [[delmar-fisk|Fisk]] separately, by Umberlee's direct action, as punishment and leverage.

- **Fisk carries** the five captains inside him.
- **The Pearl holds** the crews.
- Both the Pearl and the *Red Lady* are at the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]]' eastern edge, where the shelf descends toward the trench floor.

[[rael-corven|Rael]] was the only captain who entered the cave with Fisk; the other four commanded from outside. This makes Rael's voice the loudest and most trusted of the five.

**Delmar's return trigger:** A brass flagon — the sight or touch of one while any captain is surfaced brings Delmar back. Tied to the night after the mutiny when those five crewmates stayed: stranded, no ship, nothing left, one flagon passed around. The moment before the fleet existed. It anchors him to himself before any of this happened.

## Serafina — DM Notes

Serafina was not aboard the *Red Lady* when the fleet went down. She doesn't know Fisk is shipless and carrying five dead souls.

The marriage arrangement is open — physical encounters acceptable; the one line is denying each other's existence to get what you want. Fisk has never tested it. He knows she would know.
