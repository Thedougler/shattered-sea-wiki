# The *Velvet Noose*

> [!read-aloud]
> A four-deck man-of-war fills the horizon before the sound reaches you. Her hull is black lacquer over dark red underpaint, banded in dull iron from waterline to gunwale. Three mastheads fly long velvet pennants the colour of dried wine. The gunports are closed, all ninety of them, and that is worse than if they were open. At the prow, a gilded figurehead of a laughing magistrate holds a silk rope in one hand and a broken crown seal in the other.

## Overview

The *Velvet Noose* is a Tier 4 pirate man-of-war: not a raider, not a privateer, not a ship that hides in a reef channel and hopes the patrol passes. She is a moving crisis. When she appears, captains calculate surrender terms, harbourmasters find reasons to delay paperwork, and the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] starts asking which squadron is close enough to matter.

Her best weapon is the first hour after she is sighted, when everyone with authority aboard the target starts arguing about who is allowed to surrender.

The *Velvet Noose* does not chase small prizes. She takes convoys, ransoms officials, breaks blockades, shelters outlaw vessels, and carries enough cannon to make a fortified harbour choose between pride and architecture.

## Operational Notes

**Opening move:** Sends terms before firing. Terms are insulting, precise, and legally literate. If refused, disables the escort, captures the signal officer, and sends the same terms again with a shorter deadline.

**Escape plan:** She runs early. Fog batteries, false fleet signals, Spirit Crew night watches, and prearranged harbour debts give her options before a superior force arrives.

**Reputation:** Some captains surrender because the stories say crews are treated better when nobody wastes powder. Some refuse because being the captain who yielded can ruin a family name faster than dying.

> [!dm]
> **As an enemy:** She should change the map, not just start combat. A Tier 1–3 party cannot fight this ship directly.
> **As a prize:** Campaign-altering and immediately contested by every major naval, commercial, and religious power.
> **As an ally:** Solves impossible naval problems in ways that make the party complicit.
> **As a rumour:** Makes convoys delay, patrols consolidate, and smugglers charge more.
