# Vaka

A loose community of [[grung|Grung]] defectors and freed former slaves navigating life outside the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]. Not a formal faction — a network of people who left the caste system and need each other to find their footing.

[[felix-aho|Félix Aho]] has been building it for eight years, mostly through showing up, keeping his head down, and making it clear that Grung who leave don't have to be alone. He runs a fish stall in [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. The network is bigger than the stall suggests.

> [!dm]
> The Vaka is deliberately low-profile. Félix's approach is to make the peaceful life visible — every Grung act of violence confirms what people already believe; the Vaka's work is showing something different. Threatening the network or the people in it is the trigger that shows the violence he is choosing not to use.
>
> JC pressure point: the Vaka makes JC's exile feel less singular and more politically useful. Félix knows the Gold castes fracture between islands — information JC doesn't have.
