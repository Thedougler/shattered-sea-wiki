# Boots of Flying

**Wondrous item** · *Uncommon, requires attunement*

Wing decorations on the ankles. While attuned, the wearer gains a flying speed equal to their walking speed for up to 4 hours total. The boots regain 2 hours of flight per 12 hours not in use.

Purchased by [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] from [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] for 125gp and gifted to the party. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] won the roll but passed them to [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] — Rattkin don't wear shoes.
