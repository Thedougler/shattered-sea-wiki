# Caulking Iron

A broad flat-edged iron blade, tang-handled, for driving oakum into hull seams. Used with a caulking mallet. Dry-dock grade irons are heat-tempered to survive repeated mallet strikes without deforming.

**Price:** 2 gp at ironmongers.
