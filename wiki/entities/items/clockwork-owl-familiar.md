# Clockwork Owl Familiar

*Uncommon construct — requires attunement*

**[HB] Price: 1,800 gp** | Available at [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]]

A brass construct built by [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] using Antheri-derived techniques. The owl form is the most common shape; other compact forms exist. Common enough throughout the Shattered Sea that most people have seen one.

## Properties

- Functions as *Find Familiar* cast at will; the familiar takes a Tiny construct form (Mechanical Spider or equivalent stat block).
- Does not require a spell slot to resummon.
- Can be damaged and repaired (50 gp and 1 day of work per 10 HP lost).
- Cannot be permanently destroyed unless the core mechanism is deliberately smashed.
- Requires attunement.
