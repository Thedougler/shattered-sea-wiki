# Shortbow

*Simple ranged weapon · 25 gp · 2 lb*

## Properties

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Damage | 1d6 piercing |
| Range | 80 / 320 ft |
| Properties | Ammunition (Arrows), Two-Handed |

**Ammunition.** Each attack expends one arrow. Search action to recover half (rounded down) after combat.

**Two-Handed.** Requires both hands to attack.

## Niche

**Best at:** accessibility. No martial proficiency required — any class can use it effectively. Real range (80/320) without Heavy or Loading.

**Worst at:** damage. 1d6 is the lowest die of any ranged weapon, though the range compensates.
