# Truth Stone

A smooth grey stone, unremarkable to look at. It sits warm in the hand from being carried — that warmth is ordinary. What isn't ordinary is what happens when the holder knowingly states a falsehood.

The stone heats rapidly toward white-hot the moment a deliberate lie passes the holder's lips. The holder knows. Anyone watching knows.

## Properties

When a creature holding the Truth Stone makes a Deception check — whether it succeeds or fails — the stone deals **1d6 fire damage** at the start of each of their turns for 1 minute, unless the stone is dropped. This effect stacks: each additional Deception check adds another 1d6 per turn to the ongoing damage.

A creature who does not know their statement is false feels nothing. Dropping the stone (no action required) ends all ongoing damage immediately.

The stone does not compel honesty — it makes lying expensive. A creature willing to endure the damage can attempt deception regardless. Creatures with immunity to fire damage can lie freely while holding it.
