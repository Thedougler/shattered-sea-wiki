# Compass

A liquid-damped magnetic compass in a brass case, navigation quality. Points reliably to magnetic north under normal conditions. Available at chandleries serving serious navigators.

**Price:** 50 gp.

Reports of erratic behaviour near the Drowned Maw are consistent but unverified.

*Not to be confused with the [[compass-of-the-drowned|Compass of the Drowned]], which points toward shipwrecks.*
