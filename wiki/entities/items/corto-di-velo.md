# Corto di Velo

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Weapon (shortbow) |
| **Rarity** | Uncommon |
| **Attunement** | No |
| **Owner** | [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] |

A Calveno shortbow on a short yew stave, barely four feet, with a recurve at both limbs. Oiled linen grip — practical in wet conditions. No maker's mark. A work tool.

## Properties

**[HB] +1** to attack and damage rolls.

**[HB] Point Blank.** Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn't impose disadvantage on attack rolls made with this weapon.

> [!mechanic]
> No visual enchantment — DC 15 Arcana or *Identify* reveals both properties. Jean-Claude will notice Point Blank the first time he fires from within 5 feet and the expected disadvantage doesn't appear.

## Cost

**Purchase: 30 gp** (unidentified, Velo Quarter). The dealer bought it in an estate lot and does not know it's magical. **Market value (identified): 200 gp.**

## History

The *corto di velo* pattern — Velo Short — is a Calveno dock design built for sailors and festival operatives who need to shoot over cargo stacks or through hatch openings. The maker has been dead for fifteen years; the pattern stayed.
