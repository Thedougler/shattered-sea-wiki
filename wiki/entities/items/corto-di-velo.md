# Corto di Velo

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Weapon (shortbow) |
| **Rarity** | Uncommon |
| **Attunement** | No |
| **Owner** | [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] |

A Calveno shortbow on a short yew stave, barely four feet, with a recurve at both limbs. Oiled linen grip — practical in wet conditions. No maker's mark. A work tool.

## Properties

**[HB] +1** to attack and damage rolls.

**[HB] Whisper Draw.** This weapon produces no sound when fired — no bowstring snap, no arrow whistle. When you make a ranged attack with this weapon while hidden, you remain hidden if the attack misses. On a hit, your position is revealed as normal.

> [!mechanic]
> No visual enchantment — DC 15 Arcana or *Identify* reveals both properties. Jean-Claude will notice Whisper Draw the first time he fires from hiding and misses — nothing happens. No sound, no reveal, the target doesn't flinch. The bow simply doesn't announce itself.

> [!dm]
> Whisper Draw synergizes with Umbral Sight: JC is invisible to darkvision creatures in dim light/darkness. Normally, attacking from hiding reveals your position even on a miss. This bow lets missed shots stay silent — only hits give away his position (and even then, the target knows the direction but can't see him through Umbral Sight). At Level 5 with Extra Attack, this means his first shot from hiding can miss without losing the ambush. The bow rewards accuracy from stealth without being broken — he still reveals on a hit, and enemies with Blindsight/Tremorsense bypass it entirely.

## Cost

**Purchase: 30 gp** (unidentified, Velo Quarter). The dealer bought it in an estate lot and does not know it's magical. **Market value (identified): 200 gp.**

## History

The *corto di velo* pattern — Velo Short — is a Calveno dock design built for festival operatives and canal-side enforcers who needed a bow that wouldn't announce itself across still water. The compact stave draws quietly; the enchantment finishes the job. The maker has been dead for fifteen years. The estate sold the contents of his workshop as a lot; this bow ended up in a crate of miscellaneous sporting goods. The dealer has no idea what he's sitting on.
