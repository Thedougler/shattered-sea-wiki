# Deepwater Ink

A dense alchemical ink formulated to adhere to wet or submerged surfaces and cure permanently under water pressure. Used for marking hull drafts, writing underwater, and tagging salvage sites.

**Availability:** Sold exclusively by Studio Orsini in [[calveno|Calveno]].

**Properties [HB]:** Adheres to any surface wet or dry; cures underwater in 1 minute. Permanent once cured; resists scraping but dissolves in strong acid.
