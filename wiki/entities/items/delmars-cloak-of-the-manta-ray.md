# Delmar's Cloak of the Manta Ray

> *An old family heirloom. It kept him alive when his ship didn't.*

A long red admiral's coat, salt-worn and well-kept, with a deep hood that falls forward when raised. The lining is smooth and dark, and the coat moves in water the way cloth shouldn't.

**Owner:** [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]

## Properties

**[RAW]** Standard *Cloak of the Manta Ray* (DMG): swim speed 60 ft., breathe underwater, manta ray form while the hood is up.

## History

A family heirloom. Fisk wore it the day [[umberlee|Umberlee]] sank his fleet — [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] dove from the crow's nest and grabbed him, and the coat's buoyancy in the water carried them both to the surface when the walls closed in.
