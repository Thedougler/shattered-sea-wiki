# Depth Eye

*Ship upgrade — scouting*

**[HB] Price: 800 gp** | Available at [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]]

A convex glass lens set flush into a bronze housing bolted through the hull below the waterline, with a viewing tube rising through the decking into a below-decks cabinet. The image it produces is slightly green.

## Properties

- See through water to 60 feet (30 feet in murky water) from below decks.
- No attunement required.
