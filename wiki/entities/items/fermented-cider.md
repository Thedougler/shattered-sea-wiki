# Fermented Cider

A cloudy fermented drink brewed by [[essa-two-tooth|Essa Two-Tooth]] in a clay crock at the [[low-lamp|Low Lamp]]. She calls it cider. The fruit of origin is unclear. It is both acidic and warming, and it works. 5 cp per cup.
