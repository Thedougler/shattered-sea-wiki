# Flask of Endless Water

*Wondrous item · Uncommon · No attunement required*

Current holder: [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]

---

## Description

A heavy ceramic flask — wide-bodied, short-necked, stoppered with dense cork that fits too well to be accidental. No markings, no ornament, worn smooth at the grip from years of handling. The only tell is the stopper, which runs cold even in direct sun, and the fact that no matter how much water has come out of it, it never feels lighter.

[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] has carried it since leaving the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]. He does not explain where he got it.

---

## Properties

While the stopper is removed, the holder can speak a command word as an action to produce water:

| Command | Output | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| *"Stream"* | 1 gallon per action | Pours steadily; useful for drinking, filling containers, wetting surfaces |
| *"Fountain"* | 5 gallons per action | A pressurized upwelling; enough to drench a target or flood a small space quickly |
| *"Geyser"* | 30 gallons in a 30-ft. jet | The holder makes a ranged attack or targets a point; creatures in the jet make a **DC 13 Strength save** or take **1d4 bludgeoning** and fall prone. Structures take full damage automatically. |

The water produced is always fresh. It does not stop unless the stopper is replaced or the command word is not spoken.

Mechanically identical to a Decanter of Endless Water; reflavoured for Jean-Claude.

---

## In Play

**Session 01** — Jean-Claude used geyser mode to blast [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] off the gangplank between the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] and the [[hcs-surety|HCS Surety]] during the boarding. Rook hauled himself back up. The jet worked; the problem was that Rook is difficult to keep down.

## Lore

Origin unestablished. Jean-Claude carried it out of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] when he ran. He has never said where it came from — the flask works, it is useful, and a Grung who needs to keep his skin hydrated in salt-air ports has good reason to value it beyond its tactical applications.
