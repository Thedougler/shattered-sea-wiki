# Ghost Keel Coating

A matte grey-black hull finish that absorbs light rather than reflecting it. The treated planking looks like deep water at a glance, and the ship runs noticeably quieter through the water after application.

**Price:** 900 gp. Requires shipyard application. Available from [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] via [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]].

## Properties

**[HB]**
- Grants **advantage on Stealth checks** at sea.
- Reapplication required every **6 months**.
- Must be applied at a shipyard; cannot be applied at sea.
