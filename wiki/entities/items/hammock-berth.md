# Hammock Berth

Canvas hammock slung from ceiling beams on a shared floor, with canvas partitions between berths. The cheap overnight option at port taverns across the [[shattered-sea|Shattered Sea]]. 1 sp per night at most establishments. Secure enough for sleeping; not for valuables.
