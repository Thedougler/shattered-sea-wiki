# Hull Patch Automaton

A crab-sized brass mechanism that runs the waterline interior on six articulated legs, trailing a thin sealant line from a reservoir on its back — it finds gaps by feel and plugs them before the water comes through.

**Price:** 1,600 gp. Available from [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] via [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]].

## Properties

**[HB]**
- Automatically seals hull breaches under 1 foot in diameter without crew intervention.
- Reduces hull HP loss from combat flooding by **1d6 per round**.
- Requires monthly refill of sealant compound: **40 gp**.
- Does not repair structural damage — breach sealing only.
- Mounts internally along the waterline.
