# Hull Repaint

Full strip and repaint of a vessel's topsides — old colour completely removed, new pigment applied in two coats. Available at boatyards in [[calveno|Calveno]] ([[acqua-nera|Acqua Nera]], [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]]) and [[kalowe|Kalowe]] ([[fifitas-slip|Fifita's Slip]]). Standard rate at legitimate yards; double rate for work without Canal Commission paperwork.
