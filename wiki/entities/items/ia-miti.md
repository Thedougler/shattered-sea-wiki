# I'a Miti

*Wondrous item (consumable), common. No attunement.* `[HB]`

Dark strips of cured saltfish, slightly sticky with dried marinade, smelling of sweet brine and pepper heat — looks unremarkable, tastes better than it has any right to.

**Cost:** 2 cp per strip. Available in bundles of 5 for 8 cp at [[fare-ia|Fare Ia]].

## Properties

**On consumption:** Regain **1d4 hit points.**

**Delayed Effect — Grung Gut:** One hour after eating, the consumer makes a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw.**

- **Success:** No effect.
- **Failure:** Rapid and non-negotiable evacuation. The consumer is **incapacitated for 1 minute**, then has **disadvantage on Stealth checks for 1 hour.**
- **Immunity:** A creature that fails this save once permanently adapts. They are immune to this effect forever.

> [!dm]
> Félix knows about the effect and does not warn first-time buyers. If asked directly, he confirms it with warmth and zero apology. Customers who fail the save and return get a small nod of recognition — he considers them properly initiated.

## Description

[[felix-aho|Félix Aho]] developed the recipe over four years of selling to dock workers who needed cheap food that kept. The cure is a Grung-method brine of seawater, sweet fruit reduction, and a spice compound standard in [[karath|Karath]] cooking.

Dock workers who have made it through the adjustment wear it as a minor point of pride.
