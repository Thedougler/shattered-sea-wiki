# Musket

*Martial ranged weapon · 500 gp · 10 lb*

## Properties

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Damage | 1d12 piercing |
| Range | 40 / 120 ft |
| Properties | Ammunition, Loading, Two-Handed |
| Ammo cost | 1 gp per 20 shots (powder and ball) |

**Loading.** You can fire once per attack action. Extra Attack does not grant additional shots without additional class features.

**[HB] Misfire.** On a natural 1 to hit, the weapon misfires and cannot be fired again until cleared (an action).

**[HB] Loud.** The report carries up to 300 feet. Any Stealth advantage from hiding before attacking is lost, even on a miss.

**[HB] Powder and Ball.** If fully submerged, misfires automatically on the next firing attempt. In steady rain, make a DC 14 Dexterity check before each shot — failure is a misfire.

## Niche

**Best at:** peak burst damage. 1d12 is the highest single-shot damage of any ranged weapon — above heavy crossbow (1d10) and longbow (1d8).

**Worst at:** range and reliability. At 40/120 it has shorter effective range than every other two-handed ranged weapon. Loading prevents extra attacks. Misfire, Loud, and Powder-and-Ball are costs bows never pay.
