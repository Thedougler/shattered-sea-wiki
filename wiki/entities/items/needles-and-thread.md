# Needles and Thread

Sewing needles in both sail weight (thick, curved, for heavy canvas) and clothing weight (fine, straight, for lighter work), sold with appropriate thread. Available at general goods stores. See also [[needle-and-sail-twine|Needle and Sail Twine]] for dedicated sailmaker's sets.
