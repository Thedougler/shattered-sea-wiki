# Passage Network Introduction

A formal introduction into [[the-passage|The Passage's]] Kin-network for Dry (non-[[rattkin|Rattkin]]) who need operational access — contacts, transit routes, and standing. Available from [[marrow|Marrow]] at [[marrows|Marrow's]] in the [[warren|Warren]], but only at his discretion. 15 gp minimum. He has turned away people with good introductions before and offered no explanation.
