# Pearl of Souls — DM Notes

**Public profile:** [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]]

One of the **Seven Pearls of Umberlee** — functional instruments of divine authority, not symbolic. Each Pearl governs a different aspect of her domain; the other six are not established. This one passively and continuously collects and holds the souls of those who drown within its proximity.

## Current Location

*Red Lady* wreck site — eastern edge of the Shelfworks, where the shelf descends toward the trench floor. The wreck sits below the planar boundary: Umberlee's authority terminates above it, which is why she cannot retrieve her own Pearl. Still functioning, still collecting — holds all five crews from Fisk's fleet plus casualties from the Leviathan's incursion.

> [!dm]
> What it does when recovered — and to whom the souls are delivered — is worth deciding before the party reaches the wreck site. It is not a neutral object.

## History

**Before the theft:** Held at a Waveservant shrine (location unspecified). Passive collection instrument for the recently drowned in nearby waters.

**The theft:** Stolen by [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]], who didn't know what it was. Shrine Waveservants know this. Waveservants elsewhere know a Pearl was stolen — none will confirm details without significant reason.

**Umberlee's response:** Sent the storm that sank the *Red Lady* in rage, before accounting for Maw waters. The storm took all five fleet ships simultaneously. The Pearl — still aboard — collected all five crews as they drowned. The *Red Lady* sank below the planar boundary before she could retrieve it. Souls and Pearl both lost to the Maw.

To prevent the captains' souls from being consumed, she pressed them into Fisk — the nearest survivor. The five captains are in his mind; the five crews remain in the Pearl. She made her own situation worse.

## The Maw Entanglement

The Pearl's soul-collection signal crosses the planar boundary and is detectable from the Elemental Plane of Water. The Leviathan's incursion was not random — it followed the signal. Every subsequent entity has followed the same beacon. [[auralis|Auralis]] woke specifically because the signal crossing the boundary triggered the incursion.

- **Recovered:** signal stops; fissure reverts to passive geological anomaly. Most consequential single action available in Season 1.
- **Left in place:** continues broadcasting; next fissure event draws something larger.

## The Aldric Risk

The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] believes [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]] could use the Pearl to expand his Hierarch abilities beyond his own bloodline. The artifact's ability to gather and hold drowned souls could let him stabilise himself with external souls, create non-bloodline anchors, or convert mass drowning into a phylactery resource.

This is why the Concordat moved through [[chain-council|the Chain Council]] to have Fisk's Fleet steal the Pearl. The stated rationale was protection from Umberlee's wrath. The hidden Tessarine rationale was containment: remove the Pearl from the shrine before Aldric could take it, then divert it into Concordat custody.
