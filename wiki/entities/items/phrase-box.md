# Phrase Box

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Wondrous Item (Clockwork) |
| **Rarity** | Common |
| **Attunement** | No |
| **Cost** | 8 gp |

---

A flat brass-and-darkwood case, roughly the size of a ledger, fitted across its face with rows of small buttons. Each button is stamped with a tiny symbol and a Common glyph. Press one and the box produces a clear, slightly tinny tone — not quite a voice, not quite a bell — that resolves unmistakably into a word or short phrase any sailor would recognize.

The mechanism is a dense coil of tuned reeds and a small bellows driven by a winding key on the side. No magic. It runs until the spring runs down (about four hours of active use) and rewinds in thirty seconds.

Most Phrase Boxes are built to the **Maritime Standard** layout — a format from Midchain gnome workshops that became the default because the alternative was every box being different.

## Mechanics

- **Range:** Audible to 60 ft. in calm conditions; 30 ft. on a working deck; difficult in high winds or combat.
- **Clarity:** Any creature understanding Common and able to hear understands the tone without a check.
- **Rewinding:** After ~4 hours of active use, requires a bonus action to rewind.
- **Saltwater:** After full immersion, requires 1 hour of drying before functioning reliably.

## Notes

The Phrase Box does not replace language — it augments the absence of it. Experienced users develop a rhythm with the buttons fast enough to hold a genuine conversation at the pace of slow speech, provided the subject stays within the layout's vocabulary.

Phrase Boxes are common across [[midchain|Midchain]] free ports and [[central-strait|Central Strait]] docks wherever Kenku work port courier and runner roles. Most chandleries in [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and [[calveno|Calveno]] stock them alongside rope and lamp oil.
