# Pneumatic Grapple

A brass and leather wrist brace with a short barrel along the forearm and a pressure reservoir underneath — the hook fires with a sharp hiss and the braided steel line pays out fast enough that you hear it before you see it land.

**Price:** 600 gp. Available from [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] via [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]].

## Properties

**[HB]**
- Fires a grappling hook up to **60 feet** as a bonus action.
- Line retracts mechanically, pulling the user toward a fixed point (STR 18 for the pull).
- **Three shots** before requiring 10 minutes to repressurize from the included hand pump.
- Hook and 60 feet of braided steel line are standard components — replacements cost 15 gp.
