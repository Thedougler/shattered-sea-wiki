# Potion of Water Breathing

A stoppered glass vial, the liquid inside a murky blue-green with something suspended in it that drifts when the vial is tilted. Smells faintly of brine and cold depth.

**Properties [RAW]:** You can breathe underwater for **1 hour** after drinking this potion. The effect does not remove your ability to breathe air.

**Notes:** Standard maritime emergency kit on serious sea vessels. Mandatory on Crown patrol cutters operating near the Sunken Crown and the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]. Harder to source at smaller Midchain free ports.

One vial recovered from [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]]'s locked chest aboard the [[hcs-surety|HCS Surety]] during the ship exploration after the Saltwright boarding.
