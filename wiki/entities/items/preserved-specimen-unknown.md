# Preserved Specimen, Unknown

A glass jar, approximately one foot tall, sealed with wax and wire. The brine inside is clear enough to see the contents without distortion.

## Properties

Not magical, as far as any divination has confirmed. The specimen inside does not match any documented oceanic creature: it has the jaw structure of a deep-water anglerfish, the lateral fin arrangement of a Midchain reef species, and a bioluminescent stripe along the dorsal surface that no known fish of either type possesses. Whether it is still alive has not been established to anyone's satisfaction.

## History

[[cabinet-of-morsani|The Cabinet of Morsani]] acquired it from a salvager who found it aboard a vessel with no other cargo, already packaged this way. The salvager sold it quickly.

**For sale** at [[cabinet-of-morsani|The Cabinet of Morsani]], Calveno. Morsani will accept offers below the listed price if the buyer seems sufficiently curious about it.
