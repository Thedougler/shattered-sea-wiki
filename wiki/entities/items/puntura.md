# Puntura

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Weapon (shortsword) |
| **Rarity** | Uncommon |
| **Attunement** | No |

The blade is the forward third of a whip shark's secondary barb — fourteen inches of calcified spine ground to a point and set into an ironwood grip bound with tarred cord. The barb has yellowed to old bone. Along the spine, in shallow relief, are the natural ridges of the barb's structure. They look like decoration. They are not.

The name is [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina's]]. Calveno dialect: *puntura* — a sting, a puncture wound, the moment of contact.

## Properties

**[HB]** +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

**[HB] Venom Trace (1/day).** As a bonus action, draw on the barb's residual venom. Until the start of your next turn, the first creature hit with this weapon takes an additional **1d4 poison damage** and must succeed on a **DC 13 Constitution saving throw** or be **Poisoned** until the end of its next turn.

> [!mechanic]
> DC 15 Persuasion: bring the commission down to 120 gp — Catarina accepts the fraction without comment; she was keeping the grinding byproduct regardless. Offer to let her examine the remaining whip shark eggs before the party sells them: 100 gp, eggs returned undamaged within two hours.

## Commission

**150 gp** (party provides the barb; Catarina charges labor only). 3–5 days. She waives the material premium because the grinding byproduct is worth 20–30 gp in venom-precursor compounds, and she keeps it.
