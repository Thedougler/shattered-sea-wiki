# Spark Pistol

*[PROTOTYPE]*

**[HB] Price: 2,200 gp** | Available at [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]] by referral

A flintlock-sized brass pistol with no powder pan and a barrel that ends in a crystal emitter rather than a muzzle. The grip is wrapped in insulating leather and the whole thing smells faintly of ozone, more so after it's been fired.

## Properties

- Range 30/90 feet. 2d8 lightning damage; DC 14 Dexterity save for half.
- No powder, no ball. No misfire in wet weather.
- Recharges on a short rest.
- Barrel runs hot after firing — 1d4 fire damage on contact for 1 minute after use.
- **Prototype, one of a kind.** [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] will not make a second one until she decides what went wrong with the first.
