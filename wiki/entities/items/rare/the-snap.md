# The Snap

A slim brass bracer, fitted and hinged, housing a compressed lattice of Antheri fabric folded into careful origami tiers. In its resting state it reads as a finely made armguard — cool to the touch, faintly iridescent where the Antheri cloth is visible through the brass housing. When activated, the clockwork mechanism fires: the lattice unfurls in a single sharp motion, snapping open into a forearm-mounted buckler. A brief electrical charge stiffens the fabric rigid.

The whole sequence takes less than a second. The sound is a dry *crack*, like a sail catching wind.

## Properties

**[HB] Bracer Form.** At rest, the Snap is a slim bracer worn on the forearm. It occupies no hand.

**[HB] Snap Form.** As a **bonus action**, the wearer triggers the clockwork mechanism, unfurling the bracer into a forearm-mounted buckler. While deployed: **+2 AC**, regardless of what the wearer's hands are doing. As a **bonus action**, deactivate to collapse back into bracer form.

**[HB] Hands Free.** Does not occupy a hand in either form. The wearer may wield weapons two-handed, cast spells with somatic components, hold objects, or perform any action requiring free hands while the shield is deployed.

> [!dm]
> Intended for [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]. The practical effect: he can cast somatic spells, use the bodhran, or hold Clyde's Bestiary while still benefiting from shield AC. The bonus action cost for deployment means he can't both activate it and cast a leveled spell in the same turn — that tension is intentional.

## History

Built by [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] using salvaged Antheri fabric from the Shelfworks goldrush. The Antheri fabric compresses more tightly than any woven material has a right to — the deployed buckler is large enough to function as a real shield while the collapsed bracer is small enough for a wrist. The size calibration is notable: it was built for a small frame. Da'Virelli does not comment on this.

**Current location:** Not yet placed. DM reward — intended for [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], achievement TBD.
