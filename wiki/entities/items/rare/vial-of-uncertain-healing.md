# Vial of Uncertain Healing

A small vial of healing potion with the correct ruby-red colour and viscosity for a Greater Healing Potion. Lavinia prices it at asking because she cannot confirm the grade without testing it. She does not test her own stock. Available at [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] in [[calveno|Calveno]]. **Price: ask** (grey market).

[[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]]'s comment: *"Correct colour, correct consistency. Probably Greater. I say probably because I don't drink my own stock."*

**Properties [RAW — probable]:** On consumption, restores 4d4 + 4 HP (Greater Healing Potion). DM may substitute a standard Healing Potion (2d4 + 2) or a cursed variant — the discount is the risk.
