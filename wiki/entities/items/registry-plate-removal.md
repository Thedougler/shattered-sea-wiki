# Registry Plate Removal

Physical removal and disposal of a vessel's iron or brass registry plate, with a blank replacement plate supplied at cost. Available at discreet boatyards such as [[acqua-nera|Acqua Nera]] in [[calveno|Calveno]] and [[fifitas-slip|Fifita's Slip]] in [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. No record of the previous plate's markings is retained on site.
