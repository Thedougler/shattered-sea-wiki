# Rigging Block

A grooved iron pulley mounted in an oak shell with iron straps for attachment. Single blocks redirect rope loads; combined in pairs they form a [[block-and-tackle|block-and-tackle]] system that multiplies hauling force. 1 gp 5 sp at dry-dock ironmongers.
