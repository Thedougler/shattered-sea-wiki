# Saltseal

A thick white alchemical paste that bonds to wood and canvas fibres, curing to a flexible water-resistant film in 30 minutes. Holds against salt air and sustained rain for one full season before reapplication is needed. Sold exclusively by Studio Orsini in [[calveno|Calveno]].

> [!mechanic]
> Applied to up to 10 sq ft of wood or canvas (Action). Treated surface gains resistance to water damage for 3 months. Cures in 30 minutes.
