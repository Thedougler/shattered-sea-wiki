# Salvaged Plate Greaves

Heavy steel leg plates recovered from a wreck in the western approaches. Functional, salt-stained, no other pieces of the set. Good steel — better than the price suggests. Available at [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] in [[calveno|Calveno]]. **75 gp.**

[[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]]'s comment: *"Pulled from a wreck. Good steel. No matching set."*

**Properties:** Non-magical. Provides standard plate greave protection as part of a full plate set. No AC bonus when worn alone. The salt-work on the buckles is Aldric smithing — unusual for wreck salvage.
