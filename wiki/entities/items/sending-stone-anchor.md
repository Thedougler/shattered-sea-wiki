# Sending Stone Anchor

*Ship upgrade — communication*

**[HB] Price: 1,000 gp** | Available at [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]]

Two smooth grey stones set into a brass housing bolted to the ship's helm console — one fixed, one paired to its counterpart elsewhere. Warm to the touch and faintly humming when a message has been sent and not yet received.

## Properties

- Cast *Sending* once per day from the ship's helm.
- Allows the Captain or Navigator to issue bastion orders remotely without a courier.
