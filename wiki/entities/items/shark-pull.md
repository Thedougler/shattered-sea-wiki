# Shark Pull

Overproof [[rum|rum]] and fresh hunter shark blood from the Kalowe gap, nothing else. Not on any board at the [[the-flat-water|Flat Water]]. Only available when the dock crew has cleaned a shark recently — [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti]] knows when that is.

**Price:** 2 sp.

The [[five-blades|Five Blades]] order it when a contract has just closed. The smell is distinctive.
