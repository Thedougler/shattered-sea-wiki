# Spyglass

A brass draw-tube telescope, single extension, for shipboard and shore observation. Allows identification of flags, hull markings, and crew on vessels that would otherwise be unidentifiable.

**Price:** 1,100 gp — a serious purchase. Typically one in stock at well-provisioned chandleries.

**Properties [RAW]:** Objects viewed through a spyglass are magnified to twice their apparent size.
