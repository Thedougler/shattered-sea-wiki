# Surgical Consultation

A professional assessment of a serious injury: examination, prognosis, and a written supply list for a qualified surgeon to operate.

**Price:** 2 gp. The apothecary assesses and supplies; she does not operate. Recommended before any significant surgical procedure at sea.
