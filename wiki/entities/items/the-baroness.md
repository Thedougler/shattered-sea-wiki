# The Baroness

*Rapier · Delmar Fisk's*

A well-traveled rapier carried at [[delmar-fisk|Delmar's]] hip. Named. The weapon he reaches for first when talking stops working — ahead of the musket, behind the words.

## Properties

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Damage | 1d8 piercing |
| Properties | Finesse |

**Finesse.** Use either Strength or Dexterity modifier for attack and damage rolls.

## History

When negotiations closed, the Baroness came out first, with little wasted motion. Both she and [[the-duchess|the Duchess]] were on his back when the *Red Lady* went down. Both survived.
