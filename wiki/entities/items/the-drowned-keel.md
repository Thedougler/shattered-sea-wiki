# The Drowned Keel

An Antheri keel modification in dark iridescent alloy running the full length of the hull. Indistinguishable from standard construction until the ship submerges, at which point the hull planking turns semi-translucent and the keel glows a faint deep blue.

**[HB; Antheri salvage] Price: 25,000 gp** | Available at [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]] (late campaign)

## Properties

- Ship can submerge to 60 feet.
- Operates submerged for up to 2 hours per day.
- Crew do not need air during submersion.
- Hull becomes semi-translucent at depth.
