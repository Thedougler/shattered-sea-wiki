# Tide Reader

*Ship upgrade — navigation*

**[HB] Price: 600 gp** | Available at [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]]

A hull-mounted brass instrument with a submerged sensor vane and a dial face at the navigation station. The needle reads current direction and speed continuously; a secondary wheel tracks predicted tidal state for ports within two hundred miles.

## Properties

- Reads current speed and direction to 20-foot depth.
- Predicts tides for known ports within 200 miles.
- Grants advantage on navigation checks in reef-heavy waters.
