# Blade of the Lost Grip

A well-balanced shortsword with a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls. The grip wrap is unworn on one side and scorched on the other. Available at [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] in [[calveno|Calveno]]. **180 gp** (grey market; legitimate value ~500 gp).

[[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]]'s comment: *"Plus one. Hundred and eighty. A legitimate dealer would charge five hundred. I am not a legitimate dealer."*

**Properties [RAW]:** +1 weapon. Grants +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with it.
