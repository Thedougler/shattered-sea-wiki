# Driftlight Lantern

A brass bullseye lantern with a permanent pale blue flame that casts no heat and cannot be extinguished by wind. The flame functions as a standard Continual Flame. At the edge of its light — roughly 20 feet out — shapes occasionally appear that other people do not see. Lavinia does not guarantee they aren't there. Available at [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] in [[calveno|Calveno]]. **60 gp** (grey market).

[[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]]'s comment: *"Continual flame. Sometimes shows things at the edge of the light that may or may not be there."*

**Properties [RAW]:** Casts Continual Flame (bright light 20 ft, dim light additional 20 ft). Extinguishing and relighting with a command word. The edge-of-light visions are a quirk — DM discretion on their nature.
