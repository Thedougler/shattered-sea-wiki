# Fog Cannon

A squat deck-mounted brass cylinder with a wide flared mouth and a pressurized reservoir underneath — when triggered it exhales a dense white fog bank that spreads faster than weather has any right to.

**Price:** 1,500 gp. Available from [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] or through specialist traders via [[cats-curios|Cat's Curios]].

## Properties

**[HB; based on Fog Cloud]**

- Creates a **60-foot-radius** fog bank for **1 hour**.
- Recharges after 24 hours.
