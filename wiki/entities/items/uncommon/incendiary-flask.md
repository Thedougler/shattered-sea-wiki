# Incendiary Flask

A clay flask sealed with wax and wrapped in a cord grip. The contents smell faintly of tar and spirits even through the seal. Breaks on impact — the wax fuse groove never gets used.

## Properties

**[HB] Thrown (range 30 ft.).** As an action, hurl the flask at a point within 30 feet. It ignites on impact.

**[HB] Ignition.** Each creature within a **5-foot radius** of the impact point must succeed on a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or take **7 (2d6) fire damage** and catch fire. A burning creature takes **2 (1d4) fire damage** at the start of each of its turns. A creature, or an adjacent creature, can use an action to extinguish the flames.

## History

Standard-issue Dravosi Crown ordnance, carried as part of the [[dravosi-alchemist|Dravosi Alchemist]]'s bandolier. Produced from ship's tar, spirits of wine, and a binding compound — materials available in any working naval stores.

Lootable from the [[dravosi-alchemist|Dravosi Alchemist]]'s bandolier after the Saltwright boarding encounter.
