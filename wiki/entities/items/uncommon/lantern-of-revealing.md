# Lantern of Revealing

A hooded lantern that burns for 6 hours on a pint of oil. While lit, it sheds bright light in a 30-foot cone and dim light for an additional 30 feet. [[invisible|Invisible]] creatures and objects are visible while in the bright light.

**Properties [RAW]:** While burning, invisible creatures and objects within the bright light cone become visible. The hood can be closed to reduce light to dim in a 5-foot radius.
