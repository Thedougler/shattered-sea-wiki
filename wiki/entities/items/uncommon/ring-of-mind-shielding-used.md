# Ring of Mind Shielding (used)

A plain silver ring, slightly warm to the touch. Standard Ring of Mind Shielding properties. Three previous owners bought it from [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] and returned it; she does not explain why and pricing reflects the uncertainty. Lavinia suspects the ring currently houses the soul of one of the previous owners — a soul that is aware and listening. She has not confirmed this because confirmation would cost her a sale. Available at [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] in [[calveno|Calveno]]. **200 gp** (grey market).

[[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]]'s comment: *"Previous three owners sold it back. Draw your own conclusions."*

**Properties [RAW]:** Requires attunement. Immune to magic that reads thoughts/alignment/lies. Shielded from divination spells. On death, your soul may enter the ring if you choose — the soul inside can communicate with the wearer telepathically.

> [!dm]
> The ring contains the soul of its most recent returned owner — a fence named Aldric Vos who died with debts and information. He is lucid, bitter, and has been listening to Lavinia's shop conversations for six months. He will bargain.
