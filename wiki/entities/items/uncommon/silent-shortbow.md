# Silent Shortbow

**Weapon (shortbow)** · *Uncommon, no attunement*

Compact recurve design with a folding mechanism, made of yew. The string release is completely silent.

- **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls.
- **Silent firing:** no sound when drawn or fired. Does not reveal the shooter's position when firing from hidden (unless the target can see the arrow).

Acquired from [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] for 50gp. The shopkeeper said it came from someone passing through quickly trying to make extra coin.
