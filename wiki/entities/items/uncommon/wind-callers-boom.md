# Wind Caller's Boom

*Ship upgrade — navigation*

**[HB] Price: 1,200 gp** | Available through [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] or faction contacts.

A mainmast boom fitted with carved resonance channels along its length. When activated by the captain's command word, the channels hum at a pitch just below hearing and the sails fill regardless of what the wind is actually doing.

## Properties

- Once per day: favorable wind for 4 hours; **+30 miles/day** regardless of conditions.
- Does not function in a storm or the [[doldrums|Doldrums]].
