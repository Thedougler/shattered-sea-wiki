# Wardstone Figurehead

*Ship upgrade — warding*

**[HB] Price: 12,000 gp** | Available through [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]], senior faction reward, or significant expenditure.

A carved stone figurehead mounted at the prow — the stone is a pale grey-white that doesn't match any local quarry, with ward-marks cut into the underside that face the water rather than the viewer.

## Properties

- Ship has **advantage on saving throws against magical effects**.
- Hostile divination targeting the ship requires DC 17 spell save or fails.
