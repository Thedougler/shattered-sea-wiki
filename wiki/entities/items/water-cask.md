# Water Cask

A sealed stave-and-hoop barrel, 40-gallon capacity, for storing fresh water on extended passages. Filled at a cistern station before departure; properly sealed casks keep water fresh for weeks. Small casks (5 gal) also available.

**Price:** 8 sp for the cask; 1 sp per cistern fill. Available at ship's victuallers.
