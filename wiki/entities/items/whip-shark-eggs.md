# Whip Shark Eggs (×3, fertilized)

Three fertilized eggs recovered by [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] from the whip shark's body. Directed to [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] by a [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] shopkeeper.

Jean-Claude told [[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]] he fertilized the egg himself and that "the infant will have multiple extra muscles."

## Value — Sale to Marta Orsini

**75 gp each (225 gp for all three).**

Marta pays for alchemical utility, not rarity. Fertilized whip shark eggs are worth substantially more than unfertilized because the developing embryo concentrates venom precursors in the yolk sac — the same compounds Catarina DaVirelli extracts from the barb grinding. The amniotic fluid is a reagent for water-breathing compounds. The shell cartilage has applications in siege-hardening enchantments.

> [!dm]
> Marta examines the eggs in silence for two minutes. She will dismiss JC's claim about fertilizing them himself and the "multiple extra muscles" with a single flat look. She knows what fertilized whip shark eggs are — she has seen two in her career, both unfertilized, both from Drowned Maw expeditions. Three fertilized eggs from a specimen this far west is genuinely unusual. She will not say that. She will name a price.

> [!mechanic]
> **Base offer:** 75 gp per egg (225 gp total).
> **DC 14 Persuasion** (JC has disadvantage — Mortis): 85 gp each (255 gp).
> **DC 18 Persuasion** or offering her first pick of the three: 90 gp each (270 gp), plus she owes JC one compound at cost — a future favour worth 30-50 gp in alchemical product.
> **If JC mentions the specimen's size (60+ feet) or its anomalous range:** Marta's interest sharpens. She adds 10 gp per egg without negotiation and asks where the body is. The size data is worth more to her than the eggs — she will not explain why.
>
> JC's claim about "multiple extra muscles" earns a 5 gp penalty per egg. Marta does not appreciate being lied to. If JC corrects himself (Mortis makes sustained deception painful), she restores the price without comment.

## Transaction Purpose

JC intends to sell the eggs to fund the purchase of the [[corto-di-velo|Corto di Velo]] from [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] (30 gp, unidentified). At base offer, the sale leaves JC with 195 gp after the bow — substantial operating funds for a PC who arrived in Calveno with nothing.
