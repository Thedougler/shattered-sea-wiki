# Harbourmaster's Office

## Overview

The Harbourmaster's Office is the Crown administrative office every vessel deals with when entering [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]], covering ratings, impound processing, harbour fees, charts, and public commissions.

## Description

A long stone building at the base of the Dockfront — counter behind a partition, two clerks with ledgers. Every vessel entering Port Tidefall checks in here before clearing the inspection pier. Crown rating certificates, impound processing, and harbour fees all run through this office.

The exterior wall has a cork board, sun-warped and crowded with notices in various states of fading. Crown-sealed postings sit at the top. Below them, whatever else has been pinned by whoever thought it worth pinning.

## People & Operations

Harbour clerks operate the counter and ledgers. [[reginald-fenn|Reginald Fenn]] manages the chart counter — an elf, old enough to have surveyed some of these routes himself, working from Crown charts he once helped draft. The Crown keeps him because his accuracy is not improvable.

## Stock / Services

Official Dravosi surveys available over the counter. [[central-strait|Central Strait]] and [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] charts are kept in good supply; Midchain coverage is partial and requires asking specifically.
