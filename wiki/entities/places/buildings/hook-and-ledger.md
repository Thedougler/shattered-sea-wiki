# The Hook & Ledger

## Overview

The Hook & Ledger is a narrow Shelf tavern in [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]] where clerks, quartermasters, pursers, and captains drink close enough to the harbour paperwork to hear tomorrow's trouble early.

## Description

A narrow tavern halfway up the Shelf road, close enough to the [[harbourmasters-office|Harbourmaster's Office]] for clerks to reach it on a short break and far enough from Dockfront noise to conduct business in a normal voice.

A brass ship's hook hangs over the bar. Ledgers sit on shelves behind the bottles like holy texts. Candlelight catches in dusty green glass, and the room smells of lamp oil, wet wool, damp paper, and wine gone a little sharp in the cask. Nobody stops talking when a stranger enters, but the careful tables get quieter.

## People & Operations

**[[hector-podge|Hector Podge]]** — A heavyset human man in his fifties, grey and steady. Crown officers stop asking his name and start asking which table is free. Keeps the room respectable enough for uniforms and loose enough for side deals.

The regular crowd is the paper side of port traffic: pursers, quartermasters, freight factors, impound runners, petty officers, and shipmasters too mean to drink at home.

## Notable Areas

**Main Room** — Round tables packed closer than comfort allows, every one marked by old cup rings and older ink stains. Shipping news, berth changes, inspection gossip, and pricing rumours pass through here before they harden into official record.

**Card Alcove** — A curtained side nook with one heavy table and enough privacy for people who do not want privacy noticed. Freight factors and labour middlemen settle advances here after dark.

**Back Bar** — Upper shelves hold ledgers; lower shelves hold bottles.
