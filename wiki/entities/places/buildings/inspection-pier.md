# The Inspection Pier

## Overview

The Inspection Pier is the first stop for any vessel entering [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]], where incoming ships tie up for Crown rating, manifest checks, and fees before reaching the dock proper.

## At a Glance

| Controlled By | [[dravosi-crown|The Dravosi Crown]] |
| Public Access | Required processing point for all arriving vessels |
| Known For | Rating checks, manifest scrutiny, fees, and tide-exposed space below |

## Description

The pier runs out into the harbour mouth before the docks proper: wide planks, cleats along both sides, and a covered station at the far end where Crown clerks work through rating, manifests, and fees.

During inspection hours, the deck is all movement: sailors waiting with paperwork, petty officers looking irritated on principle, and clerks with ink-stained fingers checking cargo against what captains swear they meant to declare. The whole place has the practical, unpleasant importance of a door everyone has to pass through.

At high tide, the water sits just below the deck. At low tide, mud and barnacle-crusted pilings show beneath it.

## Notable Areas

**Deck** — Vessels tie up on either side while papers are checked, fees assessed, and questions asked with official patience.

**Below the Pier** — At low tide, enough exposed mud under the pier to walk below the deck, hunched among pilings with voices and footsteps overhead. At high tide, that space belongs to the harbour.
