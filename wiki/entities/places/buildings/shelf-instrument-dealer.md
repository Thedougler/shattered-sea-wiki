# The Shelf Instrument Dealer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Navigation instrument shop |
| **Location** | [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]], the Shelf |
| **Operator** | [[oswald-crench|Oswald Crench]] |

A narrow shopfront on the Shelf — one of the few places in the Crown Islands where a captain can buy a sextant or a proper spyglass without ordering from Calveno.

A glass-fronted case in the window holds brass instruments: sextants, compasses, and chronometers, including one with a cracked face and a reduced tag. Inside, rolled charts and printed almanacs sit on the back shelves, with a few second-hand pieces laid out on felt behind the counter.

## Operator

**[[oswald-crench|Oswald Crench]]** — A dwarf with the permanently squinted expression of someone who has spent decades looking at things through lenses. Has been calibrating instruments for Crown patrols since before most current officers were posted east. Does not offer small talk or discounts. Does not need to.
