## Overview

The Flat Water is on the second island, set back from the main causeway behind a low salt-bleached wall. Kalowe's primary eating house, drinking house, and lodging, named for the lagoon the building looks out over. Most crews working the Midchain pass through at least once. Most of the information worth having in Kalowe passes through it too, usually between the second and fourth drinks.

Low stone walls, a roof of salvaged timber and layered palm, a wide terrace facing the lagoon. The bar is a single plank of dark timber, set on two casks that have not moved in years. [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti]] works behind it. She does not rush.

## Proprietor

**[[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]]** — A tortle who works the bar from mid-morning until the last customer leaves. Shell turned slightly to the room, arms free. She does not volunteer information. She does not pretend not to have heard things. If the party is straightforwardly polite and spends money without causing a scene, she will occasionally say something useful, once, without preamble, and then not say it again.

> *"Two hammocks or the room?"*
> *"That ship's been in the primary berth eleven days. Arrived with twelve crew. I've counted nine on the causeways."* (volunteered once, not repeated)
