# The Mud Pits

| | |
|---|---|

| **Operator** | [[bala-tane|Bala Tane]] |
| **Entry Fee** | 5 sp per fighter; spectators free |

## Overview

The Mud Pits sit on Kalowe's third island, fifty feet back from the Grimaldi causeway in a natural limestone depression that catches lagoon silt when it rains and holds it until it is the right consistency. On fight nights, the torchlight carries across the water and the crowd noise reaches the Flat Water on the second island.

The fights are to unconsciousness — not to surrender, which is not a category [[bala-tane|Bala Tane]] recognises. Weapons up to a dagger are technically permitted. No one uses them. Death magic is prohibited. When a fighter is down and stays down, the fight is over.

## The Pit

Roughly oval, fifteen feet across. Mud thigh-deep at the lowest point. The limestone rim is wide enough to stand on and worn smooth where spectators lean. Torches on iron staves ring the outer edge. Betting slips move through Bala's runners before the match starts and are settled in coin on the rim when it is over.

## Proprietor

**[[bala-tane|Bala Tane]]** — An orc woman in her mid-forties who has run the Pits for fifteen years. She announces a match like it is a royal proclamation, then goes completely still for the duration of the fight. Weight classes are her decision; she makes the matchup she thinks the crowd wants to see. Her runners handle the betting slips and she handles everything else.

The Pits also serve as informal dispute resolution — two crews with a problem can settle it here with Bala as witness, the outcome binding under harbour custom.

## Entering a Fight

Pay 5 sp and present at the rim before Bala calls the match. She decides who you fight and how many. Not negotiable. Groups may fight together.

Prize money is drawn from the betting pool — 2:1 base on entry fee for a standard win; an upset against heavier odds pays accordingly.

## Current Hooks

- Bala is looking for something to put against the reigning champion — a retired orc sailor named Vort Kessen who has not lost in two years and has started visibly boring the crowd.
- The [[five-blades|Five Blades]] have reportedly been banned after an incident three weeks ago that Bala has declined to describe in detail. They watch from the rim most nights.
