# The Crown Islands

![[Crown-Islands.webp|Mountainous green Crown Islands ringed by bright reefs under clearing storm clouds]]

## Overview

Five large islands running east to west across the northern arc — broad-shouldered land with forested highlands, deep natural harbours, and pale stone fortworks placed by engineers with cannon range in mind. The Dravosi flag flies from every major anchorage. The southern coasts face the [[central-strait|Central Strait]], the northern coasts take open ocean swell, and the reef systems between islands — called the Greyteeth — have contradicted three generations of charts without apology. Local pilots are worth what they charge.

The Crown Islands are safer than the [[midchain|Midchain]] in roughly the way a locked strongroom is safer than an alley. Patrols answer distress signals. Ports keep records. Cargo moves under recognized law, which merchants value and sailors tolerate, because recognized law tends to arrive with fees attached and harbourmasters who remember what stamp you were supposed to have.

The patrols keep the big harbours clean, not the whole sea. Pirates still work the blind water between reefs, usually under captains who know exactly how long a distress signal takes to reach a fort.

## Geography

The Crown Islands form the northern arc of the Verdant Scatter. Their southern coasts face the [[central-strait|Central Strait]], their northern coasts take cold open ocean swell and upwelling. The islands are larger and more mountainous than most of the [[midchain|Midchain]], with forested highlands, deep-water approaches, productive fishing grounds, and fortified harbours.

The Greyteeth reefs between islands have contradicted three generations of charts. An adult blue dragon laired in the northern Greyteeth limestone uses the reef's reputation as cover for a second category of loss. Certain captains who know the northern passage fly specific colours and keep an unsealed chest on the aft deck. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] does not officially acknowledge this arrangement.

## People & Power

The Crown Islands are the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]]'s strongest regional foothold: forts, harbourmasters, inspection ledgers, patrol cutters, and trade houses with authority behind them. Local pilots still matter because control on shore does not make the Greyteeth honest.

> [!dm]
> The Greyteeth dragon arrangement: captains who fly the right colours and keep an unsealed chest on the aft deck survive routes that others lose to "navigational error." The Crown does not acknowledge it. An adult blue dragon (lawful) and a pragmatic Crown — it works.

## Trade & Resources

The cold upwelling off the northern Crown coast is the most productive fishing water in the Scatter: bluefin, yellowtail, and reef fish in volume. Fishing fleets out of Tidefall's South Harbour work year-round. The Greyteeth reef systems produce spiny lobster, sea urchin, and oyster beds in the shallower channels.

## Hazards

Reef and hunter sharks off the southern coasts. Giant crabs around wreck-strewn reefs. Giant boars in highland forest. Greyteeth navigation errors, and the unacknowledged blue dragon in the northern reefs.

## Current Hooks

- The Greyteeth dragon arrangement is officially unacknowledged — captains with the right protocol survive, others are listed as navigational error.
- Reports of the Glass Debt near convoy routes force Crown captains to consolidate escorts.
