# The Doldrums

![[The-Doldrums.webp|Calm glassy sea with scattered low islands under a hazy bright sky]]

## Overview

The Doldrums are a windless band of flat water running east-west below the [[midchain|Midchain]]'s southern edge. The surface goes mirror-still. Sails hang slack. The absence of wind is total, and sound crosses the flat water in ways that unsettle crews — voices from a quarter mile away arrive clearly, and the ship's own sounds seem louder than they should. The boundary is not marked on most charts. One watch you're in trade winds; the next, nothing. Frigate birds circling overhead in numbers are the standard warning. Jellyfish blooms collect in the still water, sometimes dense enough to foul a hull.

The band drifts 50 to 100 miles north or south by season. Experienced captains track its position and route around it through the [[midchain|Midchain]] back channels. Ships that don't know where it is sail into it and wait. Becalmed vessels have limited options: tow with boats, jettison weight, or sit. Most carry enough water to do this without disaster. Most.

The Doldrums do not need apex monsters to be dangerous. Still water gathers blooms, carrion, and anything that hunts by patience. Giant wasps and biting insects make any attempt to tow through mangrove shallows worse than the charts imply. A crew becalmed here learns quickly that silence is not safety.

> [!dm]
> **Pacing tool:** Drift intelligence is tradable, high-value information — knowing where the Doldrums are and when they'll move is worth money to merchant captains and smugglers alike. Start with inconvenience (route around, add days), escalate to predation only when crews fail to route around the zone.
> The black dragon: uses still water and flat calm as hunting cover. A becalmed ship cannot sail, cannot maneuver, and cannot outrun anything that surfaces alongside it. The dragon does not always attack. Sometimes it circles below and leaves. Pilots who have had this experience agree that the leaving is not reassuring.
> **Adjacent pressure:** The [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] is where drift pressure compounds — caught in the Doldrums while trying to clear the Galewall or reach the Maw is its own problem.

## Geography

The band drifts 50 to 100 miles north or south by season. The boundary is not marked on most charts. Frigate birds circling in numbers are the standard warning. The mangrove channels south of [[midchain|Midchain]]'s edge have their own apex predator — a black dragon that has used the still water and trapped quiet as hunting cover long enough that Midchain pilots treat the Doldrums' deeper southern channels as a distinct category of hazard.

## Routes & Access

Experienced captains track the Doldrums' seasonal position and route around it through the [[midchain|Midchain]] back channels. Becalmed vessels: tow with boats, jettison weight, or sit. Pirates using oared cutters can move where sail cannot — a low raider like the Knife's Wake is exactly the wrong shape to see moving through flat water at dusk.

## Hazards

Windless water, sound carrying too clearly, jellyfish blooms, water depletion, towing exposure, giant wasps, giant crocodiles, pirates moving by oar, and a black dragon using still water as hunting cover in the deeper southern channels.

- A low raider can use oared movement through flat water while sail-driven ships cannot choose distance.
