# Dreth

One of the two eastern islands of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], sitting close enough to [[orak|Orak]] that the channel between them reads more like flooded jungle than open sea. The coastline is low, the interior is dense rainforest, and the air smells of rainwater and wet limestone. There is work happening somewhere inland. You can hear it when the trees go quiet.

Dreth has less visible military presence than [[karath|Karath]]. It is still Grung territory.

## Routes & Access

The public approach is effectively none. The channel to [[orak|Orak]] looks slow and manageable from a distance, but the water and root systems favour ambush rather than travel.

A giant crocodile took a [[kalowe|Kalowe]] supply skiff in the Dreth-Orak channel — grabbed the hull at the port rail, held it, and dragged backward until the timbers folded. The pilot made the bank by leaving the boat behind. She came back to [[kalowe|Kalowe]] with two fewer fingers and a clear description of something that held still longer than anything that size should. The channel now has a reputation that most pilots respect.

## People & Power

The [[grung-clans|Grung clans]] hold Dreth with less visible military display than [[karath|Karath]], but no less practical control.

## Trade & Resources

No public trade is documented. Any productive work happens inland and out of sight.

## Fauna

The Dreth-Orak channel holds the island's most documented hazard. Giant crocodiles use the flat, shadowed, slow-moving water between the islands as ambush ground — still enough to read as safe, deep enough to conceal a body of that size until it commits.

Giant constrictor snakes use the flooded channel margins as cover — root shadow, low-hanging canopy, water that runs too shallow for a vessel but deep enough for a large body to move through without breaking the surface. The Grung path system around the eastern swamp avoids certain pools and clears others, which is the clearest evidence that the local population maps the snake territory without advertising the map.

Giant wasps nest high in the canopy on the dry eastern slope — high enough that landing parties at ground level hear the colony before they see it, which means the warning arrives after the approach is already committed.

## Hazards

Giant crocodiles, [[giant-frog|giant frogs]], giant constrictor snakes, giant wasps, shadowed channel water, and Grung boundary control.
