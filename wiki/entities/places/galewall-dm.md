# The Galewall — DM Notes

**Public profile:** [[galewall|The Galewall]]

## DM Notes

**Phoenix causality:** Ashwall pilot belief that [[arclight-phoenix|arclight-phoenix]] activity sustains the Galewall should be treated as likely true for campaign purposes. The permanent storm belt is not a natural weather feature — it is the accumulated output of something that hatches in stone and dies in lightning. The colonial academies have been trying to explain why it doesn't behave like weather for over a century.

**Crossing pressure:** Crossing attrition is normal and expected — even successful passage should cost resources, cargo integrity, crew confidence, or future leverage.

**Stormwarden ambiguity:** Intentionally unresolved — adult bronze dragon using its shapechange ability, another actor, or a patterned survivor myth. Ashwall pilot logs describe practical navigation instructions from a figure in a vessel too small for the conditions; the survival rate of ships that follow them is anomalously high. Keep outcomes consistent even when the explanation stays uncertain.

**Table use:** Every successful crossing costs something. Use the Galewall to create natural resupply pressure, condition the party to value good pilots, and establish that the sea has edges that push back.

**Recovery-lane piracy:** The [[pirate-admiral|pirate admiral]] stat block is the right template for a Velvet Noose encounter. A [[pirate-captain|pirate captain]] is enough for opportunistic recovery-lane boarding.
