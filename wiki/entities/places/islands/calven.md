# Calven

## Overview

Calven is the second island east of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]] — three days from [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]] on a fair wind, longer on anything else. It is the oldest continuously settled island in the northern arc, and has been a commercial centre for long enough that the city built on it is named as its diminutive: [[calveno|Calveno]], little Calven. The name is Tessarine in origin, which tells you something about who got there first and who wrote it down.

The Dravosi flag flies at the harbour. [[tessarine-concordat|The Tessarine Concordat]] runs the money. The island's original occupants — the [[rattkin|Rattkin]], settled deep beneath Le Paludi's oldest drainage channels — predate both, and have no particular interest in correcting the record.

## Geography

Calven is low — flat and marshy at the edges. The interior is a shallow plateau of reasonable farmland surrounded by tidal marshland on the northern and western shores. The southeast coast, where Calveno sits, is the most stable ground and the only place the stone goes reliably deep enough to build on.

The tidal flats on the north and west run wide at low water. They are not navigable to anything with a keel and not safe to cross on foot without knowing the channels. On very low tides, old timbers appear in the mud — the remnants of structures that predate the city, provenance unknown. ^[inferred]

## Fauna

The tidal flats host [[giant-crab|giant crabs]] in numbers. The harbour mouth and southern approaches hold [[hunter-shark|hunter sharks]]. Inland, [[giant-wolf-spider|giant wolf spiders]] occupy the neglected sections of the drainage network and old cisterns. ^[inferred]

## Hazards

Tidal flats, mud channels, old drainage works, giant crabs, hunter sharks, and giant wolf spiders make the low island more dangerous than its commercial face suggests.
