# Dath-Mar

> *A hard place kept for hard hours.*

The smallest of the five [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] islands. Its name preserves the local tabaxi sense of strengthened stone — a place of endurance rather than comfort. A few individuals live there year-round by choice. Most use it only when they need to.

[[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] hunters retreat here when a storm splits boats from home, when someone needs distance from the central settlement, or when a decision is too heavy to bring to the elders without sitting with it first. The island's value is not what it offers — it can hold a person above the water when the rest of the Blue Hole turns against them.

## Geography

A hard, exposed rock with leeward approaches, a sheltered cut, black stone, higher cracks, and tide-dependent access. Not comfortable. Not meant to be. The current reversals around the Blue Hole have made the approach less predictable — skiffs strand on the wrong side of the rock more often than they used to.

## The Cairns

Cairns mark the leeward approaches and the sheltered cut. They are maintained by whoever last used them. Leaving one worse than you found it is a serious breach — the next arrival may not have the luxury of doing without it.

## People & Power

Dath-Mar is held by [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] custom rather than occupation. The rule is maintenance: leave worse than you found, and the next arrival pays for it.

## Trade & Resources

No trade. Its resource is survival space: a place to wait out weather, separation, or a decision that cannot yet be brought home.

## Fauna

Giant lizards sun against the black stone after storms. Giant bats use the higher cracks when weather grounds them. Giant crabs work the tidal zone at the island's base — the cairn approaches pass through crab territory at low water. [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] hunters harvest selectively, managing the count without eliminating the population.

## Hazards

Exposure, current reversals, tide windows, lizards, bats, crabs, and failing predictability around the Blue Hole.
