# Harwick

## Overview

Harwick is the second-largest island in the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] and the military centre of Dravosi power in the Shattered Sea. Where [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]] controls the western entrance to the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] and Calveno handles commercial business, Harwick builds ships and stages fleets. The name is old Dravosi: *hær-wīc*, army place. It has not changed its purpose since.

Two days east of Calder's Tooth, Harwick sits near the middle of the northern arc — accessible from both ends of the Crown Island chain and close enough to open ocean to move the fleet quickly when it has to. The deep-water anchorage on the island's south face is the largest natural harbour in the Scatter that a Ship of the Line can enter, and the Crown has spent considerable money making sure that advantage is fortified rather than merely scenic.

## Geography

The island is broader and flatter than the limestone ridges of Calder's Tooth. The interior plateau drops slowly to marsh and tidal channels on the eastern side, and rises toward forested hills in the northwest. The southwestern harbour face is sheltered from the prevailing northeast winds by a curved headland the charts call the Bight. The bay inside it runs deep close to shore, which is why the Naval Yard is there.

The northern coast takes open ocean swell directly. It is uninhabited outside of a signal station and a few fishing hamlets. The eastern marshes drain through a network of channels into a shallow bay used by small craft and anyone who wants to avoid the Yard's official entry points.

## The Naval Yard

The Harwick Naval Yard occupies the entire western shore of the Bight — dry docks cut into the rock, covered slipways long enough to lay a three-deck hull, storehouses that smell of fresh timber, pitch, and cordage. The Yard produces every class the Crown Admiralty operates. The [[hcs-constancy|HCS *Constancy*]] and the [[hcs-ordinance|HCS *Ordinance*]] were both laid down here, on slipways that have turned out warships for six generations of Dravosi governors.

The Yard is Crown military property. A civilian can observe the outer walls and smell the tar; anything past the gate requires Admiralty permission, which is not freely given.

## The Admiralty

Harwick's Admiralty office sits on the hill above the Naval Yard, overlooking the anchorage. Patrol rotations, commission papers, privateer letters, quarterly chart updates — it all routes through here.

The Admiralty maintains a sealed key protocol for every warship in the fleet. Captains carry orders they are not permitted to open until specified conditions are met. The building is a fortress in the administrative sense — not much to look at from outside, extremely difficult to navigate inside without authorization.

## The Town

The civilian settlement clusters along the eastern edge of the Bight between the Yard wall and the market wharf. It exists because the Naval Yard needs labour, the fleet needs provisions, and sailors on liberty need somewhere to spend their pay. Chandlers, taverns, a ropemakers' district, ships' suppliers, and the usual run of lodging houses.

The naval rating pool that crews Crown ships on rotation lives mostly here, in barracks along the north edge of town. Harwick sailors are not the Scatter's most adventurous. They are, reliably, the Scatter's most sober when they need to be.

## Hazards

Military restriction, shark-filled harbour water, managed but dangerous forests, and Yard security all shape visitor movement.
