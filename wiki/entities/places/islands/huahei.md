# Huahei

A small, overgrown, marshy island in the [[midchain|Midchain]]. Notable primarily for what lives there — an unspeakably odd fey creature the locals call the Glutehorn. No governance, no trade, no good reason to stop unless you need the Glutehorn specifically.
