# Karath

## Overview

Karath looks like the other [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] from the water — low reef approaches, rainforest running close to the shore, no harbour, no smoke, no visible reason to stop. The distinction is what happens when you get close anyway. Outsiders who nose into the reef gaps are met with dart fire, no warning, no gap between the quiet beach and the response. Pilots who know these waters treat Karath specifically as the island you give extra room, not because the others are inviting but because Karath is the one with a reputation for not waiting to see what you were planning.

The [[midchain|Midchain]] coastal settlements have noticed the raids out of this direction are getting worse. The [[chain-council|Chain Council]] has received the complaints.

## Geography

Low reef approaches, rainforest close to shore, wet cuts between ridges, and no harbour, smoke, or visible invitation to land.

## Fauna

The interior makes the beach look welcoming. Giant spiders occupy the older canopy growth. Giant venomous snakes work the root tangles along the wet ridge cuts — they don't announce themselves. Giant toads occupy standing-water pools in the wet cuts between ridges. Outsiders who imagine the beach is the dangerous part of Karath have not thought through the rest of the island.

## Hazards

Dart fire, reef traps, giant spiders, giant venomous snakes, giant toads, wet ridge cuts, and unknown interior patrols.

## Current Hooks

- Raids from the direction of the Teeth are worsening; Karath is the island sailors most fear.
- The Chain Council has received complaints; whether that changes anything is unresolved.
