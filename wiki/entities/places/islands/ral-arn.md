# Ral-Arn

> *The island where memory is kept against salt and forgetting.*

The record island of the Sunken Crown — larger than [[dath-mar|Dath-Mar]] or [[meth-var|Meth-Var]], visited when a question must be answered by memory rather than appetite. The oldest family lines maintain carved marks here: tide accounts, storm signs, records of prior seasons. Ral-Arn does not replace oral history. It anchors it. A mark here is only useful if someone living can read the story around it.

## Fauna

Giant lizards bask across the stone paths — the flat, exposed rock holds heat well, and visitors to the record panels routinely wait for an animal to clear a section before working it. Giant wolf spiders have taken the abandoned shelters — any shelter that has gone unvisited for more than a season should be checked before use, and those unvisited for years are checked with a pole. Raven swarms gather wherever offerings or storm-killed fish have been left too long, and they have learned that people arriving on the island correlates with food.
