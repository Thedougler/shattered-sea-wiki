# Umberlee's Shrine — Sunken Crown

## Overview

[[umberlee|Umberlee's]] shrine on [[vel-orn|Vel-Orn]] is cut into black stone in the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]], where five small islands ring the Blue Hole. This is not a bright harbour shrine collecting routine tribute from merchants. It is older, quieter, and pointed more toward the drowned than the living.

The shrine entrance is a mouth in the cliff face, worn smooth by salt spray and years of tide. Kelp wraps the pillars. Candles burn inside where wind should have killed them. The shrine smells of brine, rendered tallow, wet stone, and something older underneath. Sound behaves strangely in the entrance. Footsteps soften.

## Function

The shrine marks debts, keeps drowned names, and preserves the older [[umberlee|Umberlee]] worship of the Blue Hole. Inside, a ledger chamber holds names, tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and bits of ship or sailor preserved as witness. Everything here has weight. Nothing feels decorative.

## Access

Not on colonial charts. Requires prior knowledge, a guide, or difficult navigation. Vel-Orn's approach is sheer, reef-bound, and unfriendly to casual landings.

> [!dm]
> The deepest chamber formerly held the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] over a tidal pool built to its requirements. The Pearl was stolen by [[delmar-fisk|Fisk's fleet]] before the campaign — the event that triggered the fleet's destruction. The shrine's protective function over the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] blessing is now failing.
>
> Stripes gets a beacon sense near this shrine. Delmar gets dread instead.

## Current Hooks

- The Pearl of Souls was held here before Fisk's crew stole it, triggering the storm that sank his fleet.
- [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] uses this shrine to petition Umberlee for terms — that function is compromised without the Pearl.
