# The Midchain

## Overview

The Midchain is the southern arc of the Shattered Sea's Verdant Scatter: dozens of low limestone and coral islands packed close, most a half-day's sail from the next. The treeline often appears before the land does. Dense rainforest runs to the water's edge on many coasts; elsewhere, pale limestone cliffs drop straight to reef. The water shifts from turquoise over the shallows to blue-black where the shelf falls away, and the line between them is abrupt.

Fresh water and shelter are rarely far away. Certainty is harder to find.

## Routes & Access

Charts for the Midchain contradict each other because the Midchain keeps changing the question. Local knowledge is worth more than paper in most of the inner channels. The same back channels that hide a ship also hide teeth. Deep blue holes are avoided unless a pilot knows them by name, because [[sawek|Sawek]] lairs look like useful shelter until something moves below the keel.

Prevailing winds turn more westerly near the Doldrums, creating back channels useful for westbound ships trying to avoid Strait inspection. Dravosi patrol cutters run intercept routes through the more predictable lanes, which is one reason experienced captains prefer the less predictable ones.

The Midchain is also where small piracy becomes geography. A cutter like [[knifes-wake|the Knife's Wake]] can disappear through channels a frigate captain refuses to enter.

## Hazards

Shifting reefs, uncertain channels, Sawek blue holes, giant crocodiles in mangrove water, constrictor snakes along root mats, Grung boundary violations, and piracy that turns local route knowledge into a weapon.
