# Osha's Slip

## Overview

A fishing village built on stilt-platforms over the southeastern mudflats of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]]. The villagers trap mud-crabs, spear river-eels, and maintain a rope-and-plank bridge network that shifts with wind and tide. There are no solid roads. Overland approach from Tidefall means crossing the mudflats, which is slow and unpleasant at any tide and impossible in armour at the wrong one.

The village is independent in the way small places far from the fort tend to be. The Crown's authority here is largely theoretical. [[the-passage|The Passage]] uses Osha's Slip as a favored night-landing site for small high-value cargo.

## Description

Homes and work decks raised over the mud, connected by rope-and-plank bridges that shift with wind and tide. Outer boardwalks extend into the reed-bed margins and tidal pools; children are kept off them after the light goes.

## Fauna

Giant crabs are the settlement's primary harvest and its baseline hazard. Giant frogs occupy the reed-bed margins, hunting at dusk. Giant constrictor snakes work the eel channels — villagers watch for crab traps dragged sideways, reed beds flattened in a single smooth line, and water moving against the tide.

## Current Hooks
