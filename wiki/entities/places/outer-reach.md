# The Outer Reach

The under-governed zone east of [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]]. No colonial presence, no reliable resupply, few landmarks a western pilot would trust. Ships go there for salvage, pilgrimage, research, desperation, and profit. The ones that have trouble tend to become warnings.

## Hazards

Route intelligence and reliable pilots are worth as much as cargo. Damaged-vessel vulnerability is the recurring threat pattern — no colonial rescue, no nearby port. Piracy this far east is rare but highly intentional: crews that operate here have made a deliberate choice to be this remote.

The Doldrums compound here: getting caught in drift pressure east of the Maw is particularly dangerous.

Known threats include [[dragon-turtle|dragon turtles]], [[ancient-sea-serpent|ancient sea serpents]], [[young-sea-serpent|young sea serpents]], killer whales, [[the-velvet-noose|the Velvet Noose]], and [[pirate-captain|opportunistic pirates]] using the remoteness as cover.
