# The Shattered Sea

![[The-Shattered-Sea.webp|Aerial view of the Shattered Sea island chain with turquoise reefs and scattered green islands]]

## Overview

The Shattered Sea is a warm archipelago of roughly sixty thousand square miles: reef, deep channel, cliff port, free harbour, old ruin, colonial ledger, storm belt, and routes that punish anyone who mistakes a chart for the truth.

Two island arcs curve east to west across a wide channel of open water called the Central Strait. The northern arc is the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]] — five large, fortified, [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]]-held islands where authority has faces and ledgers. The southern arc is the [[midchain|Midchain]] — dozens of low limestone and coral islands, free ports, reef channels, and water that can feed a careful crew and swallow a careless one.

Between them: the Strait, the trade route, the inspection chokepoint. West of the western entrance: the [[galewall|Galewall]], the permanent storm belt and the only route to the colonial homelands. East of the eastern exit: the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] and the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] — the planar trench, the last frontier, the place that ends expeditions.

Nobody owns the Shattered Sea. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] claims the northern arc. The [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] controls the money. [[umberlee|Umberlee]] controls the water tribute. Pilots, free ports, smugglers, and local bosses control the rest. Authority here is local, layered, and negotiated at each port.

## Power & Governance

**Dravosi Crown:** Controls the northern arc and the Strait inspection regime. Fleet, forts, harbourmasters, ledgers.

**Tessarine Concordat:** Controls the credit, insurance, and trade underwriting. No forts — just obligation.

**Umberlee and the Waveservants:** Water tribute is practical infrastructure. [[waveservants|Waveservants]] collect at every harbour. Skipping tribute is a choice captains make once.

**Local power:** Every free port has its own council, boss, or factor. Authority in the Midchain is neighbourhood by neighbourhood.

## Navigation

The Scatter Current runs east through the Central Strait and turns north at the eastern end. Eastbound ships get half a knot for free. Westbound ships earn every mile. Pilots earn their fee because every chart in the Scatter has been wrong at least once — reef lines shift, new shoals appear, and the Drowned Maw's compass drift increases the further east you go.
