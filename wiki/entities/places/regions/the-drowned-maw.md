# The Drowned Maw

![[The-Drowned-Maw.webp|Vast dark blue ocean sinkhole spiraling into deep water]]

## Overview

The Drowned Maw is a north-south trench running along the eastern edge of the [[verdant-scatter|Verdant Scatter]], 40 to 90 miles across. The approach is visible before any other landmark: the water changes colour from the blue-green of the Scatter shallows to something deeper and less defined, a blue with no bottom in it. The surface goes flat. Not calm — still, in a way that open ocean normally isn't. No current runs over it. Seabirds that follow ships across the Scatter turn back at the edge. No fish school over it. No sounding line has found the bottom.

Compasses drift within five miles and don't recover until you're through. Most captains navigate the crossing by stars and run it fast. Nobody anchors over the Maw. That particular piece of advice circulates without much explanation, and experienced sailors don't ask for one.

Ships cross routinely — it's the only route east to the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] — and most have no trouble. The ones that do have trouble come back quiet, which is its own kind of information.

> [!dm]
> **True nature:** Imperial-restricted trench. The [[antheri-ruins|Antheri]] built into the western wall, descending toward a planar fissure at the bottom where the mortal sea touches the [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]]. When the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] sank near the fissure, it turned passive elemental leakage into a directed attractor. The Leviathan forced through soon after.
>
> **Rook's access:** [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] accessed the Maw illegally twice. Kept no record of what he found. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] discovered this in his chart archive aboard the *Uncertainty*. The deliberate absence of records is its own data point — he didn't want anyone knowing.
>
> **Crissdalynn's task:** She was close to completing a mapping task for [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] when the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] went down. The mapping is incomplete. She knows it.

## The Vestra Water

Sailors have started using "Vestra water" for the bad stretch near the Maw where [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] lost the *Vestra* and survived alone. The public version is simple enough to travel: an ordinary fishing day, sudden heat through the hull, water boiling around the rails, then a strike from below hard enough that the ship stopped being a ship.

What hit the *Vestra* remains argued rather than proven. Perrin's signs match the old leviathan chapter in [[clydes-bestiary-of-oceanic-creatures|Clyde's Bestiary]], but they also match the practical warnings attached to [[ancient-dragon-turtle|ancient dragon turtles]]: scalding fog, heat before impact, and a shell-dark mass rising under the keel. Most captains do not care which name is correct. They care that someone survived long enough to say the water went hot before the ship died.

## The Shelfworks

On the Maw's western rim, forty feet down, there are ruins: old stone buildings, dark-alloy fittings, construction that exceeds anything currently being made. The [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] are the current gold rush of the Scatter, with salvage crews on site most weeks. The upper shelf is accessible. What lies below it is not.

> [!dm]
> The lower Shelfworks dive tier is currently abandoned — no crews will work below 60 feet after a heat event at 80 feet cooked a crew's bait line without warning. The Shelfworks gold rush is pulling safety architecture off a deeper Antheri containment system, and the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] signal is making the Maw more dangerous. One Maw crossing ship has logged a warm water patch and dead-fish smell on the Shelfworks approach; the pilot filed it as a vent anomaly.

## Who's Been Watching

The [[high-eyrie|Sentinels of the High Eyrie]] have kept aerial observation of the Maw's surface for two centuries. Their records are the most complete in the Scatter. The Waveservants dislike questions about what they think is down there, which tells you something, though not what.

## Fauna

The Maw's inhabitants are arranged by depth. The rim is [[whip-shark|whip shark]] water — fast, surface-oriented predators that follow ships slowing at the crossing and work the shelf edge where the floor drops away. The deeper shelf approach carries giant squid at night: the drop-off creates a thermal boundary that certain prey species follow upward in darkness, and the squids follow the prey.

Below the shelf, the scale stops being useful for practical planning. The Maw has room for large things: kraken, [[dragon-turtle|dragon turtles]], [[leviathan|Leviathan]]. What exists at what depth is not established in any record the pilot community considers reliable. What the records show is that some ships cross without incident, some come back with crews that don't discuss what they saw, and some don't come back.

## Hazards

Compass drift, star-only navigation, still water, no anchoring, whip sharks at the rim, giant squid at night near the drop-off, [[pirate-captain|pirate]] pressure around salvage traffic, kraken and [[dragon-turtle|dragon turtle]] rumours, and the leviathan thread.
