# Al Fondale

![[Al-Fondale.png|Al Fondale, a warm canal-side tavern in Le Paludi with amber light spilling into the narrow waterway]]

## Overview

Al Fondale sits on a side canal off Le Paludi's lower channel, in the part of [[calveno|Calveno]] where the buildings lean close enough that the upper floors almost touch. [[bice-riva|Bice]] and [[aldo-riva|Aldo Riva]] have run it for twenty-two years. Aldo cooks. Bice manages the room.

It is the kind of place that has been there long enough to stop advertising. People who eat here know about it. People who need to know about it find out.

> [!read-aloud]
> The canal narrows here until the stone walls on both sides are close enough to touch from a punt. Al Fondale is the building with the oil lamp burning amber in the ground-floor window and the smell of onions and rendered fat drifting through the half-open door. Inside: low ceiling, worn tables, someone moving in the kitchen at the back. The canal outside is quiet. In here it is warm.

## Description

The ground floor is one main room: eight tables, a low bar, and a kitchen behind a half-wall. The ceiling is original stone. The tables are worn to the specific smooth that comes from thirty years of elbows and plates.

Four rooms upstairs. Small, beds clean, linens changed. During [[il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]] festival week, the noise of Le Paludi comes straight through. Bice does not apologize for this. She does charge more.

> [!dm]
> The cellar is below the kitchen, accessed through a door behind the back shelf of dry goods. Against the north wall, behind a row of barrels set on a timber skid, is a stone panel with a catch mechanism. The passage beyond connects to the Warren's drainage network within forty feet.
>
> Nona established this entrance two years ago. Bice was told once, did not ask questions, and has not raised the subject since. She redirects anyone who lingers near the cellar door with a second portion and a topic change.
>
> **Unstable condition:** A Dravosi watch officer has taken a corner table three evenings running. He is eating, not asking questions, but he is watching the room — specifically who comes and goes from the back. If he builds a pattern of who uses the cellar stairs over two more days, it becomes an actionable report. The Rattle gets called. Nona hears. The entrance gets closed.
>
> **Link:** Perrin — Passage connection through Nona; Bice will confirm Perrin is known to Nona if Perrin identifies themselves.

## People & Operations

**[[bice-riva|Bice Riva]]** — Halfling, white-haired, barely clears the tabletops. Puts food in front of people before they finish deciding. Calls everyone *caro*. Does not ask where people are from or how long they intend to stay.

**[[aldo-riva|Aldo Riva]]** — Dwarf, white-bearded, runs the kitchen and comes out to talk about it. Has been in Le Paludi long enough to have watched the canal layout change twice.

## Access & Security

Street-side entrance and a narrow canal-side door Bice keeps latched from inside. If the window lamp is lit, the kitchen is running. If it is out, Bice has decided the day is done.

Bice will tell a Passage-credentialed visitor, quietly and once, that "the stairs are for residents."
