# The Cabinet of Morsani

![[Prospero-Morsani.webp|Prospero Morsani, gnome curio dealer and proprietor of the Cabinet of Morsani]]

## Overview

The Cabinet of Morsani is in the Velo Quarter, where [[calveno|Calveno]] stops enforcing its normal rules about closing hours. It sells magical and exotic items of uncertain provenance. [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] insists everything in the shop has a previous owner and he knows the story. The stories always end before the interesting part.

## Description

Lanterns on at all hours — outside, inside, several on chains over the canal-facing window. The shop is narrow but unexpectedly deep; the shelves go high and curve at the back where the original wall has been extended into a space that doesn't quite fit the building's exterior.

## People & Operations

**Proprietor: [[prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]]** — A gnome whose theatrical impulse has hardened into identity over a very long life, trailing rings on every finger. Greets customers by name if he has met them before and by an accurate physical description if he hasn't. Every item has a previous owner and he knows the story; the stories just never get to the point.

## Stock

*Stock turns over irregularly. Morsani does not produce a static catalogue.*

- 400 gp — [[compass-of-the-drowned|Compass of the Drowned]] — *"The previous owner was a navigator who — well. The compass works."*
- 250 gp — [[ringmakers-regret|Ringmaker's Regret]] — *"Two fifty, or a trade if what you're offering has a sufficiently interesting story."*
- 90 gp — [[preserved-specimen-unknown|Preserved specimen, unknown origin]] — *"I have documentation of the previous three owners. The first one is the interesting part."*
- 80 gp — [[salt-lantern|Salt Lantern]] — *"Came through Port Tidefall. The captain who brought it — there's a story there."*
- 180 gp — [[the-uncharted-coast|The Uncharted Coast]] — *"Two hundred miles of coastline on no published chart. The surveyor's notes are — the price is one eighty."*

## Access & Security

The lanterns outside indicate whether Morsani is in. When all lanterns are lit, walk in. The door is never locked while he is there.
