# Calveno — District Layout

Calveno has no docks. Movement is by flat-bottomed boat (faster, 2–5 cp per trip) or on foot along canal walkways and bridges (free, slower, more exposure). The city runs east–west along the main canal with the Arsenal in the southwest and the Velo Quarter on a separate island north of the Bridge.

---

## Districts

### Mercatura

The commercial spine. The main canal runs through it from the harbour mouth inward; buildings press close on both sides. The Tessarine factor house anchors the harbour end.

**Tone:** Loud, transactional, open. Numbers are said out loud. Nothing is confidential by default.

> [!read-aloud]
> The smell hits before the sound does — salt, rope, and something frying. Then the noise: boats on both sides of the main canal, voices calling across the water, the creak of rigging. At the harbour end, pale stone and no signage. The Tessarine factor house is the tallest building on the block.

|---|---|

### The Bridge

Three-tier stone crossing at the main canal's midpoint. Bottom tier: vendors, food, bodies. Middle tier: shuttered shops. Top tier: plain stone, narrow staircase that turns out of sight.

**Tone:** Transitional. Everyone passes through.

> [!read-aloud]
> The arch puts everything in shadow before you're under it. Bottom tier: vendors, food, bodies pressed close on both sides. A staircase leads to the upper levels and turns out of sight after three steps. Voices up there — words indistinct.

|---|---|

### Le Paludi

The oldest part of the city. Buildings shorter, foundations settled unevenly. The channels narrow until two boats cannot pass without touching; the water moves differently — slower, darker.

**Tone:** Close, unhurried. Correct business done correctly; incorrect business done quietly.

> [!read-aloud]
> The walkway narrows past the arch — paving stones older, less even, the canal walls close enough to touch on both sides. The water here barely moves. Sound carries differently; a conversation two doors down is fully audible, which means yours is too.

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| [[al-fondale|Al Fondale]] | Side-canal tavern. Good food, cheap wine, keeper asks nothing. Warren cellar access. |
| [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] | General goods. Ettore prices from memory; has not been wrong. |
| [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] | Off the mapped path. Marta does not ask what the compounds are for. |
| [[la-cenere|La Cenere]] | Dead-end canal spur past Casa Lupo. Lavinia Sordi; grey market magical items at steep discount. |
| [[warren|Warren]] entry | Beneath Le Paludi. Access via Tangle routes; surface-visible only to those who know. |

**From The Bridge:** Ten minutes on foot through the narrowing canal network. Five minutes by boat.

### The Arsenal

Walled shipyard in the southwest. A fortified canal separates it from the rest of the city. Non-House commissions: fourteen-month wait.

**Tone:** Closed. Entry requires a reason that holds up at the checkpoint.

> [!read-aloud]
> The pitch smell arrives before the walls do — sharp, fresh timber underneath it. The fortified canal on your left is still. The checkpoint arch appears before the wall proper. A guard with a ledger watches the water.

### Velo Quarter

A separate island attached by two low canal bridges. Lanterns at all hours. Music from more than one direction. Authority stops at the bridges.

**Tone:** Permissive. The crowd is watching performances, not deals.

> [!read-aloud]
> The light changes before you reach the far side of the bridge — warmer, from lanterns hung at different heights. Two songs from different directions, not competing. The buildings here are painted in colours the rest of Calveno doesn't permit. Whatever governs this island, it is not the Canal Commission.

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| [[cabinet-of-morsani|Cabinet of Morsani]] | Prospero's curio shop. Magical items; he knows the previous owner of everything. |
| [[giacinto|Giacinto]]'s theatrical booth | Wigs, prosthetics, ornamental beards, stage paint. |
| [[kats-curios|Cat's Curios]] | Referral only. No sign. Best artificer work in the Scatter. |
| Festival performance squares | Multiple stages active during the Beffa. |

**Getting here:** Two low canal bridges connect north from the Bridge district. Ten minutes from the Bridge on foot.

## Describing Movement

**Mercatura → Bridge:**
> The canal narrows slightly as you leave the factor house's shadow. Vendors thin out before the arch comes into view. You hear the bottom tier before you see it.

**Bridge → Le Paludi:**
> The walkway narrows past the arch. The paving stones get older, less even. The canal walls on either side are close enough to touch both at once, and the water between them barely moves.

**Le Paludi → Warren (if authorized):**
> Tangle contact leads you through a door behind a waterwheel housing, down iron stairs to a landing that doesn't appear on any city plan.

**Anywhere → Velo:**
> The low spans have no toll. The light changes before you reach the far side — warmer, from lanterns hung at different heights. The music resolves from noise into two separate songs playing at once.

**Anywhere → Arsenal approach:**
> The fortified canal on your left is the signal. The water there doesn't move for pleasure craft. The checkpoint arch appears before the wall proper; the guard has a ledger.
