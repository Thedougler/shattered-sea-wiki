# Carpenters' Slip — Stub

> [!dm]
> Stub — [[calveno|Calveno]] (Basin/La Vasca) yard run by [[savia-brentino|Savia Brentino]], referenced in [[calveno-sandbox-run-guide|Calveno Sandbox Run Guide]]. Does discreet hull work without Canal Commission filings. Cold approaches are refused and logged. Requires a Tangle/Warren warm introduction.

## Known Services

- [[hull-inspection|Hull inspection]] — Assessment report included with any job.
- [[keel-inspection|Keel inspection]] — Shallow-water or slipped. Written assessment.
- [[hull-caulking|Hull caulking]] — Sealing open or failing seams with oakum and tar.
- [[hull-plank-repair|Hull plank repair]] — Minor to moderate damage; full replank by negotiation.
- [[hull-repaint|Hull repaint]] — Full colour strip and repaint from the waterline up.
- [[mast-work|Mast work]] — Stepping, splicing, and repair; vessels up to two masts.
- [[rudder-repair|Rudder repair]] — Standard sizes in stock; priced by condition.
- [[emergency-repair|Emergency repair]] — Priority queue, double rate; Brentino decides what qualifies.
- [[nameboard-replacement|Nameboard replacement]] — New carved and fitted board. Legitimate or plain at request.
- [[timber-and-fittings|Timber and fittings]] — Spare planks, bolts, canvas, rope at chandlery rate.
