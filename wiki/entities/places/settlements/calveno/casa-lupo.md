# Casa Lupo

![[Ettore-Lupo.webp|Ettore Lupo, proprietor of Casa Lupo]]

## Overview

Casa Lupo is a general goods shop in the lower canal zone of Le Paludi, the oldest district in [[calveno|Calveno]]. It sells what people actually need rather than what ships need.

## Description

Low ceiling, oil lamps on iron hooks, floorboards that have been settling unevenly for a century and have reached a comfortable arrangement with gravity. The shelves go to the ceiling on three walls. The smell is candle wax, dried herbs, and something faintly mineral that was probably the previous shop's business.

## People & Operations

**Proprietor: [[ettore-lupo|Ettore Lupo]]** — Seventy-something, moves with the energy conservation of a man who has decided he has already moved enough. Prices everything from memory and has not been wrong. Will tell you clearly if he does not have something. Will not tell you where to get it unless he has formed an opinion of you.

## Stock

Lanterns, lamp oil, candles, rope, canvas and cloth, needles and thread, cooking tools, dried herbs and spice, basic medicines, writing supplies, locks and keys, tool sundries. Ask for what you need; stock changes.

## Notable

- Ettore keeps a sealed account of who in Le Paludi bought what and when, going back forty years. He has never been asked about it and does not volunteer its existence.
- Jean-Claude visited to ask about shortbow; Ettore redirected him to [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] for the whip shark egg inquiry.
