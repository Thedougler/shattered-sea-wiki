## Places in Calveno

### Taverns & Inns

- [[ponte-bassa|The Ponte Bassa]] — canal-level tavern at the main bridge crossing; cheap food, basic beds, Oleandro remembers every boat.
- [[al-fondale|Al Fondale]] — Le Paludi side-canal tavern; good food, cheap wine, four rooms, keeper asks no questions.

### Shops & Services

- [[moretti-and-sons|Moretti & Sons Chandlery]] — main ship outfitter on the Mercatura; rope, provisions, canvas, fittings.
- [[carpenters-slip|Carpenters' Slip]] — small independent boatyard near the Arsenal; no queue, Savia Brentino decides urgency.
- [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] — Le Paludi general goods; Ettore prices from memory, no price list.
- [[studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]] — alchemist on a Le Paludi side canal; Marta Orsini does not ask what the compounds are for.
- [[cabinet-of-morsani|The Cabinet of Morsani]] — Velo Quarter curio and magic item dealer; Morsani knows the previous owner of everything.
- [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] — Umberlee's canal-front shrine on the Mercatura; tribute paid in full view of passing boats.
- [[kats-curios|Cat's Curios]] — referral-only artificer workshop; no sign, no walk-ins.

### Hidden & Notable

- [[warren|The Warren]] — Rattkin settlement beneath Le Paludi; the deepest anchor of [[the-passage|the Passage]].
