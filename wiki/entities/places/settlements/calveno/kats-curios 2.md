# Cat's Curios

![[Catarina-DaVirelli.webp|Catarina Da'Virelli, proprietor of Cat's Curios]]

## Overview

Cat's Curios is a referral-only artificer workshop in [[calveno|Calveno]], hidden behind a narrow side-street door with brass fittings and no name. It belongs to [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]], who does not advertise, does not take walk-ins, and does not expand her circle casually.

## Description

A narrow door in a Calveno side-street — brass fittings, no name. Inside: long benches of half-finished mechanisms, a low forge, the smell of hot oil and soldering flux, and a locked glass cabinet along one wall holding pieces that don't belong to this century.

## People & Operations

**Proprietor: [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]]** — Works from the bench and does not offer chairs. All transactions happen on the workshop floor. The circle of people who can reach her is deliberately small.

## Notable Areas

**Workshop Floor** — Long benches, precision tools racked by size, half-finished mechanisms in various states of assembly.

**Specimen Cabinet** — A locked glass case along the east wall. Dark-alloy pieces, faintly iridescent, tooling marks too fine for any current forge in the Scatter. Trade only; no cash price.

## Stock / Services

- Artificer commission — *"Describe the mechanism. I'll tell you if it's something I'll do, and what it takes."*
- Mechanism repair — *"Bring it in. I'll assess it on the bench."*
- Antheri-alloy mechanisms (specimen cabinet) — trade only; no cash price

## Access

There is no sign and no public shopfront. Access depends on referral, reputation, and Da'Virelli's willingness to accept the visitor.

## Current Hooks

- A party that needs the Scatter's best artificer work needs a referral from someone Catarina trusts.
- The dark-alloy pieces in the specimen cabinet connect to the [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]] salvage economy.
