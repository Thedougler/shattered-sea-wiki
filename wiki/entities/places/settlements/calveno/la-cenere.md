# La Cenere

## Overview

La Cenere is a grey market dealer in magical and unusual items on a dead-end canal spur in Le Paludi, [[calveno|Calveno]]. The building had a fire years ago and the replacement stone came from a different quarry — darker than the pale Calveno standard, smoke-stained in places. The locals call it "the ash" and the name stuck. [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] moved in because nobody else would.

She buys and sells. Everything in the shop is discounted relative to legitimate market value. The discounts are steep and the reasons are not included in the price.

## Description

Below the canal waterline on two sides. The dark stone facade sits at the end of a spur that doesn't connect to anything — the canal dead-ends at a retaining wall fifteen feet past the door. Inside: a counter separates you from the merchandise. Shelves behind locked glass on three walls. The air smells of canal damp and something faintly metallic — old enchantment residue, or old blood, or both. A single oil lamp above the counter provides the only light Lavinia considers necessary.

The shop is small. Three customers would be a crowd. That is by design.

## People & Operations

**Proprietor: [[lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]]** — Mid-fifties, Calveno human. Hair pulled back severely, hands that move like they're counting even when they're still. She does not perform. She does not explain. She prices the risk into the discount and tells you so. If you want to know why something costs a third of market value, the answer is an additional service and it costs nearly as much as the item itself.

She buys anything. Price depends on provenance — items with clean documentation pay less because she can't add her margin. Items with no documentation pay more because they can disappear into her stock without questions. She has never been caught selling stolen property because she has never been caught.

## Buying Rates

Lavinia buys at the following rates. She does not negotiate upward.

| Provenance | Rate |
|---|---|
| No documentation, no questions | 40% of market value |
| Partial documentation (bill of sale, previous owner known) | 25% of market value |
| Full documentation (guild-certified, clean chain of ownership) | 15% of market value |

The inversion is deliberate. Clean items are worth less to her because they're traceable and she can't resell them through her usual channels without creating a paper trail.
