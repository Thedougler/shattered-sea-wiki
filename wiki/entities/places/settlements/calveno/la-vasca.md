# La Vasca

## Overview

The Black-Jaw Run has operated a private basin in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] for three generations. The canal approach is unremarkable — a low stone arch barely wide enough for a keel, flush with the waterline, indistinguishable from the drainage culverts on either side. On the far side of the arch, the channel widens into a covered basin cut from old foundation stone: high enough for a mast to stand, long enough for a patrol cutter or a heavy caravel. The dry dock cradle is iron and timber, hand-operated, original equipment from when the building above it was still a tanning yard. It still works. The tannery has not operated in forty years.

Outside, the building looks like what it officially is: a disused storage facility with a rotted roof and a collapsed beam visible through the upper windows. The beam is real. It is also not structural.

Within the Passage, the place is called La Vasca — the Basin. New members learn it as an address, not an explanation.

## Description

The basin smells of old stone, bilge, and the particular mineral damp of a space that has not seen direct sunlight in decades. A single oil lamp on a chain over the cradle. The walls are original foundation stone — Le Paludi is built on layers of older Calveno, and the basin is somewhere in the middle stratum. Rattkin-scale tool racks run along the port wall: caulking mallets, copper tacks, pitch brushes, paint pigments in sealed crocks. The starboard wall holds spare timber, a hand-pump bilge rig, and a folding worktable covered in old manifests that nobody has cleared in years.

A narrow passage in the aft wall connects to the Warren's outer channels — technically accessible on foot at low tide, not comfortable, and not discussed with anyone who hasn't already been introduced.

## People & Operations

**[[cobb|Cobb]]** — A young Rattkin in his late teens, grey-brown fur, hands hardened from caulking work. He keeps the Basin running: cradle maintained, tool stock managed, vouched vessels escorted in through the arch, word sent up the chain when the Basin is used.

He will have the cradle ready before he asks any questions, and he will not ask many. What he will do — early, calmly, without confrontation — is tell Perrin that Nona has been asking after him and will know he is here within the hour regardless.

> [!dm]
> Cobb is not the threat. He's the clock. Using the Basin is not a secret — it is a known event being reported through proper channels to the person who runs those channels. The warmth is genuine. The reporting is also genuine. These are not in conflict. Play Cobb as someone doing his job correctly, which in this case means being helpful and honest about exactly what being helpful costs.
>
> If a player tries to ask Cobb not to tell Nona: he pauses, looks at Perrin with something between sympathy and mild disbelief, and says *"I can't do that."* Not unkind. Just: no.

**Sample lines:**
- *"She's been asking around. I got a description three weeks ago — fancy rat, olive hood, oversized sword. Said to send word if he came through."*
- *"Basin's free. I'll have the cradle ready in an hour. Your shipwright know how to use a hand-pump rig?"*
- *"You should go see her before she comes to you. It'll go better."*
