# Moretti & Sons Chandlery

![[Giacomo-Moretti.webp|Giacomo Moretti, halfling chandler and proprietor of Moretti & Sons]]

## Overview

Moretti & Sons is [[calveno|Calveno]]'s main ship outfitter on the Mercatura, a long low warehouse with a canal-side loading door wide enough for two boats abreast. It handles practical shipboard needs — rope, provisions, tools, canvas, lamp oil — efficiently and without ceremony.

## Description

A warehouse built flush with the Mercatura canal, long and low, with a loading door that opens directly onto the water. The smell inside is rope and tar and salt, in that order. The counter runs the full length of the interior. Giacomo works from behind it.

## People & Operations

**Proprietor: [[giacomo-moretti|Giacomo Moretti]]** — A halfling in his middle years, grey at the temples. Can quote the current market price of oakum, sail canvas, lamp oil, and rope in bulk without looking anything up. Gives a discount if you've moored at Calveno before and he remembers you; he usually does. Does not bargain on provisions.
