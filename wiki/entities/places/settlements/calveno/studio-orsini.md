## Overview

Studio Orsini is down a Le Paludi side canal that branches off the main channel and stops being on any canal map shortly after that. Reachable by small flat-bottomed boat or a narrow footpath that exists but has never been maintained. [[calveno|Calveno]] does not officially know Studio Orsini is there.

The smell reaches you before the door. Inside: high shelves of sealed glass jars, ongoing processes at each worktable, a low lamp above each station that Orsini apparently never turns off. The ceiling drips once per minute in the far corner. A bucket has been placed there for years.

## Proprietor

**[[marta-orsini|Marta Orsini]]** — Late forties, ash-pale skin with a faint violet cast and two short horns she keeps filed flat. Permanently damp in the way of someone who never leaves Le Paludi. Speaks with the precision of someone who knows exactly what she is making and exactly what it will be used for. She does not ask. She specifies what her compounds will and will not do, gives instructions once, and expects them to be followed. Returns are not accepted.
