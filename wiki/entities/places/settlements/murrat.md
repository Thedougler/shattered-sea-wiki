# Murrat

## Overview

Murrat sits roughly midway between [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and the western edge of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], a limestone reef island low enough that the canopy is the first thing you see above the horizon. From the approach it looks like every other uninhabited Midchain island: dense forest, pale cliff faces above the waterline, reef running out ahead of the hull.

It isn't on most working charts. Pilots avoid it. The island is [[moucheron|Moucheron]] territory and they attack anything that lands — no protocol, no one to negotiate with. Any party that comes ashore is treated as prey.

## Geography

The island is a single irregular limestone mass, larger than it looks from a distance. The reef extends further east than charts show — responsible for at least two groundings. The interior rises to a low central ridge. Fresh water exists (springs and an interior lake), which explains why the island supports multiple permanent villages.

The canopy is thick. Landing parties who survived long enough to leave accounts described not seeing more than twenty feet in any direction on the ground. The Moucherons fly at sixty feet and see everything.

## Settlements & Sites

The villages are built into the cliff faces — stone platforms in natural limestone hollows, rope bridges between them. Invisible from the water. A ship close enough to see the platforms has already been seen by everyone in them.

## Hazards

**Moucherons.** Villages can scramble a response within minutes of a landing. They fly 60 feet and spot movement through the canopy from above. Multiple squads will respond — killing the first wave does not end the encounter.

**Fauna.** The interior has giant constrictor snakes, giant spiders, giant apes, and giant frogs. A party that breaks away from the Moucherons moves directly into their hunting grounds.

**Eastern reef shelf.** Extends further east than most charts show. Sharp limestone, outgoing tide pulls toward it.

## Current Hooks

- A merchant out of Kalowe is asking for charts of Murrat's interior — there's a story about a cache left by an early explorer who landed before anyone told him what the island was.
- A grounded vessel on the eastern reef shelf is visible from the Kalowe-to-Verdant-Teeth route. Three days now. No sign of the crew.
