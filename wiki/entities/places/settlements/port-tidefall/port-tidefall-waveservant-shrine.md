## Description

Cut into the cliff base at the southern end of the Dockfront. You hear it before you see it — tallow and salt, close and low. Inside: bare stone walls, a seawater basin at the centre, a ring of candles burning. Low ceiling.

## Function

The Waveservants operate on a tithe-and-communion model. Mariners pay before they sail or after they survive. Both are acceptable. Neither is optional. A junior attendant manages the candles and basin; a senior Waveservant manages everything else.

## Current Hooks

- A mariner who skips tribute creates a social and spiritual problem before the next departure.
