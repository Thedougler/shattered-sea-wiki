# South Harbour

## Overview

South Harbour faces the calmer Strait side of [[calders-tooth|Calder's Tooth]], where the island slopes into tidal mudflats and shallow estuaries. Dravosi ledgers call it a secondary anchorage. In practice it is a working sprawl of fishing fleets, independent shipwrights, small traders, and repair crews doing the work that keeps the island functional while the Crown's attention stays focused on [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]] to the west.

Crown authority here is real but intermittent — patrols arrive unevenly, and the tidal mud makes armored inspection difficult when the tide is against you. [[waveservants|The Waveservants]] maintain a quiet unofficial presence among the fishing fleets. Small cargo that might draw questions at Tidefall frequently arrives here first.

## Districts & Landmarks

**East Pier** — The most visible official infrastructure: the [[south-harbour-impound|South Harbour Impound]], where the Crown's chain and ledger keep vessels from moving.

**Mudflat Estuary** — Shallow channels, crab traps, eel lines, and mangrove edges. Giant constrictor snakes plausible in the muddier channels south of the harbour.
