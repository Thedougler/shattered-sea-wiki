## Overview

The Low Lamp is the social heart of the [[warren|Warren]] — a wide, low-ceilinged chamber dug into the widest section of the old drainage corridor, where Kin have gathered to eat, trade news, and conduct open business for as long as anyone currently alive can remember. It is the first place a trusted guest is taken when they enter the Warren.

Long communal tables of dark, scarred wood. Oil lamps set into wall niches. The central lamp — older and larger than all the others, hanging on a chain from the highest point of the ceiling — gives the place its name. Nobody knows exactly how old it is. Nobody has let it go out to find out.

Business is conducted here openly — debts acknowledged, introductions made, news exchanged — but the convention holds that no Tangle business is brought to these tables. What happens in the Low Lamp is Kin business, visible to all. What requires privacy goes elsewhere.

## Proprietor

**[[essa-two-tooth|Essa Two-Tooth]]** — A Rattkin elder who runs the Low Lamp with her grand-nephew Pip. She moves slowly and hears everything. Every person who enters is, in her assessment, too thin. She provides food before any discussion. If you eat, you have acknowledged the Low Lamp's claim on your goodwill. This is the point.
