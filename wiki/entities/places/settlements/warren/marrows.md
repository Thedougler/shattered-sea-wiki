# Marrow's

## Overview

Marrow's is a cargo broker's stall carved into the rock off the Warren's north channel corridor — three steps down from the main passage, easy to miss if you don't know it's there. Marrow handles things that needed to move without appearing on a manifest: sealed documents, consignment packages, cargo that required a different kind of routing than the surface city offers.

## Description

Three steps down from the north channel corridor is an alcove carved into the rock. Two walls hold shelves, most of them empty in a way that suggests recent turnover rather than slow business. A brass scale sits on the central table, polished on the pan surfaces and scratched everywhere else. The single lamp illuminates the table without lighting the back of the room.

Marrow is usually already seated when anyone enters and usually already watching the door. Lean, with one ear notched at the tip, wearing clothes that settle into the stone colour without effort. He weighs everything twice on the brass scale. Nobody has asked him why.

## People & Operations

**[[marrow|Marrow]]** gives no other name and is not asked for one. Has operated from this alcove for at least fifteen years. Accepts Kin by default. Dry need a Kin introduction and Marrow's own read of the situation — he has turned away people with good introductions before and offered no explanation.

## Stock / Services

- 5 gp+ — [[document-transit|Document transit]] — *"Price reflects content weight. Not physical weight. No questions."*
- 10 gp + 5% — [[cargo-brokerage|Small cargo brokerage]] — *"Three-week hold. Stated value. Non-negotiable terms."*
- 1 gp/wk — [[consignment-storage|Consignment storage]] — *"Three weeks. Hard limit. After that — my concern, not yours."*
- 15 gp+ — [[passage-network-introduction|Passage network introduction]] — *"Kin by default. Dry by my read and a Kin introduction. No guarantees."*

## Access & Security

The alcove is not marked. Finding it requires someone who already knows where it is.
