# The Tangle Chambers

| | |
|---|---|
| **Location** | [[warren|The Warren]], deep southern section |
| **Access** | Tangle summons only — even long-established Kin guests are not admitted without invitation |

## Overview

The Tangle Chambers occupy the deepest, driest section of the [[warren|Warren]] — a set of interconnected rooms carved into the rock at the Warren's southern end where the ceiling unexpectedly lifts and Calveno's noise no longer reaches. This is where the seven elders of the Tangle deliberate, where [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s apartments are, and where the [[the-passage|Passage's]] most sensitive routing memory has been maintained for generations.

## Description

The corridors at the Warren's southern end are different. The stone is older here — tooled rather than broken. The ceiling lifts unexpectedly in the main passage, high enough that a human could stand comfortably. Nobody knows why. The arguments about it are old enough to be running jokes.

Two rope bridges cross short flooded sections on the approach. The central chambers beyond are all dry stone, swept clean, with oil lamps burning behind glass. The smell is paper, oil, and old stone — the archive smell.

## Function

The Tangle deliberates here when business requires the full council. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona's]] apartments in the south rooms serve as the Black-Jaw family's private residence and, effectively, the Passage's operational centre in [[calveno|Calveno]]. The archive rooms hold routing records and network intelligence accumulated across generations, written in shard-notation that only Kin can read.

## People & Operations

The **Tangle** is seven senior Rattkin elders. They meet when they need to. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] chairs by convention and has done so long enough that the convention feels like law.

The Black-Jaw family maintains the chambers: lamps trimmed, corridors swept, archive rooms dry, bridges inspected. This is not ceremonial — it is the work.

[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] grew up moving through these rooms. He knows the way without thinking about it.

## Access & Security

No formal guard at the approach. Every Rattkin in the Warren knows these corridors and knows how to make an uninvited presence known to the appropriate elder. Kin guests with long-standing Warren access do not enter without a specific summons. Dry guests have not entered in living memory, with four acknowledged exceptions.

## Current Hooks

- The Tangle has sent one message through the Passage to Perrin's last known port of call. He has not responded.
- A summons from Nona carries no deadline and no expiration date. She does not send reminders.
