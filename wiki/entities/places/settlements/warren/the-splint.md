## Overview

The Splint is the Warren's healer and apothecary, occupying a small dry room off the east passages. Run by **Colla**, a young Rattkin who trained under his aunt. The name is old Kin slang — something close to "the fixing place."

The walls are shelved floor to ceiling with clay jars, labeled in a small, careful hand. Dried plants hang in bundles. The central table serves as both counter and treatment surface and is kept scrupulously clear between patients. Colla works in silence and moves with the economy of someone who knows exactly where everything is in the dark.

## Proprietor

**[[colla|Colla]]** — Late twenties. Silences are not hostile; he simply does not see the point of conversation during a procedure. His expression makes clear his limited patience for people who arrive with injuries they could have avoided. He will write down his reasoning if asked.

His surface-side contact is a herbalist in Le Paludi named Dosia, who supplies ingredients he cannot source underground.

## Current Hooks

- Colla needs brine-sage — a low-tidal marsh plant used in several wound preparations — that has become nearly impossible to source since a Dravosi trade restriction tightened last season. He'll pay 30 gp per reliable bundle or equivalent services.
- Someone came to the Splint three weeks ago with injuries Colla found concerning. He reported it to the Tangle. He has not seen that person since and does not discuss it further.
