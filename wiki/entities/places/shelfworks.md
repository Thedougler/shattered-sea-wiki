# The Shelfworks

![[The-Shelfworks.webp|Coral shelf dropping into deep blue water with workboats gathered near the edge]]

## Overview

You find the Shelfworks by following the boats. There is no island, no lighthouse, no harbour wall. Just open ocean with anchored salvage vessels, marker buoys, air-pump stations, and dive lines running straight down into water clear enough to make the depth look kinder than it is.

At forty feet, sunlight catches pale Antheri stonework. Walls still stand. Archways are still arched. Fish move through rooms that once held something other than fish. Coral has taken the lintels. Small reef sharks treat the old halls as territory.

Then the shelf ends. Twenty metres east of the last visible wall, the ruins drop away with no visible bottom. The water there is a different colour — darker, directionless, the kind of blue that has no floor in it. Experienced salvagers do not look east if they can help it.

## History

In 1488 DR, [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] salvaged functional [[antheri|Antheri]] components from the upper ruins and returned to Calveno with them. The wave of prospectors, scholars, and criminal contractors that followed became the Shelfworks Goldrush. The goldrush thinned the easy pickings from the upper shelf. What remains is harder to reach, harder to identify, and harder to get back to the surface intact.

The [[red-lady|*Red Lady*]] wreck sits on the eastern edge of the Shelfworks shelf. The [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] is still aboard.

## What's Here

**Upper ruins (40–80 ft):** Accessible by free-diving or short air-pump runs. Walls, archways, collapsed floors. Dark-alloy fittings that resist corrosion and cut differently than anything modern. Most portable pieces are gone; fixed architectural components remain.

**Mid-shelf (80–150 ft):** Requires air pumps. Intact room structures with sealed chambers. The [[antheri-ruins|Antheri Ruins]] proper begin here — architecture built for beings that didn't need floors or standard-height doorways.

**The drop-off (150 ft+):** The shelf ends and the water column opens into the Drowned Maw. Nothing survives an uncontrolled descent into that water. Nobody looks at it if they can help it.

## Hazards

Reef sharks in the upper ruins. Hunter sharks along the shelf edge. Giant squid in the deeper channels. The [[the-glass-debt|Glass Debt]] occasionally makes salvage traffic change course. At the drop-off: the Drowned Maw itself.
