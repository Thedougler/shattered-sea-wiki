# Sorn

![[Sorn.webp|Forested island with a pale beach and dense jungle rising inland]]

## Overview

From the water, Sorn reads as an unbroken wall of green — limestone ridge above the canopy, jungle running all the way to the reef line, no harbour, no smoke, nothing that looks like an invitation. The western shore has a strip of pale sand where the treeline has been cut back a few metres, wide enough for a few boats to pull up. That is the only point on the island where outsiders are expected to conduct business.

Sorn is the agricultural interior of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] — the island where the Grung work runs. The Botukuri clan holds the island; their name is on thirty years of Midchain trade accounts. A purple-caste garrison maintains the beach. Intermediaries come to the waterline and conduct business there. Nobody from the interior comes out, and nobody from the water goes in.

The raids out of the Teeth have been getting worse, and Sorn is not where the raids come from, which is one of the few pieces of information about it that most traders find reassuring.

## Trade & Resources

Interior agriculture, resin shipments, pigments, and alchemical specimens traded through intermediaries at the beach.

## Fauna

Giant wasps audible above the cut-back treeline. [[giant-centipede|Giant centipedes]] carried out on hooked poles. Grung patrols keep flooded interior paths clear of [[giant-frog|giant frogs]]. Giant apes apply pressure from the ridgelines. Traders see only aftermath: repaired palisades, delayed resin shipments, guards returning with crushed equipment.

## Current Hooks

- Delays in resin shipments and repaired palisades hint at giant ape pressure inland.
- Sorn is not where the recent Verdant Teeth raids originate.
