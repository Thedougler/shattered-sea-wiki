# The Sunken Crown

> *Five small islands around a blue wound in the sea.*

## Overview

The Sunken Crown is a small island region in the [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]], northeast of the Drowned Maw crossing. Five low islands ring a vast Blue Hole that occupies most of the region's usable geography. The islands are not a broad archipelago — they are the exposed rim of something drowned: black stone, salt grass, wind-bent scrub, and cliffs that show old waterlines like scars.

Colonial charts thin out before these waters. Most pilots treat the region as a rumour or a navigation note to avoid. The people who live here have been here longer than any outside record acknowledges.

The waters around the Blue Hole have been unsettled lately. Currents reversing without warning, fish avoiding channels they have always run, weather turning faster than the sky gives notice.

## Geography

The Blue Hole occupies most of the Sunken Crown. From any high point it reads as a near-perfect dark circle inside the reef rim — deep enough that soundings fail quickly, warm in places from lingering volcanic activity, clear enough near the edges that large shadows can be seen moving far below. The boundary between turquoise shallows and indigo drop is clean and sudden.

## Access

Requires crossing east of the Drowned Maw into the Outer Reach — six hours over the Maw, then open water. The five islands are surrounded by reefs and channels that do not behave consistently. Without local knowledge: reef damage risk or being drawn toward the Blue Hole's drop-off.

## People & Power

The Tabaxi of [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] organize the region through elders, watch rotations, seasonal knowledge, refuge practice, and shrine obligations.

> [!dm]
> Without the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], the shrine still marks debts and remembers the drowned, but its deepest function — stabilizing the Blue Hole and protecting nesting waters — is failing. See [[sunken-crown-blessing-crisis|Sunken Crown Blessing Crisis]]. The Pearl was stolen from [[vel-orn|Vel Orn]] by [[delmar-fisk|Fisk's fleet]].

## Fauna

Giant lizards bask on the black stone, giant bats crowd wind-cut caves, and scavenger birds gather wherever storm or tide leaves food behind. The [[sawek|Sawek]] is rumoured in the Blue Hole. Dragon turtle wyrmlings nest on the outer beaches of Dath-Mar and Meth-Var — the elders know the sites; outsiders do not.
