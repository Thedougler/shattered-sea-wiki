# The *Dead Lady*

> *She is always upwind, regardless of actual wind direction. She does not pursue. She is already where you are going.*

**Type:** Brigantine (spectral), Tier 2 | **Owner:** [[umberlee|Umberlee]] | **Status:** Dormant until triggered

The [[red-lady|*Red Lady*]], raised by [[umberlee|Umberlee]] as a punishment instrument. She exists to compel [[delmar-fisk|Fisk]] to retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], not to kill him. Umberlee cannot kill Fisk without losing the five captains' souls into the Maw a second time. She knows this. The *Dead Lady* is pressure, not execution.

---

## Appearance

Her hull matches the *Red Lady*'s outline exactly: same length, same rigging, same dark red paint — now the colour of old blood in water. The figurehead is the same carved woman, eyes still forward. Her wake does not foam. She makes no sound at any distance. Other ships that sight her know immediately what she is.

## Crew

Rank-and-file sailors of Fisk's original five-ship fleet (not the captains, who are in Fisk's mind). Approximately 30 crew. Recognisable as individuals to anyone who sailed with the fleet. They do not speak.

If Fisk sees them close enough to identify faces, he will recognise them. The five captains in his mind will also recognise them.

**Type:** Shadow stat block, ship-appropriate. Can move through solid hull material. Do not initiate boarding before Stage 3.

**She has no captain.** Umberlee drives her directly. Nothing to bargain with, deceive, or remove.

---

## Special Properties

**Dead Reckoning.** Always knows where Fisk is. Navigation tricks, false trails, fog, and weather do not affect her course.

**Forbidding Tide.** Ships within 1 mile: reversed currents and unnatural headwinds. Tier 1 ships −20 miles/day; Tier 2 ships −10 miles/day. Passive and constant.

**Spectral Boarding.** Her crew pass through solid hull material — boarding requires no entry points.

**The Voice Below Deck.** When within half a mile of Fisk, he hears voices from below her waterline — his crew, calling up through the hull. The five captains hear it too. Not a mechanical effect; information delivered without a save.

**Umberlee's Wake.** Creatures overboard within 300 feet: DC 16 Strength save or pulled 30 feet toward her hull at the start of each turn.

## Spectral Cannons

Silent at firing — the shot arrives with only displaced water.

- **vs. crew:** 4d10 cold damage; DC 14 Con save or gain 1 exhaustion level
- **vs. hull:** Double damage against Tier 1 vessel hull material

## Deployment

### Trigger

[[waveservants|Waveservants]] deliver Umberlee's terms once she learns Fisk survived and is operating. She sets a deadline (8–12 weeks from first contact) to demonstrate movement toward retrieving the Pearl. If the deadline passes without action, the *Dead Lady* is dispatched.

---

### Stage 1 — Contact

A Waveservant delivers Umberlee's terms directly to Fisk: retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from the Maw and return it. She names the deadline. She does not describe the consequence.

---

### Stage 2 — Sighting

The *Dead Lady* appears on the horizon. Matches the party's heading but does not close. Visible at dawn and dusk. Other ships give the party distance. Ports show visible unease. Not a Reputation loss yet — a warning.

---

### Stage 3 — Approach

Closes to weapon range but does not fire on Fisk's vessel directly. Attacks other ships in the party's company. Circles rather than closes. Her crew board other vessels — not Fisk's.

**Reputation loss:** 1 step with factions/ports aware of the situation.

---

### Stage 4 — Escalation

Begins targeting Fisk's ship's hull. Will not kill Fisk — but will destroy his ship and take anyone who enters the water. Her crew board through the hull. The party cannot sail, cannot port, cannot operate.

---

### Resolution

| Path | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Compliance** | Fisk demonstrates movement toward the Maw. Umberlee stands the *Dead Lady* down to the horizon. Returns if progress stops. |
| **Appeasement** | Significant offering + deed of service to Waveservants buys a deadline extension. Does not buy a Stage 4 reversal. |
| **Destruction** | Disperses 1d4 weeks. Umberlee reconstitutes. Underlying situation unresolved. |
| **Negotiation** | Fisk has real leverage — Umberlee needs him alive and cannot retrieve the Pearl herself. A player who works this out and pushes has a genuine position with a goddess. See [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] for the full leverage shape. |
