# Greyteeth Runner

> [!read-aloud]
> A lean single-masted sloop rides low and light in the water, shallow-drafted and flat-bottomed for reef work. The hull is plain, patched at the port bow, with no figurehead, no real paint left, and no registry pennant flying. A swivel gun sits on each side, four sweep oars are racked inside the hull, and nothing aboard suggests comfort or prestige.

## Current State

The *Greyteeth Runner* is a Tier 1 reef runner currently for sale in [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]] harbour through the Commission Board. She is built for speed, shallow water, and reef channels where heavier patrol cutters cannot follow.

## Overview

An Island Council-built sloop designed for Midchain reef running rather than open battle. She trades armament and comfort for speed, draft, and handling, making her one of the best small hulls available for fast movement through coastal hazards.

## Current Notes

The hull is worn but structurally sound, with visible patchwork on the port bow and a proper recaulk overdue. The deck is open aft with no stern cabin, and belowdecks is one undivided crew-and-cargo space with hammock hooks, two hatches, and no galley.

## Layout

### Weather Deck

Mostly open, with a full-width tiller aft. The two swivel guns and sweep oars give her just enough bite and calm-water mobility to protect a fast escape rather than stand in a fight.

### Belowdecks

A single undivided space handles both crew and cargo. Six hammock hooks run the length of the hull, cargo sits low and wide in the shallow hold, and access comes through two deck hatches. No galley and no private cabin.

## Crew

No standing crew established. The ship is sized for a light working complement and can run with very few hands compared to a standard patrol cutter.

## Operational Notes

**Handling:** Fastest documented Tier 1 hull currently in [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]], with no speed penalty in charted reef passages.

**Armament tradeoff:** Cannons can be mounted, but doing so costs speed and handling. The current swivel-only loadout is deliberate.

**Registry:** No Crown or Concordat registry comes with the vessel. Normal in Island Council waters; a complication elsewhere.

**Acquisition:** Listed for legal sale through the Commission Board at 1,400 gp.
