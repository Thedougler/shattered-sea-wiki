# HCS *Constancy*

> [!read-aloud]
> A two-masted frigate on a grey hull, twelve cannon ports per side on the main gun deck and eight more above on the upper deck. The white stripe runs bow to stern at the upper deck rail. Crown pennant at the mainmast. A rating officer is already visible at the rail before the ship finishes its turn into your wind. She is not threatening you. She is waiting for you to make the next move.

---

## Overview

A two-masted heavy frigate, 140 feet on the waterline, built at the Harwick Naval Yard to the Crown Admiralty's standard patrol specification. The *Constancy* is the class the Crown uses when it needs a ship that can enforce, not merely inspect: enough guns to discourage resistance, enough cargo space to transport seized goods and prisoners, and enough range to operate in the Scatter for months without returning to Tidefall.

Hull is naval grey. White Admiralty stripe at the upper deck rail, narrower than the *Ordinance*'s but identically maintained. Brass registry plate at the bow. Crown pennant at the mainmast. The stern cabin is a working captain's office — two sealed document drawers, a rating ledger bolted to the desk, and a speaking tube that runs to the gun deck below.

The name is not incidental. The Crown names its patrol class after virtues because a patrol ship represents the steady, unremarkable presence of law. The *Constancy* is not the ship that arrives for a crisis. It is the ship that was already there.

---

## Purpose

Where the *Ordinance* settles matters, the *Constancy* creates the conditions that require settling:

- Conducts **Rating Inspections** on vessels transiting Crown waters — hull registry, cargo manifest, crew papers, and charter documentation.
- Executes **Impound Orders** issued by civil or Admiralty authority.
- Escorts **Crown convoys** through contested reef passages and Greyteeth approaches.
- Maintains **patrol presence** along assigned routes, logging every vessel encountered.
- Suppresses **unlicensed privateering** in Crown waters by force if necessary.

The *Constancy* does not outrun threats. It discourages them.

## Special Facilities

### Inspection Office *(Special — 2 space units)*

A dedicated compartment off the main gun deck, fitted with a Crown registry desk, scales and weights station, and a locked evidence cabinet. Hireling: **Rating Officer** (specialist — ship's legal officer, not a crew role).

*Active (Director):* Once per Bastion Turn, the Rating Officer may conduct a formal inspection of a named vessel or individual. On success, produces a Crown-certified rating: legal status, outstanding fees, or registered infractions. Admissible in any Crown civil or Admiralty proceeding.

*Passive (All):* Crown-flagged vessels in the same harbour are treated as inspected for the week. Non-Crown captains formally rated by this ship's officer make Deception checks at disadvantage when presenting forged papers to any Crown official with access to the registry network.

### Brig and Holding Deck *(Special — 2 space units)*

A reinforced below-decks section forward of the magazine, fitted with iron cell doors, marine guard post, and four holding compartments for up to twelve prisoners. Hireling: **Marine Sergeant** (double-role: serves as Bosun on crew roster).

*Active (Director):* Once per Bastion Turn, the Marine Sergeant may process one prisoner per holding compartment: sworn depositions, extraction, or prisoner transfer to Crown civil authority.

*Passive (All):* Can hold and transport persons under Crown warrant without additional cost. Escape attempts made at disadvantage — the cells are below the waterline.

## Layout

**Weather Deck** — Wheel, compass, signal flags, Crown lamp array. Captain's cabin aft. Four swivel mounts amidships. Marine station at rail.

**Upper Gun Deck** — Eight guns per side, 18-lb long cannon. Inspection Office aft. Officers' berths and mess forward.

**Main Gun Deck** — Twelve guns per side, 18-lb long cannon. Primary fighting deck. Crew berths and mess forward. Armorer's station amidships.

**Below Decks (Orlop)** — Brig and Holding Deck forward. Surgeon's berth. Stores.

**Hold** — Magazine and shot locker aft. 75 tons cargo capacity.
