## Overview

A three-deck Ship of the Line, 210 feet on the waterline, built at the Harwick Naval Yard in the Crown Islands. The *Ordinance* was the largest vessel the Crown commissioned — the only Tier 4 hull in the Shattered Sea at its commissioning, and the clearest possible statement that the Crown's authority is not an argument it intends to have twice.

The hull is naval grey. A white Admiralty stripe runs the full length at weather deck level. Cannon ports number forty-two per side across three gun decks, fitted with cast-iron 24-pounder long guns. Six stern chasers and six bow chasers extend the battery. Total mounted guns: 96. The figurehead is Tyr — robed, blindfolded, scales extended, gilded at the scales and sandals, face left unpainted.

---

## Purpose

The *Ordinance* does not patrol. It arrives. In practice:

- **Mobile Admiralty Court** — hears military tribunals, issues commissions, revokes privateer licenses, renders Crown judgements.
- **Fleet Command** — carries an Admiral's staff and signals complement.
- **Deterrent** — reputation travels weeks ahead of the ship.

## Special Facilities

### Admiralty Court *(Special — 2 space units)*

Sealed chamber on the upper stern with raised dais, Tyr's symbol in brass, witness rail. Hireling: **Judge-Advocate** (ship's legal officer).

*Active:* Once per Bastion Turn, issue one Crown legal instrument — commission, revocation, warrant, impound order, or letters of marque. Full Admiralty authority, enforceable in all Crown-held ports.

*Passive:* Crown officers gain advantage on Intimidation checks against parties subject to Crown law.

### Registry Vault *(Special — 2 space units)*

Steel-doored archive below the waterline with fireproof caskets and a copying press. Hireling: **Ship's Registrar** (not a crew role).

*Active:* Once per Bastion Turn — produce certified document copy, forge Crown certificate (DC 18 Investigation to detect), or locate named individual's Crown registration.

*Passive:* Generates a legal paper trail for every port visited, vessel inspected, and order issued.

### Gunner's Magazine *(Special — 2 space units)*

Reinforced lower-deck compartment with alchemical desiccant and Crown-standard powder-charge casting equipment. Hireling: **Gunner-Warden** (double-role: Gunner on crew roster).

*Active:* Once per Bastion Turn — prepare specialist shot order (grapeshot, chain shot, or alchemical incendiary). Applies in the next combat encounter.

*Passive:* Cannon attacks deal maximum damage on a confirmed critical hit. Magazine has never misfired.

## Layout

**Weather Deck** — Quarterdeck aft with wheel, compass, signals, Admiral's walk. Admiralty Court aft deckhouse. Open gun positions amidships.

**Upper Gun Deck** — 14 guns per side, long 24-pounders. Officers' quarters aft, including Admiral's cabin with private chart room.

**Main Gun Deck** — 14 guns per side, long 24-pounders. Registry Vault aft. Crew berths forward.

**Lower Gun Deck** — 14 guns per side, long 24-pounders. Operates at the waterline; ports open only in fair weather. Gunner's Magazine directly below.

**Orlop Deck** — No guns. Carpenter's stores, chain locker, bosun's stores, sailcloth. Junior officer berths.

**Hold** — Ballast, water casks, provisions, spare timber. 160 tons cargo, typically impound cargo in transit.
