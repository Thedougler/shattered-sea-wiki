# HCS Relentless

A Tier 1 patrol cutter, same class as [[hcs-surety|HCS Surety]] but more heavily armed and in better condition. Naval grey hull with a white stripe at the waterline, Crown pennant at the masthead underway. Brass registry plate bolted to the bow. The gunwale is reinforced — heavier timber and iron bolting at the rail than standard patrol class. Six gun mounts, three per side, with swivel guns fore and aft. Freshly caulked, copper sheathing intact, running rigging recently replaced.

> [!dm]
> Best-armed Tier 1 vessel in [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]]'s harbour. Not for sale. Twelve armed Crown sailors aboard, garrison watches on the pier, [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]]'s battery above.

**Class:** Tier 1 Patrol Cutter (HCS — Her Crown's Ship, Dravosi designation)

**Current Location:** Main harbour pier, [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]] — under garrison watch

**Status:** Active Crown vessel; not acquirable through legitimate means

## Current State

**Hull:** Pristine. Regular garrison maintenance. Freshly caulked, copper sheathing intact, running rigging recently replaced.

**Weapons:** All mounts operational. Swivel guns positioned to sweep the deck of a vessel come alongside. Powder and shot in locked magazine below the waterline.

**Below Decks:** Officer's cabin aft. Four-bunk officer berth. Forward crew hammocks for 8. Locked magazine (two keys: commanding officer and Fort Crestwall armoury). Prisoner transfer cage (2 capacity), boarding equipment, inspection documents, spare Crown pennants in the hold.

**Registry:** White hull stripe at waterline, Crown pennant, brass registry plate on the bow. All three are removable with time and tools.
