# HCS *Sovereign*

![[raw/assets/banners/HCS-Sovereign.webp]]

> [!read-aloud]
> Three gun decks rise above a grey hull painted the colour of deep water — fifteen cannon ports per side on the lower deck alone, all closed, brass fittings catching light at every seam. A white Admiralty stripe runs the full length of the ship at the spar deck rail, wider than any other Crown vessel in the Scatter. Three mastheads carry the Crown pennant. The figurehead is a robed figure holding a crown aloft, not offering it — claiming it. She moves slowly. She does not need to move quickly.

---

## Overview

A first-rate ship of the line, 215 feet on the waterline, built at the Harwick Naval Yard over six years and commissioned as the definitive expression of Dravosi naval power. The *Sovereign* is the only vessel of her class in the Shattered Sea — the largest hull afloat, the heaviest armed, and the clearest possible answer to the question of whether the Crown's authority in these waters is negotiable.

![[raw/assets/banners/HCS-Sovereign-Full-Sail.webp]]

Hull is naval grey, copper-sheathed below the waterline. Three gun decks: lower carries thirty 32-lb long cannon, main carries twenty-eight 24-lb long cannon, upper carries thirty 12-lb long cannon. Four heavy carronades on the spar deck. Total mounted guns: 104, plus eight chasers.

The figurehead is not a god. It is a robed figure, indeterminate in feature, holding the Crown of Dravas aloft with both hands — less an offering, more evidence. The Admiralty had it carved in pale northern timber and gilt at the crown. The face was deliberately left unfinished.

---

## Purpose

The *Sovereign* does not patrol and does not escort. Deployed when the full weight of naval authority must be physically present:

- **Fleet Flagship** — carries the Admiral of the Shattered Sea and full staff. Coordinates all Crown naval assets across the Scatter.
- **Final Enforcement** — where the [[hcs-constancy|Constancy]] enforces and the patrol class deters, the *Sovereign* concludes. Its arrival ends disputes.
- **Strategic Deterrent** — reputation precedes it by weeks. Harbours that receive word of its transit make different decisions before it arrives.

## Special Facilities

### Grand Magazine *(Special — 2 space units)*

A reinforced double-chamber below the lower gun deck, climate-controlled with alchemical desiccant. Forward chamber: prepared shot for all three gun decks. Aft chamber: specialist rounds — chain, bar, heated, grapeshot — in separate sealed lockers. Hireling: **Master Gunner** (double-role: Gunner on crew roster).

*Active:* Once per Bastion Turn — prepare a specialist shot order for any one deck. Applies automatically in the next combat encounter.

*Passive:* Cannon attacks deal maximum damage on a confirmed critical hit. On a natural 1 on any cannon attack, the gun misfires but does not explode; cleared as an action.

### Admiral's War Room *(Special — 2 space units)*

Full stern cabin spanning the width of the spar deck — glazed windows, chart table, signals rack, dispatch archive. Hireling: **Flag Lieutenant** (specialist — not a crew role).

*Active:* Once per Bastion Turn — transmit fleet orders, reposition a Crown naval asset in the Scatter, or prepare a tactical assessment (provides advantage on the next navigation check or ship-to-ship encounter involving the target).

*Passive:* While the *Sovereign* is in a region, all Crown naval assets in that region treat the Captain role as filled by a skilled officer. Hostile captains within visual range must make DC 14 Wisdom save at the start of each encounter or start shaken (disadvantage on attacks until they succeed).

### Battle Surgeon's Deck *(Special — 2 space units)*

Full surgical deck on the orlop level, below the waterline. Two operating tables, mass-casualty medicine cabinet, amputation kit, ten-cot recovery bay. Hirelings: **Ship's Surgeon** + **Surgeon's Mate** (double-role: Cook on crew roster).

*Active:* Once per Bastion Turn — emergency surgery (stabilise + remove one exhaustion level), produce three Potions of Healing, or treat disease/poison in up to four crew simultaneously.

*Passive:* On a short rest aboard, each PC rolls Hit Dice twice and takes the higher result. On a long rest aboard after combat, each PC recovers one additional Hit Die.

## Layout

**Spar Deck** — Wheel and compass aft. Signal station. Two heavy carronades at quarterdeck, two at forecastle. Eight 12-lb guns per side amidships. Admiral's War Room full stern deckhouse.

**Upper Gun Deck** — Fifteen 12-lb guns per side. Officers' quarters aft with flag captain's anteroom. Marine barracks and armoury forward.

**Main Gun Deck** — Fourteen 24-lb guns per side. Primary fighting deck. Lower ports sealed in heavy weather.

**Lower Gun Deck** — Fifteen 32-lb guns per side. Heaviest weight of shot in the fleet. Grand Magazine directly below. Ports sealed except in calm water or harbour.

**Orlop Deck** — No guns. Battle Surgeon's Deck forward. Carpenter's stores, chain locker, bosun's supplies.

**Hold** — 100 tons cargo; typically stores for extended deep-water operations.

## Tactical Notes

The *Sovereign* does not chase. It presents a broadside and lets the other vessel decide. Against anything Tier 3 or below, a single full broadside from the lower gun deck ends the engagement. Its answer to shallow water, fast pursuit, and fire ships is: avoid them. A competent enemy uses those three things against it.
