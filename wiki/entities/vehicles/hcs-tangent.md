# HCS Tangent

A Dravosi Crown warship — heavier armament, full naval crew, proper gun deck. Not a patrol cutter. When it appears, something has already gone wrong for someone, and that someone is the person it's pointing at.

## Command

Captained by [[dorian-bishop|Dorian Bishop]], Crown Captain. Twice as dangerous as Rook in single combat. Known for lateral, unpredictable approaches — positions wide, forces an opponent to track and correct, engages when they've overcommitted to the correction.

## Role

The *Tangent* does not do inspections. The next Crown escalation after [[hcs-warrant|HCS Warrant]] — when the procedural enforcement has already failed and something harder is required.

> [!dm]
> Deploy the *Tangent* only when the campaign is ready for Crown pressure to become naval rather than procedural.
