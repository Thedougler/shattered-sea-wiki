# HCS Warrant

> [!read-aloud]
> A Crown brigantine comes on under full sail, naval grey from waterline to rail, the white stripe catching the light in a way the patrol cutters never quite manage at this distance. Twelve gun ports run down each side, six per beam, and the brass registry plate at the bow is large enough to read before she's within hailing range. A rating officer is already at the rail with a speaking trumpet. The Crown pennant at the masthead isn't moving — she's coming downwind and making you feel it. Whatever she is, she isn't asking.

---

## Current State

The *HCS Warrant* is the Crown's primary enforcement brigantine on the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] western patrol route, crewed from Harwick and working the approaches to [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]]. She is larger and better armed than either of Tidefall's patrol cutters and operates under a different mandate: where the cutters run waypoints and board small traders, the *Warrant* compels compliance from vessels the cutters couldn't safely challenge alone.

[[commander-gideon-ault|Commander Gideon Ault]] commands under sealed Admiralty orders. His commission authority includes vessel detention, cargo seizure, crew arrest, and the authority to issue provisional privateer letters to vessels assisting in enforcement actions.

## Layout

**Weather Deck** — Helm and binnacle aft, belfry and capstan amidships. Swivel guns cover bow and stern. Twelve cannon below with hinged covers. Crown pennant at masthead in all conditions underway.

**Captain's Cabin and Officers** — Full-width stern cabin; captain's chart table, commission papers, and a lockable iron Captain's Locker (DC 18). Three-cabin officer suite forward. Rating officer's writing desk amidships where inspection paperwork is drafted and sealed.

**Crew Quarters** — Twenty hammock berths forward lower deck. Working galley with copper range amidships.

**Below the Waterline** — Magazine aft (two keys: Ault and sealed at Harwick Admiralty). Inspection bond store (seized cargo pending determination). Prisoner hold with iron grating and six individual cages.

## Crew

24 ratings from Harwick's naval pool. Three Patrol-Lieutenant watch officers. Warrant gunner, warrant carpenter. Two rating inspectors — specifically trained in cargo inspection and manifest verification — who board alongside the armed party during enforcement actions.

## Operational Notes

**Enforcement posture:** The *Warrant* positions before hailing — comes downwind and close before any communication is attempted. A vessel that sees twelve gun ports and a rating officer with a speaking trumpet has already made its calculation.

**Privateer letters:** Ault can issue on his own commission authority, recorded in Harwick's admiralty ledger on the next resupply.

**Prize posture:** Detaining the *Warrant* is noticed within a week at Harwick. The Admiralty's response is a sealed order to every Crown vessel in the Strait.
