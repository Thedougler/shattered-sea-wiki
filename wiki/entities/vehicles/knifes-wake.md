# The *Knife's Wake*

> [!read-aloud]
> A low cutter comes on with patched tan canvas and a tired brown hull, the kind of working boat that should smell of fish and wet rope. Then the gunports open. Black-painted teeth show along the rail, boarding hooks come up from behind the bulwark, and the false trade pennant drops before the real flag rises: a narrow black streamer cut like a knife wound.

---

## Overview

The *Knife's Wake* is a Tier 1 pirate cutter built for ambush, intimidation, and clean withdrawal rather than honest freight or naval battle. She passes at distance as a worn coastal trader. Up close, the disguise fails on purpose — her gunports are too well-cut, her rail too clear of clutter, and her deck crew too ready to move.

She is a small-water predator. In reef channels, fog, harbour approaches, and the last hour before dusk, the *Knife's Wake* is exactly the wrong ship to let come alongside. In open water against a Crown patrol vessel or a properly crewed brigantine, she runs.

## Purpose

The *Knife's Wake* takes small valuable prizes: payroll chests, passenger jewels, locked dispatch boxes, contraband, spell components, officers worth ransoming, and cargo whose owner cannot safely report the loss.

Her best attack is a short deception — approaches under false colours, fires one disabling volley, and boards before the other crew finds its discipline.

## Layout

**Weather Deck** — Kept clear for rapid boarding. Four light cannons sit amidships behind removable false cargo screens. Swivel positions at bow and stern. Boarding hooks and weighted lines stowed under the rail.

**Aft Cabin** — One narrow bunk, bolted chart shelf, locked strongbox, rack of false pennants. Real papers in oilcloth below a removable sole plank.

**Below Decks** — Cramped crew hammocks forward, powder and shot amidships, plunder cages aft.

## Operational Notes

**Ambush posture:** Prefers dusk, fog, port congestion, shoals, and damaged targets. First volley aims at sails, rudder, and morale.

**Weakness:** Cannot win a sustained gun duel. A disciplined patrol cutter that keeps distance and refuses the boarding angle has the advantage.

**Legal risk:** The hull is not registered cleanly, and several fittings likely came from ships whose owners still have friends in port offices.
