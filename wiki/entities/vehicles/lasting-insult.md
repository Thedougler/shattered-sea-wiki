# Lasting Insult

> [!read-aloud]
> A courier-rigged sloop sits tied off in South Harbour impound, worn enough to look abandoned at a glance. Paint peels in strips along the waterline, the figurehead has weathered down toward featureless wood, and the rigging has been patched too many times by too many different hands. Two empty gun mounts show on the rail. Beneath the paint at the bow, an older name still ghosts through: *Tessarine Grace*.

## Current State

The *Lasting Insult* has sat impounded for three years at South Harbour in [[port-tidefall|Port Tidefall]], third pier from the end, no flag flying. She is structurally sound but visibly worn, with tired rigging reducing her top speed until refitted. Clearing the debt and berthing arrears costs 374 gp total.

## Overview

A Tier 1 sloop with a courier hull: fast for her size, low-profile, and light on cargo. She looks rougher than she is. The hull is sound, the sails are usable, and two functional cannons remain aboard unmounted in the hold.

## History

Built about fourteen years ago, she served courier routes before passing into private freight work out of Tidefall. By the time the current paperwork settled, the ship had been folded into a three-year impound case rather than sold off. She now exists as a live opportunity on the [[harbourmasters-office|Harbourmaster's Office]] board and as minor Crown port leverage.

## Current Notes

The stern bunk hides an undocumented cavity behind a false panel, large enough for about 50 pounds of cargo. One repaired foresail tear is still holding. Two 6-pound cannons are lashed in the forward hold; one carriage is repairable, one needs replacement.
