# The Saltwright

> [!read-aloud]
> A two-masted merchant brig rides broad and low in the water, her hull worked pale grey-brown by years of salt and weather. Nothing about her is ornamental: no figurehead, no bright rail, no paint left where the sea could take it. Below, the smell is brine, old cedar, and the cold deep water pressing through the seams. Every timber sounds used.

A two-masted merchant brig out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]], built for trade rather than display. Old, plain, and thoroughly practical: a deep hold, a small captain's cabin, and no space wasted on comfort or prestige. [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] has captained her on the Midchain cargo run for eleven years.

## Current Status

Departed with [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] at dawn after Session 02. No longer the party's vessel — the party now commands the [[hcs-surety|Uncertainty]] (ex-HCS Surety). Beaumont's whereabouts since are not established.

## Layout

**Weather Deck.** Plain and unadorned — a working surface for sail handling and loading. The stern is covered: a low deckhouse aft of the mainmast shelters the captain's quarters beneath it.

**Captain's Quarters.** Set into the covered stern at weather deck level. Small and practical. Beaumont keeps a lockbox under the bed.

**Gun Deck.** Middle deck, below the weather deck. Gun ports are plugged against the sea. The forward section has been converted to crew's mess and additional storage. Two 12-pound guns remain aboard, dismounted and lashed down aft.

**Cargo Hold.** Lowest deck. Roughly six feet of headroom. Cargo stacked tight among barrels, crates, and sacks. Two lanterns hang amidships. A gap above the waterline in the aft corner lets in fresh air and a sliver of outside light.
