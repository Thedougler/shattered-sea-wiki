# Ships

Vessels the party sails, has sailed, or should recognise on sight.

---

**[[saltwright|The Saltwright]]** — Hard-used two-masted merchant brig captained by [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]]. Old, plain, thoroughly practical. Party's early ship.

**[[hcs-surety|HCS Surety / Uncertainty]]** — Small Dravosi Crown patrol cutter; taken as a prize in Session 01. Renamed *Uncertainty* after Session 03 and currently docked at La Vasca.

**[[red-lady|The Red Lady]]** — [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s flagship. A privateer-rigged brigantine that led five ships into the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] and did not come back.

**[[the-narrow|The Narrow]]** — Scout sloop, second ship of the fleet. Captained by Maren. Built to go where other ships couldn't.

**[[heft|The Heft]]** — Heavy brig, third ship of the fleet. Built to take punishment and hold position.

**[[fernen|The Fernen]]** — Two-masted brigantine, fourth ship. Fitted for long-range independent operation.

**[[vestra|The Vestra]]** — [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s ship. Small two-masted brig, Black-Jaw family heirloom. Destroyed before the campaign begins.

**[[the-tallow-bell|The Tallow Bell]]** — Waveservant shrine sloop moving clergy, tribute records, and ritual supplies between ports.

**[[vethka|Vethka]]** — Grung war proa. Low, resin-sealed, double-outrigger. Blue-caste scouts navigate; purple-caste warriors raid.

**[[knifes-wake|The Knife's Wake]]** — Tier 1 armed pirate cutter built for ambush, boarding, and reef-channel escapes.

**[[the-bad-receipt|The Bad Receipt]]** — Tier 2 armed pirate schooner out of Kalowe; built around false paperwork and selective boarding actions.

**[[the-glass-debt|The Glass Debt]]** — Tier 3 armed pirate frigate built to break convoy discipline and take protected prizes.

**[[the-velvet-noose|The Velvet Noose]]** — Tier 4 pirate man-of-war built for fleet-command piracy.

**[[the-quorum|The Quorum]]** — The Chain Council's dispatch sloop.

**[[hcs-constancy|HCS Constancy]]** — Dravosi Crown heavy frigate. Standard Crown patrol vessel. Inspects, boards, impounds.

**[[hcs-relentless|HCS Relentless]]** — Tier 1 patrol cutter; more heavily armed than the HCS Surety.

**[[hcs-warrant|HCS Warrant]]** — Crown brigantine; carries sealed orders and Admiralty boarding authority.

**[[hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]]** — Three-deck Dravosi Crown Ship of the Line; 96 guns; prior flagship now superseded by HCS Sovereign.

**[[hcs-sovereign|HCS Sovereign]]** — The Dravosi Crown's first-rate flagship. 104 guns across three gun decks. The most powerful warship in the Shattered Sea.

---

**[[tessarine-amberreach|Tessarine Amberreach]]** — Concordat factor vessel; carries bonded cargo between Crown Islands ports.

**[[tessarine-silkvane|Tessarine Silkvane]]** — Concordat dispatch sloop. Pale grey hull, well-maintained.

---

**[[nimmik-vollask|Nimmik Vollask]]** — Bright orange, alarmingly boxy Tier 2 cargo brig crewed entirely by gnomes. Pulls alongside, takes your order, serves complimentary coffee and meatballs.

**[[brass-cat|The Brass Cat]]** — Copper-sheathed two-masted schooner with brass-framed glass flush in the deck amidships.

**[[dead-lady|The Dead Lady]]** — Always upwind, regardless of actual wind direction. Does not pursue. Already where you are going.

**[[greyteeth-runner|The Greyteeth Runner]]** — Lean single-masted sloop, shallow-drafted, plain hull, no figurehead.

**[[lasting-insult|The Lasting Insult]]** — Courier-rigged sloop currently in South Harbour Impound.

**[[ironmantle|Ironmantle]]** — Remnant iron-hulled vessel; current location unknown.
