# Tessarine Factor *Amberreach*

> [!read-aloud]
> The ship comes in low and wide, her pale grey hull sitting deep in the water with the deliberate weight of something built to carry rather than chase. The beam is broader than a brigantine has any business being, the freeboard heavy, and the cargo ports along her sides are sealed and numbered in neat stencilled figures. A Tessarine pennant flies at the masthead — not the Crown's military pennant, just the Concordat's registry mark rendered in cloth. There are gun ports, and they are open, and no one is particularly rushing toward them.

## Overview

A Tier 3 Tessarine cargo galleon on the fixed commercial schedule between [[calveno|Calveno]] and the western approaches through the Central Strait. She runs on a fixed schedule — departures logged three ports in advance, arrival windows insured through Tessarine underwriting, losses settled at ninety percent of assessed value within forty days. Merchants along the route do not need to like the Concordat. They need their cargo to arrive.

Senior Factor [[oriana-becht|Oriana Becht]] holds authority to sign instruments aboard in the field — credit extensions up to 2,000 gp, route underwriting renewals, salvage financing, and provisional letters of cargo obligation.

## Layout (Summary)

**Weather Deck.** Cargo logistics focus: davits, winches, cargo booms. Tessarine pennant and factor's authority pennant both fly.

**Gun Deck / Factor Suite.** 16 cannon, widely spaced for cargo movement. Factor suite at stern: Becht's cabin (Captain's Locker DC 18), contract office, sealed correspondence.

**Crew/Passenger Deck.** 60 hammocks forward; galley; six private merchant passenger cabins aft.

**Hold.** Four sealed cargo compartments; numbered sealed manifests. One reserved for Tessarine bonded cargo with legally significant wax seals. Forward compartment is the sealed bond store.

## Commercial Posture

The *Amberreach* does not need to fire to compel compliance — she compels compliance by arriving on schedule. The gun ports are open as a reminder that the Concordat has not forgotten the world has pirates. She does not board; she is boarded, and survives it.

**The bonded compartment:** Sealed manifests are legally protected from Crown inspection under the commercial treaty between the Concordat and the Dravosi Admiralty. Both parties resent this. Neither has found a way to dissolve it.
