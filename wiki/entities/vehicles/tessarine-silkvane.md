# Tessarine Courier *Silkvane*

A Tessarine Concordat dispatch sloop, single-masted, built for speed over capacity. The hull is pale grey, well-maintained, with a Concordat registry mark stencilled in black on the transom. A Tessarine pennant flies underway. Two gun mounts. The rigging is clean and recently dressed. Nothing about the exterior suggests cargo.

Below decks: a lockable stern cabin with a small writing desk and an iron-bolted document chest, four hammock hooks forward, and a divided hold — forward section open cargo, aft section locked.

## Below Decks

**Stern cabin:** Lockable; writing desk; document chest (iron, bolted to floor — DC 16 to force). Used by couriers and travelling Tessarine personnel.

**Hold:** Divided — forward section open cargo; aft section locked (DC 14 Thieves' Tools) for sealed correspondence and small valuables in transit.

## Notes

**Registry:** Tessarine pennant underway. Concordat registry mark on the transom. Both are removable.

**Crew:** Minimum 2 for short-haul Scatter runs. Full crew of 8 for longer routes or when carrying personnel. Concordat employees, not naval ratings.

**Bastion:** No facilities installed. 4 units available. Locked aft hold functions narratively as concealed storehouse but is not a formal facility.
