# The *Bad Receipt*

> [!read-aloud]
> A two-masted schooner cuts low through the channel under patched cream canvas and a merchant pennant that looks correct until it matters. Her hull is black-green, stained like old ink, with removable brass registry plates bolted over older scars. When the side screens drop, cannon mouths show in a neat row beneath cargo netting, and the figurehead resolves into a carved clerk holding a ship's hook behind his back.

## Overview

The *Bad Receipt* is a Tier 2 armed pirate schooner built for [[midchain|Midchain]] work: false papers, quick pursuit, selective violence, and retreat through channels where Crown captains start arguing with their own charts. She was refitted at [[kalowe|Kalowe]] from a seized courier hull.

She is not a slaughter ship. Her captain wants cargo, letters of marque, ransomable officers, and anything whose owner cannot report the theft without explaining why it was aboard. The crew prefers a disabled rudder, a terrified deck, and a signed transfer under duress.

## Operational Notes

**Preferred hunting ground:** [[midchain|Midchain]] approaches to the [[central-strait|Central Strait]], eastbound traffic that has already paid Tidefall fees.

**Opening move:** Raise false colours, ask for assistance, present a paperwork problem, fire into rigging once the target slows.

**Escape plan:** Fog Cannon, Ghost Keel, and reef pilots.

**Legal posture:** Maintains three competing paper identities — distressed courier, Chain Council survey vessel, and privateer under a commission whose issuing island denies knowing it.

> [!dm]
> As an enemy: Tier 2 pirate escalation after the party has outgrown cutter-scale threats.
> As a prize: complete mid-campaign ship bastion — useful, compromised, and immediately wanted by somebody.
> As a contact: buys silence, sells papers, and knows which captains carry cargo they cannot report stolen.
