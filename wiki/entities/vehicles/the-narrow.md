# The *Narrow*

![[Maren.webp]]

## Overview

A scout sloop — narrow-hulled, single-masted, the lightest ship in [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]]. Long on the waterline relative to her beam, low freeboard, built for speed and shallow survey work. The hull was painted dark grey. Two swivel gun mounts only; she was not built to fight. She sat low and quiet in the water and made very little wake at speed.

Below decks: crew hammocks, a chart table bolted to the forward bulkhead, and a shallow hold fitted with diving equipment and survey gear. Built to go places other ships couldn't and come back with information.

Second ship of the fleet. Captained by [[maren|Maren]].

## The Sinking

Lost simultaneously with the rest of [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] when [[umberlee|Umberlee]] opened [[the-drowned-maw|the Drowned Maw]] beneath them. All hands lost.
