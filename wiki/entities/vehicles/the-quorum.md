# The *Quorum*

> [!read-aloud]
> A single-masted cutter sits low at the Kalowe dock, hull tarred black to the rail with no livery and no figurehead. The waterline is copper-sheathed, the rigging is tight and spare, and a plain reef-pilot's pennant hangs from the mast. She looks like a working boat that has been given very good rope.

## Overview

A single-masted cutter, 48 feet on the waterline, built at the [[kalowe|Kalowe]] dry dock and kept by the [[chain-council|Chain Council]] as a dispatch vessel. No flag, no figurehead, no painted name — the *Quorum* is stenciled in small letters on the transom and nowhere else. Hull tarred black with a copper-sheathed waterline.

She moves what the Council cannot trust to ordinary couriers: sealed commission documents, vote records, draft proposals, representatives who need to arrive without announcing themselves. A Kalowe pilot on standing retainer navigates the back channels and reef passages that Dravosi patrol routes avoid.

The name is procedural. The Chain Council only has authority when enough affected representatives are present. The boat named *Quorum* is the Council's reminder that legitimacy is not a fixed thing.

## Layout

**Weather Deck.** Mast and boom forward, wheel aft. Two covered swivel mounts amidships. Working space is the whole deck.

**Stern Cabin.** Low-headroom compartment sealed with iron latches. Locked chest bolted to deck; fold-down chart table; two bench seats. Three people maximum. Not comfortable.

**Below Decks.** Shallow hold — water casks, lamp stores, sailcloth, rope. Crew hammocks slung forward. No galley.
