# *Vethka*

> [!read-aloud]
> Low on the water — lower than any cutter you have seen. The hull is carved from a single timber dark as heartwood, sealed with amber resin that catches the light like wet skin. Two outrigger floats ride the surface to port and starboard, lashed to the hull with braided vine cord. The bow is carved into a blunt open jaw, painted in purple and blue in alternating stripe-bands, and a line of small bones hangs from the prow to the first outrigger crossbeam. There is no wheel, no cabin, no raised deck. Twelve Grung can crouch below the rail without showing a silhouette. You smell resin first. Underneath that, something sour and sharp that is not the sea.

## Overview

*Vethka* is the Grung term for this class of vessel — not a proper name but a caste-designation: roughly, *the waterline that moves toward you*. Every raiding proa built to the standard pattern is called *vethka* by those who crew them.

The hull is single-piece ironpillar hardwood from the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], adze-worked by orange-caste artisans, sealed inside and out with boiled tree resin. The result does not absorb water, does not swell in rain, and sheds spray without effort. From more than half a mile away on a dark night, a *vethka* running low with no sail set reads as debris. This is by design.

## Caste and Crew

The *vethka* is a blue-and-purple operation. Blue-caste scouts navigate and pilot. Purple-caste warriors handle the assault. Orange-caste artisans build the vessel. The poison preparation bench — a narrow work surface sealed with flat resin, forward of amidships — is red-caste design. Purple-caste warriors touch it. No other caste does.

## Tactical Profile

> [!dm]
> In reef country, a *vethka* cannot be caught by anything with a keel. Grung Standing Leap means the crew is over the rail before the bow stops moving. The double outrigger lets it handle reef passages that would shred deeper hulls. Its threat is surprise, speed, and the poison bench — not a gun fight.
