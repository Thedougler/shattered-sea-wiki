## Predictions

1. **The Grung plot dominates the next session — but the party's intel has a critical gap.** Felix revealed 4 secondary attack points. The primary Mercatura site (Slaad summoning circle, packed ceiling, shoot-on-sight) is compartmentalized above his clearance. The party will plan around 4 targets and miss the fifth. This becomes the dramatic irony engine of the next session.
2. **The shortened timeline is a trap.** Party thinks they have 2 days from Felix's estimate. The Grung accelerated by ~half a day. The detonation will come sooner than expected.
3. **Kyzil snaps into tactical focus immediately if briefed.** He has been independently investigating his "foul wind" instinct — heard fragments about underground activity. Give the party the Grung intel and he becomes a CR 14 ally with observation-sharpened assessment. This is a massive resource if the party thinks to use it.
4. **Anzolo arrives during the crisis.** Within a day's sail, Tessarine tail behind him, Nona's attention on the bombing. His arrival at the safe house could expose the Passage's Calveno operations to the Tessarine at the worst moment. **Convergence arc:** Perrin's family thread collides with the operational crisis.
5. **Umberlee gossip creates crossfire for Delmar.** Harbor district Waveservants are watching. If Delmar operates visibly in the harbor during the Grung counter-operation, someone connects "scarlet coat" to "the man who owes Umberlee." His two identities (party member fighting Grung, debtor to a sea goddess) are about to collide in the same district.
6. **The whale is waiting for Perrin.** Bioluminescent shape in Calveno's harbor approach. When the party next takes a boat — which they will need to do to reach the Uncertainty or hit waterside attack points — Perrin gets his second contact. **Chekhov's gun.**
7. **Three toxin caches, one inventory.** The Canister evidence is accumulating below the surface. JC's silence holds but the physical proof is all in the same place now. One Grung-knowledgeable NPC examining the loot breaks it open.
8. **Verity Hollowell is searching smarter than the Crown.** Her hull-profile inquiry through the Hollowell factor network will reach Midchain contacts within the next week. The repaint doesn't help against someone who knows what a Surety-class cutter looks like.
9. **Grigori's network expanded.** Two miraculous recoveries in different noble courts. Seed these as background texture — a servant mentioning a wandering healer, a noble child who shouldn't have survived. Do not force.
10. **Delmar's abstinence and the Grung crisis.** The next session puts Delmar under extreme operational pressure while Umberlee gossip closes around him. Watch for what triggers him to drink again.
