# Lizardfolk

Medium reptilian humanoids with scaled hides, powerful jaws, and a cognitive architecture that prioritizes survival over sentiment. Lizardfolk evaluate before they react. They eat what's available. They build with what's there. Waste is the only sin that registers without translation.

In the Shattered Sea, lizardfolk occupy the tidal margins — mangrove coasts, river deltas, brackish flats where the water shifts between salt and fresh. Their settlements look temporary to outsiders. Most have stood for generations. Lizardfolk build low, build in water, and relocate without grief when the tide pattern changes. The structure served its purpose. The next one will too.

At sea, lizardfolk crew as hull-workers, salvagers, and underwater repair hands. They hold their breath for a quarter-hour, work in cold water without slowing, and their natural armor means fewer injuries from barnacle-crusted keels and broken hull planking. A ship with a lizardfolk hull-team puts in for careening half as often. [[bastian-crev|Bastian Crev]], captain of the [[loud-argument|*Loud Argument*]] in [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]], came up through salvage work before Fisk gave him a command.

## Table Traits

- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 30 ft.; swim speed equal to walking speed
- **Bite:** Natural melee weapon; 1d6 slashing on a hit.
- **Hold Breath:** Up to 15 minutes.
- **Hungry Jaws:** Once per long rest, bonus action bite attack. On hit, gain temporary HP equal to proficiency bonus.
- **Natural Armor:** Unarmored AC = 13 + Dexterity modifier. Compatible with shields.
- **Nature's Intuition:** Proficiency in two of the following: Animal Handling, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Stealth, or Survival.

## Culture

Lizardfolk pragmatism is real, but the word "cold" is wrong. A lizardfolk parent will die for a clutch without hesitation — the calculation is instant, not absent. What warm-blooded species read as emotional flatness is efficiency. A lizardfolk who sees a wounded ally doesn't feel distress and then act; they skip the first step. The help arrives at the same speed. The facial expression doesn't.

**Learned warmth.** Lizardfolk who spend years embedded in warm-blooded crews develop something that outsiders call emotional range. Whether it's learned performance or genuine feeling is a question lizardfolk themselves cannot answer and most stop asking. A lizardfolk who laughs at a friend's joke after ten years of friendship — is that mimicry or joy? The lizardfolk doesn't know. The laugh is real either way.

**Tidal settlements.** Lizardfolk communities organize around the tide cycle rather than the solar day. Activity peaks at low tide (building, foraging, crafting) and drops at high tide (rest, egg-tending, oral tradition). Visitors who arrive at high tide find a quiet village. Visitors who arrive at low tide find organized industry that puts most shipyards to shame.

**Crafting.** Lizardfolk waste nothing. Bone becomes tools. Hide becomes armor. Shell becomes currency. A lizardfolk artisan produces work that is ugly by human aesthetic standards and functionally superior by every other measure. The rare lizardfolk who learns decorative craft from another culture produces objects that unsettle — technically perfect ornamentation applied with an efficiency that strips the warmth out of the art form.

## Naming

Lizardfolk true-names in their own tongue are descriptive and functional: *Holds-Still, Sees-Under-Water, Bites-Twice.* When working among other species, lizardfolk adopt a **use-name** — something pronounceable by warm-blooded tongues, often borrowed from whatever culture they're embedded in. The use-name is not a mask. It's a tool, and lizardfolk do not distinguish between a useful tool and a real one.

Lizardfolk place-names follow Malay and Austronesian roots when rendered in Common: open syllables, nasal consonants, tidal or water references. *Tanjung, Teluk, Rawa, Pasang.*
