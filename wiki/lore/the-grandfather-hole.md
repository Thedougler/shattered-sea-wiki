# The Grandfather Hole

> *The water turns an unnatural, freezing black. Reef divers know better than to dive there.*

A legendary underwater sinkhole somewhere in the eastern [[midchain|Midchain]], avoided by every experienced reef diver out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and feared in port taverns throughout the region.

## The Myth

Midchain reef divers know to mark and avoid [[sawek|Sawek]] lairs. But tavern tales speak of a single, massive sinkhole where the water turns an unnatural, freezing black. The sea around it drops temperature faster than any natural current can account for.

## What Is Known

The Grandfather Hole is territory claimed by an ancient [[sawek|Sawek]] large enough to pull a Tier 3 galleon down by the keel. At the bottom lies a century of undisturbed shipwrecks: a tangled, rotting reef of shattered hulls, lost cargo, and scattered gold accumulated over generations of the beast's predation.

The ambient magic of the Blue Hole has animated the drowned sailors trapped within the wreck-reef — any salvagers must contend with the beast above and undead crews below.

## The Catch

- No reliable charting of the sinkhole's location exists.
- Surviving or bypassing the ancient Sawek.
- Fighting through animated undead sailors in lightless, freezing water.

No successful salvage has ever been recorded.

## Connections

- [[sawek|Sawek]] — the Grandfather Hole is the confirmed lair of an apex specimen
- [[kalowe|Kalowe]] — reef diving community maintains the strongest oral tradition; divers mark its approximate quadrant but refuse to dive it
- [[midchain|Midchain]] — sinkhole sits somewhere in the eastern arc; exact location unknown and deliberately uncharted
