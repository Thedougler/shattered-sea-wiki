# The Tithe of the Bitch Queen

> *Taking a single coin invites Umberlee's direct, personal spite. The sea follows you home.*

A legendary collection of divine treasure scattered across the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] seafloor — the physical accumulation of beautiful things [[umberlee|Umberlee]] claimed from mortal ships over centuries.

## The Myth

Before the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] was stolen, it was widely believed that Umberlee maintained a physical collection of the most beautiful treasures her storms had claimed. Not a formal hoard — an unconscious curation. The finest things the sea had taken, kept.

## What Is Known

The Tithe is scattered across the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] seafloor, not consolidated in one location. Unblemished jewels, pristine statues, gold bars that never tarnish — items with apparent divine preservation. Accessible only through tidal caves that breach the surface during the lowest tides of the decade.

## The Catch

Taking a single coin from the Tithe invites [[umberlee|Umberlee's]] direct, personal spite:

- Sailors who possess a piece cannot sail without drawing unnatural localized squalls that follow their ship.
- Squalls persist until the item is returned to saltwater.
- No theological workaround is documented in Waveservant records.
- The curse is on the object, not the bearer — transferring the item transfers the problem.

## Relationship to the Pearl of Souls

The Tithe predates the theft of the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]]. The Pearl's theft has complicated the theological picture: the Tithe represents what Umberlee passively accumulates; the Pearl represents something actively stolen from her. Waveservants will not discuss whether the Pearl belongs to the Tithe in any formal sense.

## Connections

- [[umberlee|Umberlee]] — the divine authority; the squall-curse is hers
- [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] — the seafloor region where the cache is located
- [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] — the stolen artifact; its theft contextualizes the Tithe myth
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] — his theft of the Pearl is the most famous recent instance of taking from Umberlee's collection
