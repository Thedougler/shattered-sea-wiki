# The Mud Pits — DM Notes

The Mud Pits are a sandbox attraction designed to drain the party's resources and win them local reputation. [[bala-tane|Bala Tane]] calibrates the fight to the crowd's entertainment, not to party safety — she will send them enough to make it interesting, which means enough to genuinely threaten them.

**Public page:** [[the-mud-pits|The Mud Pits]]

## Running the Fight

**Goal:** exhaust the party (hit dice, spell slots, resources) while giving them something satisfying to win. Don't kill anyone — the crowd would actually hate it.

**Format:** Brawl to unconsciousness (0 HP). No death saving throws. Lethal damage does not apply unless a fighter deliberately chooses it — Bala stops that and ejects the fighter.

**Mud effects:** Difficult terrain throughout. Any creature knocked prone must succeed on a DC 12 STR save to stand as a bonus action or spend full movement to rise. Ranged attacks from outside the pit have half cover on targets inside.

## Calibration Table

| Party Size | Base Matchup | Crowd Reaction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 PC | 2–3 dock bruisers (CR 1/8) | Curious; picks sides early |
| 2 PCs | 3–4 bruisers or 1 veteran + 2 bruisers | Invested; bets split 60/40 |
| 3 PCs | 4–5 bruisers or 2 veterans | Loud; lots of side money |
| Full party (4–5) | 6–8 opponents or special matchup | Frenzy; Bala watching closely |

**Bruiser:** Guard or Thug statblock (MM). Remove lethal special attacks.
**Veteran:** Use Veteran (MM). Bala uses these for upset matchups she wants to be close.

Increase difficulty by adding one opponent at a time if the party is winning too cleanly. Bala calls it "Kalowe rules." The crowd knows what it means.

## Opponent Roster

**Standard card** — Mixed orc, human, tortle dockworkers who fight on weekends. Reliable. Underpaid. Good sports about losing.

**Vort Kessen (champion)** — Retired orc sailor, late fifties, built like he was assembled from ship timbers. Use Gladiator (MM). Fights defensively and patiently; wins by outlasting people. Beat Vort and Bala pays the champion's cut (50gp bonus) and the crowd buys drinks all evening. Vort buys his own drink and nods.

**The Mud Moucheron (special event)** — Bala occasionally books a [[moucheron|Moucheron]] between contracts. Use Moucheron statblock without proboscis drain (honour rules). The crowd loses their minds. The party will have heard the warnings. This is technically safe. Bala finds the look on their faces very satisfying.

**Escaped Giant Crab (chaos event)** — Once per campaign, a giant crab from the lagoon gets into the pit. Bala lets the fight happen for two rounds before calling it. No entry fee refunds.

## Betting Mechanics

Bala's runners set opening odds on her read. If the party looks capable, they open as slight underdogs (2:1). Upsets pay more.

Party can bet on themselves (max 20gp per fighter through the runners; higher bets go directly to Bala and she will remember). Side bets between spectators are unregulated.

**The crowd as resource:** after a win, bettors who won on the party become friendly. The harbour boss has a runner here most nights — a win against a stiff matchup gets the party a meeting. Vort, if beaten, tells them one true thing about Kalowe he has never told anyone, because he respects the outcome.

## Triggers

- Party arrives in Kalowe and hears crowd noise from the second island
- [[the-flat-water|Reweti Kahurangi]] mentions it at the Flat Water
- The party has money and wants a fight

## Bala's Tell

When she says "Kalowe rules" before announcing the opponent count, she is adding a fighter. The crowd knows what it means.

## Consequences

- **Win:** local reputation in Kalowe, crowd goodwill, possible harbour boss introduction
- **Lose honourably:** Bala offers a rematch at a discount; the crowd is still warm
- **Refuse after showing up:** Bala remembers; the crowd boos
