# Carouser

**Source:** Astarion's Book of Hungers

You grew to adulthood in the beating heart of a large city. You spent countless evenings in taverns, playhouses, parlors, and gambling dens, savoring all the city had to offer. You're a natural at interacting with people to learn their secrets, whether over a high-stakes game or at a high-class soiree.

**Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Intelligence, Charisma
**Feat:** Tireless Reveler
**Skill Proficiencies:** Deception and Persuasion
**Tool Proficiency:** Choose one kind of Gaming Set
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Dagger, Gaming Set (same as above), Fine Clothes, Glass Bottle, Mirror, Perfume, Pouch, Tinderbox, 19 GP; or (B) 50 GP
