# Guide

**Source:** Player's Handbook

You came of age outdoors, far from settled lands. Your home was anywhere you chose to spread your bedroll. There are wonders in the wilderness — strange monsters, pristine forests and streams, overgrown ruins of great halls once trod by giants — and you learned to fend for yourself as you explored them.

**Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Constitution, Wisdom
**Feat:** Magic Initiate (Druid)
**Skill Proficiencies:** Stealth and Survival
**Tool Proficiency:** Cartographer's Tools
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Shortbow, 20 Arrows, Cartographer's Tools, Bedroll, Quiver, Tent, Traveler's Clothes, 3 GP; or (B) 50 GP
