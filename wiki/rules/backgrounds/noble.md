# Noble

**Source:** Player's Handbook

You were raised in a castle, surrounded by wealth, power, and privilege. Your family of minor aristocrats ensured that you received a first-class education. Your time at court taught you a great deal about leadership.

**Ability Scores:** Strength, Intelligence, Charisma
**Feat:** Skilled
**Skill Proficiencies:** History and Persuasion
**Tool Proficiency:** Choose one kind of Gaming Set
**Equipment:** Choose A or B: (A) Gaming Set (same as above), Fine Clothes, Perfume, 29 GP; or (B) 50 GP
