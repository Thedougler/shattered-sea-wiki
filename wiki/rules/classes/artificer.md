## Core Tactics

- **Replicate Magic Item** is the signature feature — guaranteed access to items without relying on loot.
- Tool proficiencies (3 default + subclass) can solve problems without expending resources.
- Half caster with access to healing spells; spell list combines Cleric and Wizard options.
