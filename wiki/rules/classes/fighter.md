# Fighter

*Class — Player's Handbook (2024)*

**Primary Ability:** Strength or Dexterity · **Hit Die:** d10 · **Saves:** Strength, Constitution · **Armor:** All armor + Shields · **Weapons:** Simple and Martial · **Spellcasting:** None (except Eldritch Knight)

---

## Core Features

| Level | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fighting Style (feat choice), Second Wind (2/SR), Weapon Mastery (3 weapons) |
| 2 | Action Surge (1/SR), Tactical Mind |
| 3 | Subclass |
| 5 | Extra Attack (×2), Tactical Shift |
| 9 | Indomitable (1/LR), Tactical Master |
| 11 | Two Extra Attacks (×3 total) |
| 13 | Studied Attacks, Indomitable (2/LR) |
| 17 | Action Surge (2/SR), Indomitable (3/LR) |
| 20 | Three Extra Attacks (×4 total) |

**Action Surge** — Take one extra action on your turn (not Magic). 1/SR; 2/SR at level 17.
**Extra Attack** — Attack twice per Attack action (L5); three times (L11); four times (L20).
**Indomitable** — Reroll a failed saving throw, adding Fighter level as bonus. 1/LR (scales to 3/LR).
