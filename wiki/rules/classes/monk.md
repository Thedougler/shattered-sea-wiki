# Monk

*Class — Player's Handbook (2024)*

**Primary Ability:** Dexterity and Wisdom · **Hit Die:** d8 · **Saves:** Strength, Dexterity · **Armor:** None · **Weapons:** Simple melee + Light martial melee

AC (unarmored): 10 + DEX + WIS

---

## Martial Arts Die

| Level | Die |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | d6 |
| 5–10 | d8 |
| 11–16 | d10 |
| 17–20 | d12 |

Focus Points = Monk level (starting L2). Speed bonus: +10 ft at L2, scaling to +30 ft at L17.

---

## Core Features

| Level | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1 | Martial Arts, Unarmored Defense |
| 2 | Monk's Focus (Flurry/Patient Defense/Step of the Wind), Uncanny Metabolism |
| 3 | Deflect Attacks, Subclass |
| 5 | Extra Attack, Stunning Strike (1 FP, CON save → Stunned) |
| 6 | Empowered Strikes (unarmed → Force) |
| 7 | Evasion |
| 10 | Heightened Focus |
| 18 | Superior Defense (3 FP → Resistance all except Force) |
| 20 | Body and Mind (DEX + WIS +4, max 25) |

**Uncanny Metabolism (1/LR)** — On Initiative: regain all Focus Points + HP.
**Stunning Strike (1 FP)** — After hitting: DC (8 + prof + WIS) Constitution save or Stunned until start of your next turn.
