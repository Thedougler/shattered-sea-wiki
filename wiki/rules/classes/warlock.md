# Warlock

*Class — Player's Handbook (2024)*

**Primary Ability:** Charisma · **Hit Die:** d8 · **Saves:** Wisdom, Charisma · **Armor:** Light · **Weapons:** Simple · **Spellcasting:** CHA (prepared, Pact Magic)

---

## Pact Magic

Short-rest recharging spell slots — all slots are the same level.

| Level | Slots | Slot Level | Invocations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1st | 1 |
| 2 | 2 | 1st | 3 |
| 5–10 | 2 | 3rd–5th | 5–7 |
| 11–16 | 3 | 5th | 7–9 |
| 17–20 | 4 | 5th | 9–10 |
