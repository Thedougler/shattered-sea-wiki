# Prone

While you have the Prone condition, you experience the following effects.

**Restricted Movement.** Your only movement options are to crawl or to spend movement equal to half your Speed to right yourself (standing up). If your Speed is 0, you can't right yourself.

**Attacks Affected.** You have Disadvantage on attack rolls. Attack rolls against you have Advantage if the attacker is within 5 feet of you; otherwise, that attack roll has Disadvantage.
