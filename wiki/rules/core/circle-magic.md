# Circle Magic

Multiple spellcasters combine efforts to empower a single spell. Introduced in *Heroes of Faerun* (2024). No resource cost beyond spell slots and Actions.

> [!dm]
> Extremely powerful and potentially game-breaking. Limit by keeping NPC spellcasters rare or unhelpful — even level 1 NPC Wizards acting as Secondary Casters dramatically amplify the party's output.

## How It Works

1. The **primary caster** takes the Magic action and begins casting a qualifying spell (casting time of one Action or 1+ minutes — no Reactions or Bonus Actions).
2. Primary caster selects a **Circle Casting Option** (see below).
3. **Secondary casters** are identified. Must have the Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature. Species traits and feat-based spells do not qualify.
4. Each secondary caster takes the Magic action on their turn to empower the spell (spending a spell slot if required by the option).
5. When the **final secondary caster** takes the Magic action, the spell takes effect.

Primary caster must maintain Concentration throughout. No stated time limit — the process can span multiple turns.

## Circle Casting Options

Only one option is selected per casting.

**Augment.** Adds 1,000 ft. to the spell's range, up to a 1-mile maximum. Spells with a Self range are unaffected. Applies to the range value, not the area in parentheses.

**Distribute.** Transfers Concentration on the spell to a willing secondary caster. Primary caster may then maintain Concentration on a different spell. The spell continues until any one Concentrating caster drops it.

**Expand.** Increases the area of a spell. Secondary casters expend a spell slot (any level) to contribute. No stated maximum area increase.

**Prolong.** Extends the spell's duration by 1 hour per secondary caster, up to 24 hours. Secondary casters expend a spell slot (any level). Most powerful on non-Concentration spells (Mage Armor, Blink, Mirror Image, Fire Shield, Foresight).

**Safeguard.** Excludes designated 5-ft. squares from the spell's area of effect. Allows party members to be safely inside an area spell. Works on ongoing spells (Wall of Fire, Hunger of Hadar).

**Supplant.** Secondary casters expend a spell slot of equal or higher level to reduce the spell's gp material component cost (50 gp per secondary caster). No secondary casters needed for spells without gp components.

## Circle Spells and Metamagic

Metamagic can be combined with Circle Casting Options. Interaction order for mathematical changes to range or duration is ambiguous in the rules — adjudicate at the table.
