# Siege Rules

A combat overlay for battles where normal 5e combat cannot represent the scale: armies besieging a fortress, enormous monsters attacking a city, or conflicts with too many participants to run individually. Does not replace character combat — adds a large-scale **Siege Phase** at initiative count 20, resolving before lair actions.

## Core Procedure

- Each side has AC, HP, attack bonus, damage bonus, abilities, and Siege Actions.
- Siege Phase happens at initiative count 20 (wins ties, before lair actions).
- At the start of each Siege Phase, each party rolls 1d20. Higher roll chooses and resolves a Siege Action first; lower roll follows. Alternate until both sides have used their available actions.
- Each party can take up to 3 Siege Actions per Siege Phase.
- Each Siege Action can be used only once per round.
- GM chooses enemy Siege Actions. Players collectively choose allied Siege Actions.
- **Siege Attack Actions:** deal damage on hit; half damage on miss.
- **Siege Effect Actions:** apply debuff or disable on hit; no effect on miss (limited uses not consumed).
- Natural 20s do not double Siege damage dice.

## Initiative Order with Siege Phase

1. Creatures above initiative 20 act.
2. Siege Phase resolves.
3. One available lair action resolves (if applicable).
4. Creatures below initiative 20 act.

## Design Notes

Siege rules work best when:

- The party's individual actions can meaningfully affect the siege totals (disabling siege actions, protecting key NPCs).
- Named NPC deaths have mechanical weight (Blaze of Glory: their last action resolves immediately before they die).
- The encounter's end state is not "kill everything" but a specific objective (ritual completed, creature retreats, city saves).
