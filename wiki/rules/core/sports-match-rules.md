# Sports Match Rules

5e-based rules for running a sports match as a combat encounter. The sport itself is intentionally vague — flavor it to fit the setting. Share with players the session before the match.

## Core Rules

- Goal: score more points within the time limit.
- A match lasts **10 rounds** (or 20 for a longer match).
- Score by shooting the ball into the enemy goal.
- A **15-foot-radius Goal Zone** surrounds each goal. Only the goalie may enter it.
- Each team designates one **goalie** who cannot leave the Goal Zone.
- After a goal: defending goalie takes control, all players return to starting positions, match continues.
- **Tie-breaker:** Three penalty shots per team.

## Actions

**Pass** *(Action)* — Pass to a teammate up to 30 feet. Dexterity attack + proficiency vs DC 10.

**Shoot** *(Action)* — Launch toward the goal (max 30 feet). Strength attack + proficiency determines distance traveled (10–30 ft on roll of 10–26+).

**Tackle** *(Action)* — Force carrier to drop ball and push 10 feet. Strength (Athletics) contested by Athletics or Acrobatics.

**Steal** *(Action)* — Take the ball without force. Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) vs Insight or Acrobatics.

## Bonus Actions

**Pick Up** *(Bonus Action)* — Pick up a ball within 5 feet when not in any creature's possession.

## Reactions

**Intercept** *(Reaction)* — Stop a pass/shot through your reach. Dexterity save vs passer's/shooter's roll; success gains possession.

**Lunge** *(Reaction)* — Move up to 10 feet in any direction except during your own turn.

## Concentration Rule

If a ball-carrier takes any damage, they make a Concentration save. On failure, they drop the ball.

## Role Guide

| Role | Best Build |
|---|---|
| Goalie | High Dexterity; ranged combatant |
| Defender | Casters; stay near goalie to maximize range |
| Midfielder | Dexterity-based; facilitate passes |
| Attacker | Strength-based; the only way to score is a Shoot action |
