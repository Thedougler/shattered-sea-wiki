# Mounts and Vehicles

## Mounts and Other Animals

A mount helps move more quickly through the wilderness; primary purpose is carrying gear. An animal pulling a vehicle can move weight up to 5× its base carrying capacity.

## Barding

Any armor type can be purchased as barding. Cost: 4× normal. Weight: 2× normal.

## Saddles

- **Military Saddle** — Advantage on checks to remain mounted. 30 lb., 20 GP.
- **Exotic Saddle** — Required for aquatic or flying mounts. 40 lb., 60 GP.
- **Riding Saddle** — 25 lb., 10 GP.

## Waterborne and Airborne Vehicles

**Speed:** Against strong wind: half speed. Dead calm: waterborne ships can't sail, must row. Downstream river: add current speed (~3 mph). Rowboat weighs 100 lb., can be carried.

**Ship Repair:** While berthed. 1 HP = 1 day + 20 GP. In city shipyard: half time and cost.

**Damage Threshold:** Damage below threshold is superficial (no HP loss). Damage at or above threshold applies in full.
