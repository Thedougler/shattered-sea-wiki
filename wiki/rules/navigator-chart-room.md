# Navigator's Chart Room

*2024 Facility: Arcane Study — Tier 1 (requires ship)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prerequisite** | Ability to use an Arcane Focus or tool as a Spellcasting Focus |
| **Space** | Roomy |
| **Cost to Install** | 500 gp / 7 days (rushed: 1,000 gp / 3 days) |
| **Hirelings** | 1 |
| **Order** | Craft |

A cramped, lamp-lit cabin lined with bookshelves, star charts, and locked drawers of arcane instruments.

## Passive Benefit

**Chart Room Charm.** After spending a Long Rest aboard ship, you gain a magical Charm that lasts 7 days or until used. The Charm lets you cast *Identify* once without expending a spell slot or Material components. You can't gain this Charm again while you still have it.

## Craft Order

When you issue the **Craft** order, choose one option:

**Craft: Arcane Focus.** The hireling crafts an Arcane Focus. Takes 7 days, costs nothing.

**Craft: Book.** The hireling crafts a blank book. Takes 7 days, costs 10 gp.

**Craft: Magic Item (Arcana).** *(Level 9+ only.)* Commission the hireling to craft a Common or Uncommon magic item from the Arcana tables (DMG ch. 7). Time and cost per DMG crafting rules.
