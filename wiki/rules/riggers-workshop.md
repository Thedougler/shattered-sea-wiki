# Rigger's Workshop

*2024 Facility: Workshop — Tier 1 (requires ship)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prerequisite** | None |
| **Space** | Roomy (upgradeable to Vast for 2,000 gp) |
| **Cost to Install** | 500 gp / 7 days (rushed: 1,000 gp / 3 days) |
| **Hirelings** | 3 |
| **Order** | Craft |

A multi-purpose work deck where riggers, leatherworkers, and skilled hands keep the ship and its crew outfitted.

**Tools:** When this facility is added, choose **six Artisan's Tools** from: Carpenter's, Cobbler's, Glassblower's, Jeweler's, Leatherworker's, Mason's, Painter's, Potter's, Tinker's, Weaver's, Woodcarver's.

## Passive Benefit

**Source of Inspiration.** After spending an entire Short Rest in the Rigger's Workshop, you gain **Heroic Inspiration**. Can't gain this benefit again until you finish a Long Rest.

## Craft Order

**Craft: Adventuring Gear.** The hirelings craft anything made with the workshop's chosen tools, per PHB crafting rules.

**Craft: Magic Item (Implement).** *(Level 9+ only.)* Commission a Common or Uncommon magic item from the Implements tables (DMG ch. 7). Time and cost per DMG crafting rules.

## Expansion

**Enlarging.** Spend **2,000 gp** to expand to Vast. Gains 2 additional hirelings (5 total) and 3 additional tools.
