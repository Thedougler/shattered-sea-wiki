# Aberrant Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Psionic power derived from alien influence: a psychic wind from the Astral Plane, Far Realm exposure, or an unfinished mind flayer transformation.

---

## Features

**Psionic Spells (Level 3).** Always-prepared spells by level:
- 3: Arms of Hadar, Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Mind Sliver
- 5: Hunger of Hadar, Sending
- 7: Evard's Black Tentacles, Summon Aberration
- 9: Rary's Telepathic Bond, Telekinesis

**Telepathic Speech (Level 3).** Bonus Action: telepathic link with one visible creature within 30 feet. Lasts Sorcerer level in minutes, range = CHA mod in miles (min 1).

**Psionic Sorcery (Level 6).** Cast any Psionic Spell by spending SP equal to spell level instead of a slot. Requires no Verbal, Somatic, or non-consumed Material components when cast this way.

**Psychic Defenses (Level 6).** Resistance to Psychic damage. Advantage on saves vs. Charmed or Frightened.

**Revelation in Flesh (Level 14).** Bonus Action: spend 1+ SP to gain physical alterations for 10 minutes (Aquatic Adaptation, Glistening Flight, See the Invisible, or Wormlike Movement per point spent).

**Warping Implosion (Level 18).** Magic action: teleport 120 feet, then all creatures within 30 feet of departure point make STR save (spell DC) — fail: 3d10 Force + pulled to empty space; success: half. 1/LR; costs 5 SP to restore.
