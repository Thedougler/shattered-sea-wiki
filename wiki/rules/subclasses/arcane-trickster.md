# Arcane Trickster

*Rogue subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Brings magic to the Rogue. Adds INT spellcasting (Wizard spell list) to the Rogue framework. True Strike enables weapon attacks using Intelligence, allowing stat consolidation.

---

## Features

**Spellcasting (Level 3).** INT-based (Wizard list). Sneak Attack applies only to Finesse/ranged weapon attacks, not spell attacks.

**Mage Hand Legerdemain (Level 3).** Control Mage Hand as a Bonus Action (vs. an action). Enables lock-picking and pickpocketing while fighting.

**Magical Ambush (Level 9).** If you're Hidden when you cast a spell, creatures have Disadvantage on saves vs. that spell.

**Versatile Trickster (Level 13).** Use your Mage Hand to impose Disadvantage on attacks vs. you (Bonus Action) and grant advantage on your next attack vs. the same target.

**Spell Thief (Level 17).** After a creature within 30 ft. casts a spell of 4th level or lower, you can use your Reaction to force a save — on failure, they forget the spell and you prepare it instead for 8 hours.
