# Assassin

*Rogue subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Focused on delivering devastating first-strike damage. Best when paired with high Dexterity and reliable initiative. Less effective against creatures with high Dexterity or groups with many fast targets.

---

## Features

**Assassinate (Level 3).** On the first full round of combat: bonus damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus. If you attack a creature that hasn't taken a turn yet, you also have Advantage on the attack roll.

**Infiltration Expertise (Level 9).** 1 hour of work + Disguise Kit: create a perfect disguise. Creatures have Disadvantage on checks to recognize you as an imposter.

**Ambush Master (Level 13).** Your Advantage from Assassinate extends: allies attacking a Surprised creature on your turn also have Advantage.

**Death Strike (Level 17).** When you hit a Surprised creature with a Sneak Attack, the target must make a CON save (DC 8 + DEX mod + PB) or the attack deals double damage.
