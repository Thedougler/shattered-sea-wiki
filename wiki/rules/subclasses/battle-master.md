# Battle Master

*Fighter subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Tactical fighters who augment weapon attacks with studied combat maneuvers. Uses a pool of Superiority Dice (d8, scaling to d10 at L10 and d12 at L18) to fuel special techniques — tripping, menacing, riposting, and more. The most mechanically flexible of the Fighter subclasses.

---

## Key Features

**Combat Superiority (Level 3).** 4 Superiority Dice (scales to 5 at L7, 6 at L15). Recover on Short or Long Rest. Learn 3 maneuvers from a list of ~16+ options.

**Know Your Enemy (Level 7).** 10 minutes observing a creature reveals how its stats compare to yours.

**Relentless (Level 15).** If you roll Initiative with 0 Superiority Dice remaining, regain 1.
