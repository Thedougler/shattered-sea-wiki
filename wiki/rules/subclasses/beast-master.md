# Beast Master

*Ranger subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Commands a powerful beast companion in combat. The beast acts as both pet and weapon, with separate hit points and damage output matching the Ranger's own attacks. Build around Wisdom — beast stats scale from WIS modifier.

---

## Features

**Primal Companion (Level 3).** Permanent beast companion with its own hit points. Commanding it uses your Bonus Action. Beast attacks deal damage = your weapon attacks, with stats scaling from WIS. Easily revived after a Long Rest.

**Beast Options:** Primal Beast of the Land (Pounce, ram), Air (Flyby, fly speed), or Sea (swim speed, water breathing).

**Exceptional Training (Level 7).** Your beast's attacks count as magical. Bonus Action: command it to take the Dash, Disengage, or Help action instead of attacking.

**Bestial Fury (Level 11).** On your turn: command your beast to make 2 attacks. Compatible with Hunter's Mark damage bonus.

**Share Spells (Level 15).** When you cast a spell targeting yourself, your beast can also benefit from it.
