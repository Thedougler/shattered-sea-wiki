# Clockwork Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Innate magic derived from precise, ordered nature. Borrows spells from Cleric and Wizard lists. Theme: efficiency, order, and reroll manipulation rather than chaos.

---

## Features

**Clockwork Spells (Level 3).** Always prepared: Alarm, Protection from Evil and Good (3rd); Aid, Knock (5th); Dispel Magic, Protection from Energy (7th); Freedom of Movement, Summon Construct (9th); Greater Restoration, Wall of Force (11th).

**Restore Balance (Level 3).** Reaction when a creature within 60 feet makes a saving throw: remove Advantage or Disadvantage from the roll (your choice which). Uses/LR = CHA modifier.

**Bastion of Law (Level 6).** Bonus Action: spend 1–5 SP to create a ward with that many d8s. When the warded creature takes damage, reduce by the remaining dice total (roll all at once when triggered). 1/LR per creature.

**Trance of Order (Level 14).** Bonus Action: 1 minute. Minimum 10 on attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws. 1/LR.

**Clockwork Cavalcade (Level 18).** Bonus Action: 60-ft emanation lasting 1 minute — each creature in range: (a) one spell effect on it ended (your choice); (b) 4d8 HP healed to undamaged constructs; (c) damaged nonmagical objects repaired. 1/LR.
