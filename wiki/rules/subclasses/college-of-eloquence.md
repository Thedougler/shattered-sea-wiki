# College of Eloquence

*Bard subclass — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything (2020)*

Masters of rhetoric and persuasion. Bardic Inspiration dice can't be wasted — targets can choose to use them or let them expire harmlessly. Unsettling Words adds a debuff layer to enemy saves.

---

## Features

**Silver Tongue (Level 3).** Minimum 10 on Persuasion and Deception checks.

**Unsettling Words (Level 3).** Bonus Action: expend a Bardic Inspiration die. One creature within 60 feet subtracts the die roll from its next saving throw before the end of your next turn.

**Unfailing Inspiration (Level 6).** A creature that rolls a Bardic Inspiration die and fails its check/attack/save can keep the die for another use.

**Universal Speech (Level 6).** Choose up to CHA modifier creatures within 60 feet — you can speak to them in any language (and they understand) for 1 hour. 1/LR.

**Infectious Inspiration (Level 14).** When a creature uses your Bardic Inspiration and succeeds, another creature within 60 feet gains a free Bardic Inspiration die (without expending one of yours). Uses/LR = CHA modifier.
