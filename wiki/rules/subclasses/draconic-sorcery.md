# Draconic Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

The iconic Sorcerer. Innate magic derived from draconic ancestry, a dragon's gift, or a hoard artifact. Generalist powerhouse — closes AC/HP gaps and provides elemental flexibility.

---

## Features

**Draconic Resilience (Level 3).** HP max increases by 3 + 1 per additional Sorcerer level. Unarmored AC = 10 + DEX + CHA.

**Draconic Spells (Level 3).** Always prepared: Alter Self, Chromatic Orb, Command, Dragon's Breath (3rd); Fear, Fly (5th); Arcane Eye, Charm Monster (7th); Legend Lore, Summon Dragon (9th).

**Elemental Affinity (Level 6).** Choose one damage type (Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison): Resistance to it; add CHA modifier to one damage roll per spell that deals that type.

**Dragon Wings (Level 14).** Bonus Action: sprout draconic wings, Fly Speed 60 ft for 1 hour. 1/LR; restore early for 3 SP.

**Dragon Companion (Level 18).** Cast Summon Dragon without Material components; 1/LR without a spell slot; optional non-Concentration version (1-minute duration).
