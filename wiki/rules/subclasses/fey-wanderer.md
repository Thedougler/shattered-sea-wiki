# Fey Wanderer

*Ranger subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Emphasizes spellcasting and Face skills over pure bow damage. Dreadful Strikes adds WIS-modifier psychic damage once per target per turn — free, stacks with Hunter's Mark, hits multiple targets efficiently.

---

## Features

**Dreadful Strikes (Level 3).** Your attacks deal extra psychic damage equal to your WIS modifier (once per target per turn). Free, no resources.

**Fey Wanderer Spells (Level 3).** Always prepared: Charm Person, Misty Step (3rd); Fear, Summon Fey (5th); Dimension Door, Invisibility (7th).

**Otherworldly Glamour (Level 3).** Learn one Face skill; add WIS modifier to all Charisma checks (in addition to CHA modifier).

**Beguiling Twist (Level 7).** When a creature within 120 ft succeeds against a Charmed/Frightened effect, redirect it: impose Charmed or Frightened on a different creature within 120 ft (WIS save to resist).

**Fey Reinforcements (Level 11).** Cast Summon Fey without SP/slot 1/LR.

**Misty Wanderer (Level 11).** Cast Misty Step for free (WIS mod times/LR). Can bring one willing creature.
