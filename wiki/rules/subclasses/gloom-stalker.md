# Gloom Stalker

*Ranger subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Fantastic Scout, especially in darkness where Umbral Sight grants near-permanent invisibility against non-magical sight. Front-loaded with strong turn-1 features.

---

## Features

**Gloom Stalker Spells (Level 3).** Always prepared: Disguise Self, Rope Trick (3rd); Fear, Greater Invisibility, Invisibility (5th); Dimension Door (7th).

**Dread Ambusher (Level 3).** First round of combat: +10 ft speed; first attack deals +2d6 damage (scales to +2d8 at L11); +PB to Initiative. Uses per LR = WIS modifier.

**Umbral Sight (Level 3).** Darkvision 60 ft (or +30 ft if you already have it). While in darkness, creatures without magical truesight can't see you — you are effectively permanently invisible.

**Iron Mind (Level 7).** Proficiency in WIS saves; add PB to INT and CHA saves if not already proficient.

**Shadowy Dodge (Level 11).** Reaction (1/round): impose Disadvantage on an attack roll against you. The attack must be visible to you.

**Sudden Strike (Level 15).** After attacking with the Attack action, make an additional Ranged weapon attack as a Bonus Action.
