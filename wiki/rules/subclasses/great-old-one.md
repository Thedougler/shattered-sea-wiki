# Great Old One

*Warlock subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Warlocks bound to an inscrutable entity from beyond the stars or beneath the deep — vast, ancient, and fundamentally alien. Gains telepathy, expanded knowledge of minds and aberrations, and eventually the ability to enter or influence other creatures' dreams.

---

## Features

**Awakened Mind (Level 3).** Telepathically speak to creatures within 30 ft in any language. The connection is one-way unless the creature is an aberration, beast, dragon, elemental, fey, fiend, or plant.

**Psychic Spells (Level 3).** Always prepared: Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Phantasmal Force (3rd); Detect Evil and Good, Hunger of Hadar, Tasha's Hideous Laughter (5th); Contact Other Plane, Enemies Abound (7th); Arcane Eye, Black Tentacles (9th); Modify Memory, Telekinesis (11th).

**Psychic Defense (Level 6).** Resistance to Psychic damage. Advantage on saves to avoid or end Charmed or Frightened.

**Clairvoyant Combatant (Level 6).** When telepathically linked to a creature: see through its eyes and hear through its ears. You are Blinded to your own surroundings while doing so.

**Entropic Ward (Level 10).** Reaction when attacked: impose Disadvantage on the attack. If it misses, gain Advantage on your next attack against the attacker. 1/SR.

**Thought Shield (Level 10).** Your thoughts can't be read by any effect unless you allow it. Psychic damage you take also damages the creature that dealt it (equal amount, after any resistance).

**Create Thrall (Level 14).** Touch an Incapacitated humanoid — it becomes Charmed for 30 days or until you end the effect. 1/LR.
