# Hexblade

*Warlock subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014 edition)*

Pact with a shadowy Shadowfell entity manifested in sentient magic weapons. Turns Warlocks into effective melee combatants by using CHA for weapon attacks.

## Features

**Hexblade's Curse (Level 1).** Bonus action: curse a creature within 30 ft for 1 minute. While cursed: +PB to damage vs. target; critical on 19–20 vs. target; if target dies, regain HP = Warlock level + CHA mod. 1/SR.

**Hex Warrior (Level 1).** Proficiency with Medium armor, Shields, and Martial weapons. Once per LR, touch one non-Two-Handed weapon — use CHA for attack/damage rolls until next LR. Extends to all Pact of the Blade weapons.

**Accursed Specter (Level 6).** When you slay a humanoid, raise it as a Specter (bonus THP = half Warlock level, attacks +CHA mod). Lasts until next LR.

**Armor of Hexes (Level 10).** When the cursed target hits you: reaction, roll d6 — on 4+, the attack misses.

**Master of Hexes (Level 14).** When the cursed creature dies, immediately transfer the curse to another creature within 30 ft (no HP regain for the death).
