# Soulknife

*Rogue subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Psionics-based Rogue. Psionic Energy Dice (PED) pool — d6 scaling to d12 at Level 17. Often grants benefits even on failed rolls, making it reliable.

---

## Features

**Psionic Power (Level 3).** Psionic Energy Dice pool equal to double PB. Recharge: all on LR, 1 die on SR.

**Psychic Blades (Level 3).** As part of the Attack action, manifest two Psychic Blades (light, finesse, 60 ft range, psychic damage, appear in empty hands). Vanish immediately after — no Opportunity Attacks.

**Soul Blades (Level 9).** Add effects to Psychic Blade attacks:
- *Homing Strikes (1 PED):* On a miss, add one PED to the attack roll. If it now hits, don't add the die to damage.
- *Psychic Teleportation (1 PED):* Instead of an attack, teleport up to 10 × the PED result in feet.

**Psychic Veil (Level 13).** Become Invisible for up to 1 hour (or until you attack, cast a spell, or force a save). 1/LR; restore early by spending 2 PED.

**Rend Mind (Level 17).** On a Sneak Attack hit using Psychic Blades, force a WIS save (DC 8 + DEX/STR + PB) — fail: Stunned until end of your next turn. 1/LR; restore early by spending 3 PED.
