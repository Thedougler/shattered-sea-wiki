# Spellfire Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Fiends of Fey & Flame (2024)*

Divine magic derived from radiant power. Similar to Cleric in healing and condition removal, with Counterspell defense. More support than offense.

---

## Features

**Spellfire Burst (Level 3).** When you cast a spell using Sorcery Points, gain rider effects:
- *Bolstering Flames:* Gain THP equal to 1 + spell level.
- *Radiant Fire:* Creatures within 5 feet take 1d4 Radiant damage.

**Spellfire Sorcery Spells (Level 3).** Always prepared: Command, Guiding Bolt (3rd); Lesser Restoration, Scorching Ray (5th); Aura of Vitality, Dispel Magic (7th); Wall of Fire, Flame Strike (9th); Greater Restoration, Flame Strike (11th).

**Absorb Spells (Level 6).** Reaction to a spell targeting you: attempt to absorb it (spell must deal damage, target only you). If successful, take no damage and gain SP = spell level. Uses/LR = CHA modifier.

**Honed Spellfire (Level 14).** Radiant Fire increases to 1d8. Once per SR, after taking damage, you can use Spellfire Burst's Bolstering Flames without spending SP.

**Crown of Spellfire (Level 18).** Spend Hit Dice (max 5) — each grants 2 SP and a 30-minute Radiant aura that deals +1d6 Radiant to melee attackers and heals nearby allies for 5 HP at start of their turn.
