# Thief

*Rogue subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

The iconic Scout Rogue. Fast Hands gives exceptional action economy for magic item use. Many features depend on magic item availability for peak effectiveness at high levels.

---

## Features

**Fast Hands (Level 3).** Bonus Action: Sleight of Hand check, or take the Utilize action (covers caltrops, Alchemist's Fire, scrolls, wands, and other activated magic items — a massive action economy boost).

**Second-Story Work (Level 3).** Climbing costs no extra movement. Running jump distance = DEX modifier + base.

**Supreme Sneak (Level 9).** Advantage on Stealth checks if you move no more than half your speed this turn.

**Use Magic Device (Level 13).** Ignore all class, species, and level requirements for attuning or using magic items.

**Thief's Reflexes (Level 17).** Take two turns on the first round of combat (at initiative count and initiative -10). You can't use this feature if Surprised.
