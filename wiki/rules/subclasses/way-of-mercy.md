# Way of Mercy

*Monk subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Healing and damage output combined through Focus Point expenditure. Converts martial arts into both offense and medicine without competing with action economy.

---

## Features

**Hand of Harm (Level 3).** Melee hit: spend 1 FP to add extra necrotic damage = one Martial Arts die. Multiplied on critical hits.

**Hand of Healing (Level 3).** Bonus action or Flurry replacement: spend 1 FP to heal a creature within 5 ft (Martial Arts die + WIS modifier, min 1 HP).

**Implements of Mercy (Level 3).** Proficiency in Insight and Medicine; can craft Antitoxins and Potions of Healing.

**Physician's Touch (Level 6).** Hand of Harm: adds Poisoned (no save). Hand of Healing: adds Lesser Restoration effect and can remove Stunned.

**Flurry of Healing and Harm (Level 11).** Replace one Flurry of Blows strike with Hand of Harm or Hand of Healing without spending an additional FP.

**Hand of Ultimate Mercy (Level 17).** Action + 5 FP: touch a creature that died within 24 hours, return it to life with 1 HP. 1/LR.
