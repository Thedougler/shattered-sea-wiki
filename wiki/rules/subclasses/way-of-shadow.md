# Way of Shadow

*Monk subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Shadow magic and magical darkness grant consistent combat Advantage. The Warrior of Shadow excels as a striker with excellent offense and defense in dim or dark conditions.

---

## Features

**Shadow Arts (Level 3).** Spend 1 FP to cast Darkness, Darkvision (8 hr), or Minor Illusion. Darkness on a carried object = mobile darkness zone.

**Shadow Step (Level 6).** Reaction when you move 5+ feet: teleport from dim light/darkness to dim light/darkness within 30 ft. Gain Advantage on next melee attack this turn. (Bonus Action + 1 FP to do this without the requirement.)

**Improved Shadow Step (Level 6).** Bonus Action version of Shadow Step (1 FP) ignores the darkness-to-darkness requirement.

**Cloak of Shadows (Level 11).** Bonus action + 1 FP: become Invisible until start of your next turn. Your next Flurry of Blows costs no FP.
