# Way of the Elements

*Monk subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Avatar-style elemental Monk with enhanced reach, damage versatility, forced movement combos, and eventually flight. Exceptional for striker and battlefield control.

---

## Features

**Elemental Attunement (Level 3).** Bonus action (free, no FP):
- *Extended Reach:* Unarmed strikes have 10 ft reach for one turn.
- *Elemental Strikes:* Switch unarmed damage to cold, fire, lightning, or thunder. Push/pull targets (enables vertical knockback → falling damage).

**Elemental Burst (Level 6).** Action + 2 FP: cast Fireball in any damage type (3d6, 20-ft radius). Combo with Elemental Strikes to push enemies into the zone.

**Stride of the Elements (Level 11).** Bonus action + 1 FP: Fly Speed = your Speed for rest of the turn. No landing vulnerability.

**Elemental Epitome (Level 17).** Adds to Elemental Attunement: damage resistance (chosen type); Destructive Stride (Step of the Wind AoE damage); Empowered Strikes (+1d12 damage once per turn).
