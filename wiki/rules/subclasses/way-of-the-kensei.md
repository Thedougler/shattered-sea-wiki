# Way of the Kensei

*Monk subclass — Xanathar's Guide to Everything (2014 edition)*

Monks who master the Kensei tradition treat weapons as extensions of the body — tools of precise martial artistry. Enables Monk weapons beyond the default simple/shortsword list.

---

## Features

**Path of the Kensei (Level 3).**
- *Kensei Weapons:* Choose 1 melee + 1 ranged weapon as Monk weapons (any non-Heavy/non-Special simple or martial; Longbow valid). Add 1 weapon at levels 6, 11, 17.
- *Agile Parry:* If you make an unarmed strike as part of an Attack action while holding a melee kensei weapon, gain +2 AC until start of your next turn.
- *Kensei's Shot:* Bonus action — ranged kensei attacks deal +1d4 damage until end of turn.

**One with the Blade (Level 6).**
- Kensei weapon attacks count as magical.
- *Deft Strike:* Spend 1 FP on hit to add Martial Arts die to damage. Once per turn.

**Sharpen the Blade (Level 11).** Bonus action: spend 1–3 FP to grant a kensei weapon +1/+2/+3 to attack and damage rolls for 1 minute.

**Unerring Accuracy (Level 17).** Once per turn, if you miss with a Monk weapon attack, reroll it.
