# Way of the Open Hand

*Monk subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

The classic Monk subclass. Open Hand Technique dramatically improves Flurry of Blows with forced movement and control. Push + vertical knockback = prone + falling damage.

---

## Features

**Open Hand Technique (Level 3).** When using Flurry of Blows, choose one attack to trigger:
- *Addle:* WIS save or target can't take Reactions until end of its next turn.
- *Push:* STR save or pushed up to 5 ft (allows vertical push → falling damage + Prone).
- *Topple:* STR save or knocked Prone.

**Wholeness of Body (Level 6).** Bonus action: spend 1 FP to regain HP = Martial Arts die + WIS modifier.

**Fleet Step (Level 6).** Bonus action: Dash without spending a FP. Excellent for repositioning and grapple combos.

**Quivering Palm (Level 11).** On a melee hit: spend 4 FP to set internal vibrations. Target makes CON save at start of your next turn or takes 10d12 force damage.
