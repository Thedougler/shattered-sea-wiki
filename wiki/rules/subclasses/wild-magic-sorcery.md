# Wild Magic Sorcery

*Sorcerer subclass — Player's Handbook (2024)*

Chaotic magic from unpredictable sources. Fun but underpowered vs. other Sorcerer subclasses — the single biggest weakness is no prepared spell list.

---

## Features

**Wild Magic (Level 3).** When you cast a leveled spell, roll a d20. On a 1, trigger a random Wild Magic Surge effect.

**Tides of Chaos (Level 3).** Bonus action: Advantage on next ability check, attack roll, or saving throw before end of next turn. Recharge by casting any leveled spell.

**Bend Luck (Level 6).** Reaction when a visible creature within 60 ft makes an attack, check, or save: spend 1–2 SP to add or subtract that many from the roll.

**Controlled Chaos (Level 6).** Roll twice on the Wild Magic table; choose which effect applies.

**Tamed Surge (Level 14).** 1/LR: when you cast a leveled spell, choose which Wild Magic effect triggers (no roll). Restore early for 3 SP.
