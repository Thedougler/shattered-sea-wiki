# Crew Berths

*2024 Facility: Barrack — Tier 1 (requires ship)*

---

**Prerequisite:** None
**Space:** Roomy (upgradeable to Vast for 2,000 gp)
**Cost to Install:** 500 gp / 7 days (rushed: 1,000 gp / 3 days)
**Hirelings:** 1
**Order:** Recruit

A section of the lower deck fitted with hammocks, sea chests, and shared mess space. Without Crew Berths the ship has no Bastion Defenders — any Attack event targets facilities directly.

The ship can have more than one Crew Berths facility. Each accommodates up to **twelve Bastion Defenders**.

---

## Recruit Order

**Recruit: Crew.** Each Recruit order adds up to **4 Bastion Defenders** to this berth. No cost. Cannot issue this order when the berth is full.

---

## Expansion

Spend **2,000 gp** to expand to Vast. A Vast berth accommodates up to **25 Bastion Defenders**.
