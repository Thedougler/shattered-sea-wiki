# Ship Bastion — The Shattered Sea

Shared mobile bastion rules based on 2024 DMG bastion mechanics with maritime adaptations.

---

## Core Model

The party ship is one shared bastion. Use 2024 combining-bastions rules.

- Facility slots are pooled across PCs.
- Bastion Turns resolve weekly.
- Defenders are shared.
- A PC with Crew Berths can absorb all or part of defender losses for the whole ship on an Attack event.

---

## Facility Slots & Level Gates

Bastion rules come into effect when the party acquires their own ship — not at character level 5. The ship is the bastion.

| Character Level | Slots/PC | Party Pool (5 PCs) | Unlocks | Min Ship Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Any | 2 | 10 | Tier 1 facilities | 1 |
| 9 | 3 | 15 | Tier 2 facilities | 2 |
| 13 | 4 | 20 | Tier 3 facilities | 3 |
| 17 | 5 | 25 | Tier 3 facilities | 3 |

Facility replacement on level-up requires a shipyard port and 3 days. Additional facilities: 500 gp / 7 days (rushed: 1,000 gp / 3 days), requires port access.

---

## Bastion Turn

Weekly, whether at sea or in port.

- Aboard PCs can issue orders. Absent PCs can issue orders via *Sending* or equivalent.
- If no order is issued, facility takes Maintain.
- If no orders are issued for consecutive turns equal to character level, neglect rules apply.
- Facilities function at sea if minimum crew is filled.

---

## Standard Orders

| Order | Effect |
|---|---|
| Craft | Produce items/equipment allowed by the facility. |
| Empower | Trigger facility power effect. |
| Harvest | Gather facility outputs. |
| Maintain | No order; roll Bastion Event. |
| Recruit | Hire hirelings/defenders/specialists. |
| Research | Gather information or identify items. |
| Trade | Generate income via commerce. |

## Bastion Events (At Sea)

When Maintain triggers at sea, replace location-bound events:

| 2024 Event Type | Shipboard Equivalent |
|---|---|
| Local threat/attack | Hostile vessel intercepting. |
| Beneficial contact | Passing ship offers trade, intel, or opportunity. |
| Structural damage | Storm/incident damages facility. |
| Windfall | Favorable current shortens voyage by 1d4 days. |

---

## Defenders and Defense

**Attack Event:** Roll 6d6. Each 1 kills one Defender. If no Defenders remain, one Special Facility shuts down for one Bastion Turn. [[crew-berths|Crew Berths]] can recruit up to 12 Defenders in one order.

**Armored Hull (Defensive Walls reskin):** Reinforced hull reduces Attack dice from 6d6 to 4d6. Cost: 250 gp per 5-ft exterior section.
