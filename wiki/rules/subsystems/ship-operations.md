# Ship Operations

---

## Upkeep

Paid weekly whether sailing or docked.

| Cost Component | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crew wages | 8–20 gp | 40–80 gp | 150–250 gp |
| Provisions | 5–10 gp | 20–40 gp | 70–120 gp |
| Maintenance | 5 gp | 15 gp | 40 gp |
| Berthing fees | 2–5 gp | 5–10 gp | 10–20 gp |
| **Total (approx)** | **20–40 gp/wk** | **80–145 gp/wk** | **270–430 gp/wk** |

## Navigation Checks

When conditions are uncertain — reef channels, Maw approaches, storms, unfamiliar waters — the Navigator rolls Intelligence (Navigator's Tools).

| DC | Failure |
|---|---|
| 10 | Off course. +1 day. |
| 14 | Significantly off course. +2 days, minor encounter likely. |
| 18 | Lost. +1d4 days, serious complication. |

- Tidewardens' charts grant **advantage** in charted waters.
- Midchain charts are contradictory — no advantage.
- **The Maw:** Compasses fail within 5 miles of the edge. Magical navigation gives false readings. Star navigation is unaffected.

## Downtime at Sea

On voyages of 3+ days, characters can perform downtime activities (2024 rules). Activities requiring a specific location or instructor can't be performed at sea.

**Long rests at sea:** If the Cook role is filled, characters recover an additional **1d6 HP**. If a storm hits during rest: DC 12 Constitution save or the rest grants no benefits.

## Acquiring a Ship

**Buy.** Available at any port with a shipyard. DC 12 Charisma (Persuasion) to close the typical 10–15% gap from asking price.

**Prize.** Take a ship in combat. Strip a Tier 2 ship for parts: 2,000–4,000 gp at the right buyer.

**Steal.** Tidefall's main harbour is hard. South Harbour on Calder's Tooth's eastern side is loosely administered — ships in the outer bay are accessible by rowboat.

**Mutiny.** Built on crew relationships established beforehand.

**Build.** Same cost as buying new; custom specs possible.

| Tier | Build Time |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2–4 months |
| 2 | 6–12 months |
| 3 | 18–36 months |

## Living Quarters by Tier

**Tier 1** — Captain's bunk in the stern, hammocks forward. Party and crew share close quarters.

**Tier 2** — Captain's cabin (private, lockable). Two shared officer berths. Galley. Cargo hold. **Captain's Locker** (DC 18 to break or pick).

**Tier 3** — Officer cabins, wardroom, captain's suite. Full privacy for the party.

## Ship Reputation

| Level | Effect |
|---|---|
| Respected | Reduced berth fees. Willing hires. Faction contacts available. |
| Known | Treated on actual deeds. Neutral default. |
| Wanted | Port access restricted or denied. Bounties posted. |
| Notorious | Capable crew seek them out. Some targets surrender rather than fight. |
