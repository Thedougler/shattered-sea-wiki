# Ship Stats — The Shattered Sea

---

## Ship Tiers

| Tier | Vessel Examples | Cargo | Guns | Cost (used) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Sloop, cutter, lugger | 20 tons | 2–6 | 800–2,000 gp |
| **2** | Brigantine, schooner | 60 tons | 8–16 | 4,000–10,000 gp |
| **3** | Frigate, galleon | 150 tons | 20–40 | 15,000–35,000 gp |
| **4** | First-rate ship of the line | 100 tons | 80–112 | Not for sale |

Used/prize vessels cost 40–60% of new. Tier 4 exists only as Crown property — the [[hcs-sovereign|HCS Sovereign]] is the sole example in the Scatter.

---

## Ship Stats

Vehicle stat blocks: 2024 DMG. Relevant stats:

- **Hull Points** and **AC**: From the vehicle stat block.
- **Speed**: In miles per day for travel and chase adjudication.
- **Condition**: Pristine / Worn / Damaged / Wrecked — tracked narratively with mechanical effects applied when relevant.

---

## Crew Roles

| Role | Function | Key Ability | Hireling Wage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Captain** | Command, crew direction, morale. Required. | Charisma | — (PC or major NPC) |
| **Navigator** | Course plotting, reef avoidance, Maw crossings. Required for open-water voyages. | Intelligence (Navigator's Tools) | 10 gp/wk |
| **Bosun** | Rigging, sails, on-deck crew management. Required. | Strength or Dexterity | 8 gp/wk |
| **Gunner** | Weapon maintenance and direction. Required if guns are used. | Dexterity | 8 gp/wk |
| **Carpenter** | Hull repair, below-decks damage control. Required for repairs at sea. | Intelligence (Carpenter's Tools) | 6 gp/wk |
| **Cook** | Provisions management. Required for voyages over 3 days. | Wisdom | 4 gp/wk |
| **Surgeon** | Treating wounds, managing casualties. Required for Surgeon's Berth facility. | Wisdom (Medicine) | 8 gp/wk |
| **Marine** | Boarding actions, armed watch, security. Required for boarding party actions. | Strength or Dexterity | 14 gp/wk |
| **Ordinary Sailor** | All other duties. Required in sufficient number. | — | 2 gp/wk |

**PC in a role:** Adds their relevant ability modifier. **Hireling:** Competent baseline. **Role unfilled:** Function unavailable or attempted at disadvantage.

## Minimum Crew by Tier

Below minimum: all ship-related checks at disadvantage; speed reduced 20%.

| Tier | Minimum Crew | Full Crew |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2–4 | 8–12 |
| 2 | 8–10 | 20–30 |
| 3 | 20–25 | 55–80 |
| 4 | 55 | 280 |
