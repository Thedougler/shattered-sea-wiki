# Weapons Locker

*2024 Facility: Armory — Tier 1 (requires ship)*

**Space:** Roomy | **Cost:** 500 gp / 7 days (rushed: 1,000 gp / 3 days) | **Hirelings:** 1 | **Order:** Trade

A reinforced compartment below deck holding racks of boarding weapons, spare ammunition, and maintained armor for the crew.

## Trade Order

**Trade: Stock Weapons Locker.** Cost: **100 gp + 100 gp per Bastion Defender** aboard. If the ship also has a [[carpenters-shop|Carpenter's Shop]], the total cost is halved.

While stocked, when any event causes you to roll Defender losses, **roll 1d8 in place of each d6**. After the event resolves, stock is expended — must be restocked before granting this benefit again.
