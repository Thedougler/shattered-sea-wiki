# Waveservant Shrine (Facility)

*2024 Facility: Sanctuary — Tier 1 (requires ship)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prerequisite** | Ability to use a Holy Symbol or Druidic Focus as a Spellcasting Focus |
| **Space** | Roomy |
| **Cost to Install** | 500 gp / 7 days (rushed: 1,000 gp / 3 days) |
| **Hirelings** | 1 |
| **Order** | Craft |

A small devotional space below deck — icons, tide-worn offerings, and a seawater basin. The Waveservant Shrine serves the faithful and propitious alike, honoring whatever sea-powers the party courts.

## Passive Benefit

**Shrine Charm.** After spending a Long Rest aboard ship, you gain a magical Charm that lasts 7 days or until used. The Charm lets you cast *Healing Word* once without expending a spell slot. You can't gain this Charm again while you still have it.

## Craft Order

**Craft: Sacred Focus.** Commission the hireling to craft a Druidic Focus (wooden staff) or a Holy Symbol. Takes **7 days**, costs nothing. The item remains aboard until claimed.
