# Beat 3: Delmar

*(Address Delmar.)*

*You have been leaning against that rail for ten minutes already. The night air is warm and thick with salt, and the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] moves slow beneath your feet. The red coat with the gold epaulettes — Admiral's colours.*

*Five days ago you were commanding five ships into the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] on commission from the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]. A benefactor had come to the Council with a proposition: lift a relic from a shrine on [[vel-orn|Vel Orn]] and it would protect the Midchain waters from her wrath. The Council believed it. They hired you. You went in clean, lifted the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl]], and made the outbound tide.*

*Then the [[the-drowned-maw|Maw]] opened. The name proved true — a chasm, wide and deep, and all five ships gone before anyone understood what was happening. You heard voices as the hull came apart around you. You'd be dead in that water if not for [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], who found you in the wreckage and got you onto driftwood. The [[saltwright|Saltwright]] found you both two days later.*

*Could you tell us about who we see leaning on that rail?*

[Let them finish.]

*The night has been quiet. Then [[bisou|Bisou]] — Beaumont's monkey — noticed the coin you've been turning between your fingers.*

*[[bisou|Bisou]] has been on your arm for three minutes now, slack-jawed.*

*Every time he reaches for the coin, the coin is somewhere else. Between two fingers. Gone. The other hand. Gone again. Bisou lunges, misses, makes a sound, and tries again.*

> [!check] Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) — The coin trick
> Flavour only — Bisou is not getting the coin. The result determines how decisively Delmar wins.
> DC 10: Bisou tracks the correct hand. It knows. Sits with one finger pointed at Fisk's closed fist, not lunging anymore, just waiting with immovable certainty. From the wheel, Beaumont's shoulders are shaking.
> DC 15: Bisou stares at both hands. Then at Fisk's face. Then back at the hands. Makes a low, baffled sound, like a question asked of no one in particular.
> DC 20: Bisou looks at both hands. Looks at Fisk. Then with great dignity turns its back, tucks its chin, and refuses to acknowledge that any of this is happening. It has decided the coin does not exist. From the wheel, Beaumont is no longer pretending not to watch.

**Beaumont**: *"Bisou. Leave the man alone, cher."*

[Bisou retreats — pointedly, toward the pocket the coin disappeared into. If Delmar asks Beaumont about the cutter: *"Probably just Norrington."* If he pushes — and a man who commanded five ships probably will — Beaumont gives him a look and a shrug. *"Different face on the same route."* He won't say more out here. Deck goes quiet.]

*Along the rail toward the bow, the rest of the deck has been running a different competition for the last hour. Spears and lines. No one winning yet.*

*(Address Stripes — see [[session-01-scene-04-stripes|Scene 04]].)*
