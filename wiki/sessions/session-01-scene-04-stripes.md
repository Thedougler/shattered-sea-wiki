# Beat 4: Stripes

*(Address Stripes.)*

*You're on a stranger's ship in the dark. The salt air is warm, the Midchain sliding past on both sides of the hull, and you are here simply because [[elder-anton|Elder Anton]] asked you to go.*

*[[stripes-bitemore|Stripes Bitemore]] — the greatest hunter of [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]. Twelve black basalt promontories jutting from pale water, sharp as cut stone, worn smooth where your people have walked them for generations. The channels run so clear you can see thirty feet down. At dawn the keth-fin hold position in the current, thick and silver-flanked, and your people take what they need.*

*Five days ago the keth-fin scattered. The birds fled in great flocks. The water brought a new foul smell up from the deep. The Elders gathered, and Anton — the strongest among them — sent his apprentice out into the Midchain to find the cause.*

*Could you show us what they see — who is standing on that rail?*

[Let them finish.]

*The crew has been at this for an hour and has nothing yet. Close to the hull, two feet down, a school of yellowtail snapper holds position in the [[saltwright|Saltwright's]] shadow — the kind that gathers under hulls to feed on what falls over the side. Not keth-fin. Smaller, shallower. But they are there, and they are close.*

> [!check] Dexterity (Survival) — Fishing Competition
>
> This roll is flavour only — Stripes takes a fish. The result determines by how much he wins.
>
> - **DC 10**: The spear finds a snapper cleanly, mid-water, without touching the hull. Two of the crew have been at this for an hour and have nothing yet. They look up.
> - **DC 15**: The spear enters at an angle no one on deck thought to use — following a current Stripes was reading while everyone else watched the surface. The fish on the spear is the largest in the school. The loose competition on deck has quietly stopped.
> - **DC 20**: The spear doesn't go where the fish is. It goes where the fish will be. A full second passes. The fish arrives. Someone on deck sets down their line and does not pick it back up.

*"Cher. You win." — Beaumont.*

[The crew reacts to the result — how much depends on the roll, but even at DC 10 someone who's been at this for an hour with nothing is going to look twice. Beaumont watches from the wheel. If Stripes engages him, he's curious in an unhurried way: *"Keth-Naar, yeah? Long way north."* He doesn't push it. The competition breaks up on its own. The deck settles back into rope sounds and water and the Saltwright working the channel.]

*Deck sounds carry easily on a quiet night. Below them, through two inches of planking, someone is listening.*

*(Transition to Scene 05 — Perrin.)*
