# Beat 5: Perrin

*Below the waterline, the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] is quiet.*

*Six feet of headroom, and two lanterns turned so far down they give off more warmth than light, a faint amber glow that turns the shadows amber too. Salt barrels along the starboard side, fish crates stacked to the port bulkhead, grain sacks loosely lashed in the back. The smell is brine and old wood and time, the deep-layered damp of a hull that has spent a decade below the waterline, and underneath all of it, the particular cold mineral smell of deep water very close, held back by nothing but two inches of planking.*

*It is close down here. Dark. Private.*

[Address Perrin directly.]

*You are one of the [[rattkin|Rattkin]]. [[the-passage|The Passage]] runs through every port in the Scatter on routes no colonial chart has captured. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] keeps standing bounties on your kind — rarely enforced, always present.*

*The [[vestra|Vestra]] was your grandfather's ship. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw's]] husband built her, ran her, and left her to the family when he died. You inherited the route and everything that came with it. Three days ago something came up from the deep water over the Maw and took her apart. Something large and deliberate. You went into the water with nothing but your grandfather's cloak on your back. You woke up on the beach of [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] — a voice still echoing somewhere distant, and a blade in your hand you don't remember picking up.*

*The Saltwright was in the harbour. You found the hold before anyone found you. The captain doesn't know you're here. On your way down, you helped yourself to a couple of loose gems from a crate near the gangway.*

*Could you show us — who's hiding in this hold, and where?*

[Let the player go.]

[The hold is not silent — the deck above is audible through the planking. Boots, rope work, the occasional voice. A fishing competition. If Perrin explores: the false panel in the aft wall is latched shut and well-made. Whatever Beaumont keeps behind it, it isn't loose. The gems from the gangway crate are the only find. If they ask about a better hiding spot: this is the only one. Let them settle. Then:]

*Then, against the hull, something lands.*

*Not a wave. Not the vessel settling. A weight, deliberate and controlled, pressing against the outside of the planking at waterline level, and then going perfectly still.*

> [!check] Perception
>
> This roll is flavour only — something is out there regardless of the result.
>
> - **DC 12**: Large, warm-blooded, and clinging to the hull. It came from the water. It is not struggling.
> - **DC 16**: It moved along the outside of the hull before it stopped, feeling its way. Whatever it is, it chose this spot specifically.
> - **DC 20**: There is a smell drifting through the gap in the planking above it. Amphibious, but not fish. Not salt. Something different that clears your sinuses.

*In the hold, Perrin holds very still. On the outside of the hull, whatever found this ship does exactly the same.*

*(Transition to Scene 06 — Jean-Claude.)*
