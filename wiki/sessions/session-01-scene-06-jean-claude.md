# Beat 6: Jean-Claude

*You stand on white sand, and you can smell them before you can hear them.*

*You came out of the Verdant Teeth. Five islands two days south of [[kalowe|Kalowe]], dense rainforest canopy, Grung territory as far back as anyone has records. Your people run on caste and colour — blue ranks below purple ranks below gold — and the system holds because everyone inside it believes it is the only way. You believed it too, once.*

*A month ago you ran. A gnome is dead. A sister named Simone made a choice, and you left in the dark with people screaming behind you. You have been moving through the [[midchain|Midchain]] since, one island ahead of the hunter unit Simone assembled. They know your methods. She taught them.*

*Dense scrub at your back, close enough to scratch. Mangroves to your left, their roots threading down into dark water, the whole tangle breathing salt and wet green and mud in the heat. The air clings to you.*

*Could you show us who's standing on that beach right now?*

[Let them finish. If they ask about the hunters: half a mile out, dogs in front, they have a scent and they know it. If they ask about the channel: wide and dark and warm — JC reads water the way Stripes reads fish, and this channel is swimmable, but 200 feet of open dark water with dogs on the bank is not a plan. The ship is the only option that doesn't make things worse.]

*A month without being seen. Almost.*

*They are getting closer. Half a mile in the trees, maybe less. Several of them, moving with the confidence of people who have a trail and know it. In front of them, rampaging through the underbrush, dogs.*

*The channel ahead is open water. Wide, 200 feet to the opposite shore. Dark enough that you cannot see the bottom.*

*Then you notice the ship.*

*A small boxy two-masted brig, moving slow, sitting low and full with cargo weight, stained with years of salt and wear. She is drifting in close to the shoreline, and for a moment, maybe only a couple, she is near enough.*

*Behind you, the dogs are getting louder.*

[When he goes for the ship, let the player describe the approach and the grab. Jean-Claude has a climb speed and can cast Jump as a bonus action. No roll unless they want one.]

*The wood is rough and damp under your fingers, the grain swelled with seawater, salt crystals in every groove. You find the gaps between the planking and hold.*

*The shore moves away. The barking grows thin, then small, then silent. The water around you deepens, stretching all the way to the horizon in both directions, and there is no alarm from the deck above. No boots at the rail. No raised voice. Just the occasional chitter of a monkey.*

*Trailing off the stern there's a loose rope, the end of it dragging in the water, looped once around the aft cleat and going nowhere. It bobs in the wake like an invitation.*

*And in the hull beside you, just below the waterline, there is a gap in the boarding. Just wide enough.*

## If Jean-Claude Stays Outside — The Reef Shark

[If the player decides to stay on the outside, he's got the hull grip or the trailing rope. Let him take either.]

*The water here is warm and shallow — you can just make out the sandy bottom through the green murk, maybe twelve feet down. Seagrass. A few small silver fish catching the light. Almost peaceful.*

[Let the comfort land. Then:]

*Something changes. The small fish are gone.*

*You notice a shape. Not close. Not yet. Moving in a slow, unhurried arc maybe thirty feet back in the wake, just beneath the surface — dark, low, five feet of muscle with a blunt nose and a caudal fin cutting a shallow V in the water. A [[reef-shark|Reef Shark]]. It's following the disturbance. Following you.*

> [!mechanic] Reef Shark escalation
> CR 1/2, 22 HP. It is not hunting — it is curious. Run the escalation at your pace:
>
> 1. Holds at thirty feet, circling. Nature/Survival DC 12: reef sharks don't typically attack prey this size unprovoked. *Typically.*
> 2. If he stays still: closes to fifteen feet. The eye, white and flat, finds him. "It isn't moving away."
> 3. If he kicks or splashes: startles back, then resumes the arc tighter — ten feet. The curiosity is more focused.
> 4. If he's on the rope: it noses the trailing end and tugs once. The tug travels directly into whatever body part Jean-Claude has looped it around.
> 5. Escalation ceiling: it does not bite. It bumps — one firm, investigative nudge against his foot or the rope, enough to rattle his teeth. This is the universe suggesting, firmly, that the hull gap is right there.

> [!dm]
> If he still won't go in after the bump, that's fine — cut away to another player. The shark loses interest when it decides he's not food. Give him a moment of dignity about it when you return. But the hull gap is still there.

## In the Hold

[When he takes the gap:]

*The wood is just wide enough. Inside, it smells of bilge water, rope, and something alive.*

[Shift to both Perrin and Jean-Claude.]

*Perrin. Something electric blue and very small drops through a crack in the hull directly above your head and lands in the hold with you.*

> [!dm]
> Neither of them expected company. JC is soaking wet, electric blue, and smaller than Perrin anticipated. Perrin is a Rattkin in a fish hold with stolen gems and an unexplained blade. Play the confusion before any alliance — two people with no reason to trust each other and one strong reason not to make noise. If they communicate, it's whispers and gestures. Don't rush it. This moment ends on its own when the ship moves.

*Above, the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] shifts her weight. Not the sea. Not a wave. Something coming alongside in the dark. At the wheel, Beaumont goes quiet.*
