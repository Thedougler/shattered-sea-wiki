# Beat 7: The Hold

*(On deck. Address Delmar, Crissdalynn, and Stripes.)*

*[[beaumont-sel|Beaumont]]'s voice drops a register when he speaks again. The ease is still there. So is something beneath it.*

**Beaumont**: *"Passenger, go below."* He tilts his head toward the hatch. *"Port hatch. You and the bird both."*

*"Patrol boat. Won't take long."*

[If they push back or ask if it's dangerous:]

**Beaumont**: *"Mais, I run this route eleven years. They know [[saltwright|the Saltwright]]. They know me."* A pause. *"Go below and let me handle it, cher. Ol' Rook is a pussycat."*

*Delmar, you go first. The ladder is steep and the hold swallows you whole. The smell hits before your eyes adjust: brine, old fish, the amber lantern light barely reaching the far corners.*

*Stripes — Beaumont's look finds you too. A tilt of the old head toward the hatch. Down you go.*

> [!dm]
> Three hiding zones: salt barrels along starboard, fish crates stacked against the port bulkhead, grain sacks loosely lashed at the aft wall. Crates are the best cover — deepest shadow, tightest lanes. Anyone declaring stealth does it now, before Crissdalynn comes down.

*Crissdalynn, you come down behind him. Six feet of headroom. You fit, but only just.*

> [!dm]
> Jean-Claude is afraid of birds — instinctive, not chosen. The moment Crissdalynn descends the ladder, play it: Jean-Claude presses flat against the farthest bulkhead, hand on whatever weapon is closest. Crissdalynn doesn't know why. Delmar sees it first. Give that a beat — let Jean-Claude's player decide how they handle the six-foot Aarakocra now sharing their hiding spot in the dark. If the group is being stealthy, ask Jean-Claude for a Stealth check with disadvantage. Whatever the result, the real point is the held breath — two strangers in a dark hold, one of them barely keeping it together, all of them listening to boots cross the deck overhead. Five people in this hold. Nobody planned it. Give it one beat before the ship moves.

*Then the ship moves.*

*A groan from outside the hull, low and structural, the sound of timber under pressure. Another vessel coming alongside in the dark. The [[saltwright|Saltwright]] adjusts to the new weight in a slow, unwilling shift, a change in the rhythm underfoot. Iron on rail. Rope going taut somewhere above you. The sound of it travels through the planking like a hand pressed flat against the wood.*

*Boots on the deck. Several sets, moving without hurry. Then a voice: clipped, bored.*

> [!dm]
> This is [[barnaby-rook|Rook]]. He's above the hatch, not yet visible. Play him flat and practiced — a man who runs this shakedown on every route and has stopped finding it interesting. The boredom is the threat.

*"Manifest. Mr Sel."*

*Beaumont's voice comes down through the planking, unhurried:* **"Right here, cher. Salt, dried fish, cloth out of [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. Eleven years on this route, ya'll should remember? Where's Norrington?"**

*A beat.*

*"Officer Kingsly has retired."*

*Beaumont goes quiet for just a moment.*

**Beaumont**: *"Mm."*

*Hushed orders. Footsteps spreading out across the deck above your heads.*

*"Where's your strongbox?"*

**Beaumont**: *"Non. No strongbox. I keep what I need on me."*

*"Merchant Naval Code c147b. Merchant vessels must securely lock all valuables and contain a working strongbox. You're deficient."* A small pause — the sound of a man who has memorised this line and finds it very useful. *"It's a safety issue."*

*Beaumont's voice does not change.* **"I don't know anything about that, cher. I run the same route I always run."**

*"New policy."* The footsteps shift. Slow. Moving toward the wheel. Toward Beaumont. *"Forty gold clears it tonight. Or we hold the cargo at [[calveno|Calveno]] while the paperwork sorts itself out. Could be a while."*

*Long enough that you hear the water against the hull. Long enough that you hear [[bisou|Bisou]] make one small sound above you.*

**Beaumont**: *"Don't play now, cher."*

*"Then we'll take a look below. See what else you're carrying."*

*Above you, Beaumont goes quiet. The footsteps move toward the hatch.*

*What do you do?*

> [!dm]
> Party options: fight before the crew reaches the bottom of the ladder, let themselves be found and see what Rook actually wants, or signal Beaumont and let him buy more time. All three are viable. The hatch opens in one round. See Session 01 for the boarding encounter.
