# The Saltwright Boarding

*This encounter begins the moment the party commits at the end of Scene 07 — The Hold. Initiative starts on the first hostile action.*

## Setup

[[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] has left the [[hcs-surety|HCS Surety]] and boarded the [[saltwright|Saltwright]], shaking down [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] under the pretense of an inspection. The party is below deck. When they're found, the best Rook offers is forty gold Beaumont doesn't have.

Between-phase breathing room exists. Not a short rest, but enough for a healing potion or tactical reset. Spell slots and ki points don't come back.

### Phase 1 — The Hold

*Party has every advantage. The Enforcer makes it a real fight.*

Three of Rook's crew descend: two deckhands up front, an Enforcer behind them with a heavy crossbow. Standard inspection. They expect shrimp and provisions, not five armed strangers.

Beaumont's emergency stash: 3× potions of healing (2d4+2 each) behind a false panel in the hold's aft wall — visible to any PC who looks aft.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Combatants** | 2× Dravosi Deckhands, 1× Dravosi Enforcer (heavy crossbow — effectively melee in here) |
| **Terrain** | Cramped cargo hold. Maximum engagement range ~20 ft; most lanes 5 ft wide. |
| **Lighting** | Dark. The Enforcer has no darkvision — fighting blind unless he reaches a lantern cone. |
| **Loot** | Beaumont's potion stash: 3× Potion of Healing behind a false panel aft wall. |
| **Surprise** | Party has it. |
| **Pressure** | If any crewman gets off an audible shout, Phase 2 loses surprise. |
| **Win condition** | All three crewmen silenced before they can signal above. |

**PC Spotlights — Phase 1**

- **JC:** Umbral Sight active. Invisible to darkvision creatures, advantage on attacks. Priority target: the Enforcer before he closes to melee.
- **Crissdalynn:** No darkvision. Best position near the hatch to intercept anyone breaking for the ladder.
- **Delmar:** Cramped lanes isolate targets. Rakish Audacity fires freely. Burn SA on the Enforcer first, not low-HP deckhands.
- **Perrin:** Vicious Mockery costs no attack roll and doesn't need line of sight beyond 60 ft. Good opener to shave the Enforcer's first attack.
- **Stripes:** The hold aisle is a single chokepoint he can own. Trip Attack keeps the Enforcer prone; he can't stand without eating an opportunity attack. Action Surge ends him in one round.

| Direction | Lever | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Easier | Remove the Enforcer — only two deckhands descend | Drops to ~75 XP (Trivial) |
| Easier | Alert Call requires crewman's action, not a reaction | Gives party a full round |
| Harder | Enforcer has a lit hooded lantern on his belt | Collapses JC's Umbral Sight in the same zone |
| Harder | One deckhand was already below, watching quietly | No full surprise; he shouts immediately on Round 1 |

### Phase 2 — Above Deck

*Pirates have position; party has momentum.*

The party emerges through the hatch. If Phase 1 was clean, topside crew is watching, not expecting resistance.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Combatants** | 2× Dravosi Enforcers at the hatch, 2× Dravosi Deckhands at the rail |
| **Terrain** | Open deck, night. [[beaumont-sel\|Beaumont]] is at the wheel. |
| **Lighting** | Dim — full night, only the Saltwright's lanterns. |
| **Pressure** | Deckhands at the rail can signal the gangplank to pull before Phase 3 is possible. |
| **Win condition** | Deck crew defeated or driven back before the gangplank is pulled. |

**PC Spotlights — Phase 2**

- **Crissdalynn:** Send airborne toward the rail deckhands immediately — she can intercept before they reach the gangplank.
- **JC:** Umbral Sight still operational in patches near the hull and rigging. Position matters.
- **Delmar:** Enforcers at the hatch are clustered — needs one ally adjacent or isolatable target for Sneak Attack.
- **Perrin:** Clear sight lines to rail deckhands = Eldritch Blast lanes. Priority: whoever is closer to the gangplank.
- **Stripes:** Feline Agility closes instantly. Disarming Strike breaks one Enforcer's Pack Tactics and hands Delmar a clean Sneak Attack angle.

| Direction | Lever | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Easier | Split the Enforcers — one at hatch, one at rail | Breaks Pack Tactics; reduces to single +4 attacks |
| Easier | Deckhands break for the gangplank on their first turn | Converts to chase objective rather than combatants |
| Harder | Both Enforcers focus the first PC out of the hatch with Pack Tactics | ~75% hit chance; avg 18 damage in one round — enough to drop Perrin or Crissdalynn |
| Harder | Alchemist throws an Incendiary Flask through the open hatch as party emerges | DC 13 Dex save, 2d6 fire + burning; raises Phase 2 XP to ~575 |

## What Happened (Played)

### Summary

The party cleared all three phases of the boarding encounter. Phase 1 went fast — the party had the drop on the Dravosi crew in the hold. Cap'n Gorgeous (the Dravosi Enforcer) walked into the ambush and was killed instantly before he could call a shout. The two deckhands followed. Phase 1 complete without alerting the topside crew.

Phase 2: Delmar boosted [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] through the hatchway; she burst out and knocked two deck Dravosi prone. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] came up through a gap in the decking and flanked. Both Dravosi Enforcers dispatched; one deckhand named [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] surrendered when he saw his allies fall. Delmar picked up Geoffrey's sword after he dropped it and gave it back to him — Geoffrey joined the party as the first member of their crew.

Phase 3: The party used Jean-Claude's [[flask-of-endless-water|Decanter of Endless Water]] to blast [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] off the gangplank, separating him from the Enforcer and Alchemist. They quickly finished the Enforcer and wounded the Alchemist severely. The Alchemist threw a gas canister; only Jean-Claude and Perrin were caught in the cloud and both resisted. Jean-Claude identified the batch — standard Grung hunting stock, specifically Simone's hand. The Alchemist was then dispatched. Rook hauled himself back aboard and fired his flintlock at Beaumont; the round bounced off Beaumont's shell. Beaumont entered the fight. Session wrapped here.

### Key Events

- **Phase 1 — The Hold:** Party ambushed the boarding crew in the dark. [[capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]] (Dravosi Enforcer, nicknamed for his appearance) was killed in a single round before he could alert topside. Both deckhands dispatched. Phase 1 complete with surprise intact for Phase 2.
- **Phase 2 — Above Deck:** Crissdalynn launched through the hatch with Delmar's assist, knocking two Dravosi prone. Perrin and Jean-Claude breached from a gap in the decking and flanked. Dravosi Enforcers and one deckhand killed. Geoffrey Draves surrendered. Delmar returned his sword and recruited him; Geoffrey became the party's first crew member.
- **Geoffrey Draves recruited:** Dropped his sword when he saw the fight turn. Delmar convinced him, returned the blade, and he fought on the party's side. First crew acquisition despite the party having no ship yet.
- **Phase 3 — The Gangplank:** Rook separated from his men by a Decanter blast. Party killed the Enforcer and wounded the Alchemist. Canister thrown; Jean-Claude and Perrin both resisted.
- **The Canister Reveal:** Jean-Claude passed the save and placed the gas. Cheap Grung hunting stock — specifically [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s batch. His sister is supplying the Dravosi Crown. He said nothing. Phase 3 continued.
- **Beaumont enters the fight:** Rook fired his flintlock at Beaumont from the gangplank; the round deflected off his shell. Beaumont joined combat: *"Boy, I shit out harder things than you every mornin'."* Session ended here.
- **Current state at session close:** Barnaby Rook — unharmed, alone, all minions dead. Party — full resources largely intact. Encounter still in progress.
