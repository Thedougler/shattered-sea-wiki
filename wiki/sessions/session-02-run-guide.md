# Session 02 — Run Guide: Conflict is a Surety

**4–6 hours. Opens mid-combat. Ends wherever the party sets course.**

Scripted first act (finish the fight, meet Grigori, explore the ship), then full sandbox. By the end of Scene 5 the party should understand what the ship needs to run, which crew might stay, and why sailing straight for Tidefall is a risk.

> [!dm]
> Before individual shares, pull ~120 gp into a ship's fund. Crew wages run ~36 gp/month. Repainting the Crown registry costs 75 gp at [[kalowe|Kalowe]] or 150 gp at [[port-tidefall|Tidefall]]. If nobody raises this, [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]] will — quietly, once.

**Bastion slots:** Boarding Locker = Weapons Locker (installed). Chart Table = Chart Archive (installed). Two slots open. Don't announce as a rules beat — let them discover what they already have.
