# The Rook Resolution

*Opens mid-combat. [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] hauled himself back aboard the [[hcs-surety|HCS Surety]] after being blasted off the gangplank. He fired his flintlock at [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] — round deflected off the shell. Beaumont entered the fight. The [[dravosi-alchemist|Alchemist]] and [[capn-gorgeous|Enforcer]] are dead. [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] is on the party's side.*

**Resolution:** See [[the-rook-resolution|the Rook Resolution page]] — Rook went into the water a second time in Session 02 and did not come back. Presumed dead, taken by something eel-like.

## Before He Falls — Checklist

> [!dm]
> This fight has a shape. Don't let it end before the shape is complete. If party damage is outpacing the checklist, use Parry on every triggering hit.

- [ ] All three lair actions fired in sequence: Arm the Guns → Fire the Guns → Call to Arms.
- [ ] Call to Arms fired and was refused. Grigori's voice came up from below. Rook knows he is alone.
- [ ] At least one PC downed or shoved into the water.
- [ ] At least 3 full rounds of initiative elapsed.

## The Fight

Rook spent two phases watching from the cutter deck. He knows who's hurt, who's dangerous, who the weak link is.

### Round 1 — The Opening

**Free action:** *"Uncover the lanterns."* He kicks the hood off the nearest hooded lantern. Bright light floods his end of the deck. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s Umbral Sight collapses anywhere within the lantern's 20-ft. radius.

**Naval Footwork:** He Disengages as a bonus action, moves to a position where at most two PCs can reach him.

### Round 2+ — The Fight

Two Cutlass attacks per turn, shove option whenever a PC is near the gangplank edge or rail. He Disengages after attacking. Uses rigging and cargo nets as cover, corners where only one PC can engage at a time.

**Targeting priority:**
1. Whoever looks most injured
2. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] — softest target, ranged threat
3. Whoever is closest to the gangplank edge — shove them in

### Legendary Actions

3 per round, regained at start of his turn.

| Action | Cost | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Reposition** | 1 | Move up to half speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Stay ahead of the party's positioning — not to escape. |
| **Cutlass Strike** | 2 | One Cutlass attack with shove option on a hit. Use between PC turns to threaten anyone who thinks the gap is safe. |
| **Pistol Reload** | 2 | Reload one expended flintlock; does not fire. Worth using if the party is playing ranged. |

## Lair Actions — Round Arc

On initiative count 20 each round (losing ties). Cannot repeat same lair action two rounds in a row. Run in this sequence.

### Round 1 — Arm the Guns

Rook bellows the order below. Gun port hatches scrape open. Two amidship cannon ports are uncovered. No immediate effect — guns primed for next round.

> [!dm]
> No save, no damage — setup only. **Beaumont's response:** On his turn, Beaumont tosses [[bisou\|Bisou]] to the nearest exposed gun port. She soaks the powder in the chamber. One gun disabled for the rest of the fight. Beaumont loses his action; party loses one cone of fire in Round 2. This is automatic.

### Round 2 — Fire the Guns

One cannon discharges (the one Bisou didn't reach). 30-ft. cone from its gun port along the weather deck.

> [!mechanic]
> Every creature in the cone: DC 14 Dexterity save. **Fail:** 4d10 bludgeoning. **Success:** half.

> [!dm]
> Average 22 on a fail drops most PCs at this level. Anyone chasing Rook toward amidships is in the kill zone. Use Cutlass Strike as a legendary action this round.

### Round 3 — Call to Arms

Rook grabs the bell line and hauls. Three times. The iron note rings out sharp and clean over dark water.

He waits.

No boots on the ladder. No voice called up.

Then, muffled through the hatch, flat and final: *"No."*

His jaw tightens. He draws his pistol and puts a round through the door frame.

*"You shot me?! Fuck you, Brataby!"*

Footsteps move away. A long pause. They return.

*"The crew says they quit. I can't blame them. Hostile work environment."*

Nobody comes up. The lair action fails.

> [!dm]
> The voice is [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]. The round hit the door frame — he's CR 19, the splinter barely registered. *"You shot me?!"* is genuine irritation. *"The crew says they quit"* lands in his usual register: warm, deliberate, unruffled. This is the moment the table should feel Rook's situation — his crew hates him enough not to fight for him even now. Rook continues fighting. Something shifts in his posture. He stops calling orders. He just swings. **HP threshold:** If Rook is above 55 HP when Round 3 arrives, delay Call to Arms — use Reposition instead and run it once he crosses the threshold.

## Geoffrey Crisis

Once, on any turn Rook chooses — free action, no cost:

*"Draves. You swore an oath. Look at what you're standing next to."*

> [!mechanic]
> [[geoffrey-draves\|Geoffrey]] makes a DC 12 Wisdom save (roll openly). **Fail:** spends next action doing nothing — standing, hand on sword, unable to commit. Recovers the following turn. Use when action economy feels lopsided. Not as the first thing Rook does.

## Roleplay Off-Ramp

Rook is pragmatic, not suicidal. He will not offer terms first.

**Threshold:** ≤40 HP remaining.

**What opens him:** DC 14 Persuasion with something real — leverage, face-saving offer, proof killing him makes things worse.

**What he'll give:** Manifest goes dark, Saltwright goes free, Surety pulls away. He keeps his ship.

**What he won't give:** Names from other inspections. Crown internal business.

---

## Dial

**Rook going down too fast:**
- Parry everything — no per-round limit. Average reduction 11.5 (1d10+6).
- Delay Call to Arms — use Reposition until he crosses 55 HP.
- Geoffrey hesitates one round early.
- Rook climbs the rigging (DC 10 Athletics, costs his action) — positioning problem worth one round.

**Rook surviving too long:**
- Beaumont heals via Kalowe Maneuver (3 potions; 2d4+2 each). Use freely.
- Rook drops a shove this round.
- Geoffrey flanks with a PC, granting advantage on their attack.
- Drop negotiation threshold to 55 HP.
