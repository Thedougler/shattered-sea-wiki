# Session 03 — Before the First Impact

> [!dm]
> Perrin-only contact. One second in-world — no other PC experiences or notices it. If someone was already watching closely: a blink, one hand tightening on wet wood. Cut immediately to the first hull impact.

---

## Beat 1 — Contact

> [!read-aloud]
> No sensation of falling — you are simply somewhere else.
>
> The cold arrives first. Then the pressure — everywhere at once, the weight of countless fathoms pressing down onto your chest. There is something solid beneath your feet. Your lungs scream for air.
>
> You open your mouth. Nothing comes.

*[Let the player react.]*

> [!read-aloud]
> You breathe. Somehow you breathe.

---

## Beat 2 — The Space

> [!read-aloud]
> A shape moves at the edges of the dark. Pale things, lit from within, pulsing in long slow rhythms like breath. Dozens. Hundreds. Moving in loose constellations through the water around you, unbothered by your presence. The floor beneath you is worked stone, worn perfectly smooth. Channels run through it in long deliberate lines toward the darkness. There are no walls. There is no ceiling.
>
> The space orients. Something turns its attention toward you — not from a direction you can find, not through anything you can see.

> [!dm]
> [[auralis|Auralis]] is not a figure. His attention gives the space its orientation; he attends to Perrin the way a system attends to an anomaly. The contact is a fraction of his total focus — the rest is occupied with the fissure.

> [!check] Insight — What is this attention?
> **DC 12.**
> Crit fail: reads as malicious — Perrin may react defensively, possibly breaking contact.
> Fail: nothing readable; the space feels empty.
> Near miss: something is attending, but indistinct.
> Success: monitoring, not predatory. This presence is not threatening him. It is not *for* him.
> Crit success: the attending fraction is near-infinitesimal. The rest of Auralis is occupied with something else entirely.

*[Ask Perrin: What does he do? Does he try to speak? Move? Look for an exit?]*

---

## Beat 3 — The Hunt

> [!read-aloud]
> The bioluminescent things scatter. The darkness closes in — total, moving, cold against every surface at once. There is only the water, everywhere.
>
> The breath arrives before you find it. Cold seawater moving through you in a long slow draw, through something you have no word for. Your chest is enormous. Your body is enormous. It extends behind you in the dark — longer than the Surety, longer than anything you have ever been — and the tail comes last, far back, the long lower lobe trailing through the current, the hook at its end dense and yellowed and scarred white at the joint.
>
> Your skin reads the water in frequencies you cannot name. Above: blood, diluted, days old, mixing with storm churn. The hunger registers it as a direction.

> [!dm]
> The blood above is from the Surety: Rook fight, Wouters overboard, days at anchor in chummed water. The shark has been circling since before the storm. Auralis isn't hiding it — Perrin can smell it. He is pulling attention downward. Ask: *"What does Perrin smell?"*

> [!read-aloud]
> Something else is below. Longer than two ships laid end to end, wider across the back than a warehouse floor, moving in slow unhurried arcs. Its breath comes in long cycles. The body already knows the angle. The body surges.
>
> The impact is a sound you feel in your cartilage. The hook seats. The whale decides to run. The drag is enormous. It dives. Pitches. Sounds into the dark.
>
> And then awareness narrows — contracts down through the whole length of you to one point: the joint. The base of the tail lobe. White-scarred and flexing under load. You feel it flex. Hold. Flex again.

> [!check] Nature — What is this point?
> **DC 10;** advantage with ship or rigging experience.
> Crit fail: reads as reinforced — misses the vulnerability.
> Fail: a junction, no particular significance.
> Near miss: seam under repeated tension.
> Success: structural seam where two different tissues meet under load. This seam can fail.
> Crit success (DC 14+): slashing damage, perpendicular to the grain — can sever the lobe and free the ship.

> [!dm]
> Auralis is showing Perrin the structural weak point. Don't name it. If asked, ask back: *"What does it feel like?"*

> [!read-aloud]
> The hunt does not finish. The ocean leaves.
>
> The bioluminescent things drift back to the edges of the stone. The floor is beneath your feet. You are the size you have always been.

---

## Beat 4 — The Vision

> [!read-aloud]
> The stone beneath your feet goes transparent.
>
> You are looking up through black water at the underside of a ship. From below, from the dark, something rises — fast, faster than anything that size should move. The hook catches the bioluminescent light for just a moment before the hull above you explodes inward. The hook is through. The creature drives its weight sideways and the ship lurches, then pulls. Every person grabbing at the barb is driving it deeper.

> [!dm]
> One of many attacks Auralis has watched over centuries. The sequence is accurate: Hooking Drive hooks the hull; until freed, the ship cannot move; pulling on the barb makes it worse.

> [!read-aloud]
> One more vision. Someone falling from the stricken ship into the water. You see them from below, entering the surface from underneath, bubbles and limbs and the white of a face looking down.
>
> The creature turns toward them before they are fully in the water.

> [!dm]
> Blood Frenzy (advantage on bloodied targets) + Surge reaction (30-ft move + bite when anything enters the water within 30 ft). After the Rook fight, anyone overboard is dead before the crew responds. Don't say it. Show it.

---

## Beat 5 — The Withdrawal

> [!read-aloud]
> The orientation of the room shifts. Something below — far below the transparent floor — pulls the attention of the space away. Not entirely. A fraction remains.
>
> One word arrives. The last one. It costs something — you can feel the cost without knowing what currency is being spent.
>
> **GROW.**

*[Stop. Ask Perrin: does he say it aloud? Does it mean anything to him yet?]*

> [!read-aloud]
> Then nothing. The space is still here. But the orientation is gone. You are alone at the bottom of the world.

> [!dm]
> Auralis is returning his attention to the fissure — this contact cost focus he could not spare. GROW is not an explanation or a goodbye. It is the one thing he wanted Perrin to carry.

> [!read-aloud]
> The stone goes.
>
> The sea comes back — the smell of it, the spray, the hull groaning under anchor strain in the storm. You catch the rail. One hand. One knee on the deck.
>
> The water below the ship is dark.
