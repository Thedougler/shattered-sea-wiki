# Session 03 — Aftermath

> [!dm]
> Keep this short. Three beats: damage, Grigori, depart. Don't let it breathe into a logistics scene.

---

## Beat 1 — The Damage

[[sem-holst|Sem]]'s report, delivered at the breach:

- Tail stub wedged in the hull; he and Geoffrey need two hours to work it free.
- The breach itself is manageable — packed timber and pitch holds for calm water. Not for another storm.
- They need a dry dock.
- ETA to [[calveno|Calveno]] at current pace: five days, weather holding.

> [!dm]
> One exchange with Sem, one clear message: *dry dock or she sinks in the next bad weather.* Move on.

---

## Beat 2 — Grigori

![[session-03-03-grigori-healing.png|Grigori heals a wounded crewman too quickly on the wet dawn deck while the breach work continues behind him]]

First light. [[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] comes up the companionway with a tin mug in each hand. Looks at the rail, the breach work, the state of the crew. Not alarmed.

He offers the mugs to whoever is nearest. Checks on the crew by name. If anyone is hurt, he crouches and asks if he can look — not a command. If yes, he puts a hand on the injury and murmurs. It heals faster than it should.

> [!check] Medicine — The healing
> **DC 13** casual observation / **DC 17** close examination.
> Crit fail: reads as normal magical healing — faster maybe, nothing unexpected.
> Fail: faster than expected, but maybe he's just skilled.
> Success: tissue knitting at a rate unrelated to what Grigori put into it. Injury site is too warm after.
> Crit success (DC 17+): the warmth in his hands doesn't come from body heat and doesn't withdraw when he lifts his hand.

Nobody has seen him sleep. If a player asks: he smiles and says the sea keeps him company.

> [!check] Insight — Reading Grigori
> **DC 14** surface read / **DC 18** sustained attention.
> Crit fail: reads as tense beneath the calm — misread entirely.
> Fail: unreadable; warm and professional, nothing leaks through.
> Success: genuinely unbothered. No alarm behind the calm.
> Crit success (DC 18+): whatever he is, fatigue isn't part of it. His eyes haven't changed since last night.

> [!dm]
> Don't explain the healing or name it. Move on before anyone asks a follow-up — Sem needs the stub, Noor needs something, Geoffrey drops a tool. Grigori stands, picks up his mug, watches the horizon.

---

## Beat 3 — Depart

The stub comes free. Sem patches the breach. Sails up in early morning light. The storm left the water flat and grey.

> [!read-aloud]
> The Surety moves. For the first time since the anchor dropped, the hull answers the wind the way it's supposed to — straight, clean, no drag. Old [[old-faas|Faas]] ties off the last line and looks back at the water behind them. The bioluminescent wake is long gone. The sea gives nothing back.

Set heading: [[calveno|Calveno]]. Five days.
