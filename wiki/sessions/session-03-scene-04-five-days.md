# Session 03 — At Sea — Five Days

> [!dm]
> Run day by day. Roll d20 flavour, resolve downtime, move on. Day 2 has a hard-scripted beat. Calveno closes Day 5.

---

## Opening

> [!read-aloud]
> The storm breaks by mid-morning. The sea settles into long grey swells — nothing like last night. Old Faas has lashed the sail. Thunk is bailing. The ship is moving again, slower than she should be, but moving. Five days to Calveno. What do you do?

---

## Downtime Pitch

Say once at the start of Day 1:

> "Eight hours of sleep, eight hours of ship work. That leaves eight hours a day that are yours — five periods total.
>
> [[sem-holst|Sem]]'s at the hull if you want to help with repair or learn from him. [[old-faas|Old Faas]] can teach the charts. The Chart Archive in the captain's cabin is open. If someone needs real rest, [[alys-kuiper|Alys]] has the surgeon's berth — three days clears most conditions. The crew is here if you want to know them. Casters: if you want something in your pocket before Calveno, talk to me about scrolls. Otherwise — tell me what your character would actually do with five days at sea."

## Day 2 — The Whale *(Perrin)*

> [!dm]
> Script this beat on Day 2 regardless of the flavour roll — cut to it after the roll. If Perrin isn't on watch, call him up: *"Black-Jaw. Come look at this."* The whale is still visible when he arrives.

> [!read-aloud]
> No wind, no chop, no roll. The sea has settled flat. The early light is grey. Your breath mists.

> [!check] Perception — First sighting
> **DC 10** calm/clear · **DC 14** flat water/pre-dawn · **DC 18** low visibility.
> Fail: notices nothing — creature already past; only Perrin sees the last glow.
> Near miss: bow wave as it's passing. *"There's a bow wave ahead — and it didn't come from us."*
> Success: bow wave, then the creature visible. Bioluminescence, slow exhale column.
> Crit success: full view from first movement — outline, glow, long breath.

> [!check] Nature — What is it?
> **DC 12** species recognition · **DC 16** full identification.
> Fail: calls it a sea monster.
> Near miss: breathing cycle marks it non-predatory. Nothing more.
> Success: real species. Bioluminescence and size confirm it.
> Crit success (DC 16+): same species as the thing in Perrin's Auralis dream. Give Perrin advantage — if he hits DC 16, he recognises the shape he was inside.

*Ask Perrin: does he say anything? Does he tell whoever's on watch what he knows?*

> [!dm]
> Same species as the [[session-03-scene-01-auralis|Auralis dream]]. Perrin knows that shape — he hunted one from inside. Nobody else has a name for it. The seed: he was inside something that hunts this. He is very small.

**Spotlight:** Perrin. Secondary: whoever is on watch.
