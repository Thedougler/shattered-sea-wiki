# Calveno Day One

*Two beats: the water noticing Delmar, and Branca finding him. They don't need to happen simultaneously — let the party breathe and explore first.*

---

## Beat 1 — The Gangplank

![[sessions/session-art/session-03-06-gangplank-water.png|The basin water rises toward Delmar's boot as he steps from the Surety onto La Vasca's stone quay]]

The party is crossing from ship to dock at [[la-vasca|La Vasca]]. Grigori has already walked off into the city without looking back. Sem is at the breach. Cobb is hovering.

When [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] steps off the gangplank onto Calveno's stone quay, the water in the covered basin shifts. Not a wake from the hull. A slow deliberate rise along the stone edge, close to where his foot lands — like something in the channel below recognized the weight of him. It reaches toward his foot and then it doesn't. Gone in a breath. The basin settles.

> [!dm]
> Don't narrate this unprompted. Ask Delmar's player what the water looks like if they're watching; otherwise move on. It happened regardless. It will happen again.
>
> Nobody else sees it unless they were already looking at the water at his feet. Don't make it a group event.

---

## Beat 2 — Branca Finds Delmar

She has been at the Calveno Waveservant Shrine for three weeks — the goddess told her where the party was heading before they knew it themselves. When word came up through the port this morning (a Rattkin canal crew, a word to a chandler, the chandler to the shrine), she left and started asking.

She is good at asking. She has been doing this a long time.

She finds [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] in the streets — wherever he's drifted, Mercatura or the canalside or near a tavern. She comes around a corner or steps out of a shop doorway, already consulting the booklet when she looks up.

> [!read-aloud]
> A woman in her mid-forties, salt-white hair pulled back hard, Waveservant blue-grey robes. A slight limp — the kind that has been ignored so long it's become posture. She produces a small leather booklet from her sleeve, consults it without urgency, and looks up at Fisk with the specific warmth of someone who has delivered very bad news many times and decided the kindest thing is to make it easy. *"You're a difficult man to find,"* she says, pleasantly. *"We have an appointment."*

> [!check] Insight — "We have an appointment"
> DC 12 surface read, DC 16 mechanism clear.
>
> - Crit fail: reads her as cold or threatening — misread; she's warm, not menacing.
> - Fail: professional veneer; can't read through it.
> - Near miss: she means exactly what she said. Not rhetorical.
> - Success: not metaphor. A name is in the booklet — probably his. Not improvising and not lying.
> - Crit success (DC 16+): the goddess told her to be here. Branca treats that as operationally equivalent to a calendar entry. She has done this many times.

## The Invitation

![[sessions/session-art/session-03-06-shrine-communion.png|Branca stands knee-deep in the Waveservant Shrine basin while Delmar watches the canal water listen]]

[[branca|Branca]] wants him to accompany her to the Waveservant Shrine — Calveno for her communion with Umberlee. Today, if possible. She checks the booklet: *"I've gone ahead and blocked the third tide — does that work, or should I find something that fits your schedule a little better?"*

She does not explain what the communion entails. Not because she's withholding — because she assumes he knows.

> [!dm]
> **If Delmar asks what the communion is:** she says it's routine — the goddess has questions and she conveys them. *"You don't need to say anything unless you want to."* She believes this is accurate and reassuring.
> **If Delmar refuses:** Branca smiles, notes the refusal in the booklet, says she'll find another slot. She's in Calveno until further notice.

### At the Shrine

The basin is fed directly from the canal. She steps into it up to her knees, tilts her head back, and speaks low in Aquan. Whatever passes between her and the goddess happens in silence.

> [!check] Perception (Delmar) — The basin water
> DC 10, or passive Perception 10.
>
> - Fail: misses it; only Branca knows it happened.
> - Near miss: the water near his foot rises slightly and settles — brief, gone.
> - Success: the water rises toward him when he approaches the basin, the same slow deliberate reach as the gangplank. Nobody else triggers it.
> - Crit success: the reach is directional and controlled — not responding to current or his movement. Responding to him.

She looks at Delmar: *"She sees you."* A pause. *"That's all she wanted. For now."* She steps out of the water, dries her robes without comment, and notes the appointment as complete.

> [!check] Religion — What does "She sees you" mean?
> DC 14 surface read, DC 18 full implications.
>
> - Crit fail: thinks being seen is being claimed — incorrect; confuses observation with a pact.
> - Fail: sounds significant but meaning is opaque.
> - Near miss: gods of this type maintain awareness through open water; different from prayer.
> - Success: being seen means Delmar's actions near the sea are potentially visible to Umberlee. Not passive.
> - Crit success (DC 18+): being seen is not being claimed. It is being *filed*. The beginning of a kind of attention.

> [!dm]
> **If Delmar brings the party:** the shrine is public. Any PC present sees Branca step into the basin, go still, and come back with that professional energy intact. The goddess said nothing to them. That is its own information.
>
> **If Crissdalynn is present:** Branca produces a tide-table booklet after the communion ends and hands it to Crissdalynn without ceremony. Umberlee told her to keep it until someone came in from the direction of the Maw. Don't explain what it means — this is Crissdalynn's entry hook.
