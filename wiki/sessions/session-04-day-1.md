# Session 04 — Day 1: Arrival Evening

*Pick up in the street the instant Kyzil lands — late afternoon, Le Paludi. This plays out the reunion Session 03 ended on. Give Crissdalynn the floor.*

---

## Strong Start — Kyzil Reunion

> [!read-aloud]
> The wind goes quiet. The sun is high and behind you — and then something with the sun behind it drops out of the sky in dead silence and hits Crissdalynn square in the chest, two arms around her before the wind is even back. The smell lands before the sight does: feathers, incense, home. "Oh my God, Priscilla. You're alive." [[master-kyzil\|Master Kyzil]] holds her at arm's length, then looks at the rest of you, remembering himself. "Hi. I'm — Master Kyzil. What happened?"

Let the reunion breathe. Then the evening presses in: the ship in its cradle, a city dressing for festival, and — for Delmar — a woman in a shrine across the district who has not moved since she found him an hour ago.

> [!dm]
> This is Day 1 evening; the open city day is tomorrow (registration at dawn). If the party wants to bed down, fine — but Branca does not wait *politely*, she waits *absolutely* (see below). Handle Delmar's awareness of her before he rests easy.

---

## Kyzil — Roleplay Notes

**NPC — Master Kyzil** ([[master-kyzil\|page]]): Owl Obi-Wan — relief held under monastic discipline. He grabs Crissdalynn like she's been dead for a year; then recovers formality with visible effort. Speaks softly. First question to anyone near her is always "Who." Not hostile — categorizing.

**What he knows:** He searched east of Midchain. Corrected course when word reached him that Crissdalynn was alive and heading toward the Central Strait. He knows her original ship went down to *"a foul wind from the south"* — he will not volunteer this tonight. Grief sits under discipline; he shares facts when he judges her ready, not when the party asks.

**What he offers (tonight):**
- Asks about Crissdalynn's pilgrimage progress — how many of the four trials has she completed?
- Offers to teach **Read the Current** (a monk technique) in a future sparring session.
- Warns that Maw weather has made the High Eyrie approach route dangerous.
- Will challenge the party to a four-on-one nonlethal spar if anyone wants to test him (playful, not threatening; he wins easily and enjoys himself).

> [!dm]
> Don't dump Kyzil's full lore. He is protective. Tonight he wants to feed Crissdalynn, assess the people around her, and sleep in the same building. Deeper threads — the foul wind, the Maw, the Red Lady traces — arrive on Days 2–3 through play, not exposition.

## Branca's Vigil

**[[branca\|Branca]] will not leave the [[waveservant-shrine\|Waveservant Shrine]].** She found Delmar an hour ago, he put her off, she walked to the shrine and sat down. She has not moved since. She will not — overnight, all of tomorrow, however long it takes. She is that much of a zealot, and waiting costs her nothing.

There is no skipping this scene; there is only *when* Delmar walks into it. The Pearl Summons runs in full on [[session-04-day-2\|Day 2]]. Tonight establishes the pressure.

> [!dm]
> If Delmar tries to long rest without going to the shrine, give it about ten seconds, then: he can't quite settle — and when he looks toward the Mercatura he *knows*, the way you know a held breath, that she's still in there in the dark, awake, hands folded, waiting. She'll outlast him. Don't force the visit tonight. Let the dread sit.

**If Delmar goes to the shrine tonight:** run the [[session-04-day-2#the-shrine--pearl-summons|Pearl Summons]] from Day 2 immediately. It works at any hour — the shrine is always open; the font is always breathing.

## The Evening City

The party has a few hours before dark. Calveno is dressing for festival — cloth going up on facades, registration desks being assembled on the Mercatura bridge, canal boats hung with lanterns.

| If the party wants... | Put in front of them |
|---|---|
| Food and lodging | [[ponte-bassa\|The Ponte Bassa]] — [[oleandro-fuschi\|Oleandro Fuschi]] remembers vessels, not names. A quiet tavern that smells of canal water and fried squid. |
| Ship check | [[la-vasca\|La Vasca]] — Sem is already naming repairs. The ship is safe; it is not solved. |
| City walk | Festival setup is visible: cloth, masks, registration materials. A Beffa clerk tests the bridge desk in lamplight. |
| Supplies | [[moretti-and-sons\|Moretti & Sons]] — [[giacomo-moretti\|Giacomo]] closes at sundown during festival week but will open for a real order. |
| Privacy | La Vasca's covered basin hides the ship. The party's faces are not hidden. |

## Cobb's Check-In

[[cobb\|Cobb]] finds Perrin before the evening is done — at the tavern, the dock, or wherever Perrin settles. He knows Perrin already went to see Nona.

> [!read-aloud]
> A young Rattkin in a dockworker's coat drops onto the bench beside Perrin without asking. "She was glad you came. She won't say it twice, so I'm saying it." He glances at the sending stone if it's visible. "That thing's not decorative. When she uses it, she means now, not when you get around to it."

> [!dm]
> Cobb is bridging. Perrin already met Nona, apologized, and left with the sending stone and an unconditional favor he hasn't heard yet. Cobb's role tonight is to reinforce that the stone is live and the favor is coming — not to arrange a first meeting.
