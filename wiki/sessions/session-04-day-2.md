## Beffa Registration

*Optional — the most fun; background if skipped.*

> [!read-aloud]
> The Mercatura bridge has become a registration desk. A clerk with a ledger and a long-suffering face takes team names, marks, categories, and stakes, while three would-be tricksters argue about whether a bucket counts as a "device."

**The toy:** if they register, get the table choosing — team name, mark, category, symbol, stake. Standard entry is 25 gp; Legendary-scale is 50 gp. Scoring: Craft 10, Surprise 5, Recovery 5, out of 20. Loud, dumb, theirs.

**[[giacomo-moretti\|Giacomo Moretti]]** has signed the Accord for twenty years and has never been successfully pranked. The committee finds this professionally embarrassing. He is listed as a valid mark.

**Hook:** a Beffa team picks a PC as their mark in the first hour — the *Wrong Mark* drop (fish oil from a balcony, a wrong name on a real permit; [[calveno-street-encounters\|Street Encounters #1]]).

**If ignored:** the Beffa runs without them — cloth in the streets, nervous marks, daily results at sundown. Nothing closes; it's ambient texture and a five-day clock.

## The Shrine — Pearl Summons

*Delmar (primary), Crissdalynn (was there when Branca first found him). Revelation / horror set-piece.*

This is a **fixed dramatic beat**, not a negotiation. The offer is unacceptable by design; the refusal is expected; the point is the demonstration. Run it as scripted and let the silence between boxes work.

### Arrival

> [!read-aloud]
> The [[waveservant-shrine\|Waveservant Shrine]] is a low, tide-stained vault off a Mercatura canal. The font behind the bench holds a hand's depth of seawater that will not lie flat — it rises and falls, slow, like something breathing under it. [[branca\|Branca]] is exactly where she sat down: upright, awake, salt-white hair pulled back hard, the little leather tide-table booklet open on her knee. "So," she says, bright and clipped, the warmth of someone rescheduling a missed meeting. "You came. Good. We have an appointment."

**NPC — Branca:** corporate cult executive assistant — bubbly, efficient, too much eye contact; opens "So!", closes "Does that work for you?", checks the booklet mid-sentence as if it settles every objection. She believes making divine horror *pleasant* is a kindness. She is the soft instrument; what comes through her is not.

### The Possession

Branca stops mid-sentence. Her eyes roll back to white. The voice is not hers.

> [!read-aloud]
> Branca's eyes roll white. When she speaks it is not her voice — it is the sea given a throat, cold and enormous and patient. "FISK. You carry what is Mine. Go down to the wreck. Bring Me the Pearl. Return it to My shrine — and I will grant you the one mercy you have earned: a swift death, and the souls you stole brought home to Me. This is My grace. Accept it."

**The demand (plainly):** retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls\|Pearl of Souls]] from the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] wreck at the [[the-drowned-maw\|Drowned Maw]] and return it to a shrine of Umberlee. The "reward" is Delmar's *own death* and the surrender of the souls he carries. A death sentence dressed as salvation. He will refuse — by design.

> [!dm]
> This brushes Delmar's secret. The party hears that he **carries souls Umberlee claims** and that there's a Pearl to retrieve. They need not learn the five captains / Vel Orn story — keep Her words to "what is Mine" and "the souls you stole." Only Delmar's player knows he carries voices.

### The Wrath

The instant Delmar refuses:

> [!read-aloud]
> The refusal is barely out of your mouth before the light goes wrong. Thunder walks across a sky that was clear a breath ago; the canal shivers in its channel. The thing wearing Branca *roars* — and then, almost offhand, it takes her head in her own two hands and snaps her neck. The body folds. The shrine goes silent. Branca lies on the wet stone, not breathing.

Hold the silence. Then —

### The Return

> [!read-aloud]
> Mist gathers off the canal — too fast, too deliberate — and curls in around the fallen woman like a tide coming home. Branca's chest heaves. She drags in one enormous, gasping breath, sits up, blinks the white from her eyes, finds the booklet, smooths her robe, and looks up with that same bright warmth, as if nothing happened. "I hope," she says, "you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace."

> [!check] Insight — After the Revival
> DC 12 (no advantage from prior knowledge; this is emotional, not logical).
> Fail: reads as shock; the depth doesn't register.
> Success: Branca means it completely — she experienced her own murder as a privilege and is not performing. *That* is the horror.
> Crit success: Branca has been through this before. Not once. Her faith is forged, not learned — and the thing that spoke through her left a trace of salt on the air that is not fading.

**The bind:** the only way to satisfy the goddess is to die for her, so Delmar has to find a third answer — and the Pearl she wants is inside the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] — exactly what the salvage commission is racing for. Demand and money pull at the same wreck from opposite directions.

## Warren & Nona's Table

*Perrin (primary, family), Delmar (ship silhouette). Social / logistics.*

Two linked problems live in the Warren. **(a)** The *Uncertainty* still reads as a Crown cutter to anyone who knows cutters — paint won't fix a silhouette. [[carpenters-slip\|Carpenters' Slip]] won't take a cold approach; she needs a Warren introduction, which runs through Nona. **(b)** Nona's **favor** ([[nonas-favor\|the unconditional yes]]) and the **sending stone** are live in Perrin's pocket.

### The Hook

> [!read-aloud]
> [[le-paludi\|Le Paludi]] runs on quiet water and long memory. The smell of [[nona-black-jaw\|Nona]]'s kitchen reaches the canal before her door does.

**NPC — Nona Black-Jaw** ([[nona-black-jaw\|page]]): feeds you first, names what you owe second — as accounting, not threat. She knows the [[vestra\|Vestra]] is gone (quietly grieving the men and the money while planning the response).

### The Favor

> [!dm]
> **Nona's favor (canon).** Word reached Nona that Perrin came in with a crew including a **blue Grung in a beret and moustache** ([[jean-claude-tabarnack\|Jean-Claude]]). She reads it as luck. She's had reports she'd normally dismiss as impossible this far north — **Grung moving in the old Warren tunnels that intersect the Calveno sewers** — and wants them confirmed or dismissed. Who better to hunt Grung than a Grung. **The ask:** send JC (with whoever he wants) into the tunnels to confirm. This is the door into the raid — those sewer Grung are [[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|Simone's extraction crews]]; this and the handler thread are the same investigation from two ends.

### The Ship Problem

**NPC — Savia Brentino** ([[savia-brentino\|Carpenters' Slip]]): dry, unhurried, says the hard thing once and lets it sit. *Wants:* a Warren introduction before she'll deal — three ships tried cold this month and are on her list. *Reveals (flatly, at the end):* "She'll still read as a cutter to anyone who knows cutters. That's not a paint problem."

**Terms:** 220 gp + 5 city days + one **unassigned Tangle favour**. The bow registry plate is the party's call.

> [!check] Insight — On Savia
> DC 13.
> Fail: she reads as a dead end, the silhouette stays unsolved.
> Success: she isn't hostile, she's screening; an introduction flips her.
> Crit success: the three ships she turned away this month were all asking the wrong questions. Her refusal is professional, not personal — she knows who she can trust and is waiting for proof.

**If ignored:** Nona doesn't chase — she lets the silence sit, then uses the **sending stone** on her own timing. The silhouette stays live and the [[knighton-pursuit\|Crown clock]] keeps ticking; every unpapered day is a day the ship is easier to recognize.

## First Handler Beat — Jean-Claude

*JC only. Private. One recognition beat — do not announce to the table.*

> [!dm]
> As soon as Jean-Claude is in any public space — the Mercatura, the Bridge, a Velo square — offer him one of these as a private sensory note. Let his player decide what JC does with it.

**Crowd positioning.** A Grung at the Bridge or Velo stands in the one position that gives sightlines to three exits without appearing to watch any. Jean-Claude has stood in that position. It is trained, not instinctive.

> [!read-aloud]
> A blue-caste Grung stands at the edge of a Velo square, watching a street performer. He is not enjoying it. He is watching how the crowd *moves* when the performer makes a scene — where people turn, where the gaps open, how long a knot of onlookers takes to dissolve.

**If JC acts:** this is observation, not combat. The Velo Grung is polite, says he's here for the festival. Karath accent, not Veth. No trade goods, no festival registration, no declared purpose for being this far west. If JC pushes harder, the Grung excuses himself and leaves — quickly, efficiently, without running. He files a report. JC's face enters the handler network.

**If JC reports to the party:** the party now has one confirmed handler sighting. No proof, no combat, no confirmation of the raid — just dread that Jean-Claude can name and no one else can.

**If ignored:** the handler completes his route assessment. One more lane is locked for the raid.

## Red Lady — First Threads

*Delmar (primary), Crissdalynn (Kyzil sub-beat). Investigation / social.*

Two ways this surfaces today:

**Through city gossip (any PC asking about salvage, the Maw, or unusual money):**

> [!read-aloud]
> A chandler lowers his voice without being asked. "You're not the first to ask about Maw salvage this month. There's money moving. Serious money — three buyers, or one buyer with three hands, nobody's sure. And they're not advertising a target."

> [!check] Investigation / Gather Info — Salvage Talk
> DC 12 (DC 8 if Delmar asks specifically about Maw salvage gear).
> Fail: only the rumour of "money moving."
> Success (two of three): hired diving apparatus, compressed-air bladders, a sealed chart package a courier wouldn't hand off.
> Crit success: all three, plus the name "Zusto" — a Bridge broker handling the supply chain.

**Through Kyzil (Crissdalynn only):**

[[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] knows what the party doesn't — Crissdalynn's original ship went down to *"a foul wind from the south."* If she pulls on Red Lady traces with Kyzil present, he can set that phrase against the Maw rumours. Reveal one layer at most — he's grief held under discipline; his instinct is to protect her, not dump lore.

> [!dm]
> Don't force the Kyzil reveal. If Crissdalynn asks about the Maw, wrecks, or her past in his presence, he gives one careful sentence. If she doesn't, he holds it. His protection is silence, not secrecy.

**If ignored:** the commission clock ticks. One more day of gear sourcing, crew assembly, and chart preparation. Savia mentions the suspicious enquiries to anyone who earns her trust — but only once.

## Moustache Craftsman — JC Roleplay Beat

*Optional. Drop if JC walks through the Velo Quarter.*

> [!read-aloud]
> A narrow booth between a wig maker and a vendor of theatrical smoke. The boards are covered in prosthetics, false beards, and a row of spectacles the man inside is not wearing. The spirit gum smell reaches you from three feet away. He has not looked up.

**Giacinto** — gnome, late sixties, reading glasses on forehead, permanently smelling of spirit gum. Spots JC's moustache as his own work. Points to the inner-edge cut on the right corner: *"That's one of mine. Who are you wearing it for?"*

He explains that the moustache-and-beret combination reads as "performer" — which, in a festival city, is its own kind of invisible. *"Nobody looks twice at a performer. Don't change anything."* He begins re-sealing the edges before anyone agrees to it.
