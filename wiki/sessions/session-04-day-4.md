# Session 04 — Day 4: Pre-Raid

*Second Finestra day. The raid fires tomorrow. Today the city is being tuned: false alarms conditioning the crowd, final handler deployment, Slaad pre-positioned. The party does not know any of this unless they have been pulling threads. Today is the last day they can act before the operation runs.*

---

| Thread | State at dawn |
|---|---|
| **Raid** | Day 4 of prep. Final handler deployment. All five extraction paths confirmed and clear. Slaad materials in final position. Purple-caste raid teams en route to the city via the *vethka*. **Tomorrow is the strike.** |

## Handler Cell — Combat

*Jean-Claude (primary), anyone he brings. Combat-ready encounter.*

If JC has been following the handler thread — Day 2 recognition beats, Day 3 tunnel investigation — today he can confront a handler cell directly. This is the escalation point.

### Setup

JC spots what looks like operational handler work — a pair of Grung in nondescript trade clothes at the same Bridge pillar he clocked on Day 2, different position, same posture. Not watching the performer. Watching the crowd move around the performer. The hour is wrong for the spot.

> [!read-aloud]
> Two figures in trade clothes at the same Bridge pillar Jean-Claude clocked two days ago — different position now, same posture. They are not watching the performer. They are watching the crowd move around the performer. The hour is wrong for the spot.

### The Fight

**Location:** Le Paludi — narrow canal, 5 ft alleys, two boats tied at the nearest mooring. Poor light. Festival crowd two streets over.

> [!mechanic]
> **Blue-Caste Handler x 2.** Scout stats (MM p.349). AC 13, HP 16. Shortbow +4, 1d8+3 piercing + **Grung Poison** (DC 11 Con save or poisoned 1 min). Standing Leap 20 ft as part of movement. **Breaks and runs at half HP** — they have a report to file.

**Tactics:** break contact first. Fight only if cornered. One draws pursuit down the alley while the other cuts through a boat mooring. If one escapes, they report. If both escape, Simone learns the party is in Calveno and JC's face is now a priority.

### What the Handlers Carry

One handler has:
- **Binding rope** — light, professional, pre-knotted
- **Sealed wax message cylinder** — blank on inspection, but the wax bears a mark Jean-Claude recognizes from Botukuri shipping labels

> [!dm]
> **Three outcomes, all real:**
> **Capture alive:** the handler knows arrival timing and the extraction canal route. Telling the Warren closes one of five lanes. The handler also knows the *vethka* position and can confirm the Dravosi patrol gap — giving the party the full operational picture for the first time.
> **Kill both:** one lane goes dark. Simone doesn't learn the cell was made. The raid loses one strike point without explanation.
> **Let them go:** Simone learns the party is in Calveno. JC's face is now a report. The raid proceeds at full strength with the additional knowledge that the Blue Grung Deserter is in the city.

### If JC Doesn't Engage

The handler cell completes its final route confirmation. All five lanes are open and timed. Tomorrow's raid proceeds at full capacity. No combat, no intelligence, no consequence today — but the absence of action *is* the consequence.

## Red Lady — The Convergence

*Delmar (primary), Crissdalynn. The vise tightens.*

By Day 4, three threads are pointing at the same wreck. Let the party feel the pressure without narrating the connection for them.

| Thread | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Umberlee's demand** | Retrieve the Pearl from the Red Lady wreck. Return it to a shrine. The reward is Delmar's death. |
| **Salvage commission** | A funded crew is 3–5 days from departing Calveno for the same wreck. Better equipped, better charted. |
| **Kyzil's phrase** | "Your ship didn't go down to weather, Priscilla." The Red Lady is both Delmar's wreck and part of Crissdalynn's past. |

### The Offer

If Delmar has been visible in Calveno as a Maw expert — asking questions at the Bridge, trading intel with Zusto, mentioned by name to a chandler — someone comes to him.

> [!read-aloud]
> A runner from the Bridge finds Delmar at the Ponte Bassa. Clean clothes, damp hair, a polite voice that does not carry. "My employer heard there's a captain in Calveno who's actually sailed the Maw. Not many of those. He'd like to talk. No obligation. He's buying."

> [!dm]
> The offer is genuine. The commission's operational lead wants Maw navigation expertise and is willing to pay for it. The terms are: retained navigator on the salvage expedition, standard crew share of recovered cargo, departure in 3–5 days. The trap is structural, not personal — joining the commission puts Delmar inside the operation that's racing him to the Pearl. Don't spring it as a betrayal. Let it sit as a complicated offer the party has to discuss.

**If ignored:** the commission doesn't need him. They have charts. The lugger departs on schedule and reaches the Maw ahead of the party.

## False Alarms — Crowd Conditioning

*Ambient. One or two beats throughout the day.*

The Beffa is doing its work. The crowd has been trained.

> [!read-aloud]
> A scream cuts across the Mercatura — high, sharp, genuine terror. The crowd freezes for one breath. Then someone sees the mask, and the laughter starts. A Beffa team has staged a mugging with fake blood and a victim who falls too theatrically. The crowd claps. The mark — who was the victim — takes a bow. Everyone relaxes.

> [!dm]
> This is the conditioning the raid depends on. Tomorrow, the first screams during the real operation will buy three to five seconds of crowd hesitation — people looking for the mask, waiting for the punchline, before they understand that the thing happening in front of them is not a performance. That delay is the operational gap. Let the party see the training happening. Don't explain what it's for.

**Second beat (harbour approach):**

> [!read-aloud]
> Three figures in half-masks sprint past the harbour approach and vault a canal railing — Beffa runners, mid-prank, pursued by a furious mark with wine-stained trousers. The watch at the canal gate watches them pass without moving. The gate sergeant shakes his head.

The watch is not going to respond to screams during festival week unless someone makes them. Their intervention threshold is higher than it will be on any other week of the year. The raid planners know this.

## Raid Prep Status Check

*DM reference only. Assess the board at end of day.*

The raid fires tomorrow (Beffa Day 4 / party Day 5). The party's actions over Days 1–4 determine what state it's in.

| Element | Fully intact | Partially disrupted | Fully disrupted |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Extraction lanes (5)** | All five sewer routes open and timed | 1–2 routes sealed via Warren; 3–4 remain | All five sealed (requires extraordinary party action) |
| **Handler network** | All cells operational | 1 cell destroyed or captured; Simone alerted or not | Multiple cells made; network compromised |
| **Slaad pre-positioning** | In place, ready for distraction deployment | Delayed if handlers were disrupted on Day 3+ | Intercepted only if the party found the staging area |
| **Crowd conditioning** | Festival has trained perfect hesitation | Unchanged — the Beffa did this, not the handlers | Cannot be disrupted; it's civic behavior |
| **Dravosi patrol gap** | Scheduled and holding | Unchanged — this is real naval scheduling | Could be closed only if the party warned a Dravosi authority |

> [!dm]
> The party cannot stop the raid entirely within the scope of Session 04. They can reduce its scale. Each extraction lane sealed removes one strike point. Each handler captured gives the Warren intelligence to pre-position responders. A fully disrupted raid (3+ lanes closed, handler network compromised) becomes a chaotic partial operation that the city can survive — dozens taken instead of hundreds. A fully intact raid is a watershed. Either result changes the world.

## Night Before the Storm

**If the party has assembled the picture:** they know an organized Grung operation is running through Calveno's sewers, timed to the festival. They may know the scale (five strikes), the extraction method (sewer to outer water), and the pickup (*vethka* past the patrol minimum). They have options: warn the Warren, warn the watch, position themselves at a known strike point, seal more lanes, pursue the *vethka*.

**If the party hasn't assembled the picture:** they sleep. Tomorrow is the last day of La Finestra. The committee is preparing Il Giudizio scoring. The city is relaxed, amused, a little drunk. The crowd will hesitate.

> [!dm]
> Don't tell the party what they missed. If they go to bed without acting on the handler thread, that is a valid outcome — the raid's scale is the consequence, not a punishment. If they've been focused on the Pearl, the ship, the Warren, the Beffa — those are real choices with real payoffs. The raid is what moves when they look away.
