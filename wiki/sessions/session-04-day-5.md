# Session 04 — Day 5: The Raid

*Last day of La Finestra. Crowd peak. The ground opens. This is the session's crescendo if the table reaches it — if not, hold for Session 05. Run the [[calveno-beffa-grung-raid|raid situation file]] alongside this page.*

---

## Dawn — What Changed Overnight

| Thread | State at dawn |
|---|---|
| **Raid** | All prep complete. Blackpowder magazines loaded and guarded. Summoning circle live. Purple-caste raid teams in position below each strike site. *Vethka* at anchor past the patrol line. Catching forces staged at perimeter positions. **The operation fires at crowd peak.** |
| **Beffa** | Last Finestra day. Il Giudizio scoring scheduled for sundown. The crowd is at maximum density and maximum complacency — five days of pranks have trained them to wait for the punchline. |
| **Festival Pranks** | Grung masks, Grung-themed comedy bits, and fake "raids" have been running for three days. The city laughs at Grung. |
| **Ship** | Day 5 of ~5. Hull work complete or nearly so. Registry plate installed. |
| **Red Lady** | Commission clock: **1–3 days.** Crew assembled. Departure imminent. |

> [!dm]
> The raid fires at crowd peak — late morning, when the Mercatura and Bridge are at maximum density for the final Finestra day. The party does not get a warning. They get a sound.

---

## The Detonation

**Timing:** mid-morning, maximum crowd density in the festival districts.

Five detonations fire within seconds of each other. The primary charge is the largest — the Mercatura plaza. Four secondary charges hit the Bridge, two points in Le Paludi's working yards, and the harbour approach.

### The Primary — Mercatura Plaza

> [!read-aloud]
> The sound comes from below. Not thunder — thunder has a sky. This has stone. The Mercatura plaza shudders once, a ripple running through the cobblestones like a wave through water. Then the centre drops. Twenty feet of plaza collapses inward — stone, earth, festival cloth, registration desks, people — falling into a cloud of dust and noise that swallows everything. The crowd does not scream. They are waiting for the punchline.
>
> Then something hauls itself out of the hole.

Hold for one breath. Then —

> [!read-aloud]
> It is red. It is enormous. It pulls itself up through rubble and dust on arms too long for its body, a mouth that opens wider than a mouth should, and eyes that are not looking at the crowd the way a person looks at a crowd. It is looking at the crowd the way a fire looks at a building. The Slaad shakes the dust from its back, opens that impossible mouth, and roars.
>
> The screaming starts.

> [!dm]
> The crowd's first instinct is to freeze — three days of false alarms have conditioned them to wait before reacting. That hesitation buys the Grung three to five seconds. By the time the crowd breaks and runs, the secondary detonations have fired, and the festival's trained response ("wait for the joke") has become the city's worst liability.

### The Secondary Sites — Simultaneous

At the four secondary strike points, smaller charges open holes in the ground. No collapse — a breach. Dust, debris, a gap wide enough for Grung to pour through.

> [!read-aloud]
> The ground buckles — not a collapse, not here, but the cobblestones crack and a section of yard floor drops two feet. Dust fountains up. From below, shapes — small, fast, disciplined — emerge through the gap and fan out into the crowd. They are not panicking. They know exactly where they are going.

> [!dm]
> At each secondary site: purple-caste raid teams emerge from a spent *Globe of Invulnerability* into the chaos. They push the crowd — specifically able-bodied workers, sailors, dockhands — toward a catching force positioned at the perimeter. The catching force binds targets and moves them underground through the breach within two minutes. The operation is fast, silent where it can be, and brutally efficient. Each secondary site runs independently; losing one does not stop the others.

## The Slaad — Boss Encounter

The session's climactic fight if the party reaches Day 5. The Slaad is at the primary breach point in the Mercatura.

> [!mechanic]
> **Red Slaad.** MM p.276. AC 14 (natural armor), HP 93 (11d10+33). Speed 30 ft.
> STR 16 (+3) DEX 12 (+1) CON 16 (+3) INT 6 (-2) WIS 6 (-2) CHA 7 (-2).
> **Multiattack:** 3 attacks — one Bite (2d4+3 piercing), two Claws (2d6+3 slashing). On a claw hit, target must succeed DC 14 Con save or be implanted with a Slaad egg (disease; 3 months to hatch — this is a long-term horror, not an immediate mechanic).
> **Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves vs. spells and magical effects.
> **Regeneration.** Regains 10 HP at start of its turn if it has at least 1 HP.
> **Damage Resistances:** acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder.

**Environment:** Collapsed plaza. Rubble, dust, broken stone, festival cloth tangled in debris. Dim light from dust. Civilians fleeing in all directions. 10-ft-deep crater at centre where the sewer chamber was.

**Tactics:** The Slaad is not intelligent. It attacks the nearest creature. It does not pursue fleeing civilians — it attacks whatever stands and fights. It uses the crater rim as high ground when it can. It will jump into and out of the sewer breach if pressed.

> [!dm]
> **The choice the Slaad creates:** every round the party fights the Slaad is a round the extraction runs at the secondary sites. The party hears screams from other districts. They see Grung moving people underground at the edge of their vision. If they stay and fight, they save the Mercatura — the Slaad will kill civilians left in the area. If they split, each group faces a real threat alone. This is not a punishment for fighting the boss — it is a design decision that makes the raid feel like a coordinated operation, not a single encounter.
>
> **Scaling:** for a Level 3 party of four, the Red Slaad is a Hard-to-Deadly encounter. The regeneration is the primary threat — they need sustained damage output to outpace 10 HP/round. If the fight drags: a Warren reinforcement (Ruk, armed, angry) arrives after 5 rounds. If the fight is too easy: the Slaad targets a cluster of trapped civilians in the rubble, forcing the party to reposition.

## The Secondary Strikes — What the Party Sees

If the party is not at the Mercatura when it fires, they experience a secondary strike up close.

### Strike Site — The Bridge

> [!read-aloud]
> The Bridge shakes. A section of the approach ramp cracks open and Grung pour out — purple-caste, armed, moving in formation. They ignore the bridge traffic. They are heading for the working crowd at the lower quay. A catching force is already in position — a line of small figures in festival cloaks, standing too still, arms out, herding the running crowd toward the breach.

### Strike Site — Le Paludi Working Yard

> [!read-aloud]
> The yard floor drops. Two dockhands fall with it. Dust. A hand reaches up from below and pulls one of them down. Grung emerge — fast, low, practiced. They bind the nearest dockhand before his partner has finished turning around. The rope is pre-knotted. The work takes four seconds.

### Strike Site — Harbour Approach

> [!read-aloud]
> The harbour-approach cobblestones split like a wound. Grung haul themselves up through it, already running toward the tied boats. A boatman who sleeps aboard his vessel is dragged from his cabin. His shout is cut short. Two more Grung are working the nearest mooring line, clearing the path to the outer water.

> [!dm]
> Each secondary site has a purple-caste raid team (4 Grung Elite Warriors) and a catching force (6 Grung). The catching force does not fight — they bind and extract. If the party engages a secondary site, they face the raid team. Defeating the raid team at one site saves 20–30 potential captives. The catching force scatters if the raid team falls.

> [!mechanic]
> **Purple-Caste Raid Team (per secondary site):** 4 Grung Elite Warriors. AC 13, HP 49 each. Multiattack (2 strikes): +4, 1d6+2 + 2d4 poison. DC 12 Con or poisoned. Standing Leap 25 ft. Hand crossbow backup. **Tactics:** hold the breach perimeter while the catching force works. Two fighters engage the party; two guard the breach entrance. They break and retreat underground if reduced to one fighter.

## Simone's Hand — The Purple Handlers

During the raid, the purple-caste handlers who coordinated the build-up are visible. Their disguises have been dropped — no more fake moustaches and festival hats. Purple skin, warrior posture, barking orders at catching-force teams in a Grung dialect.

> [!dm]
> If Jean-Claude is present for any secondary strike, he sees purple-caste deference hierarchy in action — the way a catching-force leader defers to the raid-team commander, the way the commander defers to a handler who is not fighting but watching from a doorway. Jean-Claude has seen this chain of command before. It is Simone's operational structure. The handler in the doorway is the moment JC knows his sister built this. Do not narrate the realization. Let his player arrive there.

If the party captures or kills a purple-caste handler during the raid, they find:
- A wax message cylinder bearing the same Botukuri shipping mark as the Day 4 handler
- A rough sketch of Calveno's sewer system with five X marks — one per strike site
- A fake moustache, poorly made, horsehair and spirit gum

## The Aftermath

The raid runs for approximately ten minutes, surface to extraction. Then the Grung are gone — underground, through the sewer network, to the egress vents, to the *vethka*. What remains is dust, debris, absence, and the sound of people calling names into crowds that have already moved on.

### The Silence After

> [!read-aloud]
> The dust settles. The Mercatura plaza has a hole in it twenty feet across. Festival cloth hangs in the rubble like flags of surrender. Dropped tool rolls. Masks in trampled water. A woman is calling a man's name, over and over, into a crowd that is not listening because the crowd is doing the same thing.
>
> The watch arrives. They are too late. They know it. The sergeant stands at the edge of the crater and looks down into the dark and does not speak.

> [!dm]
> Give the aftermath room to breathe. The party has been through combat or rescue or both. The city is in shock. This is not a failure-state for the party — even a partial raid changes the world. What matters now is what they do next.

## Nona's Emergence

[[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] emerges from her safehouses within the hour. Both bodyguards at her sides — [[enzo|Enzo]] in his tailored suit, jaw set, claws out; [[ruk|Ruk]] carrying a cauldron of stew on one shoulder and a cleaver on the other.

Nona does not come to fight. She comes to feed.

> [!read-aloud]
> A very small, very old Rattkin woman walks into the dust of the Mercatura with a ladle in one hand and a loaf of bread in the other. She is flanked by a jaguar in a suit and a lizardfolk carrying a pot large enough to bathe in. She does not look at the crater. She looks at the people sitting on the ground beside it.
>
> She starts handing out bread. She does not ask names. She does not ask what happened. She sits down next to a woman who is still calling her husband's name and puts a piece of bread in her hand and holds it there until the woman takes a bite.

Nona works the crowd for an hour. She talks to the victims. She listens. She learns.

What she hears: names. Missing names. Husbands, sons, fathers, dock workers, boatmen — gone. Taken underground. Families standing where someone stood ten minutes ago and finding nothing but dropped tools and trampled festival cloth.

And she recognizes the pain. She has heard it before. Countless Rattkin have simply disappeared and their loved ones never learned why. The Crown took them, or the sea took them, or the world took them, and nobody came to explain and nobody came to bring them back. The grief in the Mercatura is the grief of the Warren. Nona has carried it for forty years.

### The Speech

> [!dm]
> Nona does not climb on a box. She does not raise her voice. She stands in the middle of a crowd of people who have just had their world broken and she speaks the way she always speaks — flat, clear, specific, the voice of a woman who has been at war for longer than most of these people have been alive and has never once pretended it was easy.

> [!read-aloud]
> "I know what this is." She says it to no one in particular and everyone at once. "I know what it is to look for someone who was here a minute ago and find nothing. I have done it more times than I can count. My people have been disappearing for decades — taken, sold, worked until they break, and the ones left behind never get told where or why or whether there's a body to mourn."
>
> She pauses. Looks at the faces. Continues.
>
> "Today it happened to you. And you are going to feel a thing I have felt my whole life — that the world took someone you love and nobody who was supposed to stop it even tried. I am not going to stand here and tell you it gets easier. It does not."
>
> The bread is gone. She sets down the ladle.
>
> "The Passage stands with the citizens of Calveno. I am too old to swing a sword, and my knees are not what they were. But I know how to find people who have been taken. I have been doing it since before this city built its first canal. I will assemble a crew. We will make chase. And we will bring your brothers and your sons and your fathers back. I am making you that promise in front of your neighbours and in front of my God and in front of the two extremely large gentlemen behind me who will remind me of it if I try to go to bed instead."

> [!dm]
> Enzo does not smile. Ruk nods once.
>
> Nona's vow is real. She will assemble a pursuit force from Warren volunteers, Passage contacts, and anyone in Calveno willing to crew a fast ship. The pursuit is the next beat of the raid situation — whether the party joins it, leads it, or watches it leave without them.
>
> **What this changes:** Nona has publicly declared the Passage's allegiance to Calveno's citizens. This is unprecedented. The Passage has operated in the shadows for generations — Rattkin help Rattkin, and the Dry take care of themselves. Nona just broke that wall. She did it because the grief is the same grief, and because she is old enough to be done pretending otherwise.
>
> **Mechanical consequence:** the party now has access to a Passage-backed pursuit operation. Nona can provide: a fast ship (not the *Uncertainty*), Warren volunteers as crew, Passage intelligence on Grung water routes, and a Tangle contact at the nearest outer-island port who can resupply without questions.

## Pursuit Options

The party has several directions after the raid:

| Option | What it involves |
|---|---|
| **Join Nona's pursuit** | Fast ship, Warren crew, Passage intel. Chase the *vethka* through the Calven Roads toward the Midchain. The captives are aboard; the clock is hours, not days. |
| **Lead the pursuit themselves** | Take the *Uncertainty* (if repairs are done) and crew it with their own people plus Warren volunteers. More control, less Passage infrastructure. |
| **Investigate the aftermath** | The crater, the sewer network, the handler remains. Evidence of the Dravosi patrol gap, the blackpowder supply chain, the summoning circle. Leads to the collaboration question. |
| **Rally the city** | Coordinate with the watch, the Canal Commission, the Tessarine factor. Slower, broader, more political. |
| **Follow the Simone thread** | JC now has confirmation that Simone built this. The handler evidence points east, toward the Verdant Teeth, toward Sorn. This is a long thread, not an immediate pursuit. |

> [!dm]
> Do not force a choice. Present the options through NPCs who want different things: Nona wants pursuit, the watch wants order, Iacopo Fieschi wants a report. The party picks what matters to them. Any choice is valid. The world moves on every thread they don't pick.

## Raid Scale — Based on Party Actions

Assess using the [[session-04-day-4#Raid Prep Status Check|Day 4 status check]].

| Outcome | Captives taken | Mercatura | Slaad | Narrative weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fully intact** | 200–300 | Collapsed | Summoned, fought, killed or escaped | Watershed. The Shattered Sea dates from this. |
| **Partially disrupted** (1–2 lanes closed) | 100–200 | Collapsed (unless primary found) | Summoned | Still unprecedented. Smaller scale, same proof of concept. |
| **Heavily disrupted** (3+ lanes closed, primary found) | 30–60 | Intact or partially damaged | Not summoned | The attempt is the story. Calveno knows what almost happened. |
| **Broken early** (primary destroyed, handler network compromised) | 0–20 | Intact | Not summoned | The Grung came, were stopped, and the Dravosi gap is a public question. |

## Day Close

There is no "what moves overnight" for Day 5. The raid is the event. What happens next is pursuit, aftermath, or departure — and that is the next session.

### Possible Cliffhangers

- **Nona's vow is the last beat:** end on "we will bring your brothers and your sons and your fathers back." The pursuit is Session 05.
- **The *vethka* are visible:** end on the party seeing the dark-hulled ships clearing the Calven Roads with their human cargo. The gap in the Dravosi patrol line is empty water. Nobody is coming.
- **The Slaad falls and the silence lands:** end on the aftermath read-aloud. The dust, the names, the absence.
- **JC recognizes Simone's hand:** end on the purple-caste deference chain and the Botukuri mark. He knows. He has not said it yet.
