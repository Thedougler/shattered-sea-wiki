# Session 04 · Highlight 1 — "Little secret from Nona"

> [!info] Setting
> **Where:** Underwater in the flooded sewer-canals beneath [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], [[calveno|Calveno]] — green light filtering down from the surface, silt curling, the chase finally cornered.
> **Who:** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] (a three-foot electric-blue grung in a red beret, talons locked on his prisoner), [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] (fourteen-year-old crow aarakocra, blue-black feathers, immune to the grung's poison thanks to a gift from [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]), [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] (a small barefoot rattkin), and the captured [[grung-elite-warrior|grung]] (poison-skinned frogfolk, baffled that his venom isn't working).
> **Beat:** Crissdalynn delivers the line of the night to the helpless grung — and turns for a reaction from teammates who are all underwater and heard nothing.

## Screenplay

**EXT. FLOODED SEWER-CANAL — BENEATH LE PALUDI — CONTINUOUS**

*The chase ends in green water. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] plants his prisoner against the silt, talons clamped to control him, and starts binding him fast — the others swim in to surround them. The gig is up.*

**GRUNG** *(thrashing, bubbles streaming)*
Why is my poison not working on you? How can you touch me?

**[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]** *(leaning in, conspiratorial)*
Little secret from [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]].

*She turns, eyebrows up, for the perfect reaction — you see?*

> 😂 **— big table laugh —**

*Nobody's there. The others are deep in the murk, oblivious; there is no one to land the joke on. She scans the empty water, then finishes the thought to herself.*

*Off to the side, [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] — submerged, also unable to hear a word — offers a stream of approving bubbles into the dark.*
