# Session 04 · Highlight 1 — "Little secret from Nona"

> [!info] Setting
> **Where:** At the water's edge of a flooded sewer-canal in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], the oldest district of [[calveno|Calveno]] — pale-stone tunnel mouth behind, open canal water below. Crissdalynn has just flown the captured grung up out of the tunnel (~20 ft) and set him down by the surface, still clamped in her talons; the others are in the water around them.
> **Who:** [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] (fourteen-year-old crow aarakocra monk, blue-black iridescent feathers, geometric leatherwork armor, talons hooked into her prisoner — immune to the grung's poison thanks to a gift from [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]); the captured [[grung-elite-warrior|grung]] (purple-caste warrior, larger than Jean-Claude, wearing a hat and a fake Hulk-Hogan handlebar mustache, baffled his venom isn't working); [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] (three-foot electric-blue grung in a red beret and false black moustache, swimming over to tie the prisoner up); [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] (small barefoot fancy-rat rattkin bard, submerged nearby).
> **Beat:** The purple grung demands to know why his poison can't touch her — Crissdalynn answers with a single deadpan line. The art should catch her perched over the pinned, mustached frog, talons in him, delivering the secret.

## Screenplay

**EXT. FLOODED SEWER-CANAL — EDGE OF LE PALUDI — CONTINUOUS**

*[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] sets the grung down at the water's edge — she has just hauled him a good twenty feet up out of the tunnel and flown him back to the others. He stays grappled: her [[crissdalynn-khinriss|talons]] are hooked into him, holding, controlling. He's the [[grung-elite-warrior|purple one]] — bigger than [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], a fake handlebar mustache over his frog mouth. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] swims over and starts binding him fast.*

**JEAN-CLAUDE**
The gig's up.

*The grung thrashes against the talons, snarling up at the bird who shrugged off his spear.*

**GRUNG** *(furious, baffled)*
Why is my poison not working on you? How can you touch me?

**[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]** *(leaning in, conspiratorial)*
Little secret from [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]].

*A beat. She tips her head, eyebrows up.*

**CRISSDALYNN**
You see?

> 😂 **— big table laugh —**

*She glances around for someone to land the punchline on — and there's no one at her level to catch it. She finishes the thought to herself.*

**CRISSDALYNN** *(quieter)*
Well, I didn't see you, so I had to finish the thought. I look around and there's no parent.

*Below the surface, [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] — submerged, out of earshot — sends up a stream of approving bubbles and muffled sounds from underwater.*
