# Session 04 · Highlight 2 — "Pinned to his seat, Jean-Claude takes a drag"

> [!info] Setting
> **Where:** On the **rooftop of [[ponte-bassa|the Ponte Bassa]]** in [[calveno|Calveno]] — the canal-bridge tavern's flat roof above the pale-stone crossing, water below. This is a friendly spar, not a real fight ("It's just sparring. He's not going to kill us."), with fighters perched and being knocked toward the roof's edge.
> **Who:** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] (a three-foot electric-blue grung, red beret, false black moustache, worn ranger harness, a near-full hand-rolled cigarette at his lip), sitting this round out as an observer; and the opponent [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] — a tall, lean barn-owl aarakocra monk, pale facial disc, tawny-to-ash plumage, plain sentinel robes, with wings he uses as weapons and a manic, having-fun grin. He throws four strikes a round; each conjured dagger lands a hit plus a burst of *force* damage and then dissolves into smoke. Also in the spar: [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]], [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], and [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] (Kyzil's apprentice).
> **Beat:** Out of his four attacks, Kyzil spends the last not to wound the non-combatant Jean-Claude but to pin him to his seat by the shirt — and Jean-Claude answers by coolly killing his nearly-full cigarette in one drag and refusing to flinch.

## Screenplay

**EXT. PONTE BASSA ROOFTOP — CALVENO — DAY**

*The spar has run three rounds. [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] has one attack left. His arm snaps forward — one more dagger — but it isn't aimed at [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], who is sitting this one out.*

**MASTER KYZIL** *(DM, narrating the strike)*
This one's aimed at Jean-Claude — and he's not aiming at Jean-Claude. He's aiming at your clothing. He puts a dagger right through your shirt and pins it to where you're sitting.

> 😂 **— table laugh —**

*Pinned to his own seat by the shirt, Jean-Claude does not react.*

**JEAN-CLAUDE**
I reach up and I pull the dagger out.

**MASTER KYZIL** *(DM)*
It disappears in your hand. Dissolves into smoke.

**JEAN-CLAUDE**
And — Delmar, you're up. I take a long, deep drag on my cigarette. Like, it's almost full, and it goes —

*He kills the whole thing in one rip.*

**JEAN-CLAUDE** *(cont'd)*
And then I let it get away. And I just keep staring at him.

> 😂 **— big table laugh —**

*Across the gap, [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] doesn't break either. The grin stays manic. He's having fun with it.*
