# Session 04 · Highlight 2 — "The cigarette assassin"

> [!info] Setting
> **Where:** A back-channel corner of [[calveno|Calveno]] — pale stone close on both sides, turquoise water at the edge of the fight, a knot of onlookers keeping their distance. *(Exact venue not pinned in the wiki; staged from the city's canal-street character.)*
> **Who:** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] (a three-foot electric-blue grung, red beret, false black moustache, worn ranger harness, hand-rolled cigarette at his lip), and the knife-fighter facing him — a lean assassin whose thrown daggers dissolve into smoke, wearing a manic grin. *(No wiki page for the assassin — left as plain text.)*
> **Beat:** The assassin pins Jean-Claude to his seat by the shirt; Jean-Claude answers by coolly killing his nearly-full cigarette in a single drag and refusing to flinch.

## Screenplay

**EXT. CALVENO BACK-CHANNEL — DAY**

*The assassin's arm snaps forward — one last dagger. It isn't aimed at [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. It's aimed at his shirt. The blade punches clean through the fabric and buries itself in the seat beneath him, pinning him where he sits.*

> 😂 **— table laugh —**

*The assassin's turn ends. Unhurried, Jean-Claude reaches up and plucks the dagger free — it dissolves into smoke in his fingers. He raises his cigarette, almost full, and takes a long, slow, deliberate drag.*

> 😂 **— big table laugh —**

*He kills the whole thing in one rip. Then he lets the assassin slip away — and simply keeps staring him down.*

*Across the gap, the assassin doesn't break. The grin stays manic. He's having fun with this.*
