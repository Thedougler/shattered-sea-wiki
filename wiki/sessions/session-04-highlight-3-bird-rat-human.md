# Session 04 · Highlight 3 — "A bird, a rat, and a sexy human"

> [!info] Setting
> **Where:** Downtown [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], [[calveno|Calveno]] — a crowded canal-side market, pale stone rising from turquoise water, a thousand conversations going at once across the channels.
> **Who:** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] (a three-foot electric-blue grung in a red beret and false moustache — and constitutionally unable to lie or soften anything), [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] (41, sandy-red topknot, greying beard, a smile that reaches his eyes — and five borrowed captains' voices crowding his skull).
> **Beat:** Jean-Claude announces exactly what he's looking for to the entire market — the party itself: the bird, the rat, the human — and Delmar breaks into a grin out of nowhere. *(The "sexy human" is Delmar; the bird and rat are [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]].)*

## Screenplay

**EXT. LE PALUDI MARKET — CALVENO — DAY**

*It doesn't take long to find them; the party never left downtown. The crowd churns. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] cups his hands and bellows over the din — because asking quietly would never occur to him.*

**JEAN-CLAUDE** *(to the whole market)*
Hello. I am looking for a bird, a rat, and a sexy human.

*He glances at [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] — who carries five drowned captains arguing in his head at all hours.*

*And Delmar, for no reason anyone watching could name, breaks into a slow, helpless grin.*

> 😂 **— big table laugh —**

*The scene folds back together: across the market, the others catch sight of Jean-Claude in the distance, walking up to rejoin them.*
