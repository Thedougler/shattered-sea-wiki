# Session 04 · Highlight 4 — "Black powder, penis, penis, penis"

> [!info] Setting
> **Where:** A damp tunnel under [[calveno|Calveno]], close to the grung-infested sewers the party has just scouted.
> **Who:** [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] (a three-foot barefoot rattkin bard, bodhran drum at his hip, clutching the [[sending-stone-nona|sending stone]]), with [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] on the paired stone — his silver-furred grandmother, who runs her kitchen like a courtroom and discusses arson in the tone of weather.
> **Beat:** Perrin crams a panicked magical warning into the spell's 25-word limit, packs it out with absurd filler, and Nona answers at such length that the spell cuts her off — forcing a second casting.

## Screenplay

**INT. SEWER TUNNEL — BENEATH CALVENO — NIGHT**

*[[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] grips the [[sending-stone-nona|stone]] and concentrates. The party has found something bad growing down in the sewers, and the message has to fit in twenty-five words — so he fills the slack with whatever lands.*

**PERRIN** *(into the stone, urgent)*
…they're preparing a big operation. We're gonna need backup to take this down — black powder — penis, penis, penis — lots of black powder.

*A beat. The [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s voice comes back through the stone — and keeps coming.*

**NONA** *(through the stone)*
Okay — I will send [[enzo|Enzo]], and I will send more to you. Are you safe right now? Please make sure you are somewhere secure. If they are actually growing down the sewer, this is unprecedented — I do not know what to expect—

*The spell snaps shut mid-sentence; she's already well past its limit and still going. Perrin has no choice but to burn a second slot just to hear the rest.*

> 😂 **— big table laugh —**
