# Session 04 · Highlight 4 — "Black powder, penis, penis, penis"

> [!info] Setting
> **Where:** The party's ship at [[calveno|Calveno]], the captured grung handler stowed in the brig after interrogation; the operation itself sits roughly a kilometer down in the [[calveno-sewers-grung-magazines|grung-infested sewers]].
> **Who:** [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] (three-foot barefoot fancy-rat rattkin bard, black head over a white body, long pink tail, olive hooded cloak, bodhran at his hip), clutching the [[sending-stone-nona|sending stone]]; on the paired stone, his grandmother [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]], who answers in the tone of weather and promises to send [[enzo|Enzo]], her black-jaguar tabaxi bodyguard with the ever-lit cigar.
> **Beat:** Perrin reaches Nona to beg for backup, crams the spell's 25-word limit full of absurd filler, and her reply runs so long that the Sending cuts off mid-sentence and she has to recast.

## Screenplay

**INT. SHIP — DECK / BRIG — NIGHT**

*The handler is locked in the brig below. On deck, [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] closes a small clawed hand around the [[sending-stone-nona|sending stone]] and reaches across the water for [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]. The warning has to fit in twenty-five words — so he packs the slack with whatever lands.*

**PERRIN** *(into the stone, urgent)*
There are grung below the city. And they are preparing a big operation. We're gonna need backup to take this down — mention the black powder — penis, penis, penis — lots of black powder.

*The stone goes warm. Nona's voice comes straight back — and keeps coming.*

**NONA** *(through the stone)*
Oh, it's real. No shit. Okay — I will send, I will send [[enzo|Enzo]], and I will send more to you. Are you safe right now? Please make sure that you are somewhere secure. If they're actually growing down the sewer, this is unprecedented. I do not know what to expect—

*The spell snaps shut mid-sentence — she's well past the limit and still talking.*

**PERRIN** *(beat)*
She's still going.

> 😂 **— big table laugh —**

*The connection dies. Nona burns a second slot to finish the thought, and the next words come in: "—make sure you're safe where you are. I will send Enzo and I will send a group… sounds like I should speak to you in person." Apparently, she has the unlimited plan.*
