# Session 04 · Highlight 6 — "I mean the roof, you perverts"

> [!info] Setting
> **Where:** Inside [[ponte-bassa|the Ponte Bassa]] — the canal-level tavern built into the bridge masonry at Calveno's main crossing, low ceiling on the water side, the rooftop reachable up the back stair.
> **Who:** [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] (fourteen-year-old crow aarakocra monk, eager to show off), [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] (barefoot rattkin bard, mouth running ahead of his brain), and [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] — her teacher, a tall barn-owl aarakocra with a pale facial disc and a perched stillness, who asks rather than tells and never raises his voice.
> **Beat:** Kyzil wants somewhere for his apprentice to train; Perrin proposes taking her "upstairs," hears his own innuendo, and Kyzil deadpans the whole table flat.

## Screenplay

**INT. THE PONTE BASSA — CALVENO — DAY**

*[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] turns to her master, fishing for approval.*

**CRISSDALYNN**
Did I tell you about the time I lifted a shark out of the water?

**[[master-kyzil|MASTER KYZIL]]** *(mild, a question not a command)*
I'm sure there is some place here where you could practice. I would hate for my apprentice to fall behind in her studies.

*She stands and spins her staff — see? Beside her, [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] casts about for a big enough space, and lands on the obvious one.*

**PERRIN**
The roof is perfect. Take her upstairs, eh? *(then, hearing himself)* …Ew.

**MASTER KYZIL** *(perfectly deadpan)*
I mean **the roof**, you perverts.

> 😂 **— big table laugh —**

*Kyzil holds the stare a beat longer than comfortable. Then, smoothing his robes: they may go upstairs.*
