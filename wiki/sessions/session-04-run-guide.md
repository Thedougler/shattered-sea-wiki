# Session 04 — Run Guide

> Scene menu, not a script. The party picks; you react. Nothing here breaks if they wander — ignored pressure *moves*, it doesn't vanish. Read *If They Surprise You* once before play and you will not get stuck.
>
> This is the open Calveno week. The deep content already lives in [[calveno-sandbox-run-guide|the Calveno Sandbox Run Guide]] and its island files — this guide is the **Session 04 operating layer**: what's live *this* session, inline, so you run cold without opening them.

**Clock-at-a-glance:** Beffa runs ~3 days of pranks; the **Grung raid fires on Day 4** of the festival. Whatever else happens, that countdown is the spine under this session. The party does not have to engage it — but it is moving every day they don't.

## Strong Start

*Interrupted Routine. Full party present — first real morning in the city. Ends on one open choice.*

> [!read-aloud]
> The coffee here is good — black, scalding, the first you haven't had to ration in two weeks. Below the window, Calveno is putting on its face: festival cloth going up over the Mercatura, a registration drum starting somewhere uphill, a skiff stacked with paper masks higher than the boy poling it. Then the door opens and a child comes in. Soaked to the knee, a temple cord at her wrist, eyes red from a night she did not sleep. She finds Delmar's face without being told which one he is. "The lady at the shrine says to tell you. She kept her promise. She hasn't slept. And the water in the font won't lie flat anymore."

Then stop. The day is theirs — the shrine, the festival, the ship, the Warren, money, worship, whatever pulls first. Do not narrate their answer. The child knows nothing else; she was paid a copper and a blessing to carry exactly that.

> [!dm]
> This addresses last session's open Umberlee thread directly: Delmar bought a day with a 19 Deception and the day is up. The "water won't lie flat" detail is the supernatural leaking — Umberlee pressure is real, not a recruiter's patience. It also re-grounds the whole table in Calveno without a recap. If the party shrugs and goes elsewhere first, that's fine: the servant keeps not sleeping, and the font detail spreads as a minor Mercatura rumour (a Waveservant omen) until Delmar comes — see *Threads & Clocks*.

### 1. The Shrine — Umberlee's Message
*Pull: Delmar (primary), Crissdalynn (was there) · Register: revelation / horror set-piece*

> This is a **fixed dramatic beat**, not a negotiation. The offer is unacceptable by design, the refusal is expected, and the point is the *demonstration* — Umberlee's raw power and the standing demand it leaves behind. Run it as scripted; let the silence between boxes do the work.

> [!read-aloud] — Arrival
> The [[waveservant-shrine\|Waveservant Shrine]] is a low, tide-stained vault off a Mercatura canal, salt crusting the doorframe. The font behind the bench holds a hand's depth of seawater that will not lie flat — it rises and falls, slow, like something breathing under it. [[branca\|Branca]] is exactly where she said: upright, awake after a night she did not sleep, salt-white hair pulled back hard, the little leather tide-table booklet open on her knee. "So," she says, bright and clipped, the warmth of someone rescheduling a missed meeting. "You came. Good. We have an appointment."

**NPC — Branca:** corporate cult executive assistant — bubbly, efficient, too much eye contact; opens "So!", closes "Does that work for you?", checks the booklet mid-sentence as if it settles every objection. She genuinely believes making divine horror *pleasant* is a kindness. She is the soft instrument; what comes through her is not.

**The possession.** At the appointed moment Branca stops mid-sentence. Her eyes roll back to white. The voice that comes out is not hers.

> [!read-aloud] — Umberlee speaks
> Branca's eyes roll white. When she speaks it is not her voice — it is the sea given a throat, cold and enormous and patient. "FISK. You carry what is Mine. Go down to the wreck. Bring Me the Pearl. Return it to My shrine — and I will grant you the one mercy you have earned: a swift death, and the souls you stole brought home to Me. This is My grace. Accept it."

**The demand (plainly, for you):** retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls\|Pearl of Souls]] from the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] wreck at the [[the-drowned-maw\|Drowned Maw]] and return it to a shrine of Umberlee. The "reward" is Delmar's *own death* and the surrender of the souls he carries. It is a death sentence dressed as salvation. Delmar will refuse — that is the design.

> [!secret]
> This brushes Delmar's secret. The party will hear that Delmar **carries souls Umberlee claims** and that there is a Pearl he must retrieve. They need not learn the [[pearl-of-souls\|five captains / Vel Orn theft]] story — keep Her words to "what is Mine" and "the souls you stole," and let the full shape stay Delmar's to disclose or conceal. Only Delmar's player knows he carries voices.

**The wrath.** The instant Delmar refuses (he will):

> [!read-aloud] — The wrath
> The refusal is barely out of your mouth before the light goes wrong. Thunder walks across a sky that was clear a breath ago; the canal shivers in its channel. The thing wearing Branca *roars* — and then, almost offhand, it takes her head in her own two hands and snaps her neck. The body folds. The shrine goes silent. Branca lies on the wet stone, not breathing.

Let it land. Hold the silence. Then —

> [!read-aloud] — The return
> Mist gathers off the canal — too fast, too deliberate — and curls in around the fallen woman like a tide coming home. Branca's chest heaves. She drags in one enormous, gasping breath, sits up, and blinks the white from her eyes. She finds the booklet, smooths her robe, and looks up with that same bright warmth, as if nothing happened at all. "I hope," she says, "you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace."

> [!skill-check] Insight (DC 12) — after the revival
> Success: Branca means it completely — she experienced her own murder as a privilege and is not performing. *That* is the horror. Failure: reads as shock or shaken devotion; the true depth of it doesn't register.

**If engaged:** Delmar has seen, undeniably, what Umberlee is and what she does to even her own faithful. The standing demand — retrieve the Pearl — is now live, with a vivid price attached. (The retrieval itself is the [[pearl-of-souls\|Maw Pearl Crisis]], a future-session payload; this session just delivers the summons and the demonstration.)
**If ignored:** Delmar never goes to the shrine. The water that reached for him at the dock reaches *again*, harder, the next time he touches deep seawater — and Branca simply turns up later with another appointment. Umberlee does not chase; She reschedules.
**Hook out:** the Pearl is inside the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] wreck — exactly what the **Red Lady salvage commission (Scene 4)** is racing for. Umberlee's demand and the salvage money pull toward the same wreck from opposite directions.

### 2. The Warren & the Ship's Face
*Pull: Perrin (family, the favor), Delmar (the silhouette) · Register: social*

> [!read-aloud]
> [[le-paludi\|Le Paludi]] runs on quiet water and long memory. The smell of [[nona-black-jaw\|Nona]]'s kitchen reaches the canal before her door does.

**What's true here:** Two linked problems live in the Warren. **(a)** The *Uncertainty* still reads as a Crown cutter to anyone who knows cutters — paint won't fix a silhouette. The yard that does off-books hull work, [[carpenters-slip\|Carpenters' Slip]], won't take a cold approach; she needs a Warren introduction, and that runs through Nona. **(b)** Nona's **favor** ([[nonas-favor\|the unconditional yes]]) and the **sending stone** are live in Perrin's pocket.

**NPC — Savia Brentino** ([[savia-brentino\|Carpenters' Slip]]): dry, unhurried, says the hard thing once and lets it sit. *Wants:* a Warren introduction before she'll deal — three ships tried cold this month and are on her list. *Reveals (flatly, at the end):* "She'll still read as a cutter to anyone who knows cutters. That's not a paint problem." **Terms:** 220 gp + 5 city days + one **unassigned Tangle favour**. The bow registry plate is the party's call.

**NPC — Nona Black-Jaw** ([[nona-black-jaw\|the matriarch]]): feeds you first, names what you owe second — as accounting, not threat. *Wants:* the Tangle's favour balance acknowledged (she now knows the [[vestra\|Vestra]] is gone — quietly grieving the men and the money while planning the response), and **the favor below**, which she'll name through the sending stone or at her table.

> [!secret]
> **Nona's favor (canon).** Word reached Nona that Perrin came into Calveno with a crew — and one of them is a face nobody here knows: a **blue Grung in a beret and moustache** ([[jean-claude-tabarnack\|Jean-Claude]]). She reads this as a stroke of luck. She's been getting reports she'd normally dismiss as impossible this far north — **Grung moving in the old Warren tunnels that intersect the Calveno sewers.** She wants them confirmed or dismissed as rumour. And who better to send hunting Grung than a Grung. *The ask:* send Jean-Claude (with whoever he wants) down into the old tunnels to confirm whether the reports are real.
>
> **This is the door into the raid.** Those sewer Grung are [[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|Simone's extraction crews]] mapping the egress route for the Day-4 strike. Nona's favor and the handler thread (Scene 3) are the *same investigation from two ends* — the street above (handler recon, JC's recognition beats) and the tunnels below (where Nona is pointing). The favor hands the party the Warren's blessing to go down.

> [!skill-check] Insight (DC 13) — on Savia
> Success: she isn't hostile, she's screening; an introduction flips her. Failure: she reads as a dead end and the silhouette problem stays unsolved.

**If engaged:** ship gets a real disguise path (220 gp + days + a Tangle debt); the silhouette problem becomes solvable with a plate/papers decision; Warren network opens. If the favor lands, the party gains the Warren's eyes on the raid.
**If ignored:** repairs proceed cosmetically — the cutter silhouette and the [[knighton-pursuit\|Crown clock]] stay live. Nona waits to see if Perrin uses the stone before she uses it on him.
**Hook out:** Warren leads — [[warren-marrows-crate\|Marrow's Crate]] and [[warren-ferrin-locke\|Ferrin Locke]] (the leak network), [[warren-bela-silt-paw\|Bela Silt-Paw]] (hard 2–3 day clock). All optional; none gate the ship job.

### 3. The Handlers — Raid Prep
*Pull: Jean-Claude (only he can read it) · Register: exploratory → combat. SPOTLIGHT.*

> [!read-aloud]
> A blue-caste Grung stands at the edge of a Velo square, watching a street performer. He is not enjoying it. He is watching how the crowd *moves* when the performer makes a scene — where people turn, where the gaps open, how long a knot of onlookers takes to dissolve.

**What's true here:** [[simone-tabarnack\|Simone]]'s handlers are already in Calveno, mapping a mass capture operation under festival cover ([[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|the Beffa Grung Raid]]). It fires **Day 4 of the Beffa** — five synchronized snatch points, Slaad released as distraction, sewer extraction to *vethka* waiting past the Dravosi patrol gap. **Jean-Claude is the only PC who can read handler work for what it is.** This is the session's slow-burn dread, not a quest with a giver.

> **Two ways in.** This thread reaches the party from above and below. *Above:* the handler recognition beats here, on the street, that only JC can read. *Below:* **Nona's favor (Scene 2)** points him straight at the Grung in the old tunnels under the city. If the party takes the favor, the handler thread stops being ambient and becomes a job with the Warren behind it.

**Jean-Claude recognition beats** (offer privately, one per day, never to the whole table):
- **Crowd positioning** — a Grung stands where they can see three exits without watching any. JC has stood there; it isn't instinct, it's training.
- **Target assessment** — a small figure in festival colours appraises a dockhand's build, tools, guild mark, then looks away. Not a glance. An appraisal. JC has made that look hundreds of times.
- **Exit logic** — a trade-cover Grung walks one Le Paludi route twice, at pace, marking the time. Someone ran that route and handed the clock to the handlers.

**If JC pulls the thread → Handler Cell (combat-ready):**

> [!read-aloud]
> Two figures in trade clothes at the same Bridge pillar JC clocked earlier — different position now, same posture. Not watching the performer. Watching the crowd move around the performer. The hour is wrong for the spot.

**Blue-Caste Handler (×2):** AC 13 · HP 16 · shortbow +4, 1d8+3 · **Grung poison** DC 11 Con save or poisoned 1 min · Standing Leap 20 ft as part of move · *Tactic:* try to break contact and withdraw first; fight only if cornered; **run at half HP** — they have a report to file.
→ full encounter + terrain: [[calveno-street-encounters\|Street Encounters → Handler Cell]]

> [!secret]
> One handler carries binding rope and a sealed wax cylinder — blank, but the wax bears a mark JC knows from Botukuri shipping labels. **Three outcomes, all real:**
> • **Capture alive** → the handler knows arrival timing and the extraction canal route; telling the Warren closes one of five lanes.
> • **Kill both** → one lane goes dark with no explanation; Simone doesn't learn the cell was made.
> • **Let them go** → Simone learns the party is in Calveno; JC's face is now a report.

> [!dm]
> This is the historic event the world will date from. Do **not** force it. If the party never engages, the raid lands Day 4 and they witness the aftermath (see *Possible Cliffhanger*). Each lane they break shrinks the harvest; breaking the prep early turns a cutscene into a fight they can win pieces of. The **Dravosi collaboration** (patrol gap + sewer maps) stays buried — it surfaces later, with leverage, not this session.

**If engaged:** the party can blunt the raid — one cell down = one strike point fails, not all five. Warren/Passage contacts can seal specific sewer hatches.
**If ignored:** prep completes invisibly; Day 4 becomes a watershed; resentment, anti-Grung backlash, and a Tessarine crisis follow.
**Hook out:** the sewer egress points and the *vethka* anchored in the Calven Roads — chase either to find the extraction plan ([[calveno-raid-signs\|Raid Prep Signs]]).

### 4. Red Lady — Salvage Money
*Pull: Delmar (his wreck), Crissdalynn (Red Lady traces, Kyzil) · Register: social / investigation*

> [!read-aloud]
> A chandler lowers his voice without being asked. "You're not the first to ask about Maw salvage this month. There's money moving. Serious money — three buyers, or one buyer with three hands, nobody's sure. And they're not advertising a target."

**What's true here:** Someone is assembling a salvage commission for a [[the-drowned-maw\|Maw]] wreck — and the money is unusual enough that [[savia-brentino\|Savia]] turned away three enquiries and the Tangle noticed. The accessible thread is broker **[[batta-zusto\|Batta Zusto]]** on the Bridge. The commission is **8–10 days from departure** — fast action gets the party there first.

> **The wreck is the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] — and it holds the [[pearl-of-souls\|Pearl]] Umberlee just ordered Delmar to retrieve (Scene 1).** If both scenes fire, the table should feel the vise: a goddess demanding the Pearl, a funded crew racing for the same wreck, and Delmar's own buried reasons for caring. Don't spell the convergence out — let the players assemble it.

**NPC — Batta Zusto:** late fifties, stocky, dressed a touch too formally; clean information, priced, no embellishment; not hostile, not protective of clients. *Wants:* payment for what he knows. *Sells (40 gp, or Maw nav intel worth ~80, or the confirmed wreck name):* the commission is a deep-access Maw cargo recovery; three supply chains (hired diving gear, a premium sealed chart package, crew in two ports); money came through a **Port Tidefall factor** he's worked with before; **someone *else* is also asking** Calveno brokers about Maw access this month — not the same buyer. *Won't say:* the funder's identity (he doesn't know it) or the Tidefall factor's name (not without proof the party can use it).

> [!skill-check] Investigation / Gather Info (DC 12; DC 8 if Delmar asks specifically about Maw salvage gear)
> Success (two of three details): hired diving apparatus, compressed-air bladders, a sealed chart package a courier wouldn't hand off. Failure: only the rumour of "money moving," no specifics.

**Crissdalynn / Kyzil sub-beat:** [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] is in the city and knows something the party doesn't — Crissdalynn's original ship went down to *"a foul wind from the south."* If she pulls on Red Lady traces with Kyzil present, he can place that phrase against the Maw rumours. *Reveal at most one layer* — Kyzil is grief held under discipline; his first instinct is to protect her, not to dump lore.

**If engaged:** the party learns the commission's shape and can race it, intercept its chart, or let Delmar surface as a Maw expert and field the offer (the offer is not obviously a trap). The parallel second buyer stays a loaded gun.
**If ignored:** the commission assembles and departs ahead of them. The wreck is not empty when they reach it.
**Hook out:** the sealed chart package (the commission's nav edge) and the Port Tidefall factor (closest traceable link to the funder — but not findable in Calveno, not fast).

### 5. Il Gioco delle Beffe *(optional — the most fun; background if skipped)*
*Pull: all · Register: social / comic toy*

> [!read-aloud]
> The Mercatura bridge has become a registration desk. A clerk with a ledger and a long-suffering face is taking team names, marks, categories, and entry stakes, while three would-be tricksters argue about whether a bucket counts as a "device."

**What's true here:** the festival rewards public deception and clean recovery; [[giacomo-moretti\|Giacomo Moretti]] has an unbroken 20-year record the committee finds embarrassing. The party can register a team (name, mark, category, scale, symbol, stake), bet, watch, recruit contacts, or just use the five-day window as cover. **The festival is also the raid's camouflage** — every prank that trains the crowd to laugh at a staged scare makes Day 4 easier to hold open.

**The toy:** if they register, get the table choosing — team name, mark (who they prank), category, a symbol, the entry stake. Let it be loud and dumb and theirs.

**If engaged:** civic reputation, festival cover, contacts; a great low-register breather between heavier scenes.
**If ignored:** the Beffa runs without them — cloth in the streets, nervous marks, daily results at sundown. Nothing closes; it's ambient texture and a five-day clock.
**Hook out:** any Beffa beat can carry one **raid-prep sign** as texture (see *Secrets & Clues*) — a registered team that never came back to verify, a "crowd-compression timing" prank concept, a false alarm that becomes funny.

## Secrets & Clues

Keep these **unassigned** — give each to the first action that earns it, never bound to one roll or one scene.

- A Calveno repaint won't erase a Crown cutter's silhouette — surfaces via Savia, a dock regular, or a failed papers check.
- The font at the Waveservant Shrine won't go still — surfaces to anyone who enters, becomes a public omen if Delmar stays away.
- Unusual broker money is building a Maw salvage job aimed at the *Red Lady* — surfaces via a chandler, Savia (names "Zusto" once), or canal gossip.
- A Crown-adjacent commercial observer has missed his morning run two days straight; his firm is closed for the festival and *didn't* call him in — surfaces via Cobb or a harbour regular. (Raid + Crown.)
- A Beffa team registered under an unrecognized name asked the clerk how many people you can move through the bridge pinch-point in a minute — surfaces if anyone asks the registration clerk about odd teams.

> [!secret]
> Raid-adjacent — use only if players look in the right direction (these are raid threads, not free intel):
> • The Mercatura sewer collector has three access hatches; **two were re-seated this week**, no Commission record (festival inspection cycle skips that section).
> • The Dravosi patrol schedule for the Calven Roads has a **three-day minimum gap starting Day 4** of the Beffa. Not secret — any harbour factor with contacts can quote the dates. The question is who *else* already has them.
> • Three low-profile, dark-resined, double-outrigged hulls sit at anchor past the patrol line, flying no pennant — anyone with Grung/Midchain maritime knowledge IDs them as *vethka*.

## If They Surprise You

1. **Place the request.** Public deal → Mercatura or the Bridge. Quiet practical work → Le Paludi. Vice, music, strange trade → Velo. Official ship business → Arsenal edge. ([[calveno-districts\|district map]]; [[calveno-improv\|names & voices]].)
2. **Give one competent local** who wants one small thing now — a sale, a witness, a referral, a favour, not to be dragged into trouble.
3. **Attach one live pressure** from the Threads table so the new direction still feeds the session (ship exposure, Warren memory, the raid clock, Red Lady money, Umberlee).
4. **Let the choice stand.** Note what changed, tick the relevant clock, move on. Never have a second NPC repeat the same hook until they comply — charge a Calveno cost and make the new direction real.

## Possible Cliffhanger

Don't plan an ending — recognize the highest-tension stopping point if it arrives:

- **If the party broke a handler cell:** end on what the captured/dead handler reveals — *the raid is four days out and they've only found one of five lanes.*
- **If they engaged Umberlee:** end on the breath — Branca gasping back to life on the wet stone, the booklet open again, "I hope you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace." The Pearl summons now hangs over every Maw decision the party makes.
- **If they ignored the raid prep:** end on the first wrong sound on Day 4 — a scream the crowd laughs at, then doesn't. **Open with absence after motion:** a dropped tool roll, a boat tied wrong, a name shouted into a crowd that has already moved on.
