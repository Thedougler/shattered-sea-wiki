# Session 04 — Run Guide

> The whole city is the sandbox; the party drives. The situations below are what's live in it. Each has a **hook** — a thing you can throw at a specific PC to pull them in, or leave for the players to find on their own. Ignored pressure *moves*, it doesn't vanish. When they go somewhere you didn't prep, see *If They Surprise You*.

## Session Snapshot

| | |
|---|---|
| **Party is** | Calveno, **Day 1 evening** — Kyzil has just landed on Crissdalynn. *Uncertainty* in [[la-vasca\|La Vasca]] dry dock (~5-day repair). Festival ([[il-gioco-delle-beffe\|Il Gioco delle Beffe]]) on; registration opens tomorrow. |
| **Last break** | S03 ended as [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] dropped out of the sky onto Crissdalynn. Earlier that day: Perrin met Nona; a Waveservant ([[branca\|Branca]]) found Delmar and he put her off; JC delivered the egg. |
| **Hottest pressure** | Branca is sitting in the shrine and **will not leave** until Delmar comes. Tomorrow the city opens — Beffa registration, the Warren, the festival. Grung raid prep is running; it fires on the festival's **Day 4**. |
| **Table time** | ~4–5 hrs. Plan 3 situations + the sewer/handler thread (runs long if it turns to combat) + the Beffa as ambient toy. |

**The spine under everything:** the Beffa runs ~3 days of pranks; the [[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|Grung raid]] lands on Day 4. The party need not engage it — but it moves every day they don't.

## The City Frame

| | |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | **The city that plays liar.** The Beffa is masks and staged scares — deception as civic entertainment, a prank around every canal corner. |
| **Why it matters** | The festival trains Calveno to *wait before believing a scene is real*. That hesitation is the gap Simone's raid is built on. |
| **City goal** | Get the *Uncertainty* off the Crown's books and out clean — re-silhouetted, papered, repaired — before [[knighton-pursuit\|Knighton's]] reach closes. Everything else is a step toward that, a complication of it, or a reason a PC can't leave yet. |

## Strong Start

*Pick up in the street the instant Kyzil lands — Day 1, late afternoon. This plays out the reunion S03 ended on. Give Crissdalynn the floor.*

> [!read-aloud]
> The wind goes quiet. The sun is high and behind you — and then something with the sun behind it drops out of the sky in dead silence and hits Crissdalynn square in the chest, two arms around her before the wind is even back. The smell lands before the sight does: feathers, incense, home. "Oh my God, Priscilla. You're alive." [[master-kyzil\|Master Kyzil]] holds her at arm's length, then looks at the rest of you, remembering himself. "Hi. I'm — Master Kyzil. What happened?"

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-04-01-kyzil-reunion.png|Master Kyzil embraces Crissdalynn in a Le Paludi canal street as the party watches]]

Let the reunion breathe. Then the evening presses in: the ship in its cradle, a city dressing for festival, and — for Delmar — a woman in a shrine across the district who has not moved since she found him an hour ago.

> [!dm]
> This is Day 1 evening; the open city day is tomorrow (registration at dawn). If the party wants to bed down, fine — but Branca does not wait *politely*, she waits *absolutely* (see below). Handle her before Delmar rests easy.

### 1. The Shrine — Umberlee's Pearl Summons
*Delmar (primary), Crissdalynn (was there) · revelation / horror set-piece*

**Branca will not leave.** She found Delmar an hour ago, he put her off, she walked to the shrine and sat down. She has not moved since and she will not — overnight, all of tomorrow, however long it takes. She is that much of a zealot, and waiting costs her nothing. There is no skipping this scene; there is only *when* Delmar walks into it.

> [!dm]
> If Delmar tries to long rest without going, give it about ten seconds, then: he can't quite settle — and when he looks toward the Mercatura he *knows*, the way you know a held breath, that she's still in there in the dark, awake, hands folded, waiting. She'll outlast him. The scene happens when he stops putting it off, not if.

This is a **fixed dramatic beat**, not a negotiation. The offer is unacceptable by design; the refusal is expected; the point is the demonstration. Run it as scripted and let the silence between boxes work.

> [!read-aloud] — Arrival
> The [[waveservant-shrine\|Waveservant Shrine]] is a low, tide-stained vault off a Mercatura canal. The font behind the bench holds a hand's depth of seawater that will not lie flat — it rises and falls, slow, like something breathing under it. [[branca\|Branca]] is exactly where she sat down: upright, awake, salt-white hair pulled back hard, the little leather tide-table booklet open on her knee. "So," she says, bright and clipped, the warmth of someone rescheduling a missed meeting. "You came. Good. We have an appointment."

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-04-02-branca-pearl-summons.png|Branca at the Waveservant Shrine, eyes white, as the seawater font rises behind her]]

**NPC — Branca:** corporate cult executive assistant — bubbly, efficient, too much eye contact; opens "So!", closes "Does that work for you?", checks the booklet mid-sentence as if it settles every objection. She believes making divine horror *pleasant* is a kindness. She is the soft instrument; what comes through her is not.

**The possession.** Branca stops mid-sentence. Her eyes roll back to white. The voice is not hers.

> [!read-aloud] — Umberlee speaks
> Branca's eyes roll white. When she speaks it is not her voice — it is the sea given a throat, cold and enormous and patient. "FISK. You carry what is Mine. Go down to the wreck. Bring Me the Pearl. Return it to My shrine — and I will grant you the one mercy you have earned: a swift death, and the souls you stole brought home to Me. This is My grace. Accept it."

**The demand (plainly):** retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls\|Pearl of Souls]] from the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] wreck at the [[the-drowned-maw\|Drowned Maw]] and return it to a shrine of Umberlee. The "reward" is Delmar's *own death* and the surrender of the souls he carries. A death sentence dressed as salvation. He will refuse — by design.

> [!secret]
> This brushes Delmar's secret. The party hears that he **carries souls Umberlee claims** and that there's a Pearl to retrieve. They need not learn the [[pearl-of-souls\|five captains / Vel Orn]] story — keep Her words to "what is Mine" and "the souls you stole." Only Delmar's player knows he carries voices.

**The wrath.** The instant Delmar refuses:

> [!read-aloud] — The wrath
> The refusal is barely out of your mouth before the light goes wrong. Thunder walks across a sky that was clear a breath ago; the canal shivers in its channel. The thing wearing Branca *roars* — and then, almost offhand, it takes her head in her own two hands and snaps her neck. The body folds. The shrine goes silent. Branca lies on the wet stone, not breathing.

Hold the silence. Then —

> [!read-aloud] — The return
> Mist gathers off the canal — too fast, too deliberate — and curls in around the fallen woman like a tide coming home. Branca's chest heaves. She drags in one enormous, gasping breath, sits up, blinks the white from her eyes, finds the booklet, smooths her robe, and looks up with that same bright warmth, as if nothing happened. "I hope," she says, "you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace."

> [!skill-check] Insight (DC 12) — after the revival
> Success: Branca means it completely — she experienced her own murder as a privilege and is not performing. *That* is the horror. Failure: reads as shock; the depth doesn't register.

**The bind:** the only way to satisfy the goddess is to die for her, so Delmar has to find a third answer — and the Pearl she wants is in the wreck a funded crew is already racing for (§4).
**Hook out:** the Pearl is inside the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] — exactly what the salvage commission wants. Demand and money pull at the same wreck from opposite directions.

### 2. The Warren & the Ship's Face
*Perrin (family, the favor), Delmar (the silhouette) · social / logistics*

> [!read-aloud]
> [[le-paludi\|Le Paludi]] runs on quiet water and long memory. The smell of [[nona-black-jaw\|Nona]]'s kitchen reaches the canal before her door does.

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-04-03-warren-ship-face.png|Savia Brentino points out that the Uncertainty still reads as a Crown cutter in the dry dock]]

**What's true here:** two linked problems live in the Warren. **(a)** The *Uncertainty* still reads as a Crown cutter to anyone who knows cutters — paint won't fix a silhouette. The off-books yard, [[carpenters-slip\|Carpenters' Slip]], won't take a cold approach; she needs a Warren introduction, which runs through Nona. **(b)** Nona's **favor** ([[nonas-favor\|the unconditional yes]]) and the **sending stone** are live in Perrin's pocket.

**NPC — Savia Brentino** ([[savia-brentino\|Carpenters' Slip]]): dry, unhurried, says the hard thing once and lets it sit. *Wants:* a Warren introduction before she'll deal — three ships tried cold this month and are on her list. *Reveals (flatly, at the end):* "She'll still read as a cutter to anyone who knows cutters. That's not a paint problem." **Terms:** 220 gp + 5 city days + one **unassigned Tangle favour**. The bow registry plate is the party's call.

**NPC — Nona Black-Jaw** ([[nona-black-jaw\|the matriarch]]): feeds you first, names what you owe second — as accounting, not threat. She knows the [[vestra\|Vestra]] is gone (quietly grieving the men and the money while planning the response).

> [!secret]
> **Nona's favor (canon).** Word reached Nona that Perrin came in with a crew including a face nobody here knows: a **blue Grung in a beret and moustache** ([[jean-claude-tabarnack\|Jean-Claude]]). She reads it as luck. She's had reports she'd normally dismiss as impossible this far north — **Grung moving in the old Warren tunnels that intersect the Calveno sewers** — and wants them confirmed or dismissed. Who better to hunt Grung than a Grung. *The ask:* send JC (with whoever he wants) into the tunnels to confirm. **This is the door into the raid** — those sewer Grung are [[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|Simone's extraction crews]]; this and §3 are the same investigation from two ends.

> [!skill-check] Insight (DC 13) — on Savia
> Success: she isn't hostile, she's screening; an introduction flips her. Failure: she reads as a dead end, the silhouette stays unsolved.

**Hook:** [[cobb\|Cobb]] or a young Black-Jaw runner finds Perrin — Nona knows he's in the city and "wants to feed him properly," and "there's a small thing she's hoping he can help with."
**If ignored:** Nona doesn't chase — she lets the silence sit, then uses the **sending stone** on her own timing. The silhouette stays live and the [[knighton-pursuit\|Crown clock]] keeps ticking; every unpapered day is a day the ship is easier to recognize when they sail.
**Hook out:** Warren leads — [[warren-marrows-crate\|Marrow's Crate]], [[warren-ferrin-locke\|Ferrin Locke]], [[warren-bela-silt-paw\|Bela Silt-Paw]] (hard 2–3 day clock). Optional; none gate the ship job.

### 3. The Sewer Grung — Raid Prep
*Jean-Claude (only he can read it) · exploratory → combat. SPOTLIGHT.*

> [!read-aloud]
> A blue-caste Grung stands at the edge of a Velo square, watching a street performer. He is not enjoying it. He is watching how the crowd *moves* when the performer makes a scene — where people turn, where the gaps open, how long a knot of onlookers takes to dissolve.

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-04-04-velo-handler.png|Jean-Claude recognizes a blue-caste handler watching crowd movement in a Velo festival square]]

**What's true here:** [[simone-tabarnack\|Simone]]'s handlers are already in Calveno, mapping a mass capture operation under festival cover ([[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|the Beffa Grung Raid]]). It fires **Day 4** — five synchronized snatch points, Slaad as distraction, sewer extraction to *vethka* past the Dravosi patrol gap. **Jean-Claude is the only PC who can read handler work for what it is.** Two ways in: the street (recognition beats below) or the tunnels (Nona's favor, §2).

**Recognition beats** (offer privately to JC, one per day, never to the table):
- **Crowd positioning** — a Grung stands where they can see three exits without watching any. JC has stood there; it's trained, not instinct.
- **Target assessment** — a figure in festival colours appraises a dockhand's build, tools, guild mark, then looks away. Not a glance. An appraisal. JC has made that look hundreds of times.
- **Exit logic** — a trade-cover Grung walks one Le Paludi route twice, at pace, marking the time.

**If JC pulls the thread → Handler Cell (combat-ready):**

> [!read-aloud]
> Two figures in trade clothes at the same Bridge pillar JC clocked earlier — different position, same posture. Not watching the performer. Watching the crowd move around the performer. The hour is wrong for the spot.

**Blue-Caste Handler (×2):** AC 13 · HP 16 · shortbow +4, 1d8+3 · **Grung poison** DC 11 Con or poisoned 1 min · Standing Leap 20 ft as part of move · *Tactic:* break contact first; fight only if cornered; **run at half HP** — they have a report to file.
→ full encounter + terrain: [[calveno-street-encounters\|Street Encounters → Handler Cell]]

> [!secret]
> One handler carries binding rope and a sealed wax cylinder — blank, but the wax bears a mark JC knows from Botukuri shipping labels. **Three outcomes, all real:** capture alive → he knows arrival timing + the extraction canal route (telling the Warren closes one of five lanes); kill both → one lane goes dark, Simone doesn't learn the cell was made; let them go → Simone learns the party is in Calveno, JC's face is now a report.

**Hook:** a recognition beat finds JC in the first public space he enters — the Velo Grung watching the crowd "wrong." Private note to his player. (Or start him underground via Nona's favor, §2.)
**If ignored:** prep completes invisibly; Day 4 becomes the watershed and the party witnesses the aftermath instead of the prevention. Each ignored day locks another lane.
**Hook out:** the sewer egress points and the *vethka* anchored in the Calven Roads ([[calveno-raid-signs\|Raid Prep Signs]]).

### 4. Red Lady — Salvage Money
*Delmar (his wreck), Crissdalynn (Red Lady traces, Kyzil) · social / investigation*

> [!read-aloud]
> A chandler lowers his voice without being asked. "You're not the first to ask about Maw salvage this month. There's money moving. Serious money — three buyers, or one buyer with three hands, nobody's sure. And they're not advertising a target."

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-04-05-red-lady-salvage-money.png|Batta Zusto presents a sealed chart package and Red Lady token at the Bridge exchange]]

**What's true here:** someone is assembling a salvage commission for a [[the-drowned-maw\|Maw]] wreck — unusual enough that [[savia-brentino\|Savia]] turned away three enquiries and the Tangle noticed. The accessible thread is broker **[[batta-zusto\|Batta Zusto]]** on the Bridge. The commission is **8–10 days from departure** — fast action gets the party there first.

> **The wreck is the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] — and it holds the [[pearl-of-souls\|Pearl]] Umberlee just ordered Delmar to retrieve.** If both fire, let the table feel the vise — a goddess, a funded rival crew, Delmar's own buried reasons, one wreck. Don't spell it out; let them assemble it.

**NPC — Batta Zusto:** late fifties, stocky, dressed a touch too formally; clean information, priced, no embellishment; not hostile, not protective of clients. *Sells (40 gp, or Maw nav intel worth ~80, or the confirmed wreck name):* deep-access Maw cargo recovery; three supply chains (hired diving gear, premium sealed chart package, crew in two ports); money came through a **Port Tidefall factor**; **someone *else* is also asking** about Maw access this month — not the same buyer. *Won't say:* the funder's identity (doesn't know it) or the Tidefall factor's name (not without proof the party can use it).

> [!skill-check] Investigation / Gather Info (DC 12; DC 8 if Delmar asks about Maw salvage gear)
> Success (two of three): hired diving apparatus, compressed-air bladders, a sealed chart package a courier wouldn't hand off. Failure: only the rumour of "money moving."

**Crissdalynn / Kyzil sub-beat:** [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] knows what the party doesn't — Crissdalynn's original ship went down to *"a foul wind from the south."* If she pulls on Red Lady traces with Kyzil present, he can set that phrase against the Maw rumours. *Reveal one layer at most* — he's grief held under discipline; his instinct is to protect her, not dump lore.

**Hook:** make Delmar visible as a Maw expert and the offer comes *to* him — a broker's runner, or Zusto himself, seeks "the captain who's actually sailed the Maw" with a polite, well-paid, not-obviously-a-trap proposition. Or Kyzil names the salvage money to Crissdalynn unprompted, because it frightens him.
**If ignored:** the commission assembles and departs ahead of them; the wreck is not empty when they reach it — the Maw gets more crowded and the Pearl problem harder.
**Hook out:** the sealed chart package (the commission's nav edge) and the Port Tidefall factor (closest traceable link to the funder — not findable in Calveno, not fast).

### 5. Il Gioco delle Beffe *(optional — the most fun; background if skipped)*
*All · social / comic toy*

> [!read-aloud]
> The Mercatura bridge has become a registration desk. A clerk with a ledger and a long-suffering face takes team names, marks, categories, and stakes, while three would-be tricksters argue about whether a bucket counts as a "device."

![[raw/assets/session-art/session-04-06-beffe-registration.png|Beffe registration crowds the Mercatura bridge while two masked figures study the pinch point]]

**What's true here:** the festival rewards public deception and clean recovery; [[giacomo-moretti\|Giacomo Moretti]] has an unbroken 20-year record the committee finds embarrassing. The party can register a team (name, mark, category, symbol, stake), bet, watch, recruit contacts, or use the window as cover. **The festival is also the raid's camouflage** — every prank that trains the crowd to laugh at a staged scare makes Day 4 easier to hold open.

**The toy:** if they register, get the table choosing — team name, mark, category, symbol, stake. Loud, dumb, theirs.

**Hook:** a Beffa team picks a PC as their mark in the first hour — the *Wrong Mark* drop (fish oil from a balcony, a wrong name on a real permit; [[calveno-street-encounters\|Street Encounters #1]]).
**If ignored:** the Beffa runs without them — cloth in the streets, nervous marks, daily results at sundown. Nothing closes; it's ambient texture and a five-day clock.
**Hook out:** any Beffa beat can carry one raid-prep sign as texture (see *Secrets & Clues*).

## Spotlight

- **Jean-Claude** (§2 + §3): the handler thread is his and only his, and **Nona's favor names him by sight** ("the blue Grung in the beret") and hands him the underground job. Two threads converge on him. Let the dread come *to* him in private recognition beats; the choice to descend is his. His arc (Simone, the Grung, [[the-canister\|the 20 vials he hasn't disclosed]]) is the quietest and most loaded on the board.
- **Crissdalynn** (§4 sub-beat): [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] carries "a foul wind from the south" about her ship's loss — attach it to the Red Lady money so her lore arrives through play, not exposition.

## Secrets & Clues

Keep these **unassigned** — give each to the first action that earns it, never bound to one roll or one scene.

- A Calveno repaint won't erase a Crown cutter's silhouette — via Savia, a dock regular, or a failed papers check.
- The font at the Waveservant Shrine won't go still — to anyone who enters; a public omen if Delmar stays away.
- Unusual broker money is building a Maw salvage job aimed at the *Red Lady* — via a chandler, Savia (names "Zusto" once), or canal gossip.
- A Crown-adjacent observer has missed his morning run two days straight; his firm is closed for the festival and *didn't* call him in — via Cobb or a harbour regular. (Raid + Crown.)
- A Beffa team registered under an unrecognized name asked the clerk how many people you can move through the bridge pinch-point in a minute — if anyone asks about odd teams.

> [!secret]
> Raid-adjacent — use only if players look in the right direction:
> • The Mercatura sewer collector has three access hatches; **two were re-seated this week**, no Commission record (festival inspection cycle skips that section).
> • The Dravosi patrol schedule for the Calven Roads has a **three-day minimum gap starting Day 4**. Not secret — any harbour factor with contacts can quote the dates. The question is who *else* already has them.
> • Three low-profile, dark-resined, double-outrigged hulls sit at anchor past the patrol line, no pennant — anyone with Grung/Midchain maritime knowledge IDs them as *vethka*.

## If They Surprise You

When the party goes somewhere you didn't prep:

1. **Place the request.** Public deal → Mercatura or the Bridge. Quiet practical work → Le Paludi. Vice, music, strange trade → Velo. Official ship business → Arsenal edge. ([[calveno-districts\|district map]]; [[calveno-improv\|names & voices]].)
2. **Give one competent local** who wants one small thing now — a sale, a witness, a referral, a favour, not to be dragged into trouble.
3. **Attach one live pressure** from the Threads table so the new direction still feeds the session.
4. **Let the choice stand.** Note what changed, tick the clock, move on. Don't repeat a hook through a second NPC until they comply — charge a cost and make the new direction real.

## Possible Cliffhanger

Don't plan an ending — recognize the highest-tension stopping point if it arrives:

- **Broke a handler cell:** end on what the handler reveals — *the raid is days out and they've found one of five lanes.*
- **Engaged Umberlee:** end on the breath — Branca gasping back to life, the booklet open again, "I hope you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace." The Pearl summons now hangs over every Maw decision.
- **Ignored the raid prep:** end on the first wrong sound on Day 4 — a scream the crowd laughs at, then doesn't. Open on absence after motion: a dropped tool roll, a boat tied wrong, a name shouted into a crowd that has already moved on.
