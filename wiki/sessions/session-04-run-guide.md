# Session 04 — Run Guide

> **Two engines, equal weight.** Calveno is built to run *either* way, and you switch between them per table, per player, per moment:
> - **Sandbox (they drive).** A menu of live pressures. Offer them through the world when player intent points; never as a quest board. Ignored pressure *moves*, it doesn't vanish.
> - **Theme park (you pull).** Every pressure is also an **attraction** with a concrete **entry hook** — a physical thing that comes and finds a specific PC in the first hour. Fire hooks for players who like direction and momentum.
>
> Most tables are mixed. Drive the players who want to drive; throw the hook at the ones who want to be pulled. Both are first-class here — pick freely, scene by scene.

## How to Run This — the Balanced Dual Mode

Each attraction below prints **both** modes side by side:

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **▶ Directed hook** | The thing that physically comes to a named PC in the first hour. Fire it when you want momentum or a quiet player engaged. |
| **◆ Sandbox** | How it surfaces if you wait for intent — and exactly where the pressure *moves* if the party never touches it. |

**The rule that keeps both honest:** firing a hook is an *invitation*, never a rail. If the PC waves it off, the hook becomes sandbox pressure — it moves to the *If ignored* line and waits. You are never forcing the menu; you are offering doors, some of which knock.

## The City Frame

The theme-park scaffolding that *also* powers the sandbox. Lock these three and every attraction snaps into place.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | **The city that plays liar.** Calveno during the Beffa is masks, staged scares, and public deception as civic entertainment — festival cloth, paper masks, a prank around every canal corner. |
| **Structural truth** | The Beffa has trained the city to *wait before believing a scene is real* — applause-reflex on a staged scream. **That hesitation is the exact gap Simone's raid is built to exploit.** Theme → mechanism → the session's buried dread. |
| **City Goal** | Get the *Uncertainty* **off the Crown's books and out of Calveno clean** — re-silhouetted, papered, repaired — before [[knighton-pursuit\|Knighton's]] reach closes. Shore leave is the cover. |
| **How attractions connect** | Each is a **step toward** leaving (the ship, the papers), a **complication of** it (debts, exposure, divine claims), or a **reason a PC can't yet** (the thing that's been hunting them found them here). |

Use the goal as connective tissue: when a player asks "why are we doing this?", the answer is always some version of *because we can't sail clean until it's handled.*

## Strong Start

*Interrupted Routine. Full party present — first real morning. One image, one pressure, ends on an open choice. This is also the **entry hook for Attraction 1**.*

> [!read-aloud]
> The coffee here is good — black, scalding, the first you haven't had to ration in two weeks. Below the window, Calveno is putting on its face: festival cloth going up over the Mercatura, a registration drum starting somewhere uphill, a skiff stacked with paper masks higher than the boy poling it. Then the door opens and a child comes in. Soaked to the knee, a temple cord at her wrist, eyes red from a night she did not sleep. She finds Delmar's face without being told which one he is. "The lady at the shrine says to tell you. She kept her promise. She hasn't slept. And the water in the font won't lie flat anymore."

Then stop. The day is theirs — the shrine, the festival, the ship, the Warren, money, worship, whatever pulls first. Do not narrate their answer. The child knows nothing else; she was paid a copper and a blessing to carry exactly that.

> [!dm]
> This re-grounds the table in Calveno without a recap and addresses last session's open Umberlee thread head-on (Delmar bought a day with a 19 Deception; the day is up). It is a **directed hook** by design — but if the party pockets it and goes elsewhere, that's the sandbox working: [[branca\|Branca]] keeps not sleeping, the unquiet font becomes a Mercatura omen, and the hook waits (see Attraction 1, *◆ Sandbox*).

## The Board — Attractions & Pressures

> Five live situations, any order. Each runs cold from what's printed here; links are for depth you chase *between* sessions. Each carries a **▶ directed hook** and a **◆ sandbox** handling — use whichever fits the player in front of you.

### 1. The Shrine — Umberlee's Pearl Summons
*Pull: Delmar (primary), Crissdalynn (was there) · Register: revelation / horror set-piece*
*Goal connection: reframes the voyage **out** — the sea itself now has terms, and they point at the same wreck as Attraction 4.*

> This is a **fixed dramatic beat**, not a negotiation. The offer is unacceptable by design, the refusal is expected, and the point is the *demonstration*. Run it as scripted; let the silence between boxes do the work.

> [!read-aloud] — Arrival
> The [[waveservant-shrine\|Waveservant Shrine]] is a low, tide-stained vault off a Mercatura canal, salt crusting the doorframe. The font behind the bench holds a hand's depth of seawater that will not lie flat — it rises and falls, slow, like something breathing under it. [[branca\|Branca]] is exactly where she said: upright, awake after a night she did not sleep, salt-white hair pulled back hard, the little leather tide-table booklet open on her knee. "So," she says, bright and clipped, the warmth of someone rescheduling a missed meeting. "You came. Good. We have an appointment."

**NPC — Branca:** corporate cult executive assistant — bubbly, efficient, too much eye contact; opens "So!", closes "Does that work for you?", checks the booklet mid-sentence as if it settles every objection. She genuinely believes making divine horror *pleasant* is a kindness. She is the soft instrument; what comes through her is not.

**The possession.** At the appointed moment Branca stops mid-sentence. Her eyes roll back to white. The voice that comes out is not hers.

> [!read-aloud] — Umberlee speaks
> Branca's eyes roll white. When she speaks it is not her voice — it is the sea given a throat, cold and enormous and patient. "FISK. You carry what is Mine. Go down to the wreck. Bring Me the Pearl. Return it to My shrine — and I will grant you the one mercy you have earned: a swift death, and the souls you stole brought home to Me. This is My grace. Accept it."

**The demand (plainly, for you):** retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls\|Pearl of Souls]] from the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] wreck at the [[the-drowned-maw\|Drowned Maw]] and return it to a shrine of Umberlee. The "reward" is Delmar's *own death* and the surrender of the souls he carries. A death sentence dressed as salvation. Delmar will refuse — that is the design.

> [!secret]
> This brushes Delmar's secret. The party will hear that Delmar **carries souls Umberlee claims** and that there is a Pearl he must retrieve. They need not learn the [[pearl-of-souls\|five captains / Vel Orn theft]] story — keep Her words to "what is Mine" and "the souls you stole," and let the full shape stay Delmar's to disclose or conceal. Only Delmar's player knows he carries voices.

**The wrath.** The instant Delmar refuses:

> [!read-aloud] — The wrath
> The refusal is barely out of your mouth before the light goes wrong. Thunder walks across a sky that was clear a breath ago; the canal shivers in its channel. The thing wearing Branca *roars* — and then, almost offhand, it takes her head in her own two hands and snaps her neck. The body folds. The shrine goes silent. Branca lies on the wet stone, not breathing.

Hold the silence. Then —

> [!read-aloud] — The return
> Mist gathers off the canal — too fast, too deliberate — and curls in around the fallen woman like a tide coming home. Branca's chest heaves. She drags in one enormous, gasping breath, sits up, and blinks the white from her eyes. She finds the booklet, smooths her robe, and looks up with that same bright warmth, as if nothing happened at all. "I hope," she says, "you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace."

> [!skill-check] Insight (DC 12) — after the revival
> Success: Branca means it completely — she experienced her own murder as a privilege and is not performing. *That* is the horror. Failure: reads as shock; the true depth doesn't register.

**Complication (the lean-in):** the only way to satisfy a goddess is to die for her — so Delmar has to find a *third* answer, and the Pearl she wants is in the wreck a funded crew is already racing for (Attraction 4).

**▶ Directed hook:** the soaked temple child at breakfast (the Strong Start). She comes to Delmar, by name, in the first hour. Cannot be missed unless ignored.
**◆ Sandbox:** if Delmar never goes, the water that reached for him at the dock reaches *again*, harder, next time he touches deep seawater; the unquiet font spreads as a public omen ("the Waveservant's water won't lie down"); and Branca simply turns up later with another appointment. **Pressure moves, never vanishes — Umberlee reschedules.**
**Hook out:** the Pearl is inside the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] wreck — exactly what the salvage commission (Attraction 4) wants. Demand and money pull toward the same wreck from opposite directions.

### 2. The Warren & the Ship's Face
*Pull: Perrin (family, the favor), Delmar (the silhouette) · Register: social / logistics*
*Goal connection: this **is** the city goal — disguise, papers, and the Warren access that makes leaving clean possible.*

> [!read-aloud]
> [[le-paludi\|Le Paludi]] runs on quiet water and long memory. The smell of [[nona-black-jaw\|Nona]]'s kitchen reaches the canal before her door does.

**What's true here:** two linked problems live in the Warren. **(a)** The *Uncertainty* still reads as a Crown cutter to anyone who knows cutters — paint won't fix a silhouette. The off-books yard, [[carpenters-slip\|Carpenters' Slip]], won't take a cold approach; she needs a Warren introduction, which runs through Nona. **(b)** Nona's **favor** ([[nonas-favor\|the unconditional yes]]) and the **sending stone** are live in Perrin's pocket.

**NPC — Savia Brentino** ([[savia-brentino\|Carpenters' Slip]]): dry, unhurried, says the hard thing once and lets it sit. *Wants:* a Warren introduction before she'll deal — three ships tried cold this month and are on her list. *Reveals (flatly, at the end):* "She'll still read as a cutter to anyone who knows cutters. That's not a paint problem." **Terms:** 220 gp + 5 city days + one **unassigned Tangle favour**. The bow registry plate is the party's call.

**NPC — Nona Black-Jaw** ([[nona-black-jaw\|the matriarch]]): feeds you first, names what you owe second — as accounting, not threat. She now knows the [[vestra\|Vestra]] is gone (quietly grieving the men and the money while planning the response).

> [!secret]
> **Nona's favor (canon).** Word reached Nona that Perrin came in with a crew including a face nobody here knows: a **blue Grung in a beret and moustache** ([[jean-claude-tabarnack\|Jean-Claude]]). She reads it as luck. She's had reports she'd normally dismiss as impossible this far north — **Grung moving in the old Warren tunnels that intersect the Calveno sewers** — and wants them confirmed or dismissed. Who better to hunt Grung than a Grung. *The ask:* send JC (with whoever he wants) into the tunnels to confirm. **This is the door into the raid** — those sewer Grung are [[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|Simone's extraction crews]]. Nona's favor and Attraction 3 are the same investigation from two ends.

> [!skill-check] Insight (DC 13) — on Savia
> Success: she isn't hostile, she's screening; an introduction flips her. Failure: she reads as a dead end, the silhouette stays unsolved.

**Complication (the lean-in):** the ship can't leave clean without the Warren, the Warren's price is a favour, and the favour walks Jean-Claude straight into the sewers under a raid no one has named yet.

**▶ Directed hook:** [[cobb\|Cobb]] (or a young Black-Jaw runner) finds Perrin within the first hour — Nona already knows he's in the city and "would love to feed him properly," and there's "a small thing she's hoping he can help with." Lands on Perrin; pulls the family player into motion.
**◆ Sandbox:** if Perrin delays, Nona doesn't chase — she lets the silence sit, then uses the **sending stone** on her own timing. The silhouette problem stays live; the [[knighton-pursuit\|Crown clock]] keeps ticking. **Pressure moves: every day unpapered is a day the ship is easier to recognize when they finally sail.**
**Hook out:** Warren leads — [[warren-marrows-crate\|Marrow's Crate]], [[warren-ferrin-locke\|Ferrin Locke]], [[warren-bela-silt-paw\|Bela Silt-Paw]] (hard 2–3 day clock). All optional; none gate the ship job.

### 3. The Sewer Grung — Raid Prep
*Pull: Jean-Claude (only he can read it) · Register: exploratory → combat. SPOTLIGHT.*
*Goal connection: the raid will flood the harbour with Crown and Tessarine attention — bad for a ship trying to slip out quietly; and the Warren's help (the ship goal) is priced on this favor.*

> [!read-aloud]
> A blue-caste Grung stands at the edge of a Velo square, watching a street performer. He is not enjoying it. He is watching how the crowd *moves* when the performer makes a scene — where people turn, where the gaps open, how long a knot of onlookers takes to dissolve.

**What's true here:** [[simone-tabarnack\|Simone]]'s handlers are already in Calveno, mapping a mass capture operation under festival cover ([[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|the Beffa Grung Raid]]). It fires **Day 4** — five synchronized snatch points, Slaad as distraction, sewer extraction to *vethka* past the Dravosi patrol gap. **Jean-Claude is the only PC who can read handler work for what it is.**

> **Two ways in, by design.** *Above:* the recognition beats here, on the street — only JC sees them. *Below:* **Nona's favor (Attraction 2)** points him straight at the tunnels. If the party takes the favor, this stops being ambient dread and becomes a job with the Warren behind it.

**Jean-Claude recognition beats** (offer privately, one per day, never to the whole table):
- **Crowd positioning** — a Grung stands where they can see three exits without watching any. JC has stood there; it's trained, not instinct.
- **Target assessment** — a small figure in festival colours appraises a dockhand's build, tools, guild mark, then looks away. Not a glance. An appraisal. JC has made that look hundreds of times.
- **Exit logic** — a trade-cover Grung walks one Le Paludi route twice, at pace, marking the time.

**If JC pulls the thread → Handler Cell (combat-ready):**

> [!read-aloud]
> Two figures in trade clothes at the same Bridge pillar JC clocked earlier — different position now, same posture. Not watching the performer. Watching the crowd move around the performer. The hour is wrong for the spot.

**Blue-Caste Handler (×2):** AC 13 · HP 16 · shortbow +4, 1d8+3 · **Grung poison** DC 11 Con save or poisoned 1 min · Standing Leap 20 ft as part of move · *Tactic:* break contact and withdraw first; fight only if cornered; **run at half HP** — they have a report to file.
→ full encounter + terrain: [[calveno-street-encounters\|Street Encounters → Handler Cell]]

> [!secret]
> One handler carries binding rope and a sealed wax cylinder — blank, but the wax bears a mark JC knows from Botukuri shipping labels. **Three outcomes, all real:** capture alive → he knows arrival timing + the extraction canal route (telling the Warren closes one of five lanes); kill both → one lane goes dark, Simone doesn't learn the cell was made; let them go → Simone learns the party is in Calveno, JC's face is now a report.

**Complication (the lean-in):** every way JC acts costs something — acting exposes the party to Simone, *not* acting lets the harvest complete, and taking one alive means he's now holding a person who knows his face.

**▶ Directed hook:** a recognition beat finds JC in the first public space he enters — the Velo Grung watching the crowd "wrong." Private note to his player only. Lands the dread on the quiet PC immediately. (If you'd rather start him underground, fire Attraction 2's Cobb hook and let Nona send him down.)
**◆ Sandbox:** if no one engages, prep completes invisibly; Day 4 becomes the watershed; the party witnesses the aftermath instead of the prevention. **Pressure moves: each ignored day locks another lane (the raid is already at Day 2 of 4).**
**Hook out:** the sewer egress points and the *vethka* anchored in the Calven Roads ([[calveno-raid-signs\|Raid Prep Signs]]).

### 4. Red Lady — Salvage Money
*Pull: Delmar (his wreck), Crissdalynn (Red Lady traces, Kyzil) · Register: social / investigation*
*Goal connection: this is **where they sail when they leave** — and Umberlee's Pearl (Attraction 1) is in the same wreck.*

> [!read-aloud]
> A chandler lowers his voice without being asked. "You're not the first to ask about Maw salvage this month. There's money moving. Serious money — three buyers, or one buyer with three hands, nobody's sure. And they're not advertising a target."

**What's true here:** someone is assembling a salvage commission for a [[the-drowned-maw\|Maw]] wreck — unusual enough that [[savia-brentino\|Savia]] turned away three enquiries and the Tangle noticed. The accessible thread is broker **[[batta-zusto\|Batta Zusto]]** on the Bridge. The commission is **8–10 days from departure** — fast action gets the party there first.

> **The wreck is the [[red-lady\|Red Lady]] — and it holds the [[pearl-of-souls\|Pearl]] Umberlee just ordered Delmar to retrieve.** If both attractions fire, let the table feel the vise: a goddess demanding the Pearl, a funded crew racing for the same wreck, Delmar's own buried reasons. Don't spell the convergence out — let them assemble it.

**NPC — Batta Zusto:** late fifties, stocky, dressed a touch too formally; clean information, priced, no embellishment; not hostile, not protective of clients. *Sells (40 gp, or Maw nav intel worth ~80, or the confirmed wreck name):* deep-access Maw cargo recovery; three supply chains (hired diving gear, premium sealed chart package, crew in two ports); money came through a **Port Tidefall factor**; **someone *else* is also asking** about Maw access this month — not the same buyer. *Won't say:* the funder's identity (he doesn't know it) or the Tidefall factor's name (not without proof the party can use it).

> [!skill-check] Investigation / Gather Info (DC 12; DC 8 if Delmar asks specifically about Maw salvage gear)
> Success (two of three): hired diving apparatus, compressed-air bladders, a sealed chart package a courier wouldn't hand off. Failure: only the rumour of "money moving."

**Crissdalynn / Kyzil sub-beat:** [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] is in the city and knows what the party doesn't — Crissdalynn's original ship went down to *"a foul wind from the south."* If she pulls on Red Lady traces with Kyzil present, he can place that phrase against the Maw rumours. *Reveal at most one layer* — he's grief held under discipline; his instinct is to protect her, not dump lore.

**Complication (the lean-in):** going public to find the wreck *makes the party's interest public* in a city where Tessarine factors record everything — the same network that surfaces the wreck reports what they asked.

**▶ Directed hook:** make Delmar visible as a Maw expert and the offer comes *to* him — a broker's runner, or Zusto himself, seeks out "the captain who's actually sailed the Maw" with a polite, well-paid, not-obviously-a-trap proposition. Or: Kyzil, finding Crissdalynn again, mentions the salvage money unprompted because it frightens him.
**◆ Sandbox:** if untouched, the commission assembles and departs ahead of them; the wreck is not empty when they reach it. **Pressure moves: the Maw gets more crowded and the Pearl problem gets harder, not quieter.**
**Hook out:** the sealed chart package (the commission's nav edge) and the Port Tidefall factor (closest traceable link to the funder — not findable in Calveno, not fast).

### 5. Il Gioco delle Beffe *(optional — the most fun; background if skipped)*
*Pull: all · Register: social / comic toy*
*Goal connection: the five-day festival **is** the cover window the ship work happens inside — and the raid is trying to slam it shut on Day 4.*

> [!read-aloud]
> The Mercatura bridge has become a registration desk. A clerk with a ledger and a long-suffering face is taking team names, marks, categories, and entry stakes, while three would-be tricksters argue about whether a bucket counts as a "device."

**What's true here:** the festival rewards public deception and clean recovery; [[giacomo-moretti\|Giacomo Moretti]] has an unbroken 20-year record the committee finds embarrassing. The party can register a team (name, mark, category, scale, symbol, stake), bet, watch, recruit contacts, or use the window as cover. **The festival is also the raid's camouflage** — every prank that trains the crowd to laugh at a staged scare makes Day 4 easier to hold open.

**The toy:** if they register, get the table choosing — team name, mark, category, symbol, stake. Loud, dumb, theirs.

**Complication (the lean-in):** the thing that makes the city fun this week is the same thing that makes it defenceless — and once a PC sees that, the festival stops being only funny.

**▶ Directed hook:** a Beffa team targets a PC as their mark in the first hour — the *Wrong Mark* drop (fish oil from a balcony, a wrong name on a real permit; [[calveno-street-encounters\|Street Encounters #1]]). Comic, low-stakes, and it physically pulls a player into the festival without a quest.
**◆ Sandbox:** ignored, the Beffa runs without them — cloth in the streets, nervous marks, daily results at sundown. **Nothing closes; it's ambient texture and a five-day clock.**
**Hook out:** any Beffa beat can carry one raid-prep sign as texture (see *Secrets & Clues*) — a team that never verified, a "crowd-compression timing" prank concept, a false alarm that becomes funny.

## Spotlight

- **Jean-Claude** (Attractions 2 + 3): the handler thread is his and only his, and **Nona's favor names him by sight** ("the blue Grung in the beret") and hands him the underground job. Two threads converge on him this session. Let the dread come *to* him in private recognition beats; the choice to descend is his. Strongest spotlight on the board — his arc (Simone, the Grung, [[the-canister\|the 20 vials he hasn't disclosed]]) is the quietest and most loaded.

## Secrets & Clues

Keep these **unassigned** — give each to the first action that earns it, never bound to one roll or one scene. (These feed the *◆ Sandbox* mode: the players who dig find them; the players who don't still get them handed over via a fired hook.)

- A Calveno repaint won't erase a Crown cutter's silhouette — surfaces via Savia, a dock regular, or a failed papers check.
- The font at the Waveservant Shrine won't go still — surfaces to anyone who enters; becomes a public omen if Delmar stays away.
- Unusual broker money is building a Maw salvage job aimed at the *Red Lady* — surfaces via a chandler, Savia (names "Zusto" once), or canal gossip.
- A Crown-adjacent commercial observer has missed his morning run two days straight; his firm is closed for the festival and *didn't* call him in — surfaces via Cobb or a harbour regular. (Raid + Crown.)
- A Beffa team registered under an unrecognized name asked the clerk how many people you can move through the bridge pinch-point in a minute — surfaces if anyone asks the registration clerk about odd teams.

## If They Surprise You

The **sandbox safety net** — equal partner to the entry hooks. When the party goes somewhere you didn't prep, this turns "off-menu" into a tool, not a panic.

1. **Place the request.** Public deal → Mercatura or the Bridge. Quiet practical work → Le Paludi. Vice, music, strange trade → Velo. Official ship business → Arsenal edge. ([[calveno-districts\|district map]]; [[calveno-improv\|names & voices]].)
2. **Give one competent local** who wants one small thing now — a sale, a witness, a referral, a favour, not to be dragged into trouble.
3. **Attach one live pressure** from the Threads table so the new direction still feeds the session (ship exposure, Warren memory, the raid clock, Red Lady money, Umberlee).
4. **Let the choice stand.** Note what changed, tick the clock, move on. Never have a second NPC repeat the same hook until they comply — charge a Calveno cost and make the new direction real.

> The dual mode in one breath: **a fired hook is a door that knocks; the sandbox is every other door in the building.** If they ignore the knock, the door's still there — and so are all the others.

## Possible Cliffhanger

Don't plan an ending — recognize the highest-tension stopping point if it arrives:

- **If the party broke a handler cell:** end on what the captured/dead handler reveals — *the raid is four days out and they've only found one of five lanes.*
- **If they engaged Umberlee:** end on the breath — Branca gasping back to life on the wet stone, the booklet open again, "I hope you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace." The Pearl summons now hangs over every Maw decision.
- **If they ignored the raid prep:** end on the first wrong sound on Day 4 — a scream the crowd laughs at, then doesn't. **Open with absence after motion:** a dropped tool roll, a boat tied wrong, a name shouted into a crowd that has already moved on.
