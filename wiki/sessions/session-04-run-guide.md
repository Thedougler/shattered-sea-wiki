# Session 04 — Run Guide: The City That Plays Liar

Use this page as the control panel. Daily files own read-aloud text, mechanics, and detailed branches.

---

## Opening Recap — Read Aloud

> [!read-aloud]
> Last time on the Shattered Sea: the party sailed a stolen Crown cutter into a whip shark's feeding lane and barely kept the hull together. A dream from deep water told Perrin something old is watching. Shepherd Grigori healed wounds too quickly and walked into Calveno without looking back. They limped the *Uncertainty* into La Vasca's covered basin, and Perrin's name opened the dry dock — and sent word straight to his grandmother. A Waveservant named Branca found Delmar and he put her off. She walked to the shrine and sat down. She has not moved since. Then something dropped out of the sky and hit Crissdalynn in the chest. Session 04: The City That Plays Liar.

- **Jean-Claude** is the session's quietest and most loaded arc. The handler thread is his and only his; Nona's favor names him by sight ("the blue Grung in the beret"). Two threads converge on him. Let the dread come *to* him in private recognition beats; the choice to descend is his. Watch for JC falling behind — his threads are internal and easy to miss.
- **Crissdalynn**: [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] carries "a foul wind from the south" about her ship's loss — attach it to the Red Lady money so her lore arrives through play, not exposition.
- **Delmar**: the Pearl Summons is his defining beat; the Red Lady salvage makes it a vise.

## Secrets & Clues

![[wiki/assets/sessions/session-04/art/umberlee-speaks-through-branca.png|Umberlee speaks through Branca at the harbor shrine — the font erupts, Branca's eyes glow sea-green, Delmar stands defiant]]

Keep these **unassigned** — give each to the first action that earns it, never bound to one roll or one scene.

- A Calveno repaint won't erase a Crown cutter's silhouette — via **Savia** (says it flatly after a Warren intro), **a dock regular** (eyes the hull and shakes his head), or **a failed papers check** (the inspector looks at the bow, not the paint).
- The font at the Waveservant Shrine won't go still — via **anyone who enters the shrine** (the water moves); a public omen if Delmar stays away long enough for locals to notice.
- Unusual broker money is building a Maw salvage job aimed at the *Red Lady* — via **a chandler** (lowers his voice unprompted), **Savia** (names "Zusto" once), or **canal gossip** (diving gear at the anchorage).
- A Crown-adjacent observer has missed his morning run two days straight; his firm is closed for the festival and *didn't* call him in — via **Cobb** (harbour gossip) or **a harbour regular** (someone who keeps a routine).
- A Beffa team registered under an unrecognized name asked the clerk how many people you can move through the bridge pinch-point in a minute — via **anyone who asks about odd teams** or **the clerk herself** if a PC registers.

**Raid-adjacent — use only if the party looks in the right direction:**

- The Mercatura sewer collector has three access hatches; **two were re-seated this week**, no Commission record (festival inspection cycle skips that section).
- The Dravosi patrol schedule for the Calven Roads has a **three-day minimum gap starting Beffa Day 4**. Any harbour factor with contacts can quote the dates. The question is who *else* already has them.
- Three low-profile, dark-resined, double-outrigged hulls sit at anchor past the patrol line, no pennant — anyone with Grung/Midchain maritime knowledge IDs them as *vethka*.

## What If They Can't Decide?

If the table stalls or can't pick a direction, throw one of these. Each is self-contained and pulls toward a different thread. The party's response tells you which thread they care about.

| Hook | Comes to | Pulls toward |
|---|---|---|
| **The stone speaks.** Nona's voice, close as a whisper: "Come back. Bring the blue one." | Perrin | Warren / the favor / JC's tunnel thread |
| **Branca hasn't moved.** She is in the shrine at dawn, same position, booklet on her knee. A local mentions the Waveservant woman who won't leave. | Delmar (via rumour) | Pearl Summons / Umberlee |
| **A handler in the crowd.** A Grung stands at the edge of a Velo square, watching how the crowd moves — not enjoying it. Trained posture. | JC (private note) | Raid thread / handler investigation |
| **A chandler mentions money.** "You're not the first to ask about Maw salvage this month. There's money moving. Serious money." | Delmar / Crissdalynn | Red Lady / salvage commission |
| **The Beffa clerk eyes a PC.** A registered team has picked one of them as their mark. Festival mischief — loud, dumb, theirs. | Any | Beffa registration / city engagement |
| **Sem needs an answer.** "I can't paint a name I don't have." The ship's registry plate is a decision someone has to make. | Perrin / Delmar | Ship logistics / Savia |

> [!dm]
> Don't throw all of these at once. Pick the one aimed at the quietest player, or the one that connects to whatever thread the table has been circling. One hook is an invitation. Three hooks is a schedule.

## If They Surprise You

When the party goes somewhere you didn't prep:

1. **Place the request.** Public deal → Mercatura or the Bridge. Quiet practical work → Le Paludi. Vice, music, strange trade → Velo. Official ship business → Arsenal edge. ([[calveno-districts\|district map]]; [[calveno-improv\|names & voices]].)
2. **Give one competent local** who wants one small thing now — a sale, a witness, a referral, a favour, not to be dragged into trouble.
3. **Attach one live pressure** from the Threads table so the new direction still feeds the session.
4. **Let the choice stand.** Note what changed, tick the clock, move on. Don't repeat a hook through a second NPC until they comply — charge a cost and make the new direction real.

## Possible Cliffhanger

Don't plan an ending — recognize the highest-tension stopping point if it arrives:

- **Broke a handler cell:** end on what the handler reveals — *the raid is days out and they've found one of five lanes.*
- **Found the blackpowder:** end on the packed ceiling, the summoning circle, the moment JC understands the scale. The city is sitting on a bomb.
- **Engaged Umberlee:** end on the breath — Branca gasping back to life, the booklet open, "I hope you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace." The Pearl summons hangs over every Maw decision.
- **Entered the tunnels:** end on what JC finds underground — Grung signs, sealed hatches, extraction route markers. The raid is real.
- **The raid fires:** end on Nona's vow — "we will bring your brothers and your sons and your fathers back" — or on the *vethka* clearing the Calven Roads with their cargo.
- **Ignored the raid prep:** end on the first wrong sound on Day 5 — a scream the crowd laughs at, then doesn't.
