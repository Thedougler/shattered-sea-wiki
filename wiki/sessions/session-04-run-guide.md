> The whole city is the sandbox; the party drives. Each day below has its own file with read-aloud, NPCs, and mechanics ready at the table. Ignored pressure *moves*, it doesn't vanish. When they go somewhere you didn't prep, see *If They Surprise You* at the bottom.

**The spine under everything:** the Beffa runs five days; the [[calveno-beffa-grung-raid\|Grung raid]] lands on Day 4 of the festival (the last day of La Finestra). The party need not engage it — but it moves every day they don't.

## Spotlight

- **Jean-Claude** is the session's quietest and most loaded arc. The handler thread is his and only his; Nona's favor names him by sight ("the blue Grung in the beret"). Two threads converge on him. Let the dread come *to* him in private recognition beats; the choice to descend is his. Watch for JC falling behind — his threads are internal and easy to miss.
- **Crissdalynn**: [[master-kyzil\|Kyzil]] carries "a foul wind from the south" about her ship's loss — attach it to the Red Lady money so her lore arrives through play, not exposition.
- **Delmar**: the Pearl Summons is his defining beat; the Red Lady salvage makes it a vise.
- **Perrin**: family pressure through Nona; the favor he agreed to without terms.

## Secrets & Clues

Keep these **unassigned** — give each to the first action that earns it, never bound to one roll or one scene.

- A Calveno repaint won't erase a Crown cutter's silhouette — via Savia, a dock regular, or a failed papers check.
- The font at the Waveservant Shrine won't go still — to anyone who enters; a public omen if Delmar stays away.
- Unusual broker money is building a Maw salvage job aimed at the *Red Lady* — via a chandler, Savia (names "Zusto" once), or canal gossip.
- A Crown-adjacent observer has missed his morning run two days straight; his firm is closed for the festival and *didn't* call him in — via Cobb or a harbour regular.
- A Beffa team registered under an unrecognized name asked the clerk how many people you can move through the bridge pinch-point in a minute — via anyone who asks about odd teams.

**Raid-adjacent — use only if the party looks in the right direction:**

- The Mercatura sewer collector has three access hatches; **two were re-seated this week**, no Commission record (festival inspection cycle skips that section).
- The Dravosi patrol schedule for the Calven Roads has a **three-day minimum gap starting Beffa Day 4**. Any harbour factor with contacts can quote the dates. The question is who *else* already has them.
- Three low-profile, dark-resined, double-outrigged hulls sit at anchor past the patrol line, no pennant — anyone with Grung/Midchain maritime knowledge IDs them as *vethka*.

## If They Surprise You

When the party goes somewhere you didn't prep:

1. **Place the request.** Public deal → Mercatura or the Bridge. Quiet practical work → Le Paludi. Vice, music, strange trade → Velo. Official ship business → Arsenal edge. ([[calveno-districts\|district map]]; [[calveno-improv\|names & voices]].)
2. **Give one competent local** who wants one small thing now — a sale, a witness, a referral, a favour, not to be dragged into trouble.
3. **Attach one live pressure** from the Threads table so the new direction still feeds the session.
4. **Let the choice stand.** Note what changed, tick the clock, move on. Don't repeat a hook through a second NPC until they comply — charge a cost and make the new direction real.

## Possible Cliffhanger

Don't plan an ending — recognize the highest-tension stopping point if it arrives:

- **Broke a handler cell:** end on what the handler reveals — *the raid is days out and they've found one of five lanes.*
- **Found the blackpowder:** end on the packed ceiling, the summoning circle, the moment JC understands the scale. The city is sitting on a bomb.
- **Engaged Umberlee:** end on the breath — Branca gasping back to life, the booklet open, "I hope you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace." The Pearl summons hangs over every Maw decision.
- **Entered the tunnels:** end on what JC finds underground — Grung signs, sealed hatches, extraction route markers. The raid is real.
- **The raid fires:** end on Nona's vow — "we will bring your brothers and your sons and your fathers back" — or on the *vethka* clearing the Calven Roads with their cargo.
- **Ignored the raid prep:** end on the first wrong sound on Day 5 — a scream the crowd laughs at, then doesn't.
