# Session 04 — The Frog, the Owl, and the Bomb

## Durable Changes

- **Delmar confessed.** Told the party he was a real admiral — five-ship privateer fleet sponsored by the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]. They stole the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from a shrine in [[sunken-crown|Vafnar]]; the Maw destroyed the fleet hours later. Crissdalynn pulled him free. The Pearl fell into the Maw with the wreckage.
- **Umberlee spoke through Branca.** At the [[waveservant-shrine|Calveno harbor shrine]], Umberlee possessed [[branca|Branca]], demanded the Pearl's return, killed and resurrected Branca as a demonstration, then negotiated: bring the Pearl, keep your life, souls stay with Umberlee. Delmar counter-offered to deliver those who commissioned the theft. Umberlee read his mind freely and deferred: "Bring me the pearl. We will talk then."
- **Boots of flying → Delmar.** Jean-Claude bought boots of flying from [[casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]] (125gp). Perrin won the roll but doesn't wear shoes — Rattkin go barefoot. Delmar received them.
- **Jean-Claude's silent shortbow.** +1 shortbow from Casa Lupo (50gp): completely silent, does not reveal position when firing from hidden, no attunement.
- **Jean-Claude sold whip shark eggs.** 3 fertilized eggs for 300gp (30 platinum) to a Le Paludi market merchant. He has 250gp remaining after shopping.
- **Kyzil sparring match.** Non-lethal four-on-one on the Ponte Bassa rooftop, 3 rounds. Kyzil's stat block (CR 14) confirmed: 4 attacks, returning daggers (piercing + force), DC 19 open palm, Downburst legendary action, Empty Wing Parry reaction (+5 AC). Party held well — Crissdalynn grappled him (nat 1 contested), Delmar landed a sneak attack through bardic inspiration, Perrin's drum drew Kyzil's tactical concern.
- **Grung bombing plot exposed.** Party investigated [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s Grung sightings in the sewers. Found directional markers, traps, and a catamaran loaded with 8 barrels of black powder. Jean-Claude ambushed two patrol Grung; Crissdalynn kingfishered a fleeing purple Grung. Jean-Claude executed the purple captive after it refused to talk and confirmed Simone's involvement.
- **Felix Aho captured.** Green Grung laborer, hired (not ideological). Revealed: coordinated bombing from 4 underground attack points, scaffolding holding black powder against sewer ceilings, 2-day deadline timed to festival, purple garrisons and red leaders at each point, 6 ships total ferrying powder. Offered full cooperation for 1 month of protection.
- **Grung defectors exist.** Felix revealed that Grung who leave Simone's faction are kill-on-sight. Jean-Claude is considered a defector. He did not know defectors existed as a recognized category.
- **Nona's favor executed.** Sending stone call: investigate Grung in the sewers. Nona provided Hero's Feast through cooking. Party completed the investigation and returned with Felix and full intel.
- **Delmar abstaining from alcohol.** Drank from his flask before the waitress arrived — not liquor. Unusual for him.
- **Kyzil staying through the festival.** Confirmed present in Calveno at least through Il Gioco delle Beffe.
- **Ponte Bassa established as party base.** Cheap tavern near La Vasca, Passage front. Two rooms rented.

## Scene Log

1. **Le Paludi Market** — Jean-Claude sold 3 whip shark eggs (300gp as 30pt). Merchant noted fertilization reduces value (culinary delicacy, not breeding stock).
2. **Casa Lupo** — Jean-Claude bought +1 silent shortbow (50gp) and boots of flying (125gp, intended for a party member).
3. **Le Paludi streets** — Jean-Claude met Kyzil. Kyzil: "Your kind isn't from here. Why are you here?" Dismissed him as no threat. Crissdalynn vouched. Delmar bear-hugged Kyzil.
4. **Ponte Bassa** — Party established tavern as base. Kyzil discussed Crissdalynn's pilgrimage: gave her the hardest in Eyrie history because he knew she could do it. Maw has been unruly since the wreck. Kyzil senses something foul from the south.
5. **Ponte Bassa rooftop** — Boots gifted; Perrin → Delmar (retcon: Rattkin don't wear shoes). Kyzil challenged spar.
6. **Kyzil spar (Rounds 1–3)** — Crissdalynn grappled Kyzil R1 (nat 1 contest). Delmar joined with Tavern Brawler chair throw. Kyzil's Downburst broke grapple R2 (Crissdalynn saved first via bardic inspiration, failed second). R3: Kyzil rescued Crissdalynn from falling off roof, then attacked all four — pinned Jean-Claude's shirt as dominance display. Delmar hit through Empty Wing Parry via bardic inspiration (17 damage). 3 rounds, stat block held as designed.
7. **Walking to harbor** — Delmar confessed: real admiral, five ships, Chain Council privateers, Pearl of Souls theft, Maw destroyed fleet. Water tracks him when he steps off land.
8. **Calveno harbor shrine** — Umberlee possessed Branca. Demanded Pearl + Delmar's death. Delmar lied; Umberlee saw through it, killed and resurrected Branca. Revised deal: Pearl for Delmar's life. Delmar counter-offered: Pearl + the commissioners. Umberlee deferred.
9. **Ponte Bassa morning** — Crissdalynn leg-swept Delmar for meeting Umberlee without her. Nona's sending stone: "Bring your friends, especially the blue one." Festival registration opened; party signed up.
10. **Nona's safe house** — Nona briefed: 6 Grung sightings in Warren tunnels/sewers. Blue, green, one purple. Unprecedented — no Grung has crossed the Central Strait. Rattkin witness described purple Grung in market district. Hero's Feast from Nona's cooking.
11. **Calveno sewers** — Jean-Claude on ceiling (Gloom Stalker), Perrin invisible, Delmar traps, Crissdalynn rear. Found Grung directional markers ("500m →"), triggered crossbow trap (miss). Black powder scent increasing. Exited at Le Paludi coast.
12. **Le Paludi coast** — Perrin scouted boat: 3 Grung, 8 barrels of black powder. Jean-Claude ambushed 2 patrol Grung from ceiling (24 damage instant kill via Dread Strike). Ship exploded (fire arrow to powder). Crissdalynn kingfishered the purple Grung at 45ft fly speed. Jean-Claude executed it after it called him a traitor.
13. **Sewer exit interrogation** — Felix Aho (green Grung laborer) revealed the full operation: 4 attack points, scaffolding against ceilings, 2-day deadline, purple garrisons + red leaders, 6 ships total. Defectors exist — kill on sight. Felix wants 1 month protection.
14. **Nona's safe house** — Felix delivered; Ruck guards him. Festival cover made transport easy. Nona: "I knew I chose the right people."

## Rules and Rulings

- **Silent shortbow:** +1 to hit/damage, no sound on draw or fire, does not reveal position from hidden. No attunement. DM confirmed.
- **Boots of flying:** uncommon, attunement, 4hr flight, regains 2hr per 12hr not in use.
- **Grappled condition:** zero movement, no disadvantage on attacks. Grappler feat gives advantage on grappled target.
- **Deflect attacks (Crissdalynn):** reduce damage; if reduced to 0, spend 1 focus point to redirect — DC 13 Dex or 2d6+4 damage.
- **Tavern Brawler:** proficiency with improvised weapons, 1d4 + Str.
- **Bardic inspiration retroactive:** DM allowed as reaction during spar.
- **Gloom Stalker:** invisible in complete darkness to darkvision-reliant creatures. Confirmed.
- **Hero's Feast (Nona's cooking):** cured diseases/poison, immune to poison and frightened, Wis saves advantage, +2d10 max HP for 24 hours.
- **Umberlee's divine insight:** trumps Deception regardless of roll.
