## Strong Start — The Stone Speaks

> [!read-aloud]
> La Scelta morning. Calveno is putting its face on — registration desks on the Mercatura bridge, mask vendors arguing about booth placement, festival cloth going up on every facade the light can reach. The canal doubles it all and makes it prettier than it is. Then the stone in Perrin's pocket warms. Nona's voice, close as a whisper, no greeting: "Come back. Bring the blue one."

Then stop. Perrin has the stone. The table has a direction. If the party wants breakfast first, fine — the stone doesn't expire. But Nona is done asking politely.

> [!dm]
> The stone is a one-sentence channel. Nona will not explain via stone — she needs Perrin at her table. "The blue one" is [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. If other PCs come, that's fine — Nona feeds everyone. But the favor names JC by sight.

### Warren & Nona — The Favor Named

*Pull: Perrin (family), JC (named by sight) · Register: social*

The stone brought them here. The Warren kitchen under Le Paludi — warm, close, garlic and bread. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] sits at the head.

**NPC — Nona Black-Jaw** ([[nona-black-jaw|page]]): Feeds you first, names what you owe second — as accounting, not threat. She knows the [[vestra|Vestra]] is gone (grieving the men and the money while planning the response). Her relief at seeing Perrin is held, not performed.

> [!read-aloud]
> The Warren kitchen is underground, candlelit, and smells of garlic and fresh bread. Nona sits at the head of a table that could seat twelve, a bowl of soup in front of her. She does not stand. "Sit. Eat. Then we talk."

She feeds them. She asks after the voyage. She lets silence do its work. Then —

> [!dm]
> **Nona's favor (canon).** Word reached her that Perrin arrived with a **blue Grung in a beret and moustache**. She reads it as luck. She's had reports she'd normally dismiss — **Grung moving in the old Warren tunnels** that connect to the Calveno sewers — and wants them confirmed or dismissed. Who better to hunt Grung than a Grung. **The ask:** send JC (with whoever he wants) into the tunnels to confirm. This is the door into the [[calveno-beffa-grung-raid|raid]] — those sewer Grung are Simone's extraction crews.

**Savia intro:** after the favor is named, Nona drops one line unprompted. "Your ship reads wrong. I know a woman at the Slip — Savia. Tell her I sent you." This wires the ship thread through Nona; the party doesn't have to deduce the connection.

**If engaged:** JC has the tunnel job. The raid thread opens from below. Savia intro wires the ship repair.
**If ignored:** Nona waits one more day, then sends a runner. She does not chase; she escalates quietly.

#### The Rattle — Demonstration

If the party is inside the Warren during the visit, a drill triggers — a Dravosi factor's runner is spotted at the Le Paludi canal edge.

> [!read-aloud]
> A pan strikes somewhere behind the kitchen wall. One beat, two, three — a rhythm, not music. Before the echo dies another pan picks it up, farther away, deeper in the stone. Then another. Within the space of a held breath the sound is everywhere — rising through vents, ringing off pipe, carried by hands you cannot see. The kitchen stops. Nona sets down her spoon.

> [!read-aloud]
> Movement. Not panic — purpose. A Rattkin woman in a flour-dusted apron crosses behind Nona and pulls a short blade from behind a cutting board. An old Rattkin by the door slides a bar into a bracket that was not visible a moment ago. A child darts past Perrin's ankles and vanishes into a gap in the wall no wider than a fist. The whole Warren is moving, and it has been moving since the first pan struck.

Two quick taps. The sound dies. Blade behind the cutting board. Bar off the door. Child reappears from a different gap. Nona picks up her spoon. "Thirty seconds. We have been doing this since before your mother was born."

> [!dm]
> This is Nona showing Perrin what the Warren is and what she built. Thirty seconds to full lockdown. The cost: the party now knows the alarm system. Nona trusts Perrin enough to show him. She trusts his crew because he brought them.

### The Shrine — Pearl Summons

*Pull: Delmar (primary), Crissdalynn (was there when Branca found him) · Register: revelation / horror*

This is a **fixed dramatic beat** — fixed because Umberlee acts, not the PCs. The offer is unacceptable by design; the refusal is expected; the point is the demonstration. Run it as scripted and let the silence between boxes work.

#### Arrival

> [!read-aloud]
> The [[waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] is a low, tide-stained vault off a Mercatura canal. The font behind the bench holds a hand's depth of seawater that will not lie flat — it rises and falls, slow, like something breathing under it. [[branca|Branca]] is exactly where she sat down: upright, awake, salt-white hair pulled back hard, the little leather tide-table booklet open on her knee. "So," she says, bright and clipped, the warmth of someone rescheduling a missed meeting. "You came. Good. We have an appointment."

**NPC — Branca** ([[branca|page]]): corporate cult executive assistant — bubbly, efficient, too much eye contact. Opens "So!", closes "Does that work for you?", checks the booklet mid-sentence as if it settles every objection. She believes making divine horror *pleasant* is a kindness. She is the soft instrument; what comes through her is not.

#### The Possession

Branca stops mid-sentence. Her eyes roll back to white. The voice is not hers.

> [!read-aloud]
> Branca's eyes roll white. When she speaks it is not her voice — it is the sea given a throat, cold and enormous and patient. "FISK. You carry what is Mine. Go down to the wreck. Bring Me the Pearl. Return it to My shrine — and I will grant you the one mercy you have earned: a swift death, and the souls you stole brought home to Me. This is My grace. Accept it."

> [!dm]
> **The demand (plainly):** retrieve the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] wreck at the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] and return it to a shrine of Umberlee. The "reward" is Delmar's *own death* — a death sentence dressed as salvation. This brushes Delmar's secret. The party hears he **carries souls Umberlee claims**. They need not learn the five captains or Vel Orn; keep Her words to "what is Mine" and "the souls you stole."

#### The Wrath

The instant Delmar refuses (or hesitates, or tries to negotiate):

> [!read-aloud]
> The refusal is barely out of your mouth before the light goes wrong. Thunder walks across a sky that was clear a breath ago; the canal shivers in its channel. The thing wearing Branca *roars* — and then, almost offhand, it takes her head in her own two hands and snaps her neck. The body folds. The shrine goes silent. Branca lies on the wet stone, not breathing.

Hold the silence.

#### The Return

> [!read-aloud]
> Mist gathers off the canal — too fast, too deliberate — and curls in around the fallen woman like a tide coming home. Branca's chest heaves. She drags in one enormous, gasping breath, sits up, blinks the white from her eyes, finds the booklet, smooths her robe, and looks up with that same bright warmth, as if nothing happened. "I hope," she says, "you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace."

> [!check] Insight — After the Revival
> DC 12 (no advantage from prior knowledge; this is emotional, not logical).
> Fail: reads as shock; the depth doesn't register.
> Success: Branca means it completely — she experienced her own murder as a privilege. *That* is the horror.
> Crit success: Branca has been through this before. Not once. Her faith is forged, not learned.

**The bind:** the only way to satisfy the goddess is to die for her. Delmar needs a third answer — and the Pearl she wants is inside the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] wreck, exactly where the salvage commission is racing. Demand and money pull at the same wreck from opposite directions.

**If engaged:** the demand hangs. Delmar knows about the Pearl and what Umberlee wants.
**If Delmar doesn't go to the shrine:** Branca waits. By Day 3, locals have noticed — the Waveservant woman who won't leave. The shrine becomes a public curiosity that reaches the party as rumour.

### Handler Recognition — Private Beat

*Pull: Jean-Claude (only) · Register: quiet tension*

> [!dm]
> When JC enters any crowd scene — Mercatura, Bridge, Velo square — slide his player a written note or whisper the text below. Do **not** announce to the table. If his player doesn't bite, don't push — mark the lane as confirmed and move on. The beat fires once; repeating it turns a private moment into a quest marker.

> [!read-aloud]
> A blue-caste Grung stands at the edge of a Velo square, watching a street performer. He is not enjoying it. He is watching how the crowd *moves* when the performer makes a scene — where people turn, where the gaps open, how long a knot of onlookers takes to dissolve.

**The tell:** the Grung stands in the one position that gives sightlines to three exits without appearing to watch any. Jean-Claude has stood in that position. It is trained, not instinctive.

**If JC acts:** the handler is polite, says he's here for the festival. Karath accent, not Veth. No trade goods, no festival registration, no declared purpose. If JC pushes harder, the Grung excuses himself and leaves — quickly, efficiently, without running. He files a report. JC's face enters the handler network.

**If JC reports to the party:** one confirmed handler sighting. No proof, no combat. Just dread that Jean-Claude can name and no one else can.
**If ignored:** the handler completes his route assessment. One more lane is locked for the raid.

### Beffa Registration *(optional — the most fun)*

*Pull: any · Register: comedy / social*

> [!read-aloud]
> The Mercatura bridge has become a registration desk. A woman with ink-stained fingers and the patience of a saint sits behind a ledger that already has three crossed-out team names on this page alone. Behind her, someone's mask has fallen off mid-argument and rolled under the desk. She retrieves it without looking up. "Team name, mark, category, stake. If you say 'The Masked Avengers' I am crossing you out before you finish."

**The toy:** if they register, get the table choosing — team name, mark, category, symbol, stake. Standard entry 25 gp; Legendary 50 gp. Scoring: Craft 10, Surprise 5, Recovery 5, out of 20. Loud, dumb, theirs.

**[[giacomo-moretti|Giacomo Moretti]]** has signed the Accord for twenty years and has never been pranked. The committee finds this professionally embarrassing. He is listed as a valid mark.

**If ignored:** the Beffa runs without them. Festival texture, five-day clock. The mask vendor still sells them things.

### The Tunnels — Nona's Favor Fulfilled *(Day 2 or 3)*

*Pull: Jean-Claude (primary, only he can read it) · Register: exploration → possible combat*

If JC took the tunnel job, the Warren provides a rough map and a Le Paludi entry point. Two paths in: **(a)** via the Warren tunnels with Nona's blessing, or **(b)** tailing a handler from the street to a sewer hatch.

> [!read-aloud]
> The Warren tunnels start dry and narrow — old smuggling corridors under Le Paludi, stone walls black with decades of torch soot. The air turns damp after the first junction. The sound changes. Water is running somewhere ahead, and the walls show fresh scrape marks at knee height — something has been dragged through here recently.

**What JC finds below** (he reads these automatically — no roll; he ran this kind of operation):

- **Scrape marks** on tunnel walls at knee height — bound captives moved at speed.
- **A sealed hatch** between the Warren tunnels and the Mercatura sewer collector — recently re-seated, clean bolts, professional tools.
- **Grung sign** — a direction marker scratched into the hatch frame in Botukuri tunnel protocol. It points toward the outer quay.
- **Salt water** — the wet stone smells wrong. Someone has been carrying seawater into a freshwater tunnel system.

> [!check] Investigation — Reading the Tunnel Signs (non-JC party members)
> DC 12.
> Fail: the scrapes and the sealed hatch are odd but unreadable.
> Success: recently prepared extraction route, professionally sealed, heading toward outer water.
> Crit success: the direction marker is blue-caste handler protocol, not general Grung — a military operation, not smuggling.

> [!dm]
> If JC goes deep enough, he finds the egress — a maintenance vent onto the outer quay below the waterline. Short swim to open water. From open water, the *vethka* at anchor past the patrol line. Each piece is separately unremarkable. Together: a purpose-built capture pipeline. Let JC assemble it — do not narrate the conclusion for him.

**If JC reports to the Warren:** Nona's favor is fulfilled. The Warren can seal one or two tunnel access points. Each sealed point removes one of the raid's five extraction lanes.
**If JC reports to the party:** they now know Grung are running an organized operation through Calveno's sewers during the festival.
**If ignored:** tunnels confirmed and clear. Raid proceeds at full capacity.

### Red Lady — First Threads

*Pull: Delmar, Crissdalynn · Register: investigation / social*

Two ways this surfaces:

**Through city gossip** (anyone asking about salvage, the Maw, or unusual money):

> [!read-aloud]
> A chandler lowers his voice without being asked. "You're not the first to ask about Maw salvage this month. There's money moving. Serious money — three buyers, or one buyer with three hands, nobody's sure. And they're not advertising a target."

**Through Kyzil** (Crissdalynn only): if she pulls on Red Lady traces with [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]] present, he sets one phrase against the rumours. *"A foul wind from the south. And now someone is paying real money to go look at what it left behind."* One sentence. Then he watches her face to see if she's ready for the rest.

> [!dm]
> Don't force the Kyzil reveal. He gives one guarded sentence when she pulls the thread, not before. His protection is timing, not permanent silence.

## Spotlight

Crissdalynn got heavy spotlight in Session 04 (Kyzil reunion, spar). This session:

- **Delmar:** The Pearl Summons is his defining beat. The horror is personal — Umberlee knows his name, knows what he carries. Give this scene full table attention and silence.
- **Jean-Claude:** Two threads converge on him. The handler recognition beat is private and easy to miss — slide the note, don't announce. Nona's favor names him by sight. The tunnel descent is his choice. Watch for JC falling behind — his threads are internal.
- **Perrin:** Nona's favor opens through him. The family pressure is real — he agreed to something without terms and the bill is arriving. The Rattle demonstration is for him specifically.
- **Crissdalynn:** Kyzil is present and holding the "foul wind" thread. Attach it to the Red Lady money if she pulls on salvage or Maw rumours. Don't force a beat — she just had the session.

## Secrets & Clues

Unassigned — give each to the first action that earns it.

- A Calveno repaint won't erase a Crown cutter's silhouette — via **Savia** (flatly, after a Warren intro), **a dock regular** (eyes the hull and shakes his head), or **a failed papers check**.
- The font at the Waveservant Shrine won't go still — via **anyone who enters the shrine** (the water moves); a public omen if Delmar stays away long enough for locals to notice.
- Unusual broker money is building a Maw salvage job — via **a chandler** (lowers voice unprompted), **Savia** (names "Zusto" once), or **canal gossip**.
- A Beffa team registered under an unrecognized name asked the clerk how many people fit through the bridge pinch-point in a minute — via **anyone who asks about odd teams** or **the clerk herself** during registration.

**Raid-adjacent — only if the party looks in the right direction:**

- The Mercatura sewer collector has three access hatches; **two were re-seated this week**, no Commission record.
- Three dark-hulled, double-outrigged boats sit at anchor past the patrol line, no pennant. Anyone with Grung or Midchain maritime knowledge identifies them as *vethka*.

## If They Surprise You

When the party goes somewhere you didn't prep:

1. **Place the request.** Public deal → Mercatura or the Bridge. Quiet practical work → Le Paludi. Vice, music, strange trade → Velo. Official ship business → Arsenal edge. ([[calveno-districts|District map]]; [[calveno-improv|names & voices]].)
2. **Give one competent local** who wants one small thing now.
3. **Attach one live pressure** from the Threads table.
4. **Let the choice stand.** Note what changed, tick the clock, move on. Don't repeat a hook through a second NPC until they comply.

## Possible Cliffhanger

Don't plan an ending — recognize the highest-tension stopping point if it arrives:

- **Pearl Summons aftermath:** Branca gasping back to life. "I hope you understand my lady Umberlee's power. And her grace." The demand hangs over every Maw decision.
- **Tunnel discovery:** JC underground, assembling the extraction chain piece by piece. The city is sitting on a purpose-built capture pipeline and doesn't know it.
- **Handler cell:** JC confronts the handlers (if Day 3 reached). End on what the handler carries — binding rope, Botukuri shipping label, and a report about Jean-Claude's face. → Full combat: [[session-04-day-4#Handler Cell — Combat|Day 4]]
- **Nona's favor fulfilled:** JC has confirmed the Grung. Nona seals the tunnels. The Warren knows. End on what the Warren decides to do about it — and on the *vethka* sitting outside the patrol line, patient and dark.
