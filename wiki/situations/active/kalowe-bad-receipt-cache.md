# Kalowe — The Bad Receipt's Cache

## Situation

Two seasons ago, [[the-bad-receipt|the Bad Receipt]] took a prize in the Central Strait and offloaded nine crates on a nameless limestone shelf 3 miles east-southeast of the Kalowe gap while the heat died down. The pilot who led them there died four months later of fever in Veth. Captain Orina Fale has been operating without being able to collect the cache for eight months — nobody else aboard knew the exact rock.

A fisherman named Damu saw them anchor there.

He has been nursing an ale at [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]] for three days, working up to selling the location. [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]] has noticed but says nothing.

The Bad Receipt sailed into Kalowe this morning. Fale is on the second causeway with two crew, quietly asking harbour workers about the dead pilot's last job.

## Current State

**Damu** is at The Flat Water, second table from the terrace, visibly nervous. If the party looks capable and unaffiliated with any crew Reweti has reason to distrust, she will catch their eye and nod toward him. He will sell the rock's precise location — including a traced copy of his chart — for 50 gp. He wants coin, not a cut.

**Orina Fale** is not offering money yet. She will.

## The Tidal Clock

- **Move that day:** 12-hour head start on the Bad Receipt; easy pickup
- **Wait until morning:** Fale has narrowed it to the eastern approaches; she arrives 4 hours after the party
- **Wait two days:** Fale finds the rock herself; cache is gone

## The Rock

Flat limestone shelf, 60 ft wide, barely above waterline at mean tide. Single wind-bent palm; notch on eastern face where the Bad Receipt tied off. The nine crates are on the eastern face behind a natural rock shelf — covered with salvaged canvas and weighted stones. No guardians, no traps. Anyone who lands and looks will find them in 5 minutes.

## Cargo

| Crates | Contents | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Spice (pepper, nutmeg, clove) | 300 gp wholesale |
| 3 | Undyed trade cloth | 200 gp wholesale |
| 3 (sealed) | Silver plate tableware, Dravosi merchant mark | 300–350 gp |
| **Gross** | | **800 gp** |

*One of the sealed silver plate crates contains an engraved service with a noble house seal — worth 200 gp to the right buyer, or trouble if the wrong people recognise it.*

**Selling options:**
- [[taufa-fifita-and-sons|Taufa Fifita & Sons]]: 720 gp (10% cut, no questions, 2-day wait)
- Harbour direct: 680 gp (faster, fewer buyers)
- Silver plate sold elsewhere: 350 gp for those 3 crates at any non-Dravosi port

## If the Bad Receipt Arrives During Loading

Fale carries 8 crew + a swivel gun (1d10 bludgeoning, 1/round). She will not fire — she wants the cargo undamaged. She will hail and demand answers. She is a businesswoman, not a murderer; violence is expensive. If the party is armed and already has most of the cargo, she will negotiate.

*Negotiated split:* She trades the spice and cloth for the silver plate + 100 gp. Party nets ~500 gp + she respects them.
*Party refuses:* She leaves, makes a note, remembers.
*Party fights at sea:* Eight crew + swivel gun vs. the party on an open rock with no cover.

## Orina Fale

Captain of the Bad Receipt. Midchain mixed heritage, forties, sharp-featured, always dressed well for a pirate. Runs on business logic. Respects people who make sensible decisions under pressure.

## Consequences

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Party moves fast | 670 gp net; no confrontation; Damu is fine |
| Fale arrives during loading | Negotiated split; ~500 gp; Fale respects them |
| Party waits two days | Cache gone; Damu got paid for nothing |
| Party sells Damu's info to Fale | Fale pays 80 gp; Damu learns within a week and doesn't forget |
