# Kalowe — The Captain's Map

## Situation

[[sera-maddock|Sera Maddock]], 71, three days from dead by [[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]]'s estimate, is in the dispensary's back room on a charitable arrangement neither discusses out loud. She was a privateer on Dravosi-licensed runs forty years ago. Her crew buried a cache on [[orak|Orak]] and she is the last survivor who knows where.

Half the chart is tattooed on her left forearm — eastern face of Orak, compass bearing 087° from the southeastern rock stack, cache depth in fathoms. She burned the other half when the man she was going to share it with tried to sell her to the Crown.

**Her deal:** 200 gp and passage out of Kalowe (not to Tidefall — she wants a healer city). The tattoo half is enough to find it.

## Current State

Sera has been in the dispensary four days. Grimaldi mentioned the tattoo to the party unprompted (unusual forearm tattoo, patient in the back). She will arrange an introduction only if she thinks the party will honour the deal.

**Sera's clock:** lung sickness — treatable in Tidefall, not here. She is not in pain but is slow and short of breath and aware she is on a clock.

## What Sera Will Tell

- Cache is at bearing 087° from the rock stack at Orak's southeastern tip — eastern face, sea cave entrance
- Entrance is at waterline at mid-tide; walkable at low tide; 8 feet under at high tide
- Cache is 6 feet under a flat stone, 40 feet into the cave interior
- There is "some kind of animal" in the cave — she hasn't been there in 40 years

## What Sera Won't Say

- Full cache: 800 gp old Imperial coin (collector value 1,000 gp to [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Algernon Clyde]]) + matched silver earrings and bracelet, 180 gp
- The cave floods to the ceiling at full high tide — 3-hour window around low tide
- The cave animal was, 40 years ago, a giant constrictor snake; she assumes it's dead

## Orak: The Cave

- 2-hour sail east-southeast of Kalowe; small, low, overgrown; paired with [[dreth|Dreth]] across a narrow channel
- Entrance: 60 ft deep, 20 ft wide, narrows to 10 ft inside
- High tide floods the cave to the ceiling — water rises 1 foot every 10 minutes; at 2 hours the entrance is waist-deep; at 3 hours, neck-deep
- The dig: 20 minutes with tools, 40 without; flat stone 40 ft in, 2×3 ft, flush, unmarked

### Encounter 1: Giant Crabs ×4 (CR 1/8)

Tidal zone colonists. Scatter from torchlight but will attack if the party blocks the exit. HP 13, AC 15, Claw +3 / 1d6+1 bludgeoning. Nuisance, not threat.

### Encounter 2: Giant Constrictor Snake ×1 (CR 2)

Interior past the 30 ft mark. Not aggressive unless cornered or the party makes noise near its nest. HP 60, AC 12, Bite +5 / 2d6+4 piercing + grapple, Constrict +5 / 2d8+4 bludgeoning + restrained (escape DC 16).

*Torch behaviour:* DC 11 Animal Handling to keep it at bay while backing away; on failure it strikes.

## Consequences

- **Honour the deal:** 780–980 gp net; Sera gets passage and treatment; Grimaldi satisfied; Clyde delighted if in port
- **Betray Sera (take info, abandon her):** Grimaldi will not deal with them again; harbour bosses hear within a day
- **Go to Orak without Sera's bearing:** Seven cave entrances on the eastern face — no bearing, no cache

## Triggers

- Party buys supplies from Grimaldi and she mentions the patient with the tattoo (already done)
- Party follows up on the patient; Grimaldi arranges introduction if she trusts them
- Party asks around Kalowe about old privateers or eastern reef islands

## Open Questions

- Does the party take Sera anywhere, or pay her and leave her?
- Does Algernon Clyde buy the coin? He'll be excited about the provenance.
- The party is currently in Calveno (5-day dry dock). Sera has three days. Does the window close before they return?
