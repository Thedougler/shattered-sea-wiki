# Kalowe — The Drifting Hulk

## Situation

The *Pale Covenant*, a two-masted trading schooner, has been drifting 4 miles north-northeast of the Kalowe gap for two nights. No lights. No answer to hails. Under Midchain free-port custom, the first boarding party to establish presence and bring cargo to port holds salvage rights — the harbour bosses will record the claim and take 10%.

The *Pale Covenant*'s captain, Orlen Vass, died still owing eighteen months of unpaid [[umberlee|Umberlee]] tithe — a deliberate public snub. The entire crew of nine went down with him. Their shadows and his specter now inhabit the ship and will not leave it.

## Current State

Word reached the causeway by the morning market. Two other crews in Kalowe are aware of it and considering whether to try. The party has a narrow window — probably until the next morning.

**What [[suryo-wibowo|Suryo Wibowo]] knows:** The salt merchant who spotted her came through for provisions yesterday. Green stripe, ochre hull, open hatch amidships. Smelled strange even from a distance — old copper and wet stone. He did not board.

**What the [[kalowe-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant shrine]] knows:** Brother Tamoa recognises the *Pale Covenant* from the ledger — Orlen Vass, tithe balance outstanding. He will tell the party the tithe status if they describe the colours. He can also perform a consecration ritual (10gp incense + saltwater into the basin) that suppresses the specters for 1 hour from boarding — enough to load the hold.

**What the party doesn't know:**

- The crew were killed by specters and their own undead shadows within hours of Vass dying
- The captain's specter is anchored to the wheel and cannot move from the helm deck
- Permanent resolution: pay the tithe posthumously, or destroy the wheel (AC 15, 20hp; the specter fights to prevent this)

## Key Facts

- Cargo: mixed spice, undyed cloth, 3 sealed crates of silver plate tableware — ~1,000gp total
- After 10% harbour fee + 10% [[taufa-fifita-and-sons|Taufa Fifita & Sons]] cut: ~810gp net
- Ship herself is intact and seaworthy — 60-ton trading schooner, prize value ~4,000gp; fast-sale in Kalowe: ~2,400gp
- The tithe shortcut: 54gp covers the outstanding 18 months; any Waveservant can receive a posthumous payment

## DM Notes

**Aboard the *Pale Covenant*:**

- Below decks: **4 Shadows (CR 1/2)** — haunt the cargo hold and crew quarters; emerge on any light source entering the hold. HP 16, AC 12, Strength Drain (hit + DC 13 Con save or -1d4 Str; 0 Str = new shadow). Resist most damage; immune to exhaustion/frightened/grappled; vulnerable to radiant. In dim light they attack with advantage — *the hold is dim.*
- Helm deck: **1 Specter (CR 1)** (Captain Orlen Vass) — anchored to the wheel, cannot be driven off, only destroyed or appeased. HP 22, AC 12, Life Drain +4 / 3d6 necrotic (DC 13 Con or max HP reduced until long rest). Says nothing except *"Pay what you owe"* on repeat, directed at nobody visible.

*If the party brought torches:* the hold becomes bright; shadows lose advantage and attack immediately rather than lurking.

**The tithe shortcut:** 54gp, handed to Tamoa at the shrine. Once paid, all specters dissolve without a fight; shadows dissipate 1d6 rounds later, one at a time. Cleanest resolution. Tamoa remembers the favour — future shrine services at half price.

**The consecration shortcut:** 10gp ritual from Tamoa before boarding. Suppresses undead for 1 hour. Tight for loading cargo but doable.

**Captain's cabin (unlocked):** logbook showing the tithe dispute, silver compass worth 120gp (engraved *OV*), letter from a factor in Tidefall expecting the silver plate delivery.
