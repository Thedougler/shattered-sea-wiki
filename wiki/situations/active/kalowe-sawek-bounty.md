# Kalowe — The Sawek Bounty

## Situation

A [[sawek|Sawek]] has settled into the blue hole at the base of the channel between Kalowe's second and third limestone islands — the same passage inter-island skiffs use at night. Three fishing boats have been hit in the last two weeks. One man is dead. The harbour bosses are offering 300gp for proof of kill: the distinctive bony ridge-plate from the creature's forehead.

## Current State

The Sawek is well-fed and territorial. It attacked the first boat that passed over its hole and now treats the channel as its hunting ground. Fishermen have stopped using it; dry-dock workers are adding an hour to every material run between the second and third islands.

**[[kettil-ormsson|Kettil Ormsson]] at [[ormssons-fittings|Ormsson's Fittings]]** has a cracked keel plank from one of the damaged boats propped outside — repairing it as a favour. Will mention the Sawek without drama if asked about local trouble.

**[[ilaria-grimaldi|Ilaria Grimaldi]] at [[grimaldis-dispensary|Grimaldi's Dispensary]]** treated the survivor from the fatal attack. She can describe the attack pattern: the Sawek came up from directly below, seized the hull with its tentacles, pulled the boat partially under, then lost interest when it broke apart. The man who died went into the water.

**The harbour bosses** pay on sight of the ridge-plate. No witnesses required.

## Key Facts

- The blue hole is 20 feet deep at the base; the channel above is 40 feet wide, 15 feet deep at high water
- The Sawek patrols the channel 2–3 hours after dark, returns to the hole after 1–2 hours
- Its lair has accumulated debris: coin, gear, and one specific piece of cargo it has been sitting on
- Ridge-plate: wedge of dense bone, cream-coloured, about 18 inches across — harvested by removing the top of the skull post-mortem (10 minutes, cutting tool)

## DM Notes

**[[sawek|The Sawek (CR 5)]]:** HP 95, AC 14 (natural), swim 40ft, land 5ft. Tentacle attacks grapple on hit (escape DC 14). Ink cloud reaction (20ft heavily obscured, recharge on surfacing). Sunlight sensitivity — disadvantage on attacks and Perception in direct sunlight.

*Don't fight it underwater.* In its hole: 40ft swim speed, cover from channel walls, ink cloud makes retreat lethal. The party should surface the fight.

**The bait approach (recommended):** [[wibowos-provisions|Wibowo's Provisions]] sells offal — 1sp creates a blood trail. The Sawek investigates in 1d6+2 rounds. Fight at the waterline: half its body above water, half below. It can still grab the boat (grapple on hit, DC 14 Str/round to break free; failure = pulled 10ft toward the hole).

**The trap approach:** The channel narrows to 20 feet 60 yards north of the blue hole. Chain across at depth (available from Ormsson's Fittings, 5sp/ft) fouled against both walls — Sawek makes DC 13 Dex save or tentacles restrained (no tentacle attacks; uses action to attempt DC 16 Str, 2 tries/round). Removes its grapple attack.

**The lair (post-kill):** Visibility nil without light. Floor holds:

- 6 weeks of accumulated debris from passing boats
- A sealed iron box it sank two weeks before settling here — contains dry-stored pilot charts for the eastern reef channels (60gp standard; 90gp to [[algernon-reginald-clyde|Algernon Reginald Clyde]] if he's in port)
- 185gp in mixed coin, gritty with silt
- A gold chain with a Midchain trading house seal pendant — belongs to a merchant missing since last season; his family in Veth would pay 50gp reward (nobody in Kalowe knows whose it is without investigation)

## Triggers

- Party asks Kettil about what happened to the boat outside his shop
- Party asks Grimaldi about recent injuries
- Party asks at harbour offices about local hazards or open contracts

## Consequences

- **Party kills it:** 735gp total, harbour bosses grateful, inter-island channel reopens — dry-dock crew will remember this
- **Party avoids it:** The Sawek kills again within two weeks; harbour bosses quietly double the bounty to 500gp
- **Party dragged under:** Drowning rules apply; the Sawek drags grappled creatures to the blue hole floor (20ft/round while failing to escape)

## Open Questions

- Does the party return the merchant's gold chain to Veth for the reward, or sell it?
- Does anyone want the remaining body parts? (Teeth, ink sacs, tentacle segments: 40gp to an alchemist)
