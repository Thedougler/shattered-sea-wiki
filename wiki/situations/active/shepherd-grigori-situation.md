# Shepherd Grigori — The Cold Handshake

## What the Party Knows

[[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] traveled aboard the *[[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* as a cooperative guest. He healed [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s wounds with red, viscous, gaseous light that flowed from his wrist — no verbal, no somatic, no material components. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s Arcana check (18) could not identify the magic. It just flowed.

At [[la-vasca|La Vasca]], he shook [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s hand goodbye. It was icy cold — like touching a dead body.

He said he had business in [[calveno|Calveno]]. He mentioned "Impuni" — someone he needs to reach in time. He expects to be done in one to two weeks. He walked into the city alone.

## What the Party Does Not Know

Grigori is an undead sorcerer-lich and shepherd of the [[khlysty|Khlysty]] — a secret sect that threads through Dravosi and Tessarine noble courts. When he heals someone, he binds a thread of his phylactery into their life. As long as they live, he cannot be permanently destroyed. The cure is real. The patient genuinely recovers. They never know they have been bound.

His Calveno business: a hemophiliac [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] heir whose physicians gave up. The cure is now complete. The heir is recovering. Grigori has departed without asking for anything. The network expanded by one.

He considers himself a benefactor. He is not performing warmth — CHA 21 is real.

## Clues the Party Can Find

The trail exists only if the party looks. Nothing forces discovery.

**Servant rumours.** Within a day of Grigori's departure, Tessarine household servants talk: a man came after dark, was with the patient for twenty minutes, left without asking for anything. He smelled like wine and something else. Someone says he was singing under his breath the whole time. The family is not publicizing this.

**The cold handshake.** Delmar felt it. Medicine or Nature DC 15 confirms: that was not living body temperature. No illness produces cold like that.

**The unidentifiable magic.** Red viscous light, no components. Arcana DC 22 (higher than the party's failed 18) to recognize it as necrotic energy reflavoured through a sorcerous origin — the caster's body is the focus. Below DC 22, the magic remains unclassifiable.

> [!check] Arcana — Identify Grigori's healing magic
> DC 18 already failed (Session 03). DC 22 with new evidence or context (seeing a second Khlysty healing, finding a text describing the method).
> Fail: the magic has no school signature the caster recognizes. Genuinely unclassifiable.
> Success: necrotic energy channelled through the caster's own body as a living (or unliving) focus. The red light is blood-aspected. No known divine or arcane tradition does this.
> Crit success: the caster is the component. Whatever Grigori is, his body is the spell focus — which means his body is not normal.

> [!check] Medicine/Nature — The cold handshake
> DC 15. Delmar experienced it directly; advantage if he is the one rolling.
> Fail: cold hands. Could be anything.
> Success: that was not living body temperature. No circulation-based illness produces uniform cold like that. The hand felt like preserved tissue.
> Crit success: Delmar recalls exactly — not clammy, not feverish-cold. Dry, even, and the same temperature throughout. Like meat that has been sitting in a cellar.

## Pressures

- **Grigori's timeline.** He said one to two weeks. If the party is still in Calveno when that window closes, he may reappear — or he may already be gone. The party does not control this.
- **The Tessarine connection.** The healed heir's family is grateful and powerful. If the party investigates the rumours, they will find a family that considers Grigori a saint. Contradicting that assessment will not be welcome.
- **Pattern recognition (future).** Grigori has done this across multiple courts. Other healed-noble NPCs exist in the world. The Calveno heir is the nearest data point, but not the only one.

## If the Party Ignores This

Nothing bad happens to them in the short term. Grigori liked the party. He is not hostile. He moves on.

The [[khlysty|Khlysty]] network expands. Each new heir makes Grigori harder to permanently kill. If the party encounters him again in 5, 10, or 20 sessions, the network is larger and the moral problem is worse — more living children whose continued health depends on the phylactery bond remaining intact.

The thread goes dormant. It resurfaces naturally when the party meets a noble heir who survived something unsurvivable, when someone mentions a wandering healer matching Grigori's description, or when Grigori contacts them because he needs something.

> [!dm]
> Let this thread sleep. Do not force it. The horror compounds with time — a Grigori reveal at level 10 hits harder than at level 4 because more healed children are in play and the party has met some of them. Seed miraculous recoveries into noble courts the party visits. Never label them as Khlysty until the party connects the pattern themselves.

## Running This at the Table

> [!dm]
> Play Grigori's absence as unremarkable. He said he had business. He left. If no one asks about him, the session moves on. Do not prompt the party to investigate — if they care, they will ask.

> [!dm]
> If the party investigates: the Tessarine household is findable. The servants talk. The family does not. The heir is recovering and is not available for visitors. Grigori left no forwarding address. The trail ends at the household door unless the party pushes hard.

> [!mechanic]
> Grigori is CR 19. The party is level 4. If they track him down and start a fight, he will try to leave. If cornered, this is a death scenario. He does not want to kill them — he liked them. He will use dominate person and mass suggestion to end the encounter without casualties. If forced to kill, he considers it a waste.

## PC Connections

- **Jean-Claude** — was healed by Grigori's magic. The red light touched him. If the phylactery binding applies to anyone Grigori heals: Jean-Claude may be a thread. (DM decision — this has not been established in canon.)

> [!dm]
> The Jean-Claude binding question is yours to answer. If yes, it creates a ticking personal stake. If no, the mystery stays external. Decide before the party asks, not after.
