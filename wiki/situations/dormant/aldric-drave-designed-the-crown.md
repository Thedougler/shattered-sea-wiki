# Who Designed the Dravosi Crown

The Dravosi Crown was not built by colonists. It was designed by [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]] — a Hierarch who seeded his bloodline into every Dravosi noble family before the colonial project began, then built the Crown's administrative apparatus to track his own phylacteries across centuries. The Crown does not know it is operating inside a dead man's hoard.

## Key Actors

- [[aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]] — the Crown's hidden founder; a Hierarch lich whose bloodline underpins the entire Dravosi noble class; has been reading the same archives from beneath Tidefall for centuries
- [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] — the colonial institution; sincere in its bureaucracy and useful to Aldric precisely because of that sincerity
- [[fort-crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] — named after Aldric's house; contains his hidden lair and phylactery network
- [[drave-vaults|Drave Vaults]] — hidden lair and phylactery network beneath Fort Crestwall
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] — commission and heritage tie him to the Crown
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] — active species bounty pressure from the same Crown machinery
- [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] — Rattkin bounty pressure from the same Crown machinery

## The Design

The Crown's obsession with genealogy is not cultural vanity. It is infrastructure for Aldric's phylactery network. Noble marriage records, officer commissions, inheritance filings, bastardy settlements, adoption disputes, and bloodline certifications all help him locate living anchors. Most Crown officials believe this record-keeping exists to protect noble legitimacy. They are useful because they are sincere.

Fort Crestwall is named after his house. The documentation theology — ledgers before hello, every ship generating a document, every noble line kept legible — is his fingerprint. The phrase "the Crown remembers" predates the current colonial administration. In public it means institutional continuity. In truth it is almost literal: Aldric has been watching his descendants become governors, officers, smugglers, debtors, and patrol captains.

## Pressures

- The split between Governor and Admiralty is currently exploitable — the Governor in Port Tidefall can be boxed in by proper documents; the Admiralty harder to shame but easier to provoke into overreach
- Fort Crestwall has two extra companies under sealed orders the Governor never received — from the outside this only looks like a crowded garrison with sharper patrols
- [[who-commissioned-the-theft|Tessarine manipulation of the Pearl theft]] may have been intended to keep the Pearl away from Aldric

## If Ignored

Aldric continues operating through the Crown's institutional apparatus. Party members with Dravosi heritage or commission ties become more useful to his network without knowing it.

## At-Table Reveal

Late-game Crown play should reframe bureaucracy as necromancy without changing its texture. The ledgers were always creepy; the reveal is that they were also functional. Every tedious genealogy, every noble commission, every archival obsession has been feeding a dead founder's survival engine.

The Drave Vaults beneath Fort Crestwall are the phylactery network. Destroying them is not a military problem — it is a surgical one, and the Crown's archival apparatus is the only thing that can locate every anchor.
