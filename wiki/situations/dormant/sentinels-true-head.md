# Who Is the True Head of the Sentinels

The unbroken 200-year observation record is no coincidence of institutional continuity. The same consciousness has been watching the Maw for centuries. The true head of the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] is a [[soul-incarnate|Soul Incarnate]] — a truly neutral lich of the monk variety, their mummified body-phylactery sealed somewhere within the basalt sea stack beneath [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]].

It holds no colonial allegiances, seeks no power, and issues no commands that a living master like [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] would recognise as originating from above. It simply observes: the ultimate expression of the order's doctrine pushed past the threshold of death.

## Key Actors

- **The Soul Incarnate** — the order's undying head; has watched the Maw for two centuries; the current Maw escalation is the first time the record provides no comparison
- [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] — senior master; carries current party pressure; does not know the Soul Incarnate exists as a conscious entity, only that the order's founding documents are "very old"
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] — the party member closest to this secret; her pilgrimage places her closer to the Soul Incarnate's attention than anyone alive
- [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] — where the phylactery is sealed, within the basalt beneath the monastery

## What the Record Shows

The fissure record shows a clear pattern: after each disturbance, anomaly activity increases for weeks or months, then stabilises at a slightly higher baseline. The current period is the most significant in the record's history. The Sentinels logged all of it faithfully and drew no conclusions.

Two centuries of surface observation has produced no evidence of [[auralis|Auralis]]. The Sentinels do not know the Maw is a planar breach. Their discipline has kept them from the contact that would have given them the correct framework.

The Soul Incarnate has been waiting for an event with no precedent in the record. The current Maw escalation is the first time in two centuries the order's long memory cannot provide comparison. This does not make it panic. It makes it more attentive.

## Pressures

- The current Maw escalation is unprecedented — the Soul Incarnate is in active engagement with the record for the first time
- [[crissdalynns-pilgrimage|Crissdalynn's pilgrimage]] is incomplete; her return to Kyzil is the thread that leads here
- [[auralis|Auralis]] and the Soul Incarnate both watch the Maw from different sides and neither knows the other exists

## If Ignored

The Soul Incarnate continues its observation. The Maw crisis escalates without the party gaining access to the only complete record of what the Maw does across timescales — information that would reframe every other thread.

## At-Table Reveal

Late-game Sentinel play should reframe "unbroken institutional memory" as literal undead continuity. The reveal is that one consciousness carried the order's doctrine beyond death and made the record an extension of its own neutral observation.

The Soul Incarnate is not an enemy. It is the most complete witness to the Maw's history. It can tell the party what two hundred years of observation actually shows — something no living scholar could reconstruct from the ledgers alone.
