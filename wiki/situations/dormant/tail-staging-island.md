# Tail Staging Island

The primary staging point for [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] salvage is the closest inhabited [[tail|Tail]] island to the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]'s western edge. Intentionally unnamed and undetailed — it absorbs whatever pressure the Shelfworks arc requires without duplicating an existing Tail settlement.

> [!dm]
> Keep the blank deliberate. The island can become a resupply stop, salvage-camp settlement, factional pressure point, or hard-weather refuge as needed.

## Triggers

- Party needs supplies, crew, pumps, repairs, or local salvage gossip before reaching the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]].
- A faction needs a human-scale pressure point near the Maw without moving directly into the open-water site.
