# Catarina Primer

> Agent use: Read before building encounters that involve Catarina's concentration spells, ambushes/surprise rounds, or initiative-order pacing.

## Fast Read

- **Table role:** ranged blaster/controller with a stationary Eldritch Cannon; very high Investigation (18 passive) and Insight (14 passive).
- **Spotlight with:** puzzle/mechanism problems, appraising salvage or Antheri tech, turret-style firing positions.
- **Pressure with:** anything that threatens concentration — she has no War Caster, so every hit while concentrating is a flat CON save with no advantage.
- **Avoid:** surprise rounds and unseen attackers targeting her specifically — Alert makes both impossible.

## Encounter Levers

- **Give them:** a stable firing position for the cannon and line-of-sight targets; puzzles or salvage to read before anyone else can.
- **If struggling:** open lanes, isolate one target she can burn down with Fire Bolt/Shocking Grasp.
- **If dominating (the "teach her to appreciate War Caster" lever):** put her concentration spells (Faerie Fire, Web, Enlarge/Reduce, Enhance Ability, Levitate, Blur, See Invisibility) under real threat — grapplers, reach attackers, or anything that lands repeated hits while she's holding a spell. No advantage on the save means normal damage pressure already does the job; don't need to invent anti-magic gimmicks.
- **Avoid:** making it feel personal or making concentration auto-break every fight — let her make the save sometimes. The lesson lands harder if it's occasional and earned, not a standing tax.

## Alert — Ramifications

- **+5 initiative, can't be surprised, no unseen-attacker advantage against her.** Ambush encounters built around a surprise round need a different target — she'll act on a normal (usually early) initiative count and see attacks coming regardless of stealth or invisibility.
- **She will typically act first or near-first in combat.** Watch spotlight pacing — don't let her solve the opening round alone before other PCs get an action. Consider giving other PCs their own pre-combat beat (a tell, a choice) so the fight doesn't open and close on her turn.
- **Ambushes should target the rest of the party instead**, or use pressure Alert doesn't cover: ranged snipers she can see coming but not stop, environmental hazards on a delay, social/information threats, or effects that don't key off surprise or initiative at all.

## Power Spikes

| Level | Feature | Table Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Flash of Genius (add INT to a save, 1/short rest) | Her first real answer to concentration pressure without War Caster — a reactive fix, not a standing one. |
| 18 | Arcane Firearm | Turns a wand/staff/rod into a spellcasting focus with bonus damage — late-game power spike, not near-term. |

## Key Mechanics

| Factor | Use | Watch |
|---|---|---|
| No War Caster | Concentration saves are a flat roll — no advantage. | Real pressure comes from ordinary damage/grapple, not special anti-magic tools. |
| Alert | +5 initiative; immune to surprise and unseen-attacker advantage. | Personal to her — doesn't protect the rest of the party from ambush. |
| Eldritch Cannon | Stationary summon, 1/long rest. | A smart enemy threatens the cannon instead of her directly. |
| High Investigation/Insight (18/14 passive) | She reads scenes, clues, and people fast without rolling. | Let her notice things at range before anyone asks to check. |
