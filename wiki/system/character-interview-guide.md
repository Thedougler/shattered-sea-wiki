# Character Interview Guide

> Agent use: Read before interviewing a player or converting interview answers into PC canon.

## Fast Rules

- Ask short questions that expose wants, fears, boundaries, and relationships.
- Capture exact player answers before inferring lore.
- Convert answers into gravity wells, not exhaustive biographies.
- Flag gaps instead of inventing player intent.

A blank questionnaire for players to establish character foundations before or during [[session-zero|Session Zero]]. No wrong answers.

## The Basics

1. What's your character's name? Any nicknames or aliases?
2. How old are they, and do they look it?
3. What's the first thing someone notices about them?

## Who They Are

1. What do they want right now?
2. What are they afraid of — something real?
3. What do they believe that most people would disagree with?
4. What's a line they won't cross?

## Where They've Been

1. What did their life look like before all this?
2. What's the worst thing that's ever happened to them?
3. What's the worst thing they've ever done? Did they mean to?
4. Is there something in their past they're running from or trying to make right?

## Who They Know

1. Who's the most important person in their life, and where are they now?
2. Does anyone out there consider them an enemy?
3. Who do they trust, and why?

## How They Work

1. Do they talk first, fight first, or think first?
2. What do they do when they're scared?
3. How do they treat people with less power than them?

## For the DM

1. Give me one person from their past I can bring back — name, relationship, and whether it'd be a good or bad reunion.
2. Give me one thing they want badly enough to do something questionable for.
3. Give me one secret.
4. Is there anything you want to explore through this character — themes, a type of story, etc?

> Questions 18–21 are the most useful for the DM. The more honest, the better.
