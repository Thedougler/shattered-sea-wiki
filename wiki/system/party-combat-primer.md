# Party Combat Primer — Shattered Sea

> Agent use: Read before building encounters for the current party composition.

**Party level:** 4 | **Size:** 4 PCs | **Tier:** 1 (approaching tier 2)

## Fast Read

- Four-PC party, no dedicated healer — attrition pressure is real.
- Party excels at concentrated single-target damage and environmental repositioning. Design encounters that split attention or punish clustering.
- Mixed lighting is better than full darkness — [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] thrives in darkness while [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] and [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] are impaired by it.
- No reliable fire or acid damage source — regeneration or fire vulnerability creates meaningful tactical puzzles. No AoE damage — clustered enemies are a valid pressure axis.
- All four PCs have distinct mobility modes (flight, climb, swim, Cunning Action). Delmar now has boots of flying — two airborne PCs changes encounter geometry. Static encounters get trivialized; multi-axis terrain rewards the whole party.
- Bardic inspiration is the party's decisive force multiplier. Perrin's d8 repeatedly turned misses into hits and failed saves into passes across sessions 03–04. Targeting him is the smart tactical play — Kyzil identified this: "that drum is troubling."

### Incoming at Level 5

- **Jean-Claude** gets Extra Attack — his sustained damage nearly doubles. Encounters designed now for session 04 should account for this if the party might level mid-arc.
- **Crissdalynn** gets Stunning Strike or equivalent Kensei feature — save-or-stun on a PC with 3+ attacks per round is a major control spike.
- **Delmar** gets 3d6 Sneak Attack and Uncanny Dodge — harder to drop in one round.

## Observed Combat Patterns (Sessions 1–4)

| Pattern | Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Environmental repositioning | Decanter of Endless Water blasted Barnaby Rook off the gangplank (S01); party used terrain to isolate enemies in all sessions | Enemies need a way to hold ground or punish forced movement |
| Concentrated burst damage | Whip shark (S03) brought down through coordinated focus-fire; Delmar's sneak attack through Empty Wing Parry via inspiration (S04, 17 damage) | HP sponges without regeneration or damage mitigation get melted |
| Ambush lethality | S01 hold cleared without alerting topside; S04 Jean-Claude instant-killed a Grung from ceiling (24 damage, Dread Strike) — entirely one-sided when party has surprise + darkness + aerial advantage | Enemies that reward stealth approaches too easily make the encounter trivial; force a tradeoff between stealth and objectives |
| Grapple dominance | Crissdalynn grappled CR 14 Kyzil round 1 (Kyzil nat 1 contest, S04); she controls positioning against any enemy without Freedom of Movement or high contested Athletics | Enemies need grapple escape tools or the fight gets locked down fast |
| Bardic force multiplication | Perrin's inspiration saved two key moments in the Kyzil spar (S04) — rescued a failed save and enabled a hit through +5 AC reaction | Encounters with AC breakpoints at +3–5 above average rolls, multiple saves per round, or contested checks make the inspiration economy matter |
| Creative problem-solving | Jean-Claude identified Grung alchemical stock mid-fight; Delmar recruited Geoffrey mid-combat; Crissdalynn kingfishered a fleeing Grung at 45ft fly speed (S04) | Give encounters multiple solution paths — the party will find them |
| Solo monster lockdown | Kyzil spar (CR 14): party coordination (flanking, grapple, inspiration chaining) held a CR 14 for 3 rounds before it would have been trivialized (S04) | Solo monsters without legendary actions get locked down fast; design boss fights at CR 12–14 with legendary actions for 3–4 rounds of tension |
| Split-party comfort | Not yet tested at scale | The Calveno raid (4 attack points) will be the first real split-party pressure test |

## Resource Budget

No dedicated healer. Perrin has Cure Wounds (Bard) but it competes with control spells for slots. Party healing is:

- Perrin's spell slots (limited, concentration tradeoff)
- Healing potions (consumable, action economy cost)
- Crissdalynn's Patient Defense (avoidance, not recovery)
- Delmar's Uncanny Dodge (level 5)

**Design implication:** sustained multi-round fights with no rest between are genuinely dangerous. Two-encounter adventuring days hit harder than expected for this party. Don't stack back-to-back Hard encounters without a short rest opportunity. The party burns resources unevenly — Perrin's slots and inspiration go fast, Delmar's luck points are gone by round 2, Crissdalynn's focus points are steady, Jean-Claude is resource-light.

## Lighting Asymmetry

| Factor | Helps | Pressures | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full darkness | Jean-Claude (invisible + sees fine) | Crissdalynn (blinded), Delmar (blinded) | Treating it as a party buff — it splits the party's effectiveness |
| Dim light / shadow patches | Jean-Claude + sighted allies can both contribute | Enemies without darkvision | Overusing perfect darkness; dim light is almost always better |
| Low ceiling | Grounded melee tactics | Crissdalynn (flight denied) | Repeating anti-flight rooms — vary the constraint |
| Water / tight routes | Perrin, Jean-Claude (amphibious + climb) | Heavy or slow enemies | Making water only an escape button |
| Bright light / daylight | Crissdalynn, Delmar, Perrin | Jean-Claude (loses Umbral Sight) | Total shutdown of JC — patches of light are better than a flooded room |

## Prep Levers

- **Give them:** mixed terrain, mixed light, at least one target for each PC's core trick, and multiple axes of movement (horizontal + vertical + water if possible). Two PCs now fly — vertical terrain rewards more of the party than before.
- **If struggling:** add clearer lanes, dim light pockets, water/vertical options, vulnerable isolated targets, or an NPC ally with a specific tool (fire, healing, distraction).
- **If dominating:** split objectives, pressure concentration with AoE, add ranged enemies that force repositioning, introduce a clock (civilians, collapsing structure, reinforcements), or target Perrin to cut off the force multiplier.
- **Grapple pressure:** effective but use sparingly — Perrin has disadvantage on Strength and Crissdalynn/Jean-Claude are lightweight. One grappler per encounter is fair; two is oppressive. Note: Crissdalynn is the grappler now — enemies need Freedom of Movement or high contested Athletics to resist her.

## Avoid

- Stacking multiple hard shutdowns on the same PC.
- Full-darkness encounters that shut out 2 of 4 PCs for the entire fight.
- Static single-target-in-a-room encounters — the party will focus-fire and reposition it to death.
- Enemies with no answer to flight, climbing, or ranged attacks — at least one enemy needs reach, ranged, or vertical mobility.
- Regeneration or damage immunity without a discoverable counter — the party has no innate fire/acid; the environment must provide it or the puzzle is unsolvable.
- Back-to-back Hard encounters with no rest — the party's healing is too thin.
