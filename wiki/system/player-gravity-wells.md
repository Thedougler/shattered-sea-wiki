# Player Gravity Wells — Season 1

> Agent use: Read before tying prep, NPCs, temptations, or consequences to player motivations.

## Fast Map

- Use gravity wells to aim prep at player motivation.
- Each PC has dials that activate choices under pressure.
- Terminal nodes are desired futures, not guaranteed arcs.
- Cross-party collisions are high-value prep targets.

---

## [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] — player: Kaden

**Two Dials:**
- **Family loyalty** — the Tangle, the Run, the elders, what his Kin think of him. Everything he built was for them.
- **Staying in motion to avoid hard truths** — manipulation, bribery, the vengeance chase. He is not grieving; he is *doing*. The doing is the avoidance.

**Terminal Node:** Go home respected. Become a Warren elder who teaches young Rattkin how to survive the Dry.

**Active Friction:** The ship is gone and he hasn't told his family. His patron is reshaping his identity faster than he can reckon with. The vengeance chase may cost him the version of himself the Run hierarchy would accept back. He hasn't noticed that yet.

**Core tension:** The person he's becoming in pursuit of the terminal node may not be the person who can arrive there.

---

## [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] — player: Chad

**Two Dials:**
- **Uncompromising moral conviction** — slavery ends, no exceptions. Witnessing active abuse ends rational thought entirely.
- **Crushing guilt over his own past** — he was a fervent, skilled slave-catcher before his conscience woke. He has told no one.

**Terminal Node:** Return to the Botukuri clan and end the slave system. Finish what Pell's death started. Live the life [[pell|Pell]] never got to live.

**Active Friction:** Cannot lie. Uneasy around birds (grung instinct — manageable with Crissdalynn if she stays calm). [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] commands the garrison back home. His slave-catcher history is a secret that, when it surfaces, will directly contradict the person he is performing.

**Core tension:** His moral absolute is real — but so is the man who built it. The day someone learns who he was, his conviction will be tested by the exact people it's supposed to protect.

## [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] — player: Frederick

**Two Dials:**
- **Generosity and his mother's ethic** — everyone has value regardless of station. Genuine warmth. Instinctive extension of trust even when exploited.
- **Willingness to compromise everything for his captains** — he said it himself, plainly. The guardrails come off. He knows it. He's not sure it's wrong.

**Terminal Node:** Resurrect or release the five captains. Not a long game — an obsession. Everything else is incidental until this is resolved.

**Active Friction:** Five voices in his head. Umberlee needs him alive and he can't afford to ignore her. The [[dead-lady|Dead Lady]] follows him. He is avoiding his home island — his mother, Maggie, and the families of dead sailors all wait there. The enemies made by the fleet's loss have not arrived yet.

**Core tension:** The man who extends generosity to everyone will, when backed into a corner on the captains, choose wrong and know it. The question is who pays for it.

## [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]]

**Two Dials:**
- **Need for belonging and approval** — the banishment is the wound under everything. She wants to be let back in. She has not said that out loud.
- **Absolute loyalty to found family** — Fisk is the fixed point. His opinion is the measure. If he's in danger, the lightness drains out completely.

**Terminal Node:** Return to the [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] accepted. Earn [[master-kyzil|Kyzil's]] approval. Complete the pilgrimage and be let back into the thing that cast her out at fourteen.

**Active Friction:** The pilgrimage is incomplete. Fisk's mission pulls directly toward the [[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] — exactly where her unfinished task waits. JC's wariness around birds means the party member she's closest to has a companion who may be destabilized by her presence. Kyzil's approval may require things that conflict with Fisk's survival.

**Core tension:** Her two dials will eventually point in opposite directions. Completing the pilgrimage may cost Fisk something. Protecting Fisk may delay or compromise the pilgrimage. She doesn't know she'll have to choose.

## Cross-Party Collision Points

- **Perrin + Crissdalynn:** Prey response to large predatory birds + Crissdalynn's size = a dial-activating dynamic in every tense moment.
- **JC + Crissdalynn:** Wariness around birds + Crissdalynn as party member = ongoing low-level friction neither can resolve without changing who they are.
- **[[the-drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] convergence:** Delmar's Pearl of Souls is at the Maw. Crissdalynn's incomplete pilgrimage is at the Maw. Perrin's Leviathan came through the Maw. Three gravity wells pointing at one location.
- **JC's slave conviction + Verdant Teeth distance:** The moment the campaign turns toward JC's home territory, his terminal node activates hard — and his secret becomes a live grenade.
- **Dial conflict tent pole:** A faction that offers to free slaves (JC's terminal node) in exchange for betraying a Rattkin ally (Perrin's family loyalty) is structurally inevitable given the party composition. Both PCs are gravitationally compelled to engage from opposite directions.
